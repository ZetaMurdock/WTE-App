<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p><b>Xothos</b> is an alternate dimension outside of <b>Enon’s Heart</b>, a place of unique rules, civilizations, and histories.
</p><p>Those connected to Xothos live with a perspective <b>beyond the conventional multiverse</b>, where creation, destruction, and reality itself may differ from known worlds.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Your_Role">Your Role</span></h2>
<p>To select this background, you <b>must be of the Racial Class</b>:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Omenity</b> or</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Asternem</b></li></ul>
<p>Your backstory will be shaped by the <b>dimension of Xothos</b>, not by events within Enon’s Heart.
</p><p>You are free to:
</p>
<ul><li>Design a <b>world within Xothos</b>—its cultures, histories, and conflicts</li></ul>
<ul><li>Create your character’s <b>personal history and motivations</b> tied to that world</li></ul>
<ul><li>Explore unique abilities, traditions, or lore consistent with Xothos</li></ul>
