<h1><span id="The_Derived_.28Background.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Derived_(Background)">The Derived (Background)</span></h1>
<p>No two of the Derived are the same.
</p><p>But all of them remember the same thing.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p>The <b>Derived</b> come from countless origins across the galaxy. Different worlds, different cultures, different histories — often with no visible connection at all.
</p><p>And yet, every Derived shares a <b>single dream</b>.
</p><p>The details are never identical, but the feeling always is. A place that does not exist. A moment that never ends. A presence that is neither voice nor thought, but certainty.
</p><p>No one knows where the dream came from.
</p><p>Only that it is remembered.
</p><p>When two or more Derived meet, something happens — a brief mental resonance, like <b>static before a storm</b>. A flicker of recognition. A pressure behind the eyes. A connection that fades as quickly as it appears.
</p><p>It cannot be controlled.
</p><p>It cannot be ignored.
</p><p>What the dream means — and why it binds the Derived — is left to interpretation.
</p>
