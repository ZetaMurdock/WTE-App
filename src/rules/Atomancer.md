<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Atomancer">Archetype: <i>Atomancer</i></span></h1>
<p><i>Reality is not solid. It is arranged.</i>
</p><p>The <b>Atomancer</b> is an archetype centered on <b>atomic-scale control</b>, electromagnetic phenomena, radiation theory, and elemental construction. Rather than altering reality wholesale, the Atomancer manipulates the <b>fundamental interactions</b> that govern matter and energy—charges, waves, particles, and decay—allowing them to weaponize physics itself.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  The Atomancer is a <b>battlefield manipulator and destructive controller</b>, capable of reshaping engagements through electromagnetic forces, radiation saturation, and elemental restructuring. They dominate space by controlling how matter and energy behave within it.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  Early combat focuses on <b>analysis, charge placement, and radiation seeding</b>. Mid-combat sees escalation through wave manipulation, magnetic control, and compound effects. Late combat turns the battlefield into a hostile environment as radiation spreads, gravity warps, and atomic reactions chain together.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  Atomancers require <b>knowledge, setup, and positioning</b>. Their most powerful effects depend on prior analysis, existing radiation, or environmental materials. They are vulnerable to anti-energy suppression, forced close-quarters without preparation, and overexertion leading to instability.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  <b>Atomic Interface</b> — the ability to perceive, manipulate, and weaponize electromagnetic waves, radiation, and elemental structures at their source.</li></ul>
<p><i>The Atomancer rewards foresight, escalation, and intelligent abuse of physics.</i>
</p>

<p>Upon choosing <b>Atomancer</b>, your Actor gains its <b>Asymmetry</b>, reflecting the archetype’s focus on <b>layered execution and environmental dominance</b>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy">Action Economy</span></h2>
<ul><li><b><i>4</i> Standard Actions</b>  Used for attacks, charge placement, wave manipulation, crafting compounds, or radiation deployment.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>1</i> True Opportunity Attack</b>  Triggered when an enemy moves through irradiated zones, magnetic fields, or violates an established atomic condition.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>1</i> Free Action</b>  Used for toggling radiation states, shifting elemental configurations, or interacting with Manifesting Tables.</li></ul>
<p>Atomancers act deliberately—each action compounds future effects.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Atomic_Interface">Aspect: <i>Atomic Interface</i></span></h1>
<p>All <b>Atomancer</b> actors begin with the <i><b>Atomic Interface</b></i>, representing <b>direct perception and manipulation of matter and energy at the atomic level</b>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function"><i>Base Function</i></span></h2>
<p>The Atomic Interface allows the Atomancer to perceive and interact with:
</p>
<ul><li>Electromagnetic radiation (all 7 wave types)</li></ul>
<ul><li>Atomic charge, magnetism, and radiation</li></ul>
<ul><li>Elemental composition and compound structures</li></ul>
<p>Through this Aspect, the Atomancer can:
</p>
<ul><li>Place and detonate <b>electromagnetic charges</b></li></ul>
<ul><li>Magnetize objects, terrain, or entities</li></ul>
<ul><li>Emit or transform electromagnetic waves</li></ul>
<ul><li>Seed radiation that persists and spreads over time</li></ul>
<p>Certain effects escalate based on <b>existing radiation</b>, allowing chain reactions and area saturation.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b>  Physical damage, conventional illusion, mundane interference.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b>  Anti-radiation fields, null-energy zones, forced disconnection from matter or perception.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b>  The Atomancer loses consciousness, leaves the affected area, or the radiation/energy naturally decays.</li></ul>
