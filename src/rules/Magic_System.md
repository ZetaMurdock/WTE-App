<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Points">Action Points</span></h2>
<p>In the Wonderland Of the Enigma Action Points (AP) are utilized to govern the actions and casting of spells by characters. Action Points represent the character's focus, energy, and concentration. Each character has a certain number of Action Points available to them, which can be spent to perform actions, cast spells, or activate special abilities.
</p><p>Characters start each encounter with a specific amount of Action Points, which can be influenced by attributes, skills, or equipment. Throughout the encounter, characters can use their Action Points to cast spells, perform magical actions, or engage in combat maneuvers.
</p><p>== Power Scaling and Spellcasting == In our immersive magic system, the effectiveness and strength of spells are determined by the character's mastery, focus, and strategic decision-making, rather than relying solely on dice rolls.
</p><p>Power scaling is based on various factors and actions performed by the character. These factors include:
</p>
<ul><li>Mastery Level: The character's mastery level in a specific magical discipline determines the potency and effectiveness of their spells. Higher mastery levels result in increased damage or greater impact.</li>
<li>Elemental Affinity: Characters with elemental abilities can tap into their affinity, allowing them to channel more power when casting elemental spells. This affinity grants a boost to the spell's damage or effectiveness.</li>
<li>Tactical Decisions: Players can make strategic choices during combat or spellcasting, utilizing their Action Points to enhance the potency of their spells or increase their chances of success.</li>
<li>Special Abilities: Characters may possess unique special abilities or talents that augment their spells, granting additional effects or increasing their overall power.</li></ul>
<p>By expending Action Points during spellcasting, characters can enhance the strength, duration, or area of effect of their spells. These decisions are made based on the narrative context, tactical considerations, and the character's resource management.
</p>
