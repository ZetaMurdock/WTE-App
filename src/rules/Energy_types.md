<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Energy_Types_and_Classifications">Energy Types and Classifications</span></h2>
<p>The various energy types in "Wonderland of the Enigma" (WTE) break down into different magic-based mechanics, each comprising unique properties and interactions. Understanding these energy types helps to comprehend how certain abilities and phenomena interact within the game world. Each archetype and sub-archetype follows an energy type that falls under a specific classification, such as Conjuring, Manipulation, or Abjuration.
</p><p>Let's explore the four main classifications of energy types, along with the fifth special type: Enhancement, Materialization, Emission, Divination, and the special type, Trans-modification.
</p>

<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Detailed_Breakdown_of_Energy_Types">Detailed Breakdown of Energy Types</span></h4>
<p><b>Neutral Energy:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Description:</b> Neutral Energy relates to abilities considered magical but don't fit into a particular category. For example, the Verbal Annihilator class deals with Neutral Energy, allowing the user to speak destruction into the world.</li>
<li><b>Classification:</b> Trans-modification.</li>
<li><b>Example:</b> The Verbal Annihilator can speak destruction into existence, making it a prime example of Trans-modification.</li></ul>
<p><b>Eldritch Energy:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Description:</b> Eldritch Energy powers creations and fundamentally enhances them. While its primary classification is Enhancement, the creations themselves can fall under different classifications.</li>
<li><b>Classification:</b> Enhancement.</li>
<li><b>Example:</b> The Doll Archetype, which modifies existing phenomena to create new effects, originates from Eldritch Energy. While Doll's abilities might be considered Trans-modification, the energy type remains Enhancement.</li></ul>
<p><b>Null Energy:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Description:</b> Null Energy is primarily associated with modifying phenomena by absorbing, removing, or dissolving them. It's fundamentally classified as Trans-modification.</li>
<li><b>Classification:</b> Trans-modification.</li>
<li><b>Example:</b> Void Palm, an ability that creates physical constructs to erase other energy types, is a form of Trans-modification. Null Energy isn't bound by the laws of WTE's base cosmology and is considered heretical, interacting only with Eldritch Energy from parallel dimensions.</li></ul>
<p><b>Elemental Energy:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Description:</b> Elemental Energy covers a wide range of attributes and doesn't fit neatly into a single classification. It most closely aligns with Trans-modification, similar to Null Energy but without negating other energy types.</li>
<li><b>Classification:</b> Trans-modification.</li>
<li><b>Example:</b> Abilities involving fire, water, earth, and air manipulation typically fall under Elemental Energy, modifying existing elements without negating them.</li></ul>
<p><b>Kinetic Energy:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Description:</b> Kinetic Energy is straightforward, involving the movement and enhancement of physical force. It primarily falls under Emission but has strong ties to Enhancement.</li>
<li><b>Classification:</b> Emission (primary), Enhancement (secondary).</li>
<li><b>Example:</b> Abilities that involve the direct emission of force or energy, such as telekinesis or rapid movement techniques, are classified under Kinetic Energy.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Summary">Summary</span></h2>
<p>Understanding the classifications and interactions of these energy types enhances gameplay and strategic planning in WTE. By categorizing energy types into Enhancement, Materialization, Emission, Divination, and Trans-modification, players can better predict and counter various magical abilities and phenomena.
</p>
