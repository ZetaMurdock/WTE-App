# Remnant Ciphers

WTE · MECHANICS · PARADIGM CIPHER · REMNANT

## Remnant Paradigm Overview

The Remnant Paradigm distributes its Ciphers to Inquisitors based on mission completion, demonstrated usefulness, and dedication to the Paradigm's collective objectives. This Paradigm thrives on service — it exists to be indispensable, adaptable, and unwaveringly functional in fulfilling the agendas of both the Paradigm itself and its allies.

To join the Remnant Paradigm is to accept a role of complete commitment. Most who become Inquisitors of the Remnant begin with nothing, and in return, the Paradigm demands everything. Total loyalty and selfless contribution are the cornerstones of its philosophy. In return, Inquisitors gain access to the Remnant's advanced Ciphers and technologies — gifts reserved only for those willing to sacrifice comfort for purpose.

Those aligned with the Remnant Paradigm act as the swords and shields of the greater order — government contractors, enforcers, and operatives who go where they are needed most. The Ciphers of the Remnant enhance physical and environmental interaction, granting Inquisitors the ability to perform superhuman feats: leap across towering structures, excavate tunnels nearly forty feet deep, or stabilize collapsing terrain.

**REMNANT CREED** — Utility through sacrifice — the belief that purpose transcends self, and that value is measured by one's capacity to serve. A tool serves; it does not execute — every Remnant Cipher resolves through its listed damage, Checks, Saves, and Roll Paths, never past them.

## Offline Ciphers · TL ≤ 2.0 · Fixed / Unregulated

### Multitool

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 15 |
| Action | Bonus / Prepared Action |
| Rank | Wanderer |
| Component | Physical Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — Allows the attached Cipher to transform a chosen object into one of five adaptive forms. Available Forms: Screwdriver (enhances precision during tinkering/repair), Wrench (increased mechanical leverage for machinery), Mini Laser Blade (compact high-intensity energy edge for cutting; as a weapon, 1d8 Fire and the standard Roll Paths of its weapon form), Distance Enhancer (projects a simulated reconstruction of a distant object within 3 feet for full visual/spatial analysis), EMP Module (controlled pulse to disable automated systems, excluding cybernetic/cyborg-level processors).

EFFECT — Upon activation, the Cipher permanently links to the object it modifies, altering its internal structure to accommodate modular transformation. Each form functions autonomously, drawing on the user's energy or environmental reserves as needed. The Remnant Paradigm often equips field operatives with this modification due to its tactical flexibility.

### Spyder

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 15 |
| Action | Bonus action |
| Rank | Wanderer |
| Component | Inanimate Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When embedded into an object, this Cipher grants it animated properties, awakening a limited, synthetic form of motion and intent. The object becomes partially autonomous, capable of executing simple actions in response to the Inquisitor's commands or instinctual needs, gaining the ability to apply Intrusion.

EFFECT — The animated entity actively assists its wielder, performing supportive or defensive tasks. It has the unique ability to imbed itself into any Inanimate Object or Animate Subject. Once embedded, it establishes a direct Causal Connection with any active Cipher or Genus ability. Delay Activation: requires a Mental Check — Capacity (DV 20) — on a success, the activation of the connected Cipher or Genus is suspended and set to trigger automatically at any designated moment within a 1-Hour (ITT) window. Forceful Activation: converts a failed Check or Save associated with the connected capability into a Minimum Success; this forced resolution is delayed, executing immediately after the player's current turn ends in the Action Priority stack. Reactivation: bypasses standard cooling protocols to instantly reactivate a Cipher expended up to 2 turns prior.

**LIMIT:** One Causal Connection held at a time; each function (Delay, Forceful, Reactivation) cycles once per encounter. Embedding into an Animate Subject is felt — an unwilling host makes a Physical Save — Recovery to resist the embedding.

### Quick Hack

|---|---|
| SS | 15 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Wanderer |
| Component | Inanimate Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When affixed to an object, the Cipher opens a protected diagnostic channel between the Inquisitor, nearby technological infrastructure, and the Remnant Paradigm's operational framework. ### Pasting View

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 25 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Wanderer |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an animate subject, this Cipher automatically migrates to the ocular nerve, embedding within the eye — a painless integration leaving a subtle luminescent ring within the iris that activates only when in use.

EFFECT — While integrated, grants the Inquisitor the ability to visually reconstruct past events within a target area. By focusing their vision on a location, the user can perceive through any duration of time as long as a piece of the original Material present in the situation is present. This reconstruction manifests as ghostlike, translucent echoes of action — motion and behavior rendered clearly, though individual faces, identifiers, and fine details remain indistinct. Echo Extraction: after observing an event through Pasting View, the Inquisitor may select one nonliving object visible within the reconstruction and pull an energy-equivalent echo of that object into the present. The extracted object retains the functional properties it possessed within the observed event and may be used for up to 4 Actions; after the fourth Action, the echo disperses. Echo Conduction: while the extracted object exists, it may act as a remote origin for the Inquisitor's Remnant Ciphers. When activating a Cipher through the echo, choose any point within 5 ft of the extracted object and treat that point as the Inquisitor's location for determining the Cipher's origin. The activated Cipher then uses its normal Reach measured outward from that chosen point. All normal SS costs, action costs, targets, Components, limits, Checks, Saves, and Roll Paths still apply.

### Phase

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 40 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Dependent |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When affixed to a living subject, the Cipher attaches to the spinal column, integrating with the nervous system — externally a small reflective chip at the base of the neck, pulsing faintly in rhythm with the subject's energy signature.

EFFECT — While active, the subject continually produces a delayed energy reconstruction of their physical form. Whenever the subject is outside the direct sight of all hostile observers, the subject and anything they are holding, grabbing, carrying, wearing, or otherwise keeping on their person gain the Phase condition. While Phase is active, conventional vision does not perceive the subject's true physical body. When an observer gains line of sight, they instead see the subject's delayed afterimage occupying and repeating the user's visible movement. The true body remains perceptible only through Energy Vision, energy-sensitive Perception, or another effect explicitly capable of perceiving phased, energetic, or metaphysical forms. Phase does not erase sound, environmental disturbance, nonvisual senses, area effects, or other methods of locating the subject that do not depend on ordinary sight. Objects removed from the subject's possession lose Phase immediately.

**PHASE CONDITION:** the bearer exists physically but is visually represented by a delayed energy afterimage. Conventional sight tracks the afterimage rather than the bearer's true body; Energy Vision and equivalent senses perceive the bearer normally.

### Vecravel

|---|---|
| SS | 45 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Dependent |
| Component | Inanimate Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When affixed to an object, causes the item to partially submerge into the terrain beneath it without breaking cohesion or stability — absorbed slowly into the surface tension of the ground, suspended between physical and sub-physical states until activation.

### Tighten

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 35 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Dependent |
| Component | Inanimate Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When affixed to an object, instills a latent compression field within its structure. If altered, damaged, or reshaped in any way, the object begins to constrict upon itself, the material tightening as though wrapped by invisible threads of high-tension wire — silent but deliberate, with faint creaking as it resists further alteration.

EFFECT — Against an unattended object, the object's frame contracts violently and folds inward, twisting until it crushes its own mass into an unusable fragment. Any external material attached to or dependent on the object is also pulled inward, rendering the target nonfunctional and irrecoverable without advanced restorative procedures. The process causes no explosive release or shrapnel — only a controlled collapse, as though the object implodes into itself and ceases to exist in its usable form. Occupied Object: if TIGHTEN is applied to an object currently held, worn, or directly operated by a creature, the Compression Field cannot destroy the object outright. The bearer chooses either a Physical Check — Power or a Physical Save — Recovery. On failure, the affected object becomes Jammed until repaired or until the bearer spends an appropriate Action physically freeing, resetting, or reconfiguring it. A Jammed object cannot perform its primary function while the condition remains.

**KEYWORD — COMPRESSION FIELD:** unattended objects may be collapsed completely. Held, worn, or directly operated objects instead use the Occupied Object ruling; living matter is always released before the field closes.

## Online Ciphers · TL 3.0+ · Regulated / Upgradeable / Logged

### Bygone

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 65 |
| Action | Prepared Action |
| Rank | Chosen / Specialist |
| Component | Inanimate Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When affixed to an object, that object ceases to exist in the physical state and is converted into a stable metaphysical construct, sustained entirely as organized energy rather than matter. In this form it cannot be touched, moved, or affected by normal physical means, and can only be perceived or manipulated through technology or abilities specifically designed to interact with metaphysical constructs. Its internal atomic and nucleic structure remains perfectly intact and preserved within the energetic pattern.

EFFECT — When activated, anything directly touching the object at that moment becomes temporarily influenced by its properties, shifting into a similar metaphysical state — only able to be seen, affected, and interacted with by metaphysical constructs or compatible technologies. While in this state, an animate subject gains advantage on Mental Saves — Perception, Physical Saves — Evasion, and Physical Checks — Density, and their movement speed is effectively doubled (moving as if gliding yet still subject to gravity's general pull). To pass through a chosen solid object, the subject makes a Physical Check — Density and adds their NC Mod against a Dice Value determined by the object's nature and Curator interpretation. A metaphysical subject cannot deal physical-state damage until they rematerialize.
