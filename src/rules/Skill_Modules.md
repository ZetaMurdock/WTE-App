# Skill Modules

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p>Welcome to the Skills Creation Page, where we will delve into the world of Skill Modules, Skill Upgrades, and Customization. Skill Modules provide players with an additional layer of creativity and customization, enhancing their gameplay experience. These modules are organized into a tier system and can be discovered within the enigmatic Wonderland of the Enigma and the mystical realm of Enon's Heart.
</p><p>To better understand Skill Modules, let's begin by exploring their tier structure and how they contribute to the player's progression and abilities.
</p>

<pre>Within each tier, players will encounter different types of Skill Modules that align with specific themes and gameplay styles. These modules are carefully crafted to fit into the overall narrative and mechanics of the game, offering a cohesive and immersive experience.
