# Hybrid Weapon

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #0a2014;padding:8px 14px;margin-bottom:12px;font-family:monospace;font-size:0.82em;color:#408060;">
<p><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Weapons_Guide">Weapons</a>&nbsp;› <strong class="mw-selflink selflink">Hybrid Weapon</strong>
</p>
</div><div style="background:#121212;border-left:4px solid #1a4828;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:18px;"><div style="font-size:1.6em;font-weight:bold;color:#4a9860;letter-spacing:0.06em;font-family:monospace;">HYBRID WEAPON</div><div style="color:#408060;font-size:0.85em;margin-top:4px;font-style:italic;">Two-system weapons: kinetic+energy, bio+tech, organic+mechanical. Adaptive. Complex to manufacture.</div></div><div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #0a2014;border-left:3px solid #1a4828;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;color:#80b090;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>Hybrid weapons combine two mechanical systems in a single frame. Some are engineering achievements — a kinetic rifle with a neural guidance overlay, a plasma lance that also fires a ballistic sabot. Others are something closer to alive: organic-grown chassis threaded with energy filament, blades that listen to the Incept state of the person holding them.
</p><p>Hybrid weapons are the most adaptable category. They can be configured, switched between modes, or enhanced by both Genus abilities and Cipher systems. They are more expensive and harder to maintain than single-category weapons — but the tactical options they open are often unique to them.
</p>
Translated from WH40k's combi-weapons and force-tech composites, Trench Crusade's adapted relic-weapons, and WTE's own bio-engineering and Paradigm R&amp;D programs.<div style="background:#0a1a10;border-left:2px solid #1a4828;padding:8px 12px;margin-top:10px;color:#608060;font-size:0.88em;">
<p><b>Mode Switching:</b> Many Hybrid weapons have two modes. Switching costs 1 Action — or 1 Reaction if the character has Hybrid Mastery Varna 1+.
</p>
</div></div>

<p><b>Resonant Blade</b> — Two modes: Kinetic mode (2d8 Physical, Penetrating 1) or Energy mode (2d6+1d4 Energy). Switch as 1 Action.
</p><p><b>Incept Blade</b> — Blade resonates with wielder's Incept Expression State. Dormant: 2d6 Physical. Suppressed: 3d6 Physical+Void. Normal: 3d8 Void. Stable Active: 4d10 Void, gains Sever.
</p><p><b>Bio-Pistol</b> — Organic launcher with kinetic internals. Bio-linked charge turns Corrosive to (2) and adds 1d4 Neural for 1 attack.
</p><p><b>Tactical Emitter</b> — Multi-mode utility tool. Kinetic mode: 1d8 Physical, Burst 2, 25 ft. Energy mode: 1d8+1d4 Heat, Melee, Penetrating 1.

<div style="background:#1a1a1a;border-left:3px solid #1a4828;padding:10px 14px;margin-top:10px;color:#709080;font-size:0.88em;">
<p><b>Biomech Rifle</b> — Living rifle that grows around the wielder. Bio-linked charge adds 1 round of Corrosive (2) to all shots. After 3 kills, weapon temporarily evolves: +1d4 to all damage for the scene. Evolution Paradigm Signature at Rank 2.
</p><p><b>Genome Blade</b> — Adapts to target DNA — first hit tags the target; subsequent hits against same target deal +1d6. Incept-Reactive damage adds to the Bio component. Evolution Signature at Rank 3.
</p><p><b>Isothermic Lance</b> — Science-engineered precision hybrid. Both damage types apply simultaneously. At full range (100–150 ft), damage is maximized (no distance penalty). Science Paradigm Rank 3+ only; Paradigm-crafted Signature weapon.
</p><p><b>Amplified Kinetic</b> — Standard rifle with neural guidance overlay. Default: standard Kinetic ranged. Neural mode: converts to Neural keyword (costs 1 SS per activation).
</p><p><b>Remnant Composite</b> — Field-assembled from kinetic and energy recovery cells. Cannot malfunction (Remnant-Forged). Can run on either Kinetic or Energy mode — switch is free.
</p>

</p><p><b>Amplified Strike Gauntlet</b> — Power fist equivalent. Creatures 2+ Size Classes smaller are automatically Prone on hit. Cannot be Parried. Sever on critical.
</p><p><b>Living Artillery</b> — Self-targeting bio-cannon. Can be fired autonomously (uses wielder's Perception; −2 vs. wielder's Action). Bio-linked: cannon targets two separate points in one Action (splitting dice between both).
</p><p><b>Reality Composite Siege</b> — Kinetic frame with Exotic payload. Physical damage bypasses armor; Void damage bypasses DHP separately — <b>both apply independently.</b> Requires 2 Actions to fire. Triggers Sanction-level Audit in Managed zones.
</p><p><b>Harrower-Prime</b> — Warfare Paradigm pinnacle. Rank 4+ only; one per Inquisitor lifetime. Paradigm-issued. Burst shots share the Energy component. Breach applies to both damage types.
