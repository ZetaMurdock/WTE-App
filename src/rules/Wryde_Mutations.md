# Wryde Mutations

Whenever an Incept manifests, it **automatically** produces a Wryde — a visible,
behavioural, or sensory alteration tied to that Incept's expression.

Two rules bound every Wryde:

*   A Wryde affects **only the character**. It never alters the external world.
*   The type is **fixed at the moment the Incept is gained** — you do not re-roll
    it later, and it does not drift on its own. *Growth* is the one exception,
    and it says so.

---

### The Table

| Wryde | Chance | d100 | Expression |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stagnant** | 40% | 1–40 | Cosmetic and stable; does not evolve or change over time |
| **Radioactive** | 15% | 41–55 | Emits pressure, heat, energy, or discomfort without environmental alteration |
| **Growth** | 20% | 56–75 | Evolves slowly as more Incepts are gained |
| **Mental Fog** | 10% | 76–85 | Causes cognitive interference, intrusive thoughts, or perceptual distortion |
| **Harder Anatomy** | 5% | 86–90 | Physical structure toughens or densifies |
| **Sentient** | 3% | 91–93 | The mutation possesses awareness or limited intent |
| **Corrupt** | 7% | 94–100 | Dangerous, malformed, or unstable — may carry risk to the character |

---

### Weight Class decides how wild it gets

An Incept's **Weight Class** sets how many times you roll this table. You keep
the **most chaotic** result — the table runs benign at the top, dangerous at the
bottom.

| Weight | Rolls | Focus cost |
|---|---|---|
| Light | 1 | 3 |
| Medium | 2 — keep the worse | 3 |
| Heavy | 3 — keep the worst | **5** |

A heavy Incept warps whatever carries it. It is not that a Heavy *can* roll
Corrupt where a Light cannot — both can — it is that a Heavy almost never
settles for something merely cosmetic.

**What that does to the odds:**

| Wryde | Light | Medium | Heavy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stagnant | 40% | 16% | 6% |
| Radioactive | 15% | 14% | 10% |
| Growth | 20% | 26% | 26% |
| Mental Fog | 10% | 16% | 19% |
| Harder Anatomy | 5% | 9% | 12% |
| Sentient | 3% | 6% | 8% |
| **Corrupt** | 7% | 14% | **20%** |

Read the bottom four rows together — the genuinely disruptive outcomes run
**25% → 44% → 58%** as Weight rises. A Heavy Incept is better than a coin flip to
leave you with a body that causes you problems, and one time in five that problem
is *Corrupt*.

---

### The cost of weight

A Heavy Incept costs **5 Synaptic Focus** — more than maxing a Genus outright,
which is 4. That is deliberate. A Heavy Incept charges you twice:

*   **In Focus**, at 5 against a rank-9 budget of 30.
*   **In Wryde**, by rolling three times and keeping the worst.

At rank 0, with 3 points, a Heavy Incept is **not affordable at all** — the
capstone abilities are things you grow into, not things you open with.

---

### Curator note

Wrydes also fire outside normal advancement. An **Incept Drift** from the
[Revelation Snap](wte://rules/The_Polarized_Soul_System) rolls on this table too,
as the body mirrors a violent moral realignment.

When a character carries several Incepts, their standing Wryde tier is set by
their **heaviest** one — a single Heavy pick drags the whole sheet into chaotic
territory, however many Light Incepts sit beside it.
