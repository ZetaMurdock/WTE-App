# Wryde Mutations

Whenever an Incept manifests, it **automatically** produces a Wryde — a visible,
behavioural, or sensory alteration tied to that Incept's expression.

Two rules bound every Wryde:

*   A Wryde affects **only the character**. It never alters the external world.
*   The type is **fixed at the moment the Incept is gained** — you do not re-roll
    it later, and it does not drift on its own (unless it is a *Growth* Wryde,
    which is the one type that evolves).

---

### The Table

| Wryde | Chance | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| **Stagnant** | 40% | Cosmetic and stable; does not evolve or change over time |
| **Radioactive** | 15% | Emits pressure, heat, energy, or discomfort without environmental alteration |
| **Growth** | 20% | Evolves slowly as more Incepts are gained |
| **Mental Fog** | 10% | Causes cognitive interference, intrusive thoughts, or perceptual distortion |
| **Harder Anatomy** | 5% | Physical structure toughens or densifies |
| **Sentient** | 3% | The mutation possesses awareness or limited intent |
| **Corrupt** | 2% | Dangerous, malformed, or unstable — may carry risk to the character |

---

### Weight Class decides how wild it gets

An Incept's **Weight Class** sets how many times you roll this table. You keep
the **most chaotic** result — the table runs benign at the top to dangerous at
the bottom.

| Weight | Rolls |
|---|---|
| Light | 1 |
| Medium | 2 — keep the worse |
| Heavy | 3 — keep the worst |

A heavy Incept warps whatever carries it. It is not that it *can* roll Corrupt
where a light one cannot — both can — it is that a heavy Incept almost never
settles for something merely cosmetic.

**What that does to the odds:**

| Wryde | Light | Medium | Heavy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stagnant | 42% | 18% | 7% |
| Radioactive | 16% | 16% | 12% |
| Growth | 21% | 29% | 30% |
| Mental Fog | 11% | 18% | 22% |
| Harder Anatomy | 5% | 10% | 13% |
| Sentient | 3% | 6% | 9% |
| Corrupt | 2% | 4% | 6% |

Read the bottom four rows together — the genuinely disruptive outcomes run
**21% → 38% → 51%** as Weight rises. Taking a Heavy Incept is a coin flip on
whether your body starts causing you problems.

---

### Curator note

The published percentages total **95**, not 100. Rolls of 96–100 are re-rolled,
which keeps every listed probability in exact proportion rather than inventing an
eighth outcome. If a Wryde is ever written for that band, the table closes on its
own and nothing else changes.

Wrydes also fire outside normal advancement: an **Incept Drift** from the
[Revelation Snap](wte://rules/The_Polarized_Soul_System) rolls here too, as the
body mirrors a violent moral realignment.
