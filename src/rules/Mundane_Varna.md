<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mundane_Varna">Mundane Varna</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="What_Mundane_Varna_Is">What Mundane Varna Is</span></h3>
<p>The physical body learns faster than the conscious mind. Repeated exposure to the same weapon, the same weight, the same feedback through handle or barrel, builds something that training alone cannot — a pre-response. A reflex that exists before the thought fully forms.
</p><p>In WTE, this manifests as Mundane Varna: a set of instantaneous combat techniques that activate alongside weapon attacks without requiring a separate action, decision, or cost. Mundane Varna is not taught. It is <b>demonstrated</b>. The Director observes and declares when it has been earned.
</p><p>A character with Mundane Varnas does not choose to activate them. They simply occur — add-on effects that resolve at the end of the action that triggered them, stacking with every other eligible Varna and all standard weapon keywords.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Earning_Mundane_Varna">Earning Mundane Varna</span></h3>
<table class="fandom-table">
<tbody><tr>
<th>Requirement
</th>
<th>Detail
</th></tr>
<tr>
<td>Weapon Category Consistency
</td>
<td>Use the same weapon category (Kinetic, Energy, Exotic, or Hybrid) as your primary offensive tool across 3+ consecutive Designation Rank levels.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td>What "Primary" Means
</td>
<td>The majority of offensive actions in combat use that weapon type. The Director tracks this — a character who uses a Kinetic weapon in 70%+ of combats qualifies; one who splits evenly between two categories does not.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td>Director Declaration
</td>
<td>At the next Reclassification Event after conditions are met, the Director declares the Varna earned. It does not activate retroactively.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td>One Track Per Cycle
</td>
<td>A character earns one Mundane Varna technique per qualifying period (3 Rank levels). Techniques accumulate — they are never replaced.
</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Suppression:</b> A character may choose not to activate a Varna on a specific attack. Suppressing a Varna is a Free Action (declared before the attack resolves).
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Varna_Weight_and_Fatigue">Varna Weight and Fatigue</span></h3>
<p>Each Mundane Varna technique has a weight: <b>Light</b> or <b>Medium</b> or <b>Heavy</b>. Weight determines cumulative fatigue.
</p>
<table class="fandom-table">
<tbody><tr>
<th>Weight
</th>
<th>Fatigue Cost
</th>
<th>Notes
</th></tr>
<tr>
<td>Light
</td>
<td>No fatigue cost
</td>
<td>Activates freely regardless of how many times used per session
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td>Medium
</td>
<td>1 Fatigue point per activation beyond the first per encounter
</td>
<td>First activation per encounter: free. Each subsequent: 1 Fatigue.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td>Heavy
</td>
<td>1 Fatigue point per activation (always)
</td>
<td>Every use costs 1 Fatigue. Fatigue accumulates and imposes −1 to all rolls per 3 Fatigue.
</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p>Fatigue clears at short rest or long rest depending on severity. The Director tracks cumulative Fatigue across the session.
