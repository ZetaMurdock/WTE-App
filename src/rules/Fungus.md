<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Fungus-The_Infested">The Fungus-The Infested</span></h2>
<p>An archetype doesn’t always have to begin as human.
</p><p>Our story starts with <b>the gods</b> in deep conversation, debating whether they should introduce a new life form. As they deliberate, a <b>servant</b> observes from the shadows, curiosity growing with each passing moment. Wandering through the divine halls, the servant’s gaze falls upon a <b>blueprint</b>—a design for a new species. Hesitating for only a moment, they <b>seize it</b>, eager to study its secrets.
</p><p>As they examine the blueprint, another divine figure enters the room, nearly catching them in the act. The intruder, however, is unable to discern what the servant is truly doing and continues on their way. Heart pounding, the servant rushes to the <b>Onset Chamber</b>, the sacred place where <b>new creations</b> are brought into existence and sent down into the lower dimensions. Just as they begin studying the blueprint, the same figure from before sneaks up on them once more. Startled, the servant <b>panics</b>, hurling the blueprint <b>down to the world below</b>. It lands within the <b>Southern Sector</b>, embedding itself into the very fabric of the landscape. The design seeps into the grass, the soil, and the fungi of the region—until the fungi <b>become sentient</b>.
</p><p>Meanwhile, the gods are furious. They strip the servant of their divine status and <b>banish them to the surface world</b>, ordering them to retrieve the blueprint before catastrophe strikes. Upon arrival, the former servant immediately senses <b>something is wrong</b>—terribly wrong. The entire area is overrun with <b>mushrooms</b>, an unnatural infestation unlike anything seen before. As they cautiously step forward, they notice something even more unsettling: <b>one of the mushrooms is looking at them</b>. Before they can react, it <b>disappears</b>.
</p><p>Turning back to the spot where the blueprint landed, they see only a remnant of what it once was. As they attempt to pick it up, the same <b>watchful fungus</b> appears beside them—only this time, it <b>lunges</b>, latching onto their skin.
</p><p>A sharp, <b>burrowing pain</b> spreads through their body as the fungal mass <b>consumes them</b>, absorbing their very essence into an ever-growing <b>biomass</b>. The sentience of the fungi expands, evolving beyond its origins. What was once a divine servant is now something else entirely—<b>a vessel for the fungal hive-mind</b>.
</p><p>High above, the <b>Director</b> watches with amusement, munching on popcorn.
</p><p><i>"Amateurs."</i>
</p><p>With a smirk, he descends, appearing before the newly formed entity. Before the <b>fungal consciousness</b> can react, an <b>unknown force</b> restrains it. A voice, calm yet unwavering, echoes through the air.
</p><p><i>"I know where you come from. And I don’t know why they let him of all people tamper with this… But hear me now: under no circumstances are you to harm another creature. If you do—"</i>
</p><p>The unseen force tightens.
</p><p><i>"—I will come for you."</i>
</p><p>The fungal entity remains within the forest, trapped within its new body, its story spreading like wildfire across the sectors. Many seek it out, hoping to <b>gain power from its mysterious nature</b>, and in time, it is officially <b>recognized as an Archetype</b>.
</p><p>Yet, despite its classification, it is <b>forbidden to leave the forest</b>, leaving the gods in <b>utter disbelief</b> at what has transpired.
</p>
<h2><span id="Theme_.26_Role"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Theme_&amp;_Role">Theme &amp; Role</span></h2>
<p>The Fungus archetype thrives on infection, decay, and manipulation of life forces. It spreads its influence through spores, toxic growths, and corruptive symbiosis. A blend of offense and self-sustainability, the Fungus thrives in prolonged battles, where its control over the environment and the spread of toxins slowly wear down opponents.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Key_Aspects_of_the_Theme">Key Aspects of the Theme</span></h2>
<ul><li><b>Poison &amp; Decay</b> – Inflicting damage over time through fungal growth and toxins.</li>
<li><b>Symbiosis</b> – Drawing strength from the environment and even enemies.</li>
<li><b>Environmental Control</b> – Manipulating terrain with fungal growths that hinder and harm enemies.The Fungus archetype specializes in battlefield manipulation, forcing enemies into toxic zones and spreading debilitating effects. They drain life and weaken foes through persistent damage, making them a control-oriented class</li></ul>

<h2><span id="Classification_.26_Playstyles"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Classification_&amp;_Playstyles">Classification &amp; Playstyles</span></h2>
<p>The Fungus archetype offers a mix of offensive and control-oriented sub-archetypes that manipulate enemies and the battlefield:
</p>
<h4><span id="Sub-Archetypes_.26_Specializations"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Sub-Archetypes_&amp;_Specializations">Sub-Archetypes &amp; Specializations</span></h4>
<ol><li><b>Ergot – A twisted entity who manipulates necrotic fungus to inflict mental and physical dysfunction.</b> Best for disrupting foes’ mental stability and controlling their movements while draining them of vitality.  Can create zones of instability that weaken enemies and heal the Ergot, while also spreading infesting fungus that damages and debilitates.</li>
<li><b>Cryposes – A manipulator of fungal networks who spreads decay and confusion through nature’s growth.</b> Best for controlling the battlefield with fungal infestations, causing mental destabilization and manipulating the terrain.  Can create vast fungal networks that infect enemies’ minds, causing hallucinations and flinching, or even take control of their bodies with enough spread.</li>
<li><b>Lichen-mías – A spectral being that thrives through its connection to the mycelial network, manipulating the environment and healing allies with the power of lichen.</b> Best for battlefield control, environmental awareness, and supporting allies through symbiotic regeneration.  Can use its mycelium to manipulate the terrain, entangle foes, teleport across connected areas, and release bioluminescent spores to illuminate, mark targets, or impair enemies.</li>
<li><b>Fuēsar – A necromancer who commands the dead with mycotoxic spores, raising fungal thralls and spreading toxic decay.</b> Best for controlling the battlefield with fungal minions, poisoning foes, and sustaining oneself through necrotic energy.  Can reanimate fallen enemies with fungal spores, causing them to emit toxic clouds and weaken foes. The Necromancer can also summon necrotic weapons forged from fungus to deal devastating damage.</li>
<li><b>Corzepas – A master of decay and corruption, wielding the forces of decomposition to weaken and distort the living.</b>  Best for debilitating foes through rapid decay, causing grotesque transformations, and manipulating the environment into a decaying wasteland.  Can inflict Necrotic, Poison, and Psychic Damage to wear down enemies while creating hazardous terrain that hampers movement and healing. Their ultimate power unleashes a devastating wave of decay and corruption that transforms the battlefield into a nightmare of rot.</li></ol>

<h2><span id="Summary_.26_Playstyle"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Summary_&amp;_Playstyle">Summary &amp; Playstyle</span></h2>
<p>The Fungus archetype suits players who prefer a <b>methodical</b>, <b>strategic</b> approach to combat. Players can control areas, slowly wear down enemies with toxins, and turn the battlefield into a hazardous environment. Best for those who enjoy <b>long-term strategies</b> and <b>environmental manipulation</b>, but less ideal for players who prefer fast-paced, burst damage gameplay.
</p>
