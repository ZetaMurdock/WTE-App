<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetypes">Archetypes</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetypes_in_Wonderland_of_the_Enigma">Archetypes in Wonderland of the Enigma</span></h3>
<p>The <b>Archetype</b> is the foundation of a character’s <b>playstyle, progression, and overall experience</b> in the game. It defines <b>how</b> a player interacts with the world, their abilities, and the fundamental mechanics that govern their play.
</p><p>Every Archetype is built upon a <b>core Aspect</b>—a singular idea that shapes its identity. An Aspect could be something tangible, like a <b>sword or shield</b>, or something more abstract, depending on how it is flavored by an <b>Injunction</b>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Injunctions:_Defining_the_Boundaries_of_an_Archetype">Injunctions: Defining the Boundaries of an Archetype</span></h3>
<p>There are <b>four Injunctions</b>, each governing, limiting, and expanding an Archetype’s abilities in unique ways:
</p>
<ol><li><b>Physical Injunction</b> – Tied to the tangible and material world.</li>
<li><b>Metaphysical Injunction</b> – Governs abstract yet tangible constructs.</li>
<li><b>Mental Injunction</b> – Relates to the mind and perception-based manifestations.</li>
<li><b>Conceptual Injunction</b> – Brings metaphors and ideas into reality.</li></ol>

An Aspect is the core essence of an Archetype—it is the central idea, object, or force that defines the Archetype's abilities and interactions with the world. Everything an Archetype can do stems from its Aspect, and how it functions depends on the Injunction chosen.</p></div><div class="wds-tab__content"><p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p><p>An Aspect is a singular, defining element of an Archetype.
</p><p>It can be physical, abstract, mental, or even conceptual, depending on the player's chosen Injunction.
</p>
Every Aspect is governed by the rules of its Injunction, meaning that the same Aspect can take many different forms.<p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div><div class="wds-tab__content"><p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p><p>Defining the Archetype’s Abilities – All skills and powers stem from the Aspect.
</p><p>Shaping Playstyle – A player with a Shield Aspect will play defensively, but their Injunction determines how they defend.
</p><p>Allowing Versatility – The same Aspect can be vastly different between two players due to their Injunction choice.
</p>
Providing Thematic Depth – The Aspect isn't just a tool; it represents a core idea that shapes the character's role in the world.<p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div></div><p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div><div class="wds-tab__content"><p class="mw-empty-elt">

</p><p>Choosing this Injunction makes the Aspect purely physical. If you select a sword, for example, it will be a completely material weapon. You can:
</p><p>Imbue it with effects.
</p><p>Channel energy through it.
</p>
Use it as a standard item, gaining physical buffs but also experiencing drawbacks tied to its material nature.<p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div><div class="wds-tab__content"><p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p><p>This Injunction allows you to create abstract constructs that are not physical objects yet remain tangible to those you choose.
</p><p>Example: A Metaphysical Sword would not be a standard metal blade but rather a weapon composed of abstract energy or non-baryonic matter.
</p><p>It does not obey physical properties but still interacts with the world in ways that transcend standard physics.
</p>
Metaphysical Injunctions give access to powerful, non-traditional weaponry and structures that exist outside normal constraints.<p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div><div class="wds-tab__content"><p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p><p>This Injunction revolves around mental conjuration and control. Rather than physically wielding an object, you manifest and manipulate it with your mind.
</p><p>Example: A Mental Sword could exist purely in the mind yet affect the real world by cutting through mental defenses or creating psychic damage.
</p><p>This allows for abilities like telekinetic weaponry, thought-formed constructs, and psychic manipulation of the Aspect.
</p>
Mental Injunctions blur the line between imagination and reality, granting control over thought-projected entities.<p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div><div class="wds-tab__content"><p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p><p>The most unbound yet paradoxically restrictive of the Injunctions. This allows you to manifest Conceptual versions of an Aspect.
</p><p>Example: A Conceptual Sword wouldn’t be a normal blade but an embodiment of an idea—such as the concept of "Severing Bonds," where a swing could metaphorically and literally cut relationships, oaths, or fate itself.
</p><p>This Injunction allows for metaphor-made-real mechanics, but it significantly weakens control over physical interactions.
</p>
Characters using the Conceptual Injunction will likely rely on metaphysical or mental manifestations alongside actual weapons since their ability to interact with the material world is diminished.<p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div></div><p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div></div>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Summary">Summary</span></h3>

Your <b>Aspect</b> serves as the focal point of your abilities, while the <b>Injunction</b> you choose <b>alters how you manifest and interact with that Aspect</b>, opening up <b>vastly different playstyles</b> even for similar concepts.

<p><i>Injunction Type: Physical Injunction of Chains</i>
All abilities adhere to the Physical Injunction's constraints: they are physically expressed, involve linking, or visually represent chains.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="1._Soul_Construct_Manifestation">1. <b>Soul Construct Manifestation</b></span></h3>
<p><b>Type:</b> Manifestation
<b>Roll:</b> d4, d6, d8 (Success determined by combined threshold or tier-based outcomes)
</p><p><b>Description:</b>
You physically manifest your soul into a visible construct—such as a humanoid echo, armor, weapon, or totem—that remains <b>chained to you</b>. The form is semi-solid, ethereal in glow but physical in impact.
</p><p>Upon success, immediately link a physical chain between the construct and yourself.
</p><p>The chain ensures you always know the construct’s location and status.
</p><p>While linked, you may inhabit the construct, moving your awareness into it (within a limited range), or use it to inhabit another creature by transferring through the link.
</p><p><i>Note: The construct must always feature visible chains, anchors, or binding motifs to remain functional under the Physical Injunction.</i>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="2._Soul_Seizure">2. <b>Soul Seizure</b></span></h3>
<p><b>Type:</b> Grapple Effect
<b>Roll:</b> d8 vs. opponent's d8
</p><p><b>Description:</b>
You reach physically into the soul-space of a creature and <b>grab their soul</b>, locking it down using an invisible, but physically represented <b>chain grasp</b>. The target becomes <b>soul-locked</b> for 5 minutes (or 5 turns depending on time scale).
</p><p>While soul-locked, the target cannot use any soul-derived abilities.
</p><p>The grip is anchored to your hand with a visible chain, which must remain connected or the effect ends.
</p><p>This action leaves a residual <b>Soul Mark</b>, allowing future abilities to target them as <i>touched by your soul</i>.
</p><p><i>Visual Compliance: The soul is grabbed as if tethered by force-chains or pulled partially from the body, staying within your physical reach.</i>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="3._Chainquake">3. <b>Chainquake</b></span></h3>
<p><b>Type:</b> AoE (Area of Effect)
<b>Roll:</b> d8 (One roll per target to determine strength/magnitude of effect)
</p><p><b>Description:</b>
You stomp the ground, sending out a shockwave of <b>soul-chains</b> through the terrain and air. Every creature whose soul has been <b>linked, touched, marked, or restrained</b> by you is immediately <b>re-chained directly to you</b>.
</p><p>They are pulled physically or tethered spiritually, depending on distance.
</p><p>Roll a separate d8 for each target to determine the pull effect:
</p>
<ul><li><ul><li>1–2: Slowed movement</li>
<li>3–4: Cannot escape your proximity</li>
<li>5–6: Pulled violently toward you (take damage)</li>
<li>7–8: Shackled completely; target loses next movement action</li></ul></li></ul>
<i>Visual Compliance: Chains erupt visibly from the point of impact and fly toward affected targets, wrapping around limbs, torsos, or spirit projections.</i><p class="mw-empty-elt"></p></div><div class="wds-tab__content"><p class="mw-empty-elt">

<p>Imprison a creature near you by causing chains to burst violently from their own body or blood, forming a prison made of bone-wrapped or blood-slicked chains.  
- Strength and durability are determined by a d6 roll + Control and Inspiration checks.  
- The prison claws itself out of the environment, or from the creature’s own tissue if marked by blood.  
</p>
<ul><li><b>2. Enchained Strike:</b></li></ul>
<p>When you land a melee blow, sharp and jagged chains surge from the point of impact.  
- These chains deal <b>piercing damage</b> and cause <b>Bleed</b>.  
- If the target fails a Resistance/Fortitude check, the chains begin burrowing into them, partially restraining or destabilizing their form.  
</p>
<ul><li><b>3. Chain Infusion:</b></li></ul>
<p>Grab a creature, tear a wound into their form, and begin infecting it with chain-matter.  
- Roll d5, then check Control and Inspiration.  
- On success, they are forcibly restructured into a “<b>Loving Chain</b>”—a twisted sentient form of chain and bleeding muscle that serves you for 3 minutes of passive time.  
</p>
<h3><span id="Passive_Ability_.E2.A7.89"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive_Ability_⧉">Passive Ability ⧉</span></h3>
<ul><li><b>Chained Body:</b></li></ul>
<p>Your entire body is composed of living, jagged chains, pulsing and stained with old blood—except for your vital organs or soul-core.  
- You passively form replacement limbs or body parts by pulling nearby chains into your body, binding flesh and metal as one.  
- These limbs react instinctively to blood in the air, vibrating when exposed to the scent of wounds.  
</p>

<p>If you're about to be detained, bound, or trapped, your body auto-reacts.  
- Roll d7 versus your captor’s d7.  
- If you win, your blood flares violently, tearing open wounds in the captor and chaining them in place using their own pain as anchor points.  
</p>
<ul><li><b>2. Prison Evasion:</b></li></ul>
<p>If you have any chains active on your body and you're in combat:  
- The moment you strike or are struck, a blood-infused chain lashes out, attempting to <b>bind a part of your opponent’s body</b>.  
- If successful, that limb or organ becomes chain-restricted until removed by brute force or a cleansing ability.

<p><b>Rule:</b><br>
You are bound by the Physical Injunction of Chains, limiting your capabilities:  
All of your non-physical abilities are universally restricted unless they conceptually or visually represent a linked object, material, or idea. If they do not, you suffer a -200 penalty to all rolls associated with those abilities.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Damage:</b><br>
All of your attacks inherently deal Slashing damage and cause the Bleeding condition.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Passive:</b><br>
You gain the ability to Link any object you touch, as long as multiple identical or near-identical versions of that object exist nearby—this includes both visual and atomic similarity.

</p><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Metaphysical_Injunction_of_Chains">Metaphysical Injunction of Chains</span></h2>
<p><b>Rule:</b><br>
You are bound by a metaphysical injunction. If you attempt to use an ability that is not metaphysical in nature, the action will automatically backfire, resulting in a Critical Failure. Additionally, all non-metaphysical abilities you possess are severely debuffed, rendering them highly ineffective in this state.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Damage:</b><br>
All of your attacks now deal Force and Spirit damage by default, replacing their original damage types.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Passive:</b><br>
You can transmute any physical or mental concept—an object, action, or even a mental focus—into a metaphysical chain, linking its essence to another target or effect. This transformation can happen immediately before or during an action.  
Upon transformation, you can manifest a linked metaphysical construct—an energy thread, chain, or tether that binds multiple targets or actions together, allowing them to interact in ways that exceed their normal limits. This manifestation can be used as a Bonus Action on the same turn.
</p><p>You can link multiple targets, causing effects to spread or amplify in connected ways. For example, a linked chain of force might strike multiple enemies with one action, or a mental link could disrupt the thoughts of connected targets.
</p>

<p><b>Action Economy:</b><br>
The Metaphysical Injunction of Binding grants the following action benefits:

</p><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="mental_Injunction_of_Chains">mental Injunction of Chains</span></h2>
<p><b>Rule:</b><br>
Any physical ability or action—such as swinging a sword, performing a physical maneuver, or any task that relies on your body’s strength and physical capabilities—is prone to a misfire if used too frequently or under excessive strain.  
Initiating these physical actions will lead to a temporary loss of strength in that specific aspect of your body, making future physical actions more difficult. This fatigue or strain inadvertently causes a misfire, resulting in an unintended effect or failure of the action.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Damage:</b><br>
All damage will now be inherently mentally conjured and created.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Passive:</b><br>
You gain the ability to link objects together within your mind, merging them into a cohesive whole. This fusion allows you to mentally alter any aspect of these objects—changing their form, function, or properties—so that they fit your desires and needs.  
Once linked and altered, the objects become a single entity in your mind. You can now share your senses through them, perceiving the world as they do. Additionally, you may use abilities through these linked objects, channeling your power through them as if they were an extension of yourself.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Action Economy:</b><br>
The Physical Injunction of Misfire grants the following action benefits:

<ul><li><b>3 Bonus Actions</b></li>
<li><b>1 Free Action</b></li>
<li><b>1 Standard Action</b></li>
<li><b>2 True Opportunity Attacks</b></li></ul>

You can create conceptual versions of chains and links. When manipulating something physical or metaphysical, you can instead manipulate its conceptual counterpart, allowing you to affect actions indirectly. For example, if you swing a sword, you can use the concept of an attack via conceptual chains.

</p><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Injunction_of_Metaphysical_Strings">Injunction of Metaphysical Strings</span></h2>
<p><b>Rule:</b><br>
You are bound to manipulate Metaphysical Strings, energy constructs that are intangible and abstract. These strings cannot interact with the physical world in direct ways. Any action attempting to manipulate physical objects or perform physical tasks (e.g., lifting, breaking barriers, or creating physical tools) will result in Critical Failure. Instead, the strings can only be used for metaphysical purposes, and failure in attempting physical interaction will cause unintended consequences.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Damage:</b><br>
When you attack using Metaphysical Strings, the type of energy you channel changes based on your intent and the flow of energy. Instead of declaring the damage type beforehand, the Metaphysical String adapts to the situation, creating effects tailored to the moment of impact.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Passive:</b><br>
You can manifest metaphysical strings that indirectly interact with physical concepts. While your strings cannot physically manipulate objects, you can alter the conceptual nature of physical things—such as manipulating momentum, redirecting energy flows, or influencing gravity. This allows for creative uses of your metaphysical strings, enabling you to indirectly affect physical elements of the world.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Action Economy:</b><br>
The Metaphysical Strings grants the following action benefits:
</p>

<p><b>Rule:</b><br>
You are unable to directly manipulate or interact with the Physical world in any tangible way. Instead, you must use your mind as a conduit to interact with physical objects. Should you attempt to physically interact with the world, whether through strength, skill, or force, you will be paralyzed for 20 minutes of passive time, during which all damage inflicted upon you will be doubled.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Damage:</b><br>
Instead of relying on physical force, you can tap into nearby sources or objects you control to draw mental energy from them. This allows you to channel select damage types based on objects or concepts within your mental reach. The damage type depends on the mental connection to the object, like manipulating mental energy or force from a nearby element or your surroundings.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Passive:</b><br>
You gain the ability to transform physical matter into mental constructs with a mere touch. Upon contact with any physical object, you can convert it into Mental Strings, visually resembling whatever you touched. Once transformed, you can mentally manipulate it, and your changes to the object will manifest back into the Physical world in a modified form, as if you had physically interacted with it—except entirely through mental influence.

This backlash causes 4 times the normal damage and is 5 times more effective against you and your allies. The more you try to manipulate the physical world directly, the more the world retaliates with its raw conceptual force.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Damage:</b><br>

</p>

<li><b>Slashing Damage:</b> If you are struck by a slash or cutting attack, you can imbue your next attack with slashing damage, turning your strike into a razor-sharp concept that cuts deep.</li></ul>
<p>Your damage adapts based on the environment, channeling the conceptual nature of the world around you into your attacks.

<hr>
<p><b>Passive:</b><br>

You can then use these conceptual strings to influence the world around you in unexpected ways, even without direct interaction with physical objects.

</p><p>Attempting to use any ability that is not Physical, or not in direct relation to Biofilm, will cause your Biofilm to perceive you as an enemy. This results in <b>permanent bodily disintegration</b> through your own limbs. Each violation triggers the Biofilm to attack and break down your body, causing irreversible damage. This cannot be prevented, healed, or mitigated by outside sources.

All of your damage now inherently includes the following:

The original action’s effects, status inflictions, or lingering passives are absorbed and stored within the Biofilm. When you repurpose that Biofilm (through attacks, growths, or abilities), these absorbed traits are applied to your target or environment.

<p><b>Rule:</b><br>
You gain the ability to blur the lines between reality and the Biofilm by manifesting a Biofilm Orb inside your brain or central processing unit (whether that be a crystal, core, or other mental anchor). This Orb enables you to mentally communicate with and control the Biofilm. However, it acts as a parasitic entity that must be prioritized at all times. 
</p><p>If you mentally focus on anything not related to the Biofilm, you will suffer catastrophic consequences:
</p>
<ul><li>You become mentally unresponsive, entering a vegetative state.</li>
<li>If a non-Biofilm-aligned ability is used, the Biofilm will devour your body and form a new one to inhabit and control.</li>
<li>This loss is permanent and cannot be recovered by any means.</li></ul>

<p><b>Damage:</b><br>
All of your attacks now inherently deal:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Pathogenic Damage</b> – Infectious and lingering, weakening the target over time.</li>
<li><b>Soul Damage</b> – Targets the internal spiritual structure and bypasses most defenses.</li>
<li>Optional: <b>Psychic Damage</b>, depending on your intent or contextual application.</li></ul>
<hr>

</p><p>Through Quorum Sensing, you gain complete omnidirectional awareness of every Biofilm extension, allowing silent communication, environmental feedback, and instant threat detection. The more terrain or entities you control through Biofilm, the more enhanced your battlefield perception becomes, granting advantage on reaction-based rolls or checks related to perception and awareness.

</p>
<ul><li>Detect their surface thoughts or emotional states.</li>
<li>Manipulate their decisions slightly (Director's discretion) if they fail a Will/Resolve check.</li>
<li>Use them as conduits to expand or redirect Biofilm growth further.</li></ul>

<p><b>Rule:</b><br>

However, this comes with a hard restriction: you are unable to mentally engage with or construct anything not rooted in or resembling computer logic. Any attempt to generate thoughts, creations, or manifestations from your mind that do not align with digital or computational concepts will trigger an <span style="color:red;"><i>Internal Firewall</i></span>. When triggered, this firewall disables your mental faculties for <b>2 hours of Passive Time</b>, simulating a complete shutdown. Afterward, you suffer <span style="color:red;"><i>Permanent Mental Burnout</i></span>, applying a stacking <b>-10 penalty to all actions</b> each time the Rule is broken.

<hr>
<p><b>Damage:</b><br>

</p>

<li><b>Phenomenon-Imbued Damage</b> — Environmental effects (light, sound, motion, etc.) can be harnessed to empower strikes.</li>
<li>Optional: <b>Simulated Override Damage</b> — If used within your simulation realm, bypasses resistances by rewriting the entity’s behavior or state in code.</li></ul>
<hr>
<p><b>Passive:</b><br>

The realism, control, and persistence of this Simulation scale with your <b>Ode</b> (used as a multiplier). Within this digital realm, you have total control over the environment, and all actions are performed via written code you create. You may bring in any objects or entities, even ones stronger than you. However, any damage taken—either by you or by the Simulation itself—results in direct mental strain. If the Simulation suffers enough damage, it activates the Rule’s restrictions. While inside the Simulation, the effects of the Rule are suppressed, allowing for temporary cognitive freedom.

</p><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Metaphysical_Injunction_of_the_Vitalis">Metaphysical Injunction of the Vitalis</span></h2>
<p><b>Rule:</b><br>
You are bound to the metaphysical manifestation of the Vitalis, an aspect that functions similarly to a Soul Vial. You lose the ability to manipulate the world physically but gain the power to influence it through metaphysical will. You communicate with and embody the Vitalis, which takes primary control of your body—your physical form becomes secondary, acting as an extension of the Vitalis itself. As the Vitalis, you enact its directives while your original consciousness follows its lead.
</p><p>Any attempt to perform physical actions or act in discordance with the Vitalis's will results in the Vitalis being ripped apart and reconfigured, effectively killing you and recreating you in a new form.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Damage:</b><br>
You deal innate True Damage and Pathogenic Damage. As a bonus effect, whenever you successfully attack a creature, a Vitalis grows within their psyche. This allows you to manifest another body from the affected creature without their knowledge, granting you additional forms or vessels.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Passive:</b><br>
As the Vitalis, you have direct control over its will and can manipulate your environment in godlike ways. All information around you can be copied and stored in a metaphysical realm at your discretion. Once stored, this information can influence or empower you, but overuse drains your physical manifestation. You passively absorb creatures, beings, constructs, or anything else, then recreate them for your metaphysical uses. This process passively generates new Vitalis instances, which you can use to reconstruct yourself at any point, in any desired form.
</p>

</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_Injunction_of_Primordial_Blueprints">Physical Injunction of Primordial Blueprints</span></h3>
<p><b>Rule:</b>  
You’re Bound to the Physical Injunction of “PBP” you're able to manifest a Blueprint that you can create and draw schematics on and you're unable to create any form of object without the use of your Blueprints and in the instance you use any Physical based Abilities without the incorporation of your blue prints will result in the action being dismantled and you're unable to use any form of mental conceptual or metaphysical alterations and or abilities. Attempting to violate these rules triggers a <b>Blueprint Fracture</b>, where your active blueprints shatter, scattering fragments that deal 1d6 piercing damage to nearby allies and yourself for 1 hour, forcing creative paralysis. Violations escalate: the first imposes a permanent -10% efficiency debuff to creations; the second warps a tool or limb into unusable scrap; the third risks a "primordial cascade," destabilizing structures and erasing schematics.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Damage:</b>  
</p><p>Damage from Primordial Blueprints depends on the creation but inherently includes imbuable effects that scale with blueprint complexity:  
</p>
<ul><li>1d12 Radiation Damage – Base infusion for all creations, causing lingering contamination that reduces target's regeneration by 10% for 1d4 turns.</li>
<li>Schematic Overload Damage – Overcharges a blueprint for explosive bursts, dealing bonus damage equal to the schematic's "complexity level" (e.g., +5 for advanced designs), with a chance to chain to adjacent targets.</li>
<li>Optional: Blueprint Echo Damage, reflecting the creation's purpose (e.g., a weapon blueprint deals slashing echoes, a shield blueprint causes defensive rebounds that stun on critical hits).</li></ul>

<p><b>Passive:</b>  
You have the ability to Manifest the “PBP” Upon Manifesting it all creatures around you will be forced back by a 1d20 the roll will determine the amount of feet they are pushed back This will have a guaranteed success and upon doing this any object you come into contact with become inscribed in in the “PBP” as schematics allowing you to create it given you have the proper materials and the creations can be as advanced as an entire house to as simple as a lead pencil you also obtain a pencil that you can use to create schematics and anything your pencil comes into contact with will be able to be turned into a schematic but as one that has been completed allowing you to recreate it at any time but this only works with inorganic matter. Schematics are stored in "blueprint slots" (start with 5, scaling +1 per 10 reputation), each with a complexity rating determining cost and time. Spend slots to forge basic, advanced, or legendary creations, but overloading causes a "forge meltdown," destroying items and dealing backlash.

</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Mental_Injunction_of_Primordial_Blueprints">Mental Injunction of Primordial Blueprints</span></h3>
<p><b>Rule:</b>  
You are bound to the Mental Injunction of Primordial Blueprints. You are unable to interact with the physical world in terms of the Blueprints, and any attempt to do so results in Subdivision Failure. This causes your body to contort violently, requiring you to roll a 1d8 to determine the affliction: on a 1-4, you suffer minor bodily afflictions (e.g., temporary pain or bruising); on a 5-8, you suffer major bodily afflictions (e.g., arm loss or severe injury).
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Damage:</b>  
Your creations and alterations inherently deal Vertice damage and matter affected by your aspect take Vertice damage as well.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Passive:</b>  
You perceive the world differently from others. You can see complex geometric shapes and manipulate them mentally. Once conceptualized, this requires Control, Inspiration, and Intelligence rolls to determine the intensity, accuracy, and potency of the creation. Once conceptualized, you can then create or modify objects, altering existing materials with your Primordial Blueprints. This manifests as innate control over surrounding materials, functioning like built-in software. As you observe the world, you can create objects or modify existing ones—such as reshaping trees or forming a cube from environmental materials—drawing from the surroundings. However, this has debilitating effects on the environment around you.

</p><h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_Injunction_of_Contracts">Physical  Injunction of Contracts</span></h3>
<p><b>Rule:</b>  
Your soul belongs to the contract, and you must honor it in its physical state. If the contract allows for manipulation beyond the Physical Injunction, so be it. The contract is a deep give-and-take agreement negotiated with the Director (DM). Upon breaking the contract, you become a Contract Breaker, lose the Aspect of Contracts, and obtain a dagger that severs connections between things. Using this dagger costs 3% of your permanent health per use. The dagger can be controlled telepathically and grants +1 Standard Action, +1 Bonus Action, and +1 Free Action when wielded. If you use all granted actions in one turn, the dagger turns to dust and regenerates upon entering downtime.
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Damage:</b>  
Your abilities can apply any damage type as long as it is stated within the contract, but the baseline damage type is Soul Damage (a metaphysical harm that weakens the target's essence, reducing their maximum health or abilities temporarily).
</p>
<hr>
<p><b>Passive:</b>  
You have the innate ability to create contracts, but you must first have an initial contract made outside of the one required by the Rule. This initial contract must be written by you in some way, shape, or form and can be enhanced by the Rule. Once the prerequisites are met, you can manifest contracts between yourself and another creature, between opposing creatures, or between yourself and up to your Ode Level in creatures (e.g., if your Ode Level is 3, you can maintain three contracts at a time, each affecting up to three creatures). While these contracts are active, you can sacrifice a clause to instantly teleport to a targeted creature or bring that creature to you. You can also break clauses to empower yourself, granting temporary boosts based on the clause's terms.
</p>
