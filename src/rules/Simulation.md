<p>The <b>Simulation Paradigm</b> questions the nature of perception itself. To its adherents, reality is just a construct — a system of inputs and expectations waiting to be rewritten. Those who walk this path use illusion, holographic systems, and sensory manipulation to blur the lines between truth and fiction, shaping entire encounters through manufactured experience. The world is code, and code can be rewritten.
</p>
