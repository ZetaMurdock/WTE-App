# Simulation

> "The real is only as real as the mind decides it to be."

The Simulation Paradigm questions the nature of perception itself. To its adherents, reality is just a construct — a system of inputs and expectations waiting to be rewritten. Those who walk this path use illusion, holographic systems, and sensory manipulation to blur the lines between truth and fiction, shaping entire encounters through manufactured experience. The world is code, and code can be rewritten.

## Paradigm Identity

**Who Joins** — Pragmatists, tacticians, mind-hackers, illusionists, and those obsessed with control over sensory space. These Inquisitors thrive on uncertainty and forcing others to question whether anything they see is truly real.

**Culture** — Playful but secretive, clever yet detached. They find comfort in ambiguity and power in perception. Many value observation, strategy, and psychological manipulation over brute strength. "If you can dream it clearly enough, it's real for everyone else."

**Archetype Feel** — Thief / Illusionist. The Simulation Inquisitor doesn't fight reality — they rewrite it from the inside, making others doubt what they see, hear, and experience.

## Roll Axis Profile

Simulation occupies 5 of the 7 universal Roll Paths. Its native mechanics emphasize bodily avoidance and survival, cognitive operation, active perception, sensory defense, and the manipulation of intent through constructed experience. Simulation Ciphers do not natively specialize in Power or Density, though Simulation Inquisitors may still make those rolls normally whenever the situation calls for them.

| Roll Path | Native | Direction | Simulation Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power | — | Check | Not native to Simulation mechanics; direct physical force remains universally available. |
| Density | — | Check | Not native to Simulation mechanics; rapid physical execution remains universally available. |
| Evasion | ● | Save | Avoid sudden threats, reposition through distorted battlefields, and escape physical consequences when simulated phenomena become materially dangerous. |
| Recovery | ● | Save | Endure neural feedback, sensory overload, physical backlash, or destabilization caused by immersive or projected systems. |
| Capacity | ● | Check | Maintain simulations, operate layered sensory systems, coordinate projected environments, and sustain multiple artificial processes at once. |
| Perception | ● | Check / Save | Construct, inspect, detect, or resist altered sensory information, illusions, false environments, hidden signals, and manipulated experience. |
| Influence | ● | Check / Save | Use manufactured experience to redirect behavior, create doubt, manipulate expectations, or resist external control of one's own intent. |

**PROFILE STANDARD** — Simulation's Roll Profile describes the Paths its native Paradigm mechanics are built around. It does not prevent a Simulation Inquisitor from making Power or Density rolls during ordinary play, and it does not overwrite the natural Roll Path of weapons, Genera, copied abilities, environmental effects, or other external systems.

## Simulation Paradigm Affinity

Simulation does not favor every statistic contained inside its Roll Profile. Its Affinities represent the capabilities its doctrine most aggressively develops: interpreting sensory information, constructing believable experience, and maintaining complex artificial realities under cognitive load.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Favored Attributes | Intelligence · Wisdom |
| Favored Specialties | Perception · Mental Fortitude |

Intelligence reinforces recognition and interpretation of sensory information. Wisdom reinforces the cognitive workload required to maintain constructed realities. Perception reinforces sensory discrimination and observation. Mental Fortitude reinforces sustained operation of simulations and layered cognitive systems.

| Designation Rank | Favored Attribute | Favored Specialty |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | +1d5 | +1d10 |
| 3–5 | +2d5 | +2d10 |
| 6–8 | +3d5 | +3d10 |
| 9 | +4d5 | +4d10 |

**SIGNATURE CONVERGENCE · PERCEPTION** — Intelligence + Perception creates Simulation's primary sensory Convergence. When both legitimately govern a Perception Check or Perception Save, Simulation receives both the Favored Attribute and Favored Specialty Affinity pools. This represents expert discrimination between authentic and manufactured experience, deliberate observation of sensory systems, detection of hidden inconsistencies, and construction of illusions built from precise sensory understanding.

**SIGNATURE CONVERGENCE · CAPACITY** — Wisdom + Mental Fortitude creates Simulation's primary operational Convergence. When both legitimately govern a Capacity Check, Simulation receives both Affinity pools. This represents maintaining layered simulations, processing several artificial sensory feeds, sustaining large projected environments, and controlling complex synthetic experiences without cognitive collapse.

**WHAT SIMULATION DOES NOT FAVOR** — Simulation has native access to Evasion, Recovery, and Influence, but it does not automatically gain Affinity on Dexterity, Balance, Endurance, Adaptation, Charisma, or Cunning. These Paths remain part of Simulation's mechanical vocabulary without being institutional areas of exceptional training.

## Authority Profile

Members have secondary access to research labs and illusion networks, often borrowing tech from Science. Their clearance applies to virtual training programs, sensory mapping tools, and holographic simulation chambers. They can requisition data, neural interfaces, or sensory nodes for experimentation or field performance.

**Advancement** — Authority increases through consistent and successful use of simulation technology — running tests, designing virtual environments, or inducing controlled illusions. All data must be logged and transmitted to authorizing labs, forming a continuous research-exchange loop.

## Authority Ranks · Designation Progression

| Rank | Title | Role | Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sim Intern | Entry-level analyst learning sensory algorithms and virtual interfaces | Basic lab access; can use commercial training simulations |
| 2 | Simulator | Field specialist managing mission scenario projections | Authorized to build and deploy localized simulation spaces |
| 3 | Simulation Agent | Department or team leader coordinating sensory field operations | Can override minor system locks and create composite holographic environments |
| 4 | Formal Simulations | Senior architect of large-scale virtual systems | Full authority over institutional simulation grids and virtual command protocols |

### Rank 4 Specialization Fields

**Computer Simulation** — Coding predictive behaviors and running physical models. Most predictive modeling resolves through Capacity, while identifying deviations between expected and observed behavior commonly engages Perception.

**Virtual Simulation** — Complete sensory immersion environments. Large-scale sensory distortion most commonly engages Capacity to maintain the construct and Perception or Influence when the simulation must deceive or redirect observers.

## Combat Specialty

Primarily tactical and adaptive. Simulation Inquisitors are masters of unpredictability — they distort visibility, manipulate enemy targeting systems, and alter sensory perception to control the battlefield. Their native mechanics most often reward Perception, Capacity, Influence, Evasion, and Recovery rather than direct Power or Density specialization.

**Style** — Quick, elusive, and precise. Rather than overpowering foes, they craft confusion — turning sight, sound, and expectation into weapons of deception.

## Weapon & Gear Access

| Weight | Slot | Class · Examples |

Simulation operatives often favor lightweight or modular gear that can shift function mid-combat, allowing them to blend technological deception with physical precision.

## Specialties · Genera Domains

**Photonic Energy** — Manipulates directed force through simulated recoil or contact fields, enhancing momentum or deflection control.

**Null Energy** — Generates zones of false feedback or visual nullification, effectively masking presence or distorting perception.

Both domains highlight Simulation's duality — offense through illusion of power or defense through illusion of absence.

## Interactions & Philosophy

To Simulation adherents, perception is truth. Their philosophy unsettles those from more tangible paradigms like Warfare or Evolution, yet their ability to construct synthetic experiences makes them invaluable for testing theories, training units, or destabilizing enemies psychologically.

**Allies** — Science (technology access), Cognition (mind-network integration)

**Tensions** — Remnant and Warfare (distrust of "fabricated combat"), Evolution (opposing views on real vs. constructed change)
