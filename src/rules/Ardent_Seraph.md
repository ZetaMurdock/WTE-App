<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>Ardent Seraph are what happens when a Seraph does not restrain their resonance. Most Seraph manage the intensity of their divine connection through discipline, ritual, and careful architectural maintenance of their internal geometry. Ardent Seraph either cannot or choose not to. The result is a being who radiates divine energy visibly -- light bleeds from their wings in patterns that sensitive creatures can read like text. They are not volatile so much as they are overful. Everything they do carries more weight than intended: their words carry conviction past the point of argument, their proximity makes others feel observed, their damage exceeds what their strikes should produce. They burn hot and they know it.
</p>
