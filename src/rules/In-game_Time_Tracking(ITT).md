# In-game Time Tracking(ITT)

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Index_Time_System">Index Time System</span></h2>
<p>The Clocks System is a fundamental mechanic that tracks the passage of time in the world, directly influencing combat, character actions, and dynamic events. It allows the Director and players to measure not just <b>what happens</b>, but <b>when it happens</b>, creating a fluid and realistic sense of temporal consequence. Time is divided into <b>Active Time</b> and <b>Passive Time</b>, both of which operate simultaneously and affect the world differently.
</p>
<hr>
<h3><span id="Active_Time_.28Combat_.26_High-Intensity_Actions.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Active_Time_(Combat_&amp;_High-Intensity_Actions)">Active Time (Combat &amp; High-Intensity Actions)</span></h3>
<p>Active Time represents moments of <b>heightened activity</b>, such as combat, complex maneuvers, or other intense scenarios that require a character's focus. It is measured in increments relative to the action being performed (seconds, minutes, or custom units depending on the Genus or ability used).
</p><p><b>Mechanics:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Tracking:</b> Each action, ability, or Genus performed advances the active clock by its designated duration. The Director records the flow of time to maintain consistency.</li>
<li><b>Concurrent Actions:</b> Multiple entities act simultaneously, but actions of longer duration occupy that entity’s attention, making them <b>busy or vulnerable</b> until the action completes. Short or basic actions advance the clock minimally but still contribute to the overall flow of combat.</li>
<li><b>Dynamic World Impact:</b> While characters perform actions, the environment and other entities continue to evolve. This may include enemy reactions, reinforcement arrivals, environmental hazards, or mission-critical events. Long-duration actions create windows for both risk and opportunity.</li>
<li><b>Strategic Importance:</b> Active Time ensures that <b>time is meaningful</b> in combat, emphasizing multitasking, prioritization, and the potential consequences of committing to lengthy actions. Players must weigh the <b>benefits of a powerful action against the exposure it creates</b> while the world continues to move around them.</li></ul>
<hr>
<h3><span id="Passive_Time_.28World_Evolution_.26_Decisions.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive_Time_(World_Evolution_&amp;_Decisions)">Passive Time (World Evolution &amp; Decisions)</span></h3>
<p>Passive Time represents the <b>general passage of time outside of immediate high-intensity actions</b>, capturing the ongoing evolution of the world, political dynamics, social interactions, and environmental changes.
</p><p><b>Mechanics:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Tracking:</b> The Director advances the passive clock based on the cumulative duration of player activities, such as traveling between locations, performing rituals, crafting, studying, or engaging in extended dialogue.</li>
<li><b>World Events:</b> As passive time progresses, NPCs act independently, events unfold organically, and the world state changes. For example, rival factions may shift power, supply chains may be disrupted, or natural phenomena may alter the landscape.</li>
<li><b>Decision Impact:</b> Actions taken by players can accelerate or decelerate passive time. A hurried mission or a prolonged negotiation can produce ripple effects, leading to <b>unforeseen consequences</b> and shaping the narrative in dynamic ways.</li></ul>
<p><b>Strategic Importance:</b>
</p><p>Passive Time emphasizes that <b>the world does not wait</b>. Even while characters are planning or performing mundane tasks, their choices affect the unfolding timeline. By tracking passive time, the system reinforces immersion and ensures <b>player agency has tangible consequences</b> within the larger world.
</p>
<hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Why_Clocks_Matter">Why Clocks Matter</span></h3>
<ol><li><b>Temporal Realism:</b> Actions, whether combat-related or not, take measurable time, preventing unrealistic simultaneity or “frozen world” scenarios.</li>
<li><b>Risk &amp; Reward:</b> Long-duration actions create windows of vulnerability, making strategic planning and timing crucial.</li>
<li><b>Dynamic Storytelling:</b> By differentiating between active and passive time, the system allows the Director to simulate evolving situations, creating emergent challenges and opportunities.</li>
<li><b>Player Agency &amp; Consequence:</b> Players’ choices influence the clocks, which in turn shape the world, the narrative, and potential outcomes — reinforcing the idea that <b>time is a resource, and how it is spent matters</b>.</li>
<li><b>Interconnected Mechanics:</b> Clocks tie together combat, exploration, and social interaction into a single temporal framework, making all aspects of gameplay feel cohesive and impactful.</li></ol>
