<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Offensive_Talents">Offensive Talents</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Melee_Combat">Melee Combat</span></h3>
<ul><li>Power Strike: Increases damage dealt by basic attacks.
<ul><li>Roll a D6 for Bludgeoning damage. On a roll of 4 or 6, immediately attack again.</li>
<li>Roll for True and Chaos damage. If the combined score is 25-30, apply a status effect of any kind.</li>
<li>Roll a D40 if its 20 or higher you can either activate the Attribute "<b>Force Enhancement</b><sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>" or you roll a D6 and if 4 or higher attack again</li></ul></li>
<li>Dual Wield: Allows wielding two weapons simultaneously for increased damage.
<ul><li>Roll an additional 1d6 for damage. If both dice show the same result, deal additional Piercing damage equal to your level.</li>
<li>Roll D8 if 5 or higher activate the Attribute "<b>Precision</b><sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-0" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li>
<li>Berserker Rage: Temporarily boosts attack power at the cost of defense.
<ul><li>Roll a D6. On a roll of 4 or higher, gain temporary Defensive hit points equal to twice the roll.</li>
<li>Roll a D8 and if you get 3 or higher you can activate "<b>Force Enhancement</b><sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li>
<li>Crippling Blow: Has a chance to slow down or cripple enemy movement.
<ul><li>Roll a D8. On a roll of 6 or higher, the target's movement speed is halved for a number of rounds equal to your level.</li>
<li>Roll a D6 if you roll higher than a 3 and an Enemy is knocked down roll a D12 if you roll 6 or higher you will apply true damage to your next attack and activate "<b>Debilitation</b><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Ranged_Combat">Ranged Combat</span></h3>
<ul><li>Precision Shot: Increases critical hit chance for ranged attacks.
<ul><li>Roll a D12 for true damage. If you get 7 or higher, the damage is changed to critical.</li>
<li>if the attack is critical your ranged attack will apply "<b>Precision</b><sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li>
<li>Roll a D20 if you roll a 10 or higher they will become stunned if stunned all dmg to them will be True damage which is Double</li></ul></li>
<li>Sniper: Increases damage and critical hit chance for ranged attacks from a distance.
<ul><li>Roll an additional 1d8 for damage. If the attack roll is a natural 20, the target is knocked prone.</li>
<li>Roll an additional 1d6 if over 3 activate "<b>Precision</b><sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-2" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>" which will make the damage plus 10 for an attack done at a distance</li></ul></li>
<li>Piercing Shot: Ignores enemy armor, dealing increased damage.
<ul><li>Roll a D6. On a roll of 5 or higher, ignore resistance or immunity to the damage type for that attack.</li>
<li>if the dice get 5 or higher activate "<b>Decay Control</b><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>" making the shot do an Addition 1D6 of piercing damage</li></ul></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Magic_and_Spells">Magic and Spells</span></h3>
<ul><li>Elemental Mastery: Enhances damage and effects of elemental abilities.
<ul><li>Roll a D10. On a roll of 8 or higher, the spell's damage ignores resistance or immunity to that element.</li>
<li>if the dice Rolled below 8 activate "<b>Elemental</b><sup id="cite_ref-:2_5-0" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>" and if the roll for that is 14 or higher take the base damage add a Plus 10 along with the boost from the roll the dice rolled on</li></ul></li>
<li>Arcane Knowledge: Enhances the power and effects of arcane spells.
<ul><li>Roll a D10. On a roll of 7 or higher, the spell's duration is extended by 1 round, or its area of effect is increased.</li>
<li>if the dice roll is exactly 6 activate "<b>Adaptability</b><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li>
<li>Magical Amplification: Boosts the power and effectiveness of magical spells and abilities.
<ul><li>Roll a D6. On a roll of 4 or higher, the spell's damage or effect is increased by a percentage equal to your level.</li>
<li>if the dice rolls 3 or higher activate "<b>Conjuration</b><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li>
<li>Chain Lightning: Unleashes a lightning attack that jumps between multiple targets.
<ul><li>Roll a D8. On a roll of 6 or higher, the lightning jumps to an additional target or deals bonus damage.</li>
<li>roll a D8 for lightning Damage if its 7 or higher activate "<b>Elemental</b><sup id="cite_ref-:2_5-1" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li>
<li>Arcane Nova: Unleashes a powerful explosion of arcane energy, damaging nearby enemies.
<ul><li>Roll a D8. On a roll of 6 or higher, the explosion's radius is increased, and affected enemies are pushed back 10 feet.</li>
<li>Role a D10 if its 7 ,9 or 3 Apply force damage and if force damage is applied activated "<b>Nova</b><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>" making the force do whatever damage the user choses so it will be force plus the other additive damage</li></ul></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Defensive_Talents">Defensive Talents</span></h2>
<ul><li>Defensive Stance: Increases defense and reduces incoming damage.
<ul><li>Roll a D8. On a roll of 6 or higher, gain a temporary shield that absorbs a portion of incoming damage for a number of rounds equal to the roll.</li>
<li>if you absorb damage roll a D20 if you roll an 8 or higher activate "<b>Protection</b><sup id="cite_ref-:3_9-0" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li>
<li>Thick Skin: Increases overall resistance to damage.
<ul><li>Roll a D8. On a roll of 5 or higher, gain temporary resistance to a random damage type for the duration of the encounter.</li>
<li>if that activates then roll a D8 if its 7 or higher either activate "<b>Command</b><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>" or activate "<b>Resilience</b><sup id="cite_ref-:4_11-0" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li>
<li>Magical Barrier: Creates a protective shield that absorbs incoming damage.
<ul><li>Roll a D6. On a roll of 4 or higher, gain resistance to a random damage type for the duration of the barrier. Roll a D8 if it's a h or higher activate "Enhancement Imbuement" if this is successful then roll a D10 if you get a 7 or higher then activate "<b>Protection</b><sup id="cite_ref-:3_9-1" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li>
<li>Shield Wall: Creates a powerful barrier that reduces damage taken.
<ul><li>Roll a D8. On a roll of 6 or higher, gain temporary resistance to all damage types for the duration of the barrier.</li>
<li>roll a d10 if its 6 or higher activate, "<b>Resilience</b><sup id="cite_ref-:4_11-1" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Evasion_and_Control">Evasion and Control</span></h3>
<ul><li>Swift Reflexes: Improves evasion and increases chance to dodge incoming attacks.
<ul><li>Roll a D8. On a roll of 5 or higher, gain advantage on your next attack after successfully dodging an attack.</li>
<li>if you're successful and are about to be countered in some way roll a D20 if you roll a 14 or higher you will activate "<b>Mobility</b><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li>
<li>Counterattack: Allows a chance to counter enemy attacks with a powerful strike.
<ul><li>Roll a D6. On a roll of 4 or higher, the counterattack deals bonus damage equal to your level. once the attack land then roll A D10 if you roll 4 or higher you can activate another talent (if you own it) "Power Strike" then roll a D10 if above 1d6 activate "<b>Force Enhancement</b><sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li>
<li>Evasive Maneuvers: Increases chances to avoid area-of-effect attacks.
<ul><li>Roll a D6. On a roll of 4 or higher, gain temporary resistance to that damage type for the duration of the encounter. Roll a D8 if you roll 4 or higher activate "<b>Protection</b><sup id="cite_ref-:3_9-2" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>" and if this is successful gain 10 Defensive Hit points for the duration of the scene</li></ul></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Healing_and_Support">Healing and Support</span></h3>
<ul><li>Healing Touch: Increases effectiveness of healing spells and abilities.
<ul><li>Roll a D20. On a roll of 10 or higher, the target also gains temporary hit points equal to the roll. if you gain over 7 hit points activate "<b>Vitality</b><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li>
<li>Regeneration: Increases natural health and mana regeneration.
<ul><li>Roll a D6. On a roll of 5 or higher, regain additional hit points or mana equal to your level. Roll a D20 if you get above 14 Activate "<b>Healing</b><sup id="cite_ref-:5_14-0" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li>
<li>Life Drain: Converts a portion of damage dealt into health restoration.
<ul><li>Roll a D8. On a roll of 6 or higher, gain temporary hit points equal to half the damage dealt. if you gain 1-3 hit points activate "<b>DNA Manipulation</b><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li>
<li>Resurrection: Allows reviving fallen allies with a portion of their health restored.
<ul><li>Roll a D6. On a roll of 5 or higher, if the roll is exactly 6 activate the Attribute "<b>Revival</b><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup><b>"</b>  the revived ally gains temporary hit points equal to half their maximum hit points.</li></ul></li>
<li>Chain Heal: Restores health to multiple allies in a chain effect.
<ul><li>Roll a D8. On a roll of 6 or higher, affected allies also gain a bonus to their next saving throw against a harmful effect. Roll a D20 if you roll over a 15 you can double you chain effectively making a double chain heal doing your first roll again and if you do activate "<b>Healing</b><sup id="cite_ref-:5_14-1" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Strategic_Talents">Strategic Talents</span></h2>
<ul><li>Tactical Mind: Boosts strategic thinking and increases critical hit chance for tactical actions.
<ul><li>Roll a D8. On a roll of 6 or higher, gain advantage on Intelligence Rolls.</li>
<li>if you a 15 or higher on a Intelligence roll you can activate "<b>Adaptability</b><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li>
<li>Battlecry: Boosts attack power and morale of nearby allies.
<ul><li>Roll a D6. On a roll of 5 or higher, affected allies gain temporary hit points equal to your level in addition to the attack power boost.</li>
<li>Roll a D20 and if its 6 or higher activate "<b>Force Enhancement</b><sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>" and if successful your entire party will temporarily gain power strikes on their next physical attack</li></ul></li>
<li>Leadership: Provides a morale boost and increased stats for nearby allies.
<ul><li>Roll a D6. On a roll of 4 or higher, affected allies gain temporary Defensive hit points equal to your level and advantage on saving throws against fear effects for the duration.</li>
<li>if your allies gain Defensive hit points above 5 you and your allies will be allowed to have Resolve on one of each of your attacks.</li></ul></li></ul>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Elemental_and_Status_Effects">Elemental and Status Effects</span></h3>
<ul><li>Elemental Infusion: Temporarily imbues attacks with elemental damage.
<ul><li>Roll a D6. On a roll of 5 or higher, the infused attacks also have a chance to apply a corresponding status effect, such as burning or freezing the target.</li>
<li>if Burning is applied activate the Talent "<b>Infectious Logic</b><sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>"</li></ul></li>
<li>Elemental Convergence: Combines multiple elements to create a devastating attack.
<ul><li>Roll a D8. On a roll of 6 or higher, affected enemies also suffer from a random status effect such as burning, freezing, or being poisoned.</li>
<li>if the status is Chaos or Force activate the attribute "<b>Hybridization</b><sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>" and if the energy is transformed into Neutral you can freely use a magic attack without the need to roll and it will always do 50 damage</li></ul></li>
<li>Elemental Nova: Creates a powerful explosion of elemental energy, damaging enemies in an area.
<ul><li>Roll a D8. On a roll of 6 or higher, the explosion also applies a random elemental status effect such as burning, freezing, or shocking to affected enemies.</li>
<li>Roll a D40 and if the roll is higher than 37 activate the Attribute "<b>Nova</b><sup id="cite_ref-:6_20-0" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>" and the damage will be a base of 50 and scale based on the number you roll for "<b>Nova</b><sup id="cite_ref-:6_20-1" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>" so if its 2 it will be times 2 so 100 damage</li></ul></li>
<li>Poisoned Strike: Adds poison effect to all attacks, dealing damage over time.
<ul><li>Roll a D6. On a roll of 4 or higher, the poison's duration is extended by 1 round.</li>
<li>if the Poison's Duration was extended by 1 round activate Power strikes and Apply Necrotic damage</li></ul></li>
<li>Cursed Touch: Inflicts a curse on enemies, reducing their abilities and effectiveness.
<ul><li>Roll a D6. On a roll of 5 or higher, affected enemies also have disadvantage on rolls against your spells and abilities for a number of rounds equal to your level.</li>
<li>if the number of rolls is 10 or higher you will apply Poisoned strike and if you don't have it the effectiveness will be -10</li></ul></li>
<li>Mind Control: Temporarily takes control of an enemy, turning them against their allies.
<ul><li>Roll a D6. On a roll of 4 or higher, extend the duration of control by an additional round.</li>
<li>if you have them under your control activate "<b>Fear Induction</b><sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>" and if they have fear status roll a d20 if you get 16 or higher activate "<b>Frenzy</b><sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>" and whatever you make them attack will have "<b>Absorption</b><sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><span><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></span></sup>" applied and the healing this time will be a base of 10 then scale up</li></ul></li></ul>

<li>if the weapon returns activate the Talent Tactical Mind</li></ul></li>
