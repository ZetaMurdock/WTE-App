<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Inderi_Incepts">Inderi Incepts</span></h2>
<p><i>The Incept pool for the Inderi species. Used during Director Splitting to unlock latent ancestral potential. Inderi Inceptions reflect their primordial cognitive-void identity: mental singularity, eldritch perception, temporal manipulation, and consciousness projection. The Inderi are the only species whose players choose which Inceptions are Active versus held as latent potential.</i>
