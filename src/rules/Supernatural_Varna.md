<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Supernatural_Varna">Supernatural Varna</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="What_Supernatural_Varna_Is">What Supernatural Varna Is</span></h3>
<p>Where Mundane Varna is the body learning, Supernatural Varna is the energy recognizing you.
</p><p>Every use of a Genus energy type is a conversation between the practitioner and the force they channel. The Null field that accepts your control. The eldritch bloodline that responds to your intent. The element that moves before you direct it. Repeat the conversation enough — deal enough, survive enough, shape enough — and the energy stops waiting for your instruction. It begins to anticipate.
</p><p>Supernatural Varna techniques are instantaneous add-ons that activate alongside Genus ability uses, Cipher activations, and weapon attacks that carry the relevant energy type. They require no additional action, no SS expenditure (unless noted), and no activation declaration. They simply occur because the energy recognizes the pattern.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Activation_Rules">Activation Rules</span></h3>
<p>Supernatural Varnas activate when the trigger condition is met. A character with multiple Supernatural Varnas in the same path may activate all eligible techniques from one action simultaneously, at no cost, in the order listed.
</p><p><b>Suppression:</b> Like Mundane Varnas, Supernatural Varnas can be suppressed as a Free Action on any specific use. Some situations require suppression — activating Void Skin in a social encounter would be inappropriate.
</p><p><b>CAS Interaction:</b> Supernatural Varnas that produce visible energy effects trigger CAS zone rules at the same level as the ability that caused them. A Manipulation Varna activating in a Sanction Zoned area carries the same Audit risk as a Null ability used there.
