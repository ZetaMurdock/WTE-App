<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Soul_Link-_The_inter_linker">Soul Link- The inter linker</span></h2>
<p><b>Luencin Vandross</b> was a scientist obsessed with death. Not just the concept of it, but every intricate mystery it held. He dedicated his entire life—every waking moment and even his dreams—to understanding the dead, searching for a way to bring back his beloved wife from the cold, callous embrace of oblivion.
</p><p>Years turned into decades, and before he knew it, he was celebrating his 90th birthday. But this birthday was different. It was the day he finally found the answer he had been searching for.
</p><p>Tucked away in an old, decrepit house not far from his lab, he discovered an <b>amulet</b>—its dark aura pulsing with an eerie presence. He knew better than to touch it directly, so with a calculated mind, he cut off a piece of his own finger and used it to interact with the artifact. The moment it made contact, the amulet <b>bound itself to the severed flesh.</b> He carefully bagged the finger and continued exploring the house.
</p><p>Eventually, he found himself in the attic, where a <b>rotting corpse hung from a noose.</b> The moment his eyes met the decayed remains, an unnatural chill crept up his spine, locking him in place. He tried to move, but his body refused. <b>Something was watching.</b> He turned away, forcing himself to leave, unaware that he had just become bound to an unseen presence.
</p><p>The spirit, <b>Celesti Bondr,</b> had been waiting. Back in his lab, Luencin wasted no time analyzing the severed finger, running test after test. With everything in place, he <b>began the operation.</b> Hours passed—<b>ten long hours of meticulous, desperate work</b>—and finally, he stood over the reconstructed form of his wife, waiting for her to awaken.
</p><p>Minutes passed.
</p><p>Nothing.
</p><p>Then Celesti had an idea—she could <b>inhabit the body.</b>
</p><p>She did.
</p><p>For a fleeting moment, the corpse <b>stirred.</b> Then something went horribly, <b>terribly wrong.</b>
</p><p>The body convulsed violently, as if rejecting her. Celesti was <b>forcefully expelled,</b> flung back to the house she had been tethered to. But before Luencin could process what had happened, <b>another entity entered the body.</b>
</p><p>Luencin took a step back. Confusion turned to horror as the <b>thing</b> in his wife's form slowly rose.
</p><p>The <b>doors slammed shut.</b> The <b>windows darkened.</b> Shadows swallowed the room as the <b>entity's gaze fixed on him.</b> Its head hung <b>unnaturally still,</b> its arms and legs <b>dangling limply.</b>
</p><p>Then, without warning, <b>Luencin was lifted into the air.</b>
</p><p>He tried to scream, but his voice was stolen. An <b>invisible force</b> began peeling away his flesh, ripping it from his bones layer by layer. His <b>eyes melted into nothingness,</b> his hair disintegrated, his body was stripped down <b>to the skeleton beneath.</b> Then came the final horror. The amulet, still bound to his severed finger, <b>forced itself into his exposed ribcage.</b> His hollow frame convulsed as dark energy <b>rewove his existence,</b> chaining him to an unnatural, undead form.
</p><p>The entity loomed closer, its voice an echo from the void:
</p><p><b>"Seek us again, and the punishment will be worse than death."</b>
</p><p>Then, silence.
</p><p>Luencin sat there, unable to think. Trapped in his skeletal husk, bound to the amulet, lost in the echoes of his failure.
</p><p>Until <b>he was found.</b> A presence—a being far greater than what had just cursed him—manifested in the room. A <b>strong, commanding voice</b> broke the silence:
</p><p><b>"Hiya there! How would you like to become an archetype?"</b>
</p><p>Before he could respond, a surge of <b>green energy</b> enveloped him. A <b>phylactery</b> materialized before him—an artifact of undeath, granting him the very power that had been forced upon him. He could now <b>inflict the same fate upon others,</b> twisting the cycle of life and death. More than that, he could <b>bestow phylacteries upon others,</b> sharing his power in ways that defied nature itself.
</p><p>Before he could speak, the figure—<b>the Director</b>—was gone.
</p><p>The room brightened. The doors creaked open. The suffocating darkness <b>lifted.</b>
</p><p>A new life had begun. A life after death. 
</p><p>And after the dust had settled the Amulet reconstructed itself in the same place still awaiting for the right one to take it. 
</p>
