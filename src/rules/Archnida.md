<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>Archnida did not emerge from a genetic program or Director edict -- they emerged from patience. Insectoid populations isolated in vertical or enclosed environments gradually shifted toward arachnid utility: extra limbs, spinnerets, compound ambush instincts. They are not widely regarded as threatening among those who have never encountered one in their natural environment. Those who have do not make the same mistake twice. An Archnida-prepared battlefield looks empty. It is not empty. It is ready.
</p>
