# Action Types

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_System_with_Fatigue_Mechanic">Action System with Fatigue Mechanic</span></h2>
<p>Types of Actions
</p>
<ol><li>Basic Action: Perform one ability without any AP cost.</li>
<li>Additional Actions (AP): Use AP to perform more actions beyond the basic action.
<ul><li>First Additional Action: Costs AP equal to the user's level.</li>
<li>Second Additional Action: Costs AP equal to half the user's level (rounded up).</li>
<li>Subsequent Actions: Continue with the cost equal to half the user's level (rounded up).</li></ul></li>
<li>Opportunity Actions: React to enemy triggers.</li>
<li>True Opportunity Actions: Powerful reactions under specific conditions.</li>
<li>focused Actions: Minor actions that don’t consume AP.-Prepared action used for an ability that you have.</li>
<li>Bonus Actions: Quick, additional actions with limitations.</li></ol>
<p>Fatigue System
</p>
<ol><li>Accumulation:
<ul><li>Each AP action taken after the basic action adds 3 fatigue points to the character.</li>
<li>Fatigue points accumulate with each additional AP action taken during a single turn.</li></ul></li></ol>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Recovery">Recovery</span></h2>
<ul><li><b>Downtime</b>: Characters can recover from fatigue points during downtime, which is equivalent to a short rest.
<ul><li>Example: During downtime (10 minutes), characters recover 1 fatigue point.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Special Abilities</b>: Certain abilities or items can reduce fatigue points.
<ul><li>Example: A healing spell or potion that specifically reduces fatigue by 2 points.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Environmental Factors</b>: Specific locations or events may help in reducing fatigue.
<ul><li>Example: Resting at a sanctuary or using a rejuvenation pool reduces fatigue points.</li></ul></li></ul>
