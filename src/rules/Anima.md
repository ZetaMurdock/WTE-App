<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Anima">Anima</span></h2>
<p><i>They are not born. They are not summoned. They are what happens when an emotion accumulates enough celestial energy to become real.</i>
</p><p>Anima are manifestations of celestial energy -- the raw substrate threading through the Enigma's cosmology -- given form by concentrated emotional resonance. They are not creatures in any conventional sense. They have no goals, no memories, no preferences. They have a <b>direction</b>. An Anima of Wrath destroys because Wrath destroys. An Anima of Gluttony consumes because Gluttony consumes. The behavior is not chosen. It is the emotion, expressed as action.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Nature_of_Anima">The Nature of Anima</span></h3>
<p>Celestial energy responds to emotion. This is not metaphor -- in the Enigma's cosmology, emotion generates measurable resonant signatures in the celestial field. Under the right conditions (areas of concentrated psychic output, catastrophic events, prolonged dimensional instability), that resonance nucleates into a physical form.
</p><p>The resulting entity expresses its source emotion <b>completely</b>, without restraint, and without the moderating influence of a self. This is what makes Anima dangerous even at low tiers. A creature that is angry can choose to stop. An Anima of Wrath cannot.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Stalkers_and_Celestial_Exposure">Stalkers and Celestial Exposure</span></h3>
<p>Stalkers -- scavengers and opportunists who work in celestial-energy-rich environments -- are among the most frequently exposed to active Anima. Over time, this exposure has consequences. The celestial field begins to respond to the Stalker's own dominant emotional resonance. They begin to change.
</p><p>This process is called <b>Alter Anima</b> -- the point at which a Stalker's inner self begins to manifest physically. Greed becomes physical accumulation. Hatred becomes a weaponized form. Fear becomes something that hunts in the dark. The self does not disappear. It becomes the body.
</p><p>See <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Alter_Anima">Alter Anima</a> for full mechanics and statblocks.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Engaging_Anima">Engaging Anima</span></h3>
<p>Anima cannot be negotiated with. They can be disrupted, suppressed, redirected, or destroyed. Certain Incepts and Genera abilities interact specifically with celestial energy, making them more effective against Anima than conventional attacks. Directors should note that killing an Anima at Manifested or Apex tier does not necessarily destroy the underlying celestial resonance -- it disperses the form. If the emotional conditions persist in the area, a new Anima may coalesce.
</p>
