<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Lexiconist">Archetype: <i>Lexiconist</i></span></h1>
<p><i>Words are the windows to understanding; let’s change that.</i>
</p><p>The <b>Lexiconist</b> is a linguistic shaper and reconstructor, able to <b>manipulate words, their meanings, and their relations to other words</b>. They can fully understand any language they hear and bend it to their will. Lexiconists excel in <b>manipulation</b>, capable of acting as <b>frontliners or strategic support</b> depending on the context, influencing reality itself through the power of language.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  The Lexiconist <b>alters reality by reshaping language</b>, controlling objects, concepts, and creatures through the logic, syntax, and meaning of words. They function as <b>strategic manipulators</b>, controlling the battlefield or social interactions with precision.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  Lexiconists adapt their pace according to the flow of combat. They can <b>strike rapidly with precise word manipulations</b> or <b>act methodically</b>, reshaping environments and targets over time. Their rhythm is a balance of mental planning and direct linguistic execution.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  Vulnerable to <b>mind-altering effects that obscure understanding</b>, languages they cannot comprehend, or attacks that bypass semantic and syntactic logic. Direct physical attacks are a threat if the Lexiconist is overextended.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  <b>Syntactic Reality &amp; Word Dominance</b> — the ability to <b>manifest words and structures that rewrite reality</b> and enforce new meanings, bending the natural order within a controlled space.</li></ul>
<p><i>Lexiconists excel in tactical and manipulative play, rewarding careful observation, creativity, and precise execution of language-based abilities.</i>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function"><i>Base Function</i></span></h2>
<p>Lexiconists can <b>perceive, manipulate, and restructure words, phrases, and their semantic connections</b>, reshaping reality in a controlled area. Effects include:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Word Dominator:</b> Alter the meaning of any word under control; new definitions are recognized by the world locally.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>True Word Relation:</b> Change relationships between concepts to alter outcomes.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Composite Word Creation:</b> Merge roots, prefixes, and suffixes to generate new effects or beings.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Syntactic Reality:</b> Construct metaphysical syntactic trees to manipulate objects, creatures, and events.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Viral Words:</b> Spread logical or semantic influence through language to reshape perception.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b> Physical destruction, normal magic, or misunderstanding by unaware creatures.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b> Soul manipulation, anti-linguistic magic, overwhelming chaos that disrupts semantic coherence.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b> The Lexiconist releases control, the target escapes the linguistic field, or the environment nullifies semantic influence.</li></ul>
