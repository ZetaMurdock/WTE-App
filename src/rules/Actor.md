<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Actors">Actors</span></h2>
<hr>
<h3><span id="The_Actors_.28Players.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Actors_(Players)">The Actors (Players)</span></h3>
<p>In <i>Wonderland of the Enigma</i> (W.T.E.), the <b>Actors</b> are the heartbeat of the story, as their actions directly shape the narrative. These players possess the ability to adapt, evolve, and overcome various challenges, often making sacrifices for the greater good or survival. They have the power to switch roles during downtime to fill gaps in the party’s needs—becoming healers, tanks, or damage dealers as the situation demands. Actors are dynamic, creative, and often hold the fate of the story in their hands, empowered by the <b>Actor Scene Cards</b>, their key tools for shaping the world and their journey.
</p>
<h4><span id="Actor.27s_Cards"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Actor's_Cards">Actor's Cards</span></h4>
<p>These specialized cards represent actions, abilities, and interactions that Actors can perform during gameplay. Each card has specific mechanics that tie into the narrative and gameplay, enhancing the Actor's influence on events.
</p>
<hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actor_Scene_Cards">Actor Scene Cards</span></h3>
<p><b>Script Chain</b> <i>(AP-1)</i>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Description:</b> Initiates a sequential series of connected actions, allowing an Actor to build upon their previous moves.</li>
<li><b>Mechanics:</b> Grants a +1 bonus to the next action roll. If successful, the chain continues, adding +1 for each consecutive action (max +3).</li>
<li><b>Interaction with Stats:</b>
<ul><li><b>Proficiency:</b> Reduces AP cost by 1 for every 3 levels of proficiency.</li>
<li><b>Improvisation:</b> Allows a re-roll for any failed action in the chain once per encounter.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><b>Counter</b> <i>(AP-4)</i>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Description:</b> Enables the Actor to counteract an opponent's action, mitigating or nullifying its effects.</li>
<li><b>Mechanics:</b> Roll against the opponent’s action roll. On a success, negate the action; on a critical success, reflect the action back at the opponent.</li>
<li><b>Interaction with Stats:</b>
<ul><li><b>Notability:</b> Reduces opponent’s roll by the Actor's Notability modifier if high.</li>
<li><b>Proficiency:</b> Provides +2 to the counter roll for every 5 points of Proficiency.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><b>Interaction</b> <i>(AP-X)</i>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Description:</b> Initiates a general interaction with the environment, NPCs, or other actors.</li>
<li><b>Mechanics:</b> AP cost varies by complexity; simple interactions cost 1 AP, while complex ones may cost up to 5 AP.</li>
<li><b>Interaction with Stats:</b>
<ul><li><b>Improvisation:</b> Reduces AP cost by 1 per 4 Improvisation points (min 1 AP).</li>
<li><b>Notability:</b> Grants bonuses to persuasion/intimidation checks during interactions.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><b>Counter-Interaction</b> <i>(AP-4)</i>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Description:</b> Allows the Actor to respond to an opponent’s interaction, countering or altering its effects.</li>
<li><b>Mechanics:</b> Similar to Counter, but for interactions (e.g., negotiations). Roll against the opponent's interaction roll to alter the outcome.</li>
<li><b>Interaction with Stats:</b>
<ul><li><b>Proficiency:</b> Adds +1 to the counter roll for every 3 Proficiency points.</li>
<li><b>Improvisation:</b> Grants advantage on the roll once per encounter.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><b>Past Vision</b> <i>(AP-10)</i>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Description:</b> Enables the Actor to glimpse a brief vision of the past, aiding in the current situation.</li>
<li><b>Mechanics:</b> Grants +5 to the next perception/investigation roll. Reveals hidden clues or secrets.</li>
<li><b>Interaction with Stats:</b>
<ul><li><b>Proficiency:</b> Reduces AP cost by 1 for every 3 points of Proficiency.</li>
<li><b>Notability:</b> Enhances the vision, adding narrative insights.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><b>Hidden Agenda</b> <i>(AP-10)</i>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Description:</b> Lets the Actor set a hidden goal, affecting future interactions.</li>
<li><b>Mechanics:</b> Provides advantage on deception/persuasion rolls related to the hidden agenda. Can reveal itself as a plot twist.</li>
<li><b>Interaction with Stats:</b>
<ul><li><b>Improvisation:</b> Reduces AP cost by 1 per 3 Improvisation points.</li>
<li><b>Notability:</b> Grants +2 to the first roll involving the hidden agenda.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><b>Flashback</b> <i>(AP-15)</i>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Description:</b> Recalls a significant memory that aids the Actor in the current situation.</li>
<li><b>Mechanics:</b> Grants a +3 bonus to relevant stat for the next three rolls.</li>
<li><b>Interaction with Stats:</b>
<ul><li><b>Proficiency:</b> Reduces AP cost by 1 for every 3 Proficiency points.</li>
<li><b>Notability:</b> Increases the bonus to +5 if Notability is high.</li></ul></li></ul>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Actor_Ability_Scores">Actor Ability Scores</span></h3>
<p>Actors have three core ability scores that define their role in the story and determine their effectiveness in various actions:
</p><p><b>Improvisation</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Description:</b> Reflects an Actor’s ability to adapt and think on their feet, enhancing creativity in unpredictable situations.</li>
<li><b>Mechanics:</b>
<ul><li>Reduces AP cost for various actions.</li>
<li>Allows re-rolls in certain scenarios.</li>
<li>Provides advantage on rolls requiring quick thinking.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><b>Proficiency</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Description:</b> Measures the Actor’s skill and expertise in their chosen abilities.</li>
<li><b>Mechanics:</b>
<ul><li>Grants bonuses to action rolls, making success more likely.</li>
<li>Reduces AP cost for actions as proficiency increases.</li>
<li>Increases the effectiveness of actions.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p><b>Notability</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Description:</b> Represents the Actor’s presence and influence within the narrative, with the ability to impact other characters.</li>
<li><b>Mechanics:</b>
<ul><li>Reduces opponents' rolls by leveraging Notability.</li>
<li>Enhances persuasion and intimidation checks.</li>
<li>Provides narrative influence and deeper character insights.</li></ul></li></ul>

</p>
