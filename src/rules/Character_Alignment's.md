<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Alignments">Alignments</span></h2>
<pre>Lawful Good: Characters who uphold order, justice, and compassion, and follow a strict code of ethics.
</pre>
<pre>Neutral Good: Characters who value goodness and helping others but are more flexible in their adherence to laws and rules.
</pre>
<pre>Chaotic Good: Characters who prioritize personal freedom and individual rights and are willing to break laws for the greater good.
</pre>
<pre>Lawful Neutral: Characters who are guided by a sense of duty, order, and discipline, without a strong focus on morality.
</pre>
<pre>True Neutral: Characters who are neutral in all aspects, not favoring any particular alignment or morality.
</pre>
<pre>Chaotic Neutral: Characters who prioritize personal freedom and individualism above all else, often disregarding laws and moral conventions.
</pre>
<pre>Lawful Evil: Characters who use laws, rules, and order to further their own selfish and evil goals.
</pre>
<pre>Neutral Evil: Characters who are primarily motivated by their own self-interest and are willing to harm others to achieve their goals.
</pre>
<pre>Chaotic Evil: Characters who embrace chaos, destruction, and violence and have no regard for laws, morality, or the well-being of others.
</pre>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Religion_Requirements">Religion Requirements</span></h2>
<pre>Cult of the Elder Gods: Perform daily meditations to commune with ancient knowledge. Seek out and study forbidden texts.
</pre>
<pre>Order of the Forbidden Tome: Engage in scholarly pursuits and share knowledge with others. Seek out rare and forbidden tomes to expand the order's library.
</pre>
<pre>Coven of the Eldritch Moon: Conduct lunar rituals during specific moon phases. Create and maintain moonlit sanctuaries to honor the eldritch moon's influence.
</pre>

</pre>

</pre>

</pre>
<pre>Cult of the Crawling Chaos: Embrace chaos and disrupt order. Engage in random acts of mischief and unpredictability to spread the influence of the Crawling Chaos.
</pre>

</pre>
<pre>Cult of the Nameless City: Explore ancient ruins and unearth forgotten artifacts. Decode cryptic symbols and languages to unlock the secrets of the Nameless City.

</pre>
<pre>Disciples of the Black Goat: Participate in fertility rituals and ceremonies. Care for and protect animals associated with the Black Goat as a sign of devotion.
</pre>
<pre>Sect of the Dreaming Eye: Practice lucid dreaming and strive to interpret prophetic visions. Keep dream journals and share insights with fellow sect members.
</pre>
<pre>Order of the Starry Wisdom: Observe and study celestial phenomena. Build observatories and stargazing towers to honor the cosmic nature of the religion.

</pre>

</pre>
<pre>Cult of the Unseen Masters: Engage in acts of subtle manipulation and control. Use invisible means to guide and influence others towards the cult's goals.
</pre>
<pre>Society of the Arcane Glyphs: Study ancient glyphs and symbols. Create intricate and powerful magical sigils to manifest desired outcomes.
</pre>
<pre>Order of the Shrouded Abyss: Embrace darkness and shadows. Conduct rituals in complete darkness and strive to manipulate and control shadows.
</pre>

</pre>
<pre>Sect of the Formless Spawn: Embrace shapelessness and engage in shapeshifting practices. Explore the boundaries of physical form and identity.
</pre>
<pre>The Eldritch Choir: Perform arcane chants and hymns to invoke otherworldly forces. Seek out rare musical instruments and compose haunting melodies.
</pre>

</pre>
<pre>Cult of the Tenebrous Eclipse: Seek out rare celestial events and perform rituals during eclipses. Study the influence of cosmic forces on mortal existence.
</pre>
<pre>Society of the Whispers of Madness: Indulge in madness-inducing substances and rituals. Engage in mind-altering experiences to transcend mortal limitations.
</pre>
<pre>The Haunted Mystics: Explore haunted locations and communicate with spirits. Use divination tools to receive messages from the spiritual realm.
</pre>
<pre>Brotherhood of the Veiled Shadows: Engage in covert operations and subterfuge. Infiltrate organizations and gather secretive information to further the brotherhood's agenda.
</pre>
<pre>Cult of the Whispering Void: Engage in rituals of void worship and meditation. Seek out places of utter silence to listen for the whispers of the void.
