<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>Unlike baseline Hyomen, Neo-Humans cannot be subjugated -- no Nigraldi override vector exists in their genome. This single biological fact has made them the most politically significant species in the Northern Territories. They govern through diplomacy rather than military force. They are resented by species who distrust any group that has simply removed its greatest institutional vulnerability while remaining biologically proximate to the species that carries it. Neo-Humans face discrimination dressed up as suspicion -- accusations that their political dominance is a kind of biological entitlement, that their safety from Nigraldi control is itself a form of power. They have learned to be very careful about how they move through the world.
</p>
