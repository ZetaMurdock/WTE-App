</div><div style="background:#121212;border-left:4px solid #507028;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:18px;"><div style="font-size:1.6em;font-weight:bold;color:#80b040;letter-spacing:0.06em;font-family:monospace;">MIRGA</div><div style="color:#508040;font-size:0.85em;margin-top:4px;font-style:italic;">Group: Humanity — A species defined entirely by mimicry — biologically, socially, and emotionally.</div></div><div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #182810;border-left:3px solid #507028;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;color:#a0c080;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>Mirga are not a unified species in the traditional sense — their biology IS adaptability. They don't just copy forms; they replicate auras, emotional states, energy signatures, and perceived identities. A Mirga interacting with a crowd doesn't stand out because standing out is itself a form they can discard.
</p><p>Their low Dom/Rec spread makes Mirga hybrids genuinely unpredictable. Traits may emerge in any pattern, any combination, or suppress entirely. Their moderate Civilized Eminence (+10) reflects how they're regarded: trusted enough to integrate into most social structures, but watched — because confirming a Mirga's true identity requires more than looking at them.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="HYBRID_INTERACTION">HYBRID INTERACTION</span></h2>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #182810;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:16px;color:#a0c080;font-size:0.9em;">
<p>Mirga Dom 20 / Rec 15 — a low, close-spread hybrid profile that makes Mirga trait expression genuinely unpredictable. Unlike high-Dom species where traits reliably assert, Mirga hybrids may express any combination of parental traits in any pattern, or suppress Mirga traits nearly entirely. This reflects the species' nature: even their heredity resists categorization.
</p>
