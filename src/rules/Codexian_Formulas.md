# Codexian Formulas

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p>Here's a structured formula system for your <b>W.T.E. Energy Mechanics</b>, designed to be clear, mathematically grounded, and able to generate <b>in-universe phenomena</b> using calculations.
</p>
<hr>


<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Energy_Formula_System_for_W.T.E."><b>Energy Formula System for W.T.E.</b></span></h1>
<p>This system mathematically governs all energy interactions, allowing players and world-builders to define <b>how energy behaves, scales, and creates phenomena</b> in the game.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="General_Formula_Structure"><b>General Formula Structure</b></span></h2>
<p>Every energy type follows a universal equation based on:
</p>
<ul><li><b>IN</b> = Imaginary Number (Baseline Energy Input)</li>
<li><b>G</b> = G-Force Scaling (1-10, logarithmic progression)</li>
<li><b>D</b> = Density (Energy per unit of space)</li>
<li><b>V</b> = Volume of Influence (Range/Area affected)</li>
<li><b>T</b> = Time (Duration before dissipation)</li>
<li><b>F</b> = Frequency (How often an ability cycles/refreshes)</li>
<li><b>S</b> = Stability (Risk Factor Mitigation)</li></ul>

<h2><span id="Risk_Factor_System_.28S-Value.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Risk_Factor_System_(S-Value)"><b>Risk Factor System (S-Value)</b></span></h2>
<p>Since <b>high energy levels come with danger</b>, the <b>S-Stability value</b> determines <b>how controlled the energy is</b>:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Perfect Stability (S = 1)</b> → No risk</li>
<li><b>Unstable (S &lt; 1)</b> → Increases risk of failure, backlash, or reality distortion</li>
<li><b>Overload (S &gt; 1.5)</b> → Energy cannot be contained, triggering catastrophic effects</li></ul>
