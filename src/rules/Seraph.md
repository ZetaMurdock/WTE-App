# Seraph

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #282010;padding:8px 14px;margin-bottom:12px;font-family:monospace;font-size:0.82em;color:#807030;">
<p><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Species">Species</a>&nbsp;› <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Asternem">Asternem</a>&nbsp;› <strong class="mw-selflink selflink">Seraph</strong>
</p>
</div><div style="background:#121212;border-left:4px solid #706020;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:18px;"><div style="font-size:1.6em;font-weight:bold;color:#b09838;letter-spacing:0.06em;font-family:monospace;">SERAPH</div><div style="color:#807030;font-size:0.85em;margin-top:4px;font-style:italic;">Group: Asternem — Divine-origin species of exceptional power. The highest Civilized Eminence in the game.</div></div><div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #282010;border-left:3px solid #706020;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;color:#c0b060;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>Seraph carry the highest Civilized Eminence of any species (+40) — they move through institutions with near-unconditional backing. Doors that are closed to others open for Seraph. Systems that flag other species pass them without scrutiny. Whether they deserve this deference is a question the setting does not answer definitively.
</p><p>Their abilities suggest a species optimized for information, mobility, and spatial control rather than raw combat. The Antimatter Wings are the most visually striking, but Spatial Rupture and Distant Vision are arguably more strategically significant — the ability to reshape an area and see across extraordinary distances without moving creates an intelligence advantage most other species cannot replicate.
</p><p>Seraph tie with Hyomen and Voaulton for the highest Dominance (45) and tie with Inderi and Voaulton for the lowest Recessiveness (5). In hybrid offspring, Seraph traits almost always dominate and almost never recede.
</p><p>No variants are currently documented.
</p>
</div>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="HYBRID_INTERACTION">HYBRID INTERACTION</span></h2>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #282010;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:16px;color:#c0b060;font-size:0.9em;">
<p>Seraph Dom 45 / Rec 5 — the most dominant hybrid profile in the species roster, tied at the extremes. Seraph traits almost always assert in hybrid offspring and almost never recede. Playing a Seraph hybrid means the Seraph parent's traits will define the lineage. The institutional Eminence benefit (+40 Civilized) does not carry fully into hybrid offspring — Eminence is assessed by institutional perception, not biology, and mixed-lineage Seraph are assessed on their full profile.
</p>
</div>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="EMINENCE_CONTEXT">EMINENCE CONTEXT</span></h2>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #282010;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:16px;"><div style="background:#201808;border-left:3px solid #706020;padding:10px 14px;color:#b09838;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>Seraph sit at one extreme of the Eminence spectrum. At the opposite end: Stygians at Feral +20. The gulf between these two species in institutional terms is the widest in the game. A Seraph and a Stygian navigating the same CAS-managed space are treated as fundamentally different categories of entity — one trusted by default, one flagged by default — regardless of their personal behavior or record.
</p><p>This dynamic is a deliberate design element of the WTE Eminence system.
</p>
