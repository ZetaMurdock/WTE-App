# Seraph

</div><div style="background:#121212;border-left:4px solid #706020;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:18px;"><div style="font-size:1.6em;font-weight:bold;color:#b09838;letter-spacing:0.06em;font-family:monospace;">SERAPH</div><div style="color:#807030;font-size:0.85em;margin-top:4px;font-style:italic;">Group: Asternem — Divine-origin species of exceptional power. The highest Civilized Eminence in the game.</div></div><div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #282010;border-left:3px solid #706020;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;color:#c0b060;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>The Seraph are what the Mogul Government classifies as Mulmauth Individuals—beings capable of interacting with something that appears almost magical. Their abilities defy natural order and biological logic, allowing them to manipulate energy through a strange medium unlike anything ever documented. A term frequently associated with them is <i>tones</i>, a concept the Mogul Government has tried—and failed—to decipher.
</p><p>If the government ever succeeds, they believe it would grant access to Subspace technology, ushering in an entirely new era of power and inquisitors. Even the combined strength of all major superpowers would struggle against a Mogul force equipped with such advancements.
</p><p>Because of this, the Seraph live in seclusion, often adopting new names and identities when serving as inquisitors. They prefer a faceless existence rather than one where elders whisper about them in corners for the rest of their days.
</p>
</div>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="SPECIES_AND_ROLL_AXIS">SPECIES &amp; ROLL AXIS</span></h2>
<div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #282010;border-left:3px solid #706020;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:16px;color:#c0b060;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>Species abilities use the universal Roll Axis. Seraph traits may modify, substitute, restrict, force, or automatically resolve a Check or Save when explicitly stated, but Seraph does not grant Favored Attributes or Favored Specialties by itself.
</p><p>Paradigm Affinity remains separate: if a Seraph or Seraph Variant ability changes which Attribute or Specialty legitimately governs a resolution, Paradigm Affinity follows the statistic actually used. Automatic species effects remain Automatic and do not create a roll unless the ability specifically calls for one.
</p>
</div>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="NONSAPN-DOKERU">NONSAPN-DOKERU</span></h3>

<p>Seraph who abandoned the traditional external Medium and engraved the Voice directly into their own flesh. Once treated as outsiders for this taboo practice, their unusual intimacy with the Voice eventually earned them the title Dokeru — wise teachers. Many later became elders of Seraph society and codified unsanctioned self-mediuming as Unkraft, a forbidden practice. Nonsapn-Dokeru remain the only lineage traditionally permitted to survive and practice it.

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="ORIS">ORIS</span></h3>
<div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #282010;border-left:3px solid #706020;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;color:#c0b060;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>The Oris are the culmination of the once-Orialia and the modern Seraph after the two lineages encountered and reintegrated with one another. They retain the Orialian tendency toward environmental adaptation while expressing Seraph Tones through a body capable of dispersing into a fine, luminous stardust. To an Oris, physical form is less a boundary than a material they may temporarily redistribute.
</p>
