<p>The <b>Neutral Genus</b> is the domain of physics-adjacent manipulation and utility. Neutral energy operates as a direct interface with physical law — not breaking it, but bending it through precise application. Neutral practitioners are the ultimate utility specialists: animators, builders, scouts, and support pillars. Neutral is the only domain with no SNR and no mandatory offensive design — its damage comes from creative application.
