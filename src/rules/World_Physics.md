<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Augurian_Physics">Augurian Physics</span></h2>
<p>All things within <b>W.T.E - Enigmatica</b> contain energy, and this energy can be manipulated in various ways to create what appears to be magical effects. All creatures within Enigmatica possess a <b>sensory organ</b>  called the Augo Gland within their bodies that enables them to manipulate this energy in a myriad of ways. However, this organ adapts its method of energy manipulation in response to several factors, including:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Light</b></li>
<li><b>Time</b></li>
<li><b>Density</b></li>
<li><b>Weight</b></li>
<li><b>Mass</b></li>
<li><b>Counteracting forces</b> (e.g., intervention of some kind initiating an ability)</li>
<li><b>Will</b></li>
<li><b>Drive</b></li>
<li><b>Motivation</b></li>
<li><b>Creativity</b></li>
<li><b>Ode Level</b></li>
<li><b>Blood pressure</b></li>
<li><b>Mood</b></li></ul>
<p>This energy can be manipulated, but only within the confines of its existence—it cannot be altered unless <b>Bile Law</b> is in effect.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Bile_Law">Bile Law</span></h2>
<p>Bile Law is the process by which energy is converted and manipulated through external intervention or counteracting forces.
</p><p>This principle is the foundation of how <b>Archetypes</b> function. Archetypes are formed through the application of Bile Law, shaping energy based on the multitude of influencing factors that govern its manifestation.
</p>
<h3><span id="Energy_Manipulation_.26_Ode_Level"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Energy_Manipulation_&amp;_Ode_Level">Energy Manipulation &amp; Ode Level</span></h3>
<p>When utilizing this energy, it must remain within the confines of its bearer. This limitation is determined by the user's <b>Ode Level</b>—a measure of their ability to manipulate energy.
</p><p>A user's Ode Level can naturally be upgraded <b>twice</b>, but through <b>external intervention</b>, it can be upgraded <b>almost infinitely</b>. This phenomenon is governed by the <b>Enlil Law of Energy Transference</b>.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Enlil_Law_of_Energy_Transference">Enlil Law of Energy Transference</span></h2>
<p>The <b>Law of Transference</b> describes the process of taking a portion of an energy source and replacing it with another. However, this energy must be transferred to a suitable location; otherwise, the originator of the energy would cease to exist.
</p><p>Since existence is dependent on energy, the removal of all energy from an entity results in <b>erasure</b>—a state in which the being is broken down and returned to raw energy. This occurs due to another principle known as the <b>Law of Origin</b>.
</p><p>In practical terms, the Law of Transference allows for two outcomes:
</p>
<ol><li><b>Partial Transference</b> – If only a portion of energy is transferred, the original bearer remains alive and functional.</li>
<li><b>Complete Transference</b> – If the entire energy source is replaced, the original entity ceases to exist. However, a <b>new version</b> of that being is created, evolving into something greater.</li></ol>
<p>In the case of complete transference, the new being can either:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Create a new body</b> using their current energy, though their Ode Level would reset or decrease.</li>
<li><b>Inhabit an existing body</b> that has lost its energy, effectively taking over its form.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Law_of_Origin">Law of Origin</span></h2>
<p>The <b>Law of Origin</b> states that energy must always be present within a given space, across all phenomena, at all times. If energy is absent from a space, that space will collapse and cease to exist.
</p><p>This principle applies to all aspects of <b>Enigmatica</b>, including both <b>organic and inorganic life</b>. Since all things originate from some form of energy, they <b>cannot exist</b> without it—and if energy is completely removed, they will <b>cease to be entirely</b>.
</p>
<h3><span id="Energy_.26_Its_Eternal_Cycle"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Energy_&amp;_Its_Eternal_Cycle">Energy &amp; Its Eternal Cycle</span></h3>
<p>Energy will always exist, but when it is used, it must be replenished. This necessity leads to the <b>Law of Conservation &amp; Decay</b>.
</p>
<hr>
<h3><span id="The_Law_of_Conservation_.26_Decay"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Law_of_Conservation_&amp;_Decay">The Law of Conservation &amp; Decay</span></h3>
<blockquote><p><b>Energy cannot be created or destroyed, but it can decay into a lesser, unusable form unless actively maintained.</b> This decay can be countered by replenishing energy from external sources.</p></blockquote><p>This law ensures that energy is never infinite—prolonged use without proper restoration leads to degradation, forcing individuals to seek replenishment methods to maintain their abilities.
</p><hr>
<h3><span id="Eyulis.E2.80.99_Law_of_Spatial_Persistence"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Eyulis’_Law_of_Spatial_Persistence">Eyulis’ Law of Spatial Persistence</span></h3>
<blockquote><p><b>Energy is in a constant state of movement unless altered by an external force.</b> It remains in this active state and cannot change on its own, except in rare cases where internal forces cause a shift.</p></blockquote><p>This law dictates that energy does not remain stagnant; it continues flowing, shifting, and interacting with its environment unless deliberately manipulated.
</p><hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Law_of_Catalyzation">The Law of Catalyzation</span></h3>
<blockquote><p><b>Energy cannot be used in its raw form—it must go through a catalyst or conversion process to be adapted for a specific function.</b></p></blockquote><p>This law explains why energy users require techniques, conduits, or biological adaptations to channel their power effectively. Without proper conversion, raw energy remains inert or unstable.
</p><hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Ode_Limit_Law">The Ode Limit Law</span></h3>
<blockquote><p><b>Surpassing one's natural Ode Level is impossible unless external forces, such as intervention from a higher being, are involved.</b> Attempting to forcefully exceed one’s limits will always result in catastrophic consequences.</p></blockquote><blockquote><p><b>A failed attempt to surpass the Ode Limit will result in a 100% chance of backlash, which may include:</b></p></blockquote>
<ul><li><b>Loss of an ability</b></li>
<li><b>Mental or physical blockages, preventing further energy use</b></li>
<li><b>Permanent loss of access to the Augo Gland</b> (the organ responsible for energy manipulation)</li></ul>
<p>Even if the Augo Gland becomes permanently inaccessible, the individual will still contain energy, but they will no longer be able to manipulate it through their natural abilities.
</p>
