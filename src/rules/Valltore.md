<p>&gt; The **Valltore** were created as the ultimate hybrid by the Oracle, combining traits from the Kinba, Hyomen, Voaulton, and Mirage, with the Hyomen as the primary biological foundation. Designed to be both physically striking and biologically versatile, the Valltore possess a unique symbiosis with their Augo Gland, which can be externalized as a sentient entity called the **Aahg**. This connection allows the Valltore to act in tandem with the Aahg, using it for offense, defense, and strategic manipulation of organic matter.

<ol><li><ol><li>**Innate Species Abilities**</li></ol></li></ol>
<p>1. **Aahg Manifestation**
</p>
<pre>   You can externalize your Augo Gland as a sentient entity. The **Aahg** can act independently, replicate your actions, and enhance rolls using your Ode Level. If the Aahg dies, you also perish.
</pre>
<p>2. **Organic Corruption**
</p>
<pre>   Using the Aahg, you can manipulate organic matter in targets with a lower Ode Level, causing mutations, weakening functions, or partial control. This is limited by your Ode Level versus the target’s.
</pre>
<p>3. **Bacterial Generation**
</p>
<pre>   Your Aahg can generate and control bacteria, allowing it to alter the properties of attacks or defenses. This can heal, poison, corrode, or reinforce matter, including some inorganic materials.
</pre>
<p>4. **Archetypal Mastery**
</p>
<pre>   At creation, you gain **5 Archetype Points** that can be assigned to abilities across different archetypes. These abilities are permanently available to the Aahg, enabling unique combinations and tactical versatility.
</pre>
