<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>Key figures in the Northern Territories political and technological infrastructure. Specifically targeted by certain powers due to their Tauron lineage -- their connection to the First Ruina event makes them politically vulnerable despite their civilized cultural role. The Cyborg are the Father Species: the hybridization event that created them also created the conditions for the N-T1. They carry that weight. Some have sublimated it into a genuine dedication to repair and education. Others wear the stigma like armor. The Tauron-87 catastrophe is in their genome whether they like it or not, and most institutions will never let them forget it.
</p>
