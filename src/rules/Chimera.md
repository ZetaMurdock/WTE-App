<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>Chimera did not evolve naturally -- they are the result of forced Mirga evolution through external pressures. Their culture is deeply divided. One faction pursues scholarly mastery of their modular biology -- treating each segment as a discipline, each transformation as a study. Another faction surrenders to rage-driven transformation, some eventually corrupted by or drawn toward the Nigraldi. The line between the two factions is sometimes one bad day. Every Chimera knows that Colossal configuration feels better than it should -- that the rage-state is easier to enter than leave.
</p>
