<p>SubDermin occupy a deliberately ambiguous place in WTE taxonomy. Some sources classify them under Humanity; others under Omenity. This taxonomic tension is a designed feature of their species identity — they are genuinely at the threshold between the two groupings, and that ambiguity reflects something real about their culture and biology.
</p><p>Their dual Eminence signature (+10 Feral, +10 Civilized) is unique in the species roster. Equal institutional trust and institutional suspicion — a species that surface civilization has partially accepted and partially never stopped watching. Their most extreme engineered creation, the Construct, represents one of the most catastrophically powerful beings in the setting. Interitus Enmortem weapons are the sole known counter.
</p>
</div>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="HYBRID_INTERACTION">HYBRID INTERACTION</span></h2>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #201808;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:16px;color:#b0a070;font-size:0.9em;">
<p>SubDermin Dom 30 / Rec 20 — a moderate spread with a slight dominance edge. SubDermin geological and radioactive traits carry forward in hybrids with moderate reliability. Forsaken Touch and Radioactive Anatomy are among the most persistent in mixed lineages, as they derive from fundamental biology rather than trained ability.
