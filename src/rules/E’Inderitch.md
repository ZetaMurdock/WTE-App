<h2><span id="e.E2.80.99Inderitch"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="e’Inderitch"><i>e’Inderitch</i></span></h2>
<p>From the loneliest parts of the Enigmatica cosmology, the e’Inderitch, the Exiled, exist. Said to be older than the Velesti and possibly the Oracle themselves, the e’Inderitch hail from the Outer Realm. They slept for eons, untouched, undisturbed, and never awakened. As one of the original species that lived during the early days of the Oracle, when the multiverse was young, the e’Inderitch have witnessed the birth and evolution of countless worlds.The e’Inderitch are formidable beings, towering between 94 to 237 feet in height. Their bodies, resembling those of malnourished people, belie their true strength and resilience. Despite their gaunt appearance, they are among the most biologically efficient species in existence. Their skin is dark, adding to their aura of mystery and tranquility.
</p><p>Known for their peaceful nature, the e’Inderitch remain an enigma, their motives and desires hidden from the other races.In their ancient past, the e’Inderitch devoted themselves to a mysterious deity named Xaeil’Untais, a god from an alternate dimension known as Keno. This allegiance has defined much of their existence, influencing their actions and culture.
</p><p>Currently, the e’Inderitch reside either in the Outer Realm or within Keno, maintaining a low profile.However, within the shifting currents of Enigmatica, change is on the horizon. The e’Inderitch, long dormant, are poised to rise again. Their reawakening could herald significant transformations within the multiverse, as their ancient wisdom and unparalleled biology come to the forefront once more. The future of Enigmatica is bound to be profoundly impacted by the resurgence of these enigmatic giants, whose true potential and purpose remain to be fully understood.
</p>
<h2><span id="Skill.E2.80.99s_and_attributes"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Skill’s_and_attributes">Skill’s and attributes</span></h2>
<p><b>Enhanced Regeneration:</b> The e’Inderitch possess the ability to regenerate at an enhanced rate. All damage types inflict 10% less damage to them. Additionally, roll a d20; the result (DC) is subtracted from all damage types except for chaos and spirit.
</p><p><b>Perfect Reflexes:</b> The e’Inderitch have perfect reflexes, allowing them to make true opportunity attacks against any creature whose action priority is less than theirs.
</p><p><b>True Eye:</b> After many years of solitude, the e’Inderitch have gained the ability to see the unseen. By rolling Wisdom with a DC of 10 or higher, they can access information about anything they look at.
</p><p><b>Eldritch Body:</b> The e’Inderitch have bodies honed by thousands of years of deep space travel and void exposure. With blood of magma and skin of tungsten, they are immune to all negative status effects. Their only vulnerabilities are to spirit and chaos-based abilities.
</p>
