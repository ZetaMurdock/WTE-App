<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p>The <b>Nulin Corporation</b> claims to exist for the advancement of mankind and its subsidiaries.
</p><p>Mankind no longer exists.
</p><p>So Nulin seeks to <b>recreate it</b>.
</p><p>Through Fyber integration, synthetic reconstruction, and experimental hybridization, the corporation attempts to rebuild <b>Humanity</b> — not as it was, but as something controllable, repeatable, and optimized. Their greatest obsession is not the body, but the <b>mind</b>: reviving great thinkers, leaders, and innovators long lost to time.
</p><p>To fuel this work, people disappear.
</p><p>Those labeled <b>Droids</b> — the term used by higher echelons for the lower population — vanish from records, districts, and memory. Families are silenced. Questions are buried. No explanations are given.
</p><p>You come from one of those families.
</p>
