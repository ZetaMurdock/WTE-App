<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"> <div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:8px;padding:24px 36px;margin-bottom:16px;"><div style="font-family:monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:3px;color:#4e7fa5;margin-bottom:4px;"><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Wonderland_of_The_Enigma_Wiki">HOME</a> / <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Character_Creation">CHARACTER CREATION</a> / WHAT IS A PARADIGM</div><div style="font-size:26px;font-weight:700;color:#dedede;letter-spacing:1.5px;">What is a Paradigm?</div><div style="color:#565656;font-size:12px;margin-top:6px;font-style:italic;">Philosophy of power · Institutional authority · Combat methodology · Character identity</div><div style="background:#181818;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:4px;padding:10px 14px;font-size:12px;color:#909090;">
<p>A <b>Paradigm</b> represents a character's governing philosophy of power, science, and combat specialization. Each Paradigm defines not only a user's capabilities in the field but also the <b>authority</b>, <b>technology access</b>, and <b>combat methodology</b> they command. Together, Paradigms serve as a social and mechanical hierarchy that organizes how individuals interact with the world's systems — from labs and genetics facilities to battlefields and psychic networks.
</p>
