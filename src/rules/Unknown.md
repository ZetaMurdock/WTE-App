<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p>You emerge from <b>the Fog</b>—a thick, mysterious mist that erases history, memory, and identity.
</p><p>No records exist of your past. No history remains of your origin. You awaken <b>as if walking into a forest blind</b>, unsure of where you come from or why you are here.
</p><p>By default, the fog deposits you somewhere on <b>Aulorn</b>, though you may choose another planet or environment with your Curator’s approval.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Your_Role">Your Role</span></h2>
<p>The Unknown background is <b>completely customizable</b>:
</p>
<ul><li>You define your species, past hints, and potential abilities.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Your story begins <b>at the moment of emergence from the Fog</b>.</li></ul>
<ul><li>You are free to create a <b>mysterious narrative</b>, with secrets revealed over time.</li></ul>
<p>The fog represents your <b>blank slate</b>, a narrative device giving you and your Curator the freedom to shape your origin story dynamically.
</p>
