<p>UTILITY EQUIPMENT
</p>
</div>
<div style="color:#d4a76b;font-size:0.85em;margin-top:4px;font-style:italic;">
<p>Mission support devices, field tools, emergency equipment and tactical gadgets designed to expand an operator's versatility.
</p>
</div>
</div>
<div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #101828;border-left:3px solid #d18b2f;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;color:#e3c090;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>Utility equipment supplements an operator's primary arsenal through tactical support, mobility enhancement, battlefield recovery and environmental control. While these devices rarely determine victory alone, experienced Operators often rely on utility systems to create opportunities, escape impossible situations or keep their squad operational. Some advanced equipment supports <b>Enhanced Device Extensions (EDE)</b>, granting temporary Overclock functionality.
</p>
</div>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="UTILITY_NOTES">UTILITY NOTES</span></h2>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #101828;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;">
<div style="background:#1a1a1a;border-left:3px solid #d18b2f;padding:10px 14px;color:#e3c090;font-size:0.88em;">
<p><b>General Equipment:</b> Utility items provide tactical flexibility rather than raw offensive or defensive power. Operators may freely mix Utility equipment with weapons, armor and cybernetics.
</p><p><br><br>
</p><p><b>EDE Devices:</b> Equipment marked with <b>EDE</b> can temporarily exceed its normal operating limits through Overclock functionality.
</p><p><br><br>
</p><p><b>Consumables:</b> Consumables are depleted through use and must be replenished or replaced according to campaign rules.
