<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Doll_-_The_Mastermind_Puppeteer">Doll - The Mastermind Puppeteer</span></h2>
<p>Kazuo Kazeki was experimenting with his theory creation capabilities when he came across an anomaly, the place around him began to shift. Shifting into an eerie space where a house laid in the center, a place similar to the utopia and clarity he sought for, but not the fundamentals. This place wasn't in the confines of the real world, something never found being a place of mystery. Kazuo felt a need to further study into the unknown, seeking answers of his own volition. The place showed to be on the outside of <b>time,</b> a place that wasn't supposed to exist at all, a place seemingly taking the appearance of what was supposed to be his lab. Kazuo climbed up the stairs to the entrance of the manor, he reached out to the door handle to find out the secrets. T<b>he clock strikes 15.</b> His perception as he knows it shifts right in front of his eyes, his body feels anew but oddly strange. He finds himself back where he originally began. Dropping everything he was doing, he ran outside to look for details, trying to comprehend. Textbooks, Replication, and Information, nothing worked at retrieving what he experienced in that small moment. Being damned with every ounce of his being, resorting to tactics he never would've ensued normally.
</p><p>"It appears you're different than these other fools I've met, you're strange... it appears we've met somewhere before. Either way, you seek what is above your simple comprehension. Logic isn't the answer to all, this will help you understand."
</p><p>At a moment's glance, thoughts and information began to flood into Kazuo's mind and memory. This wasn't just things granted by the strange man but information unlocked by a blockage relieved, as early as the storm approached, it became clear. He was once where he started, in front of the manor.
</p><p>Dolls, Dolls, and Dolls. All that he could create, everything he further made. He turned into a shadow of what he was, but this quest for knowledge of the unknown brought him close to what he sought. The truth to it all, the clarity that was given was a push in the direction, a direction of darkness. He jot down all of his learned in his book, one that he would leave behind. Later time after his lab was in ruin destroyed by time, this book would be found and studied by others, others who learned what he studied, but the ideology to breathe life into and manipulate Dolls. Kazuo however, was nowhere to be seen. 
</p>

<h2><span id="Summary_.26_Playstyle"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Summary_&amp;_Playstyle"><b>Summary &amp; Playstyle</b></span></h2>
<p>The Doll <b>Archetype</b> is an <b>advanced control class</b>, best suited for players who enjoy:
</p>
<ul><li><b>✅ Tactical Play:</b> Manipulating enemies with puppeteering abilities, traps, and control-based techniques to keep foes under their influence.</li>
<li><b>✅ Resource Control:</b> Siphoning life force, abilities, or actions from manipulated enemies, weakening them while empowering the Doll.</li>
<li><b>✅ Long-Term Strategy:</b> The Doll excels at setting up intricate control over time, patiently building pressure and influencing the battlefield.</li></ul>
<p><b>However, it is not ideal for players who prefer:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>❌ Fast-paced, high-damage combat:</b> The Doll is more focused on control and manipulation, rather than quick bursts of damage.</li>
<li><b>❌ Free Movement &amp; Mobility:</b> Rather than mobility, the Doll’s strength lies in anchoring enemies in place and influencing a specific area through strategic control.</li></ul>
<p>This archetype is perfect for players who enjoy controlling the flow of battle through meticulous preparation, gradual shifts in power, and executing long-term plans.
</p>
