<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Disciple">Archetype: <i>Disciple</i></span></h1>
<p><i>“Through Fists, I will beat Compassion into my enemies.”</i>
</p><p>The <b>Disciple</b> is a master of close-range combat, embodying discipline, restraint, and overwhelming precision. Where others wield weapons or spells, the Disciple turns the body itself into doctrine—every strike a lesson, every movement a decision.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  The Disciple is a <b>frontline combatant</b> specializing in <b>fast-paced, close-quarters damage</b>. Through extreme bodily control, internal energy manipulation, and refined technique, the Disciple delivers devastating blows with speed and intent. Their strength lies not in excess, but in <b>perfect execution</b>—using only what is necessary, exactly when it is needed.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  The Disciple flows effortlessly between <b>burst aggression and controlled stillness</b>. They can accelerate into overwhelming flurries of attacks or slow the battlefield to a measured cadence, adapting their fighting style instantly based on enemy behavior, terrain, and momentum.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  The Disciple struggles against <b>indirect manipulation</b>, particularly effects that alter terrain, space, or non-energetic systems beyond bodily influence. Environments that deny footing, distort reality externally, or sever access to internal energy can disrupt their otherwise flawless control.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  <b>Manifested Combat Ward</b> — a personal manifestation of disciplined force that augments the Disciple’s body. This Ward may take the form of physical melee implements, spectral limbs, or an autonomous manifestation that fights alongside the Disciple, all rooted in hand-to-hand combat doctrine.</li></ul>
<p><i>[Playstyle:</i> hyper-mobile frontline striker with adaptive pacing, chaining actions through mastery of body and intent.]_
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetry:_Disciple_Action_Economy">Asymmetry: <i>Disciple Action Economy</i></span></h1>
<p>Upon choosing <b>Disciple</b>, your Actor gains an Asymmetry focused on <b>continuous pressure, reactive dominance, and bonus-action chaining</b>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy">Action Economy</span></h2>
<ul><li><b>2 Standard Actions</b>  Used primarily for attacks, stance changes, or high-commitment techniques.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>1 True Opportunity Attack</b>  Triggered when an enemy enters, exits, or disrupts the Disciple’s personal combat space.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>5 Bonus Actions</b>  Used for follow-up strikes, repositioning, stance shifts, Focus effects, or Ward interactions.</li></ul>
<p><i>Disciple combat revolves around converting precision into momentum—successful actions frequently generate additional actions, allowing experienced Disciples to overwhelm opponents through flawless execution.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Doctrine_of_the_Living_Fist">Aspect: <i>Doctrine of the Living Fist</i></span></h1>
<p>All <b>Disciple</b> actors begin with the <b>Doctrine of the Living Fist</b>, representing the philosophy that the body itself is both weapon and scripture.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function">Base Function</span></h2>
<p>Through this Doctrine, the Disciple manifests a <b>Combat Ward</b>, chosen upon activation:
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Manifestation_Options">Manifestation Options</span></h3>
<ul><li><b>Physical Armament:</b>  Gauntlets, knuckles, wraps, or arm-mounted constructs worn on the body.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Spectral Extension:</b>  Manifested limbs (arms, legs, fingers, or full-body echoes) that strike independently.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Imbued Form:</b>  The Ward is absorbed into the Disciple’s fists or limbs, causing attacks to apply <b>secondary sure-hit effects</b>.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Ward_Interaction_Rules">Ward Interaction Rules</span></h3>
<ul><li>Manifested limbs or entities gain <b>their own turn and one Bonus Action</b>.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Imbued Wards grant a <b>Bonus Action</b> when a successful hit lands.</li></ul>
<ul><li>The Ward always mirrors the Disciple’s intent and cannot act independently against their will.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b> Conventional physical damage</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b> Energy disruption, internal flow suppression, anti-manifestation effects</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b> Dismissed, internal energy is severed, or the Disciple is incapacitated</li></ul>

<p>Unlocked after achieving <b>6 Asymmetries</b>.
</p><p>Each subtype represents a <b>Teaching</b>—a philosophical path governing how the Disciple understands motion, energy, and intent.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Teaching_of_Samsara"><span class="new" title="Teaching of Samsara (page does not exist)" data-uncrawlable-url="L3dpa2kvVGVhY2hpbmdfb2ZfU2Ftc2FyYT9hY3Rpb249ZWRpdCZyZWRsaW5rPTE=">Teaching of Samsara</span></span></h2>
<p><i>“All motion returns.”</i>
</p><p>The Teaching of Samsara focuses on <b>cycles, rebirth, and relentless momentum</b>, using bodily rhythm—heartbeat, blood flow, breath—as weapons.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Subtype_Features">Subtype Features</span></h3>
<ul><li><b>Samsara – Reoccurring Manifestations</b>  The Disciple amplifies attacks through their heartbeat. While in <b>Focus</b>, damage is increased by <b>10 + Reputation</b>, scaling every 7 levels. Successful attacks generate Bonus Actions, allowing devastating chained strikes.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Instant Reflection</b>  While in Focus, the Disciple can react to movement within 35 feet. If their Action Priority is higher, reactions are reflected instantly back at the attacker.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Piercing Retributions</b>  A continuous awareness field surrounds the Disciple. Stored bodily energy multiplies damage and constricts opponents’ muscles, potentially immobilizing them and generating additional actions.</li></ul>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Teaching_of_Monk"><span class="new" title="Teaching of Monk (page does not exist)" data-uncrawlable-url="L3dpa2kvVGVhY2hpbmdfb2ZfTW9uaz9hY3Rpb249ZWRpdCZyZWRsaW5rPTE=">Teaching of Monk</span></span></h2>
<p><i>“Stillness is accumulation.”</i>
</p><p>This path emphasizes <b>passivity, restraint, and inevitability</b>, turning inaction into overwhelming force.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Subtype_Features_2">Subtype Features</span></h3>
<ul><li><b>Harmonic Fists</b>  Each attack siphons energy from enemies, draining Action Points and destabilizing their balance. The Monk gains Free Actions as equilibrium collapses.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Passiveness</b>  Choosing to do nothing during Free Actions generates <b>Zen</b>, a stacking stat that mitigates and multiplies damage until a threshold forces vulnerable downtime.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Force of Nature</b>  Physical damage is applied twice. High-damage strikes unlock True Opportunity Attacks, turning momentum into inevitability.</li></ul>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Teaching_of_Mu-Mind"><span class="new" title="Teaching of Mu-Mind (page does not exist)" data-uncrawlable-url="L3dpa2kvVGVhY2hpbmdfb2ZfTXUtTWluZD9hY3Rpb249ZWRpdCZyZWRsaW5rPTE=">Teaching of Mu-Mind</span></span></h2>
<p><i>“Strike without self.”</i>
</p><p>Mu-Mind Disciples erase ego entirely, weaponizing emptiness and psychological collapse.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Subtype_Features_3">Subtype Features</span></h3>
<ul><li><b>Mu Fists</b>  Attacks carry no intent, inflicting disadvantage on enemies’ rolls. Targeted strikes disrupt blood flow and apply true damage.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Guided Silence (Mu-Will)</b>  Movement produces no sound or presence. Through meditation, the Disciple creates a silence zone that freezes perception and cognition within a vast radius.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Indiscriminate Mind</b>  By sacrificing an Action, the Disciple briefly phases between Active and Passive time, dodging attacks or countering through them.</li></ul>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Teaching_of_Nirvana"><span class="new" title="Teaching of Nirvana (page does not exist)" data-uncrawlable-url="L3dpa2kvVGVhY2hpbmdfb2ZfTmlydmFuYT9hY3Rpb249ZWRpdCZyZWRsaW5rPTE=">Teaching of Nirvana</span></span></h2>
<p><i>“Peace beyond reaction.”</i>
</p><p>Nirvana represents instinctual transcendence—acting before thought itself.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Subtype_Features_4">Subtype Features</span></h3>
<ul><li><b>Instinctual Power</b>  Before being attacked, the Disciple may evade regardless of Priority and gain advantage on all rolls for an extended duration.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Zero Movement</b>  Movement allows shifting between Active and Passive time, rendering the Disciple untouchable during transit and enabling surprise strikes.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Silent Fists / State of Peace</b>  By halting all bodily functions, the Disciple enters deathlike stillness before instantly rebooting into catastrophic retaliation.</li></ul>
