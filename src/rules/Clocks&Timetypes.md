<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Downtime_System">Downtime System</span></h2>
<p><b>Downtime-</b>in W.T.E serves as the game's equivalent to short rests, allowing players to recover and strategize. Here's a detailed breakdown of how the downtime system works:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Duration</b>: 10 minutes in-game.</li>
<li><b>Recovery</b>:Players recover a specific amount of health, mana, or other resources. This can be a percentage of their total or a fixed amount based on their level or abilities.</li>
<li><b>Actions</b>:During downtime, players can perform minor actions such as using healing items, reloading weapons, preparing spells, or discussing strategies.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Interruption</b>:Downtime can be interrupted by enemy actions or environmental hazards, depending on the Director's discretion.</li></ul>
<ol><li class="mw-empty-elt"></li></ol>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Clocks_System">Clocks System</span></h2>
<p>The <b>clocks</b> system tracks time in a way that influences both combat and world events. It divides time into <b>active</b> and <b>passive</b> periods, each affecting the game differently.
</p>
<ol><li class="mw-empty-elt"></li></ol>
<h4><span id="Active_Time_.28Combat.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Active_Time_(Combat)">Active Time (Combat)</span></h4>
<h4><span id="Active_time_represents_moments_of_heightened_activity.2C_typically_during_combat_or_other_intense_scenarios."></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Active_time_represents_moments_of_heightened_activity,_typically_during_combat_or_other_intense_scenarios.">Active time represents moments of heightened activity, typically during combat or other intense scenarios.</span></h4>
<ul><li><b>Tracking</b>:The Director uses a clock or timer to track the passage of time in rounds or seconds.</li>
<li><b>Effects</b>: Each round of combat or intense activity advances the clock by a specific amount, influencing the world around the players.</li>
<li><b>Dynamic Events</b>:As the clock progresses, predetermined events can trigger, such as environmental changes, enemy reinforcements, or critical mission objectives becoming available or threatened.</li></ul>
<ol><li class="mw-empty-elt"></li></ol>
<h4><span id="Passive_Time_.28World_Changes_and_Decisions.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive_Time_(World_Changes_and_Decisions)">Passive Time (World Changes and Decisions)</span></h4>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Passive_time_accounts_for_the_general_passage_of_time_outside_of_combat_and_intense_scenarios.">Passive time accounts for the general passage of time outside of combat and intense scenarios.</span></h4>
<ul><li><b>Tracking</b>:The Director moves the clock forward based on player actions and decisions. For example, traveling between locations, performing rituals, or engaging in extended dialogue.</li>
<li><b>World Events</b>:As passive time progresses, the world evolves. NPCs might move, political events could unfold, or weather patterns might change.</li>
<li><b>Decision Impact</b>: Certain player actions can significantly advance the passive time clock, leading to major world changes.</li></ul>
<ol><li class="mw-empty-elt"></li></ol>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Integrating_the_Systems">Integrating the Systems</span></h2>
<p>Combining downtime and clocks creates a dynamic and responsive world in W.T.E.
</p><p><b>Action Timing</b>:
</p><p>Every player action advances the clock. Simple actions might advance it by a minute, while complex ones could take longer. The Director tracks this and adjusts the world accordingly.
2. **<b>Event Triggers</b>:** The Director has a list of events tied to specific times on the clock. When the clock reaches these times, the corresponding events occur. This keeps the game world active and responsive.
3. **<b>Tactical Decisions</b>:** Players must consider the passage of time when planning their actions. They might need to balance the benefits of downtime against the risks of advancing the clock and triggering new events.
4. **<b>Narrative Flow</b>:** The clocks system ensures that the narrative flows naturally, with time-sensitive events unfolding in a way that feels organic and immersive.
</p>
