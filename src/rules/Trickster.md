<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Trickster">Archetype: <i>Trickster</i></span></h1>
<p><i>“The Cards will tell the truth; even in your death, you still remain strong.”</i>
</p><p>The <b>Trickster</b> manipulates cards imbued with effects to create chaos and confusion. A fast and tactical archetype, the Trickster thrives on momentum, rapid movement, and battlefield disruption. Their playstyle focuses on generating chaos through card effects, dealing bursts of damage that accumulate over time, and utilizing stealth or high-speed assaults for maximum impact.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  <i>Move rapidly while dealing damage and teleporting, using cards to facilitate attacks and control.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  <i>High-speed, fluid combat interspersed with moments of tactical thinking.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  <i>Vulnerable to strong, crushing force and sustained attacks that stop momentum.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  <i>Ability to inscribe symbols and gestures onto objects, creating dynamic effects. Requires a Joker, King, and Queen symbol, each with unique effects; the Joker always triggers chaotic outcomes.</i></li></ul>
<p><i>Tricksters excel in chaos-driven encounters, large battles, and stealth missions. Primary playstyle revolves around high-speed, disruptive combat and tactical positioning.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetry:_Trickster_Action_Economy">Asymmetry: <i>Trickster Action Economy</i></span></h1>
<p><i>Upon choosing <b>Trickster</b>, your Actor gains its <b>Asymmetry</b>, which defines <b>Action Economy</b>, <b>mechanical philosophy</b>, and the visual/manipulative style of card and dagger usage.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy">Action Economy</span></h2>
<ul><li><b><i>1</i> Standard Action(s)</b>  <i>Used to play cards, inscribe effects, or manipulate the battlefield.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>5</i> True Opportunity Attack(s)</b>  <i>Triggered when interacting with inscribed cards, objects, or exploiting battlefield openings.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>0</i> Free Action(s)</b>  <i>Used to reposition, activate minor effects, or adjust inscriptions.</i></li></ul>
<p><i>The Trickster’s actions focus on movement and chaos, minimizing openings for enemies while maximizing effect application and burst damage.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Card_and_Dagger">Aspect: <i>Card and Dagger</i></span></h1>
<p><i>All Trickster actors begin with the <b>Card and Dagger</b>, representing the archetype’s speed and precision. The dagger is manifested at the start, while cards are created by inscribing symbols onto objects using the dagger.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function"><i>Base Function</i></span></h2>
<p><i>You manifest a dagger, visible or hidden, which facilitates card creation. Attacks with the dagger require a DC check equal to your level plus your Cunning Modifier, rolling 1d4 for minor damage/effects.</i>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b> <i>All conventional means.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b> <i>Health and condition of the Trickster.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b> <i>Incapacitation of the Trickster.</i></li></ul>

<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Circus_of_Joy"><span class="new" title="Circus of Joy (page does not exist)" data-uncrawlable-url="L3dpa2kvQ2lyY3VzX29mX0pveT9hY3Rpb249ZWRpdCZyZWRsaW5rPTE=">Circus of Joy</span></span></h1>
<p><i>The Trickster from the Circus of Joy uses the dagger to spread amusement, inscribing toys and objects to produce whimsical illusions and chaotic effects. Deals persistent Vertice Damage.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Subtype_Features">Subtype Features</span></h2>
<ul><li><b>Feature 1:</b> <i>Dagger can be comically enlarged to colossal size; cannot be wielded in this state and is immune to localized effects.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Feature 2:</b> <i>Any object you hold can be enlarged to colossal size.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Feature 3:</b> <i>You can animate inscribed objects, causing them to act according to your instructions.</i></li></ul>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Circus_of_Withering"><span class="new" title="Circus of Withering (page does not exist)" data-uncrawlable-url="L3dpa2kvQ2lyY3VzX29mX1dpdGhlcmluZz9hY3Rpb249ZWRpdCZyZWRsaW5rPTE=">Circus of Withering</span></span></h1>
<p><i>The Trickster from the Circus of Withering uses the dagger to drain vitality, withering creatures and inscribed objects. Withered tokens act as cards that can be sacrificed for power or effects.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Subtype_Features_2">Subtype Features</span></h2>
<ul><li><b>Feature 1:</b> <i>“Death’s Wishful Eye”: carve into a carcass and place two tokens on its eyes, reanimating it with Hit Dice equal to your level rounded down.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Feature 2:</b> <i>All creatures used to create tokens exist in limbo; their souls can act during a True Opportunity Action at double movement speed, using your actions to attack once for weapon damage.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Feature 3:</b> <i>Withered tokens can be consumed to amplify card effects or grant temporary boosts to the Trickster.</i></li></ul>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Circus_of_Mellows"><span class="new" title="Circus of Mellows (page does not exist)" data-uncrawlable-url="L3dpa2kvQ2lyY3VzX29mX01lbGxvd3M/YWN0aW9uPWVkaXQmcmVkbGluaz0x">Circus of Mellows</span></span></h1>
<p><i>The Trickster from the Circus of Mellows uses the dagger to induce mass sadness and debilitation, slowing and weakening targets.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Subtype_Features_3">Subtype Features</span></h2>
<ul><li><b>Feature 1:</b> <i>Cards cause conditions like blindness, slowness, nausea, fear, or hunger for one turn.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Feature 2:</b> <i>You can target a creature under these effects and summon your dagger from them if they fail a Wisdom DC check against yours.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Feature 3:</b> <i>Effects can spread to objects or areas, amplifying debilitation.</i></li></ul>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Circus_of_Silence"><span class="new" title="Circus of Silence (page does not exist)" data-uncrawlable-url="L3dpa2kvQ2lyY3VzX29mX1NpbGVuY2U/YWN0aW9uPWVkaXQmcmVkbGluaz0x">Circus of Silence</span></span></h1>
<p><i>The Trickster from the Circus of Silence uses the dagger silently, gaining advantage in stealth and soundless movement. Inscribed objects and creatures can be silenced.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Subtype_Features_4">Subtype Features</span></h2>
<ul><li><b>Feature 1:</b> <i>Manipulate all inscribed objects psychically, as long as they make no sound.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Feature 2:</b> <i>Step on an inscribed object to move at double speed; creatures can see the object but fail to perceive you if they fail an Action Priority DC against yours.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Feature 3:</b> <i>Inscribed objects and creatures can remain silent indefinitely, allowing stealth and ambush tactics.</i></li></ul>
