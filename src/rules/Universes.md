<p>Overview
</p>
</div><div style="color:#b8b8b8;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;">
<p>Reality is not singular.
</p><p>Existence is composed of countless Universes connected through a larger Multiversal framework. Every Universe possesses its own history, civilizations, and conflicts, yet all remain organized according to an identical cosmological structure.
</p><p>While worlds may differ, the orientation of reality never changes. Every Universe is divided into the same sixteen directional Sectors, each influenced by competing Dominions whose control shifts according to local history.
</p><p>Above every Universe exists a hierarchy of organizations responsible for preserving order, interpreting law, and ultimately carrying out the will of the highest authority known only as <b>Indrah</b>.
</p>
</div></div> <div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:10px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#bcbcbc;font-weight:bold;border-bottom:1px solid #262626;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">

</p>
</div><div style="line-height:1.8;color:#b8b8b8;">
<p>Five Spirals exist throughout the Multiverse.
</p><p>Each Spiral consists of:
</p>
<ul><li>6 Chapters</li>
<li>3 Companies per Chapter</li>
<li>Each Company governs a Dominion</li></ul>
<p>Although the Spirals enforce Indrah's will, every transmission of authority introduces slight distortion. Consequently, each Spiral represents an increasingly imperfect interpretation of the original decree.
</p>

<p>Azimuth
</p>
</div><div style="line-height:1.8;color:#b8b8b8;">
<p>At the exact center of every Sector lies <b>Azimuth</b>, the Center Universe.
</p><p>Azimuth serves as:
</p>
<ul><li>the convergence point of all Sectors,</li>
<li>the primary seat of Indrah's authority,</li>
<li>the birthplace of most Inquisitors,</li>
<li>the political center of the known Multiverse.</li></ul>
<p>Nearly every major Multiversal institution traces its origin back to Azimuth.
</p>
</div></div> <div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:10px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#bcbcbc;font-weight:bold;border-bottom:1px solid #262626;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>Hex Worlds
</p>
</div><div style="line-height:1.8;color:#b8b8b8;">
<p>Hex Worlds are planets reconstructed by autonomous droids through planetary-scale engineering.
</p><p>Entire continents are excavated and reshaped into immense hexagonal plates that tessellate across the world's surface while extending thousands of kilometers into the atmosphere.
</p><p>Viewed from orbit, a Hex World no longer resembles a natural planet, but a deliberate artificial construct.
</p>
</div></div> <div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:18px;"><div style="font-size:10px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#bcbcbc;font-weight:bold;border-bottom:1px solid #262626;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>Hex Helixes
</p>
</div><div style="line-height:1.8;color:#b8b8b8;">
<p>As a Hex World ages, its geometric order begins to fail.
</p><p>The hexagonal plates lose cohesion before stretching into colossal spirals and circular arcs. Eventually the planet completely abandons its original orbital anchor, transforming into a free-moving celestial structure known as a <b>Hex Helix</b>.
</p><p>A completed Hex Helix drifts freely through space, no longer behaving as a conventional planetary body.
</p>
