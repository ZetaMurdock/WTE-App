# Mogul Influence & the Geofenced Margin

As Inquisitors you carry **ECP-certified Voyager Credentials**, which let you
traverse spatial filaments and jump between sectors. They also bind you to a
coordinate margin, and you are expected to know exactly where you are inside it.

---

### The three bands

| Zone | Territory | Character | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core Worlds** | Mogul Core Administration | Absolute Order | Safe area |
| **Marginal Zone** | Mogul-Influenced Dominions | High-Pressure Border | Warning zone |
| **Sovereign / Void** | Unmonitored / Lawless Space | ECP Connection Lost | **Extreme hazard** |

```
[ CORE WORLDS ]         [ MARGINAL ZONE ]        [ SOVEREIGN / VOID ]
◄─── SAFE AREA ───────► ◄─── WARNING ZONE ───►   ◄─── EXTREME HAZARD ───►
```

Influence is **not** the same axis as a sector's
[alignment code](wte://rules/The_16_Sectors) — On Sequence, Detached, Rogue,
Excursioned. Alignment is a sector's political posture; influence is your
distance from ECP coverage at a specific coordinate. A Rogue sector can still sit
inside the geofence, and a Core-aligned world can have a hole in its coverage.

---

### The Geofenced Margin

So long as you remain in territory logged as **Mogul-Influenced**, the ECP network
maintains your life-support channels, stabilises your ciphers, and processes your
authority.

Every sector has a boundary threshold. Venture deep enough into any cardinal
sector and you cross the **Geofenced Horizon**.

---

### Beyond the line

Outside Mogul territory the Regency has no jurisdiction. Four things follow:

*   **Operator blackout** — the connection to your Operator's neural Bio-Tank
    flickers and fails, leaving you completely blind in the dark.
*   **Cipher destabilisation** — Paradigm Ciphers no longer receive systemic power
    stabilisation, risking severe backlashes.
*   **Foreign dominion** — the cold, unfeeling superpowers of the outer rims, such
    as the Nigraldi Swarms or the entropic Tribulas Celestials, hold absolute
    dominion over this space.
*   **High treason** — Voyager paths must stay aligned to registered
    Mogul-Influenced space-lanes. Deviating without a signed, notarised execution
    warrant from your Coordinator is classified as an immediate act of high
    treason.

These are **Curator prompts, not automatic penalties.** The directive says the
Regency has no jurisdiction out there; it does not say roll a d20. What a blackout
or a backlash costs is the Curator's call, scene by scene.

---

### At the table

The Curator announces arrivals from the VTT's **Dialogue** desk (scene menu →
Dialogue → Beacon), which formats the readout consistently:

> Inquisitors — new location reached · Planet: Ashfall · Sector: Boren · Mogul Influence: High

Setting the band to Sovereign / Void unlocks an **Announce crossing** button,
which states plainly that jurisdiction is gone, and lists the four consequences
for the Curator to draw on.

**Operational directive:** keep Voyager navigation paths strictly inside
registered Mogul-Influenced space-lanes.
