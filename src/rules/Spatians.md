<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>The only known species to consistently evade or circumvent the Nigraldi. Spatians consider themselves the true successor to Hyomen heritage -- not a variant, but the purest expression of what Hyomen were always capable of becoming. Their culture is intensely supremacist, not out of malice but out of genuine biological conviction: they survived the void, they breathe vacuum, they move through space without tools or suits. What does that make baseline Hyomen? Nomadic and solitary in organization, Spatians rarely establish permanent settlements. They drift in small clusters, occasionally descending on inhabited systems for resources or information before vanishing back into the void.
</p>
