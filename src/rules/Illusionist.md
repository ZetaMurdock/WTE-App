<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Illusionist">The Illusionist</span></h2>
<p>Fergoat Varnum was born and raised in the northern sector, spending most of his life under government custody due to his <b>uncanny ability to create illusions using his eyes</b>—a gift inherited from his father, a <b>Codexian Mechanician</b>. The realism of his illusions was so profound that the government deemed him a threat, seizing him and <b>draining him of his power</b>—or so they thought. Once released back into the world, Fergoat found reality to be nothing more than a <i>false image</i>. Stripped of his gift, he turned to <b>Codexian Mathematics</b>, particularly a forbidden branch known as <b>Illusive Uncertain Math</b>—a field seen as <b>Null and Void</b> by traditional scholars. Through relentless study and practice, he <b>reconstructed his lost sense of self</b>, mastering illusion through sheer logic and calculation. Yet, despite regaining control, he could still feel the void left behind—the absence of his true power.
</p><p>Years passed, and resentment toward the government festered within him. He longed for the return of his <b>real</b> abilities, hoping and praying for someone—something—to <b>restore what had been stolen</b>.
</p><p>One night, as he knelt in silent desperation, his prayers were answered.
</p><p>A presence emerged, an entity beyond comprehension, introducing itself as <b>The Director</b>. It loomed over him, questioning if <i>this</i>—power—was truly all he desired in life. Without hesitation, he answered <b>yes</b>.
</p><p>And so, the Director obliged.
</p><p>Before Fergoat could express gratitude, the entity <b>seized him by the neck</b>, slamming him against the wall. It reached toward his face, <b>tearing one of his eyes from its socket</b>. In its place, the Director embedded a <b>dark amulet</b>, fusing it seamlessly into his flesh. As the amulet reconstructed his eye, the Director merely sighed.
</p><p><i>"Useless. All of you lower beings are useless."</i>
</p><p>And then, it vanished.
</p><p>Fergoat awoke from what felt like a dream—his <b>eye intact</b>, yet fundamentally changed. His illusions were no longer bound by Codexian Math. They had <b>evolved</b>—becoming 100 times more potent, more vivid, more <i>real</i> than ever before. He no longer calculated illusions; he simply <b>manifested them into existence</b>. When the government discovered his newfound abilities, they <b>declared him an Archetype</b> and launched a relentless manhunt. But Fergoat was no longer the same powerless prisoner they once controlled. Over the years, he gathered apprentices, refined his craft, and spread his influence across the world.
</p><p>Now, he is known as <b>The Illusive One—Fergoat of the House of Varnum</b>.
</p>
