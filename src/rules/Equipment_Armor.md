<p>Armor serves as the primary defensive equipment within W.T.E., protecting operators from kinetic impacts, environmental hazards, and advanced Genus-based threats. While heavier armor dramatically increases survivability, it often reduces mobility or dexterity. Certain advanced armor systems feature <b>Enhanced Device Extensions (EDE)</b>, allowing temporary Overclock functions or specialized battlefield adaptations.
</p>
