# Cognition

> "The world is an interface — and with my awareness, I control its code."

The Cognition Paradigm explores the mind as both tool and weapon — the bridge between perception and power. Its Inquisitors perceive patterns invisible to others, reading emotion, intent, and behavior as if they were lines of code. To them, knowledge is control, and control begins with insight. Understanding through connection: every emotion and memory carries data that can be interpreted and redirected.

## Paradigm Identity

**Who Joins** — Analysts, interrogators, diplomats, telepaths, and empathic savants drawn to the intersection of human psychology and information systems. Those who believe every truth can be extracted if you know how to ask.

**Culture** — Cognitives value wit, restraint, and charisma. They often see conversation as combat and empathy as infiltration. Their greatest flaw: the line between empathy and manipulation often blurs until even they no longer know whether they care or calculate. "Know the mind, own the world."

**Archetype Feel** — Bard / Warlock. The Cognition Inquisitor is a reader of rooms — fluent in the language of people, systems, and secrets simultaneously.

## Roll Axis Profile

Cognition occupies 5 of the 7 universal Roll Paths. Its native mechanics emphasize bodily survival, cognitive operation, active perception, resistance to deception, and the projection or preservation of intent. Cognition Ciphers do not natively specialize in Power or Density, though Cognition Inquisitors may still make those rolls normally whenever the situation calls for them.

| Roll Path | Native | Direction | Cognition Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power | — | Check | Not native to Cognition mechanics; direct physical force remains universally available. |
| Density | — | Check | Not native to Cognition mechanics; rapid physical execution remains universally available. |
| Evasion | ● | Save | Avoid sudden physical threats, reposition under pressure, and survive when psychic or informational conflicts produce material consequences. |
| Recovery | ● | Save | Endure neural strain, psychic backlash, bodily stress, and physical destabilization caused by invasive cognitive effects. |
| Capacity | ● | Check | Process complex mental workloads, sustain psychic links, coordinate information, and operate multiple cognitive effects at once. |
| Perception | ● | Check / Save | Read intent, detect deception, identify hidden emotional or informational patterns, and resist false memories, illusions, or sensory manipulation. |
| Influence | ● | Check / Save | Persuade, intimidate, command, manipulate, project emotion, redirect behavior, or preserve personal intent against outside control. |

**PROFILE STANDARD** — Cognition's Roll Profile describes the Paths its native Paradigm mechanics are built around. It does not prevent a Cognition Inquisitor from making Power or Density rolls during ordinary play, and it does not overwrite the natural Roll Path of weapons, Genera, copied abilities, environmental effects, or other external systems.

## Cognition Paradigm Affinity

Cognition does not favor every statistic contained inside its Roll Profile. Its Affinities represent the capabilities its doctrine most deliberately develops: reading intent, identifying hidden information, projecting social or psychic pressure, and controlling the direction of thought and behavior.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Favored Attributes | Charisma · Intelligence |
| Favored Specialties | Cunning · Perception |

Charisma reinforces projection and preservation of intent. Intelligence reinforces recognition, interpretation, and discrimination of information. Cunning reinforces manipulation, misdirection, and social pressure. Perception reinforces the reading of behavior, patterns, contradictions, and concealed information.

| Designation Rank | Favored Attribute | Favored Specialty |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | +1d5 | +1d10 |
| 3–5 | +2d5 | +2d10 |
| 6–8 | +3d5 | +3d10 |
| 9 | +4d5 | +4d10 |

**SIGNATURE CONVERGENCE · INFLUENCE** — Charisma + Cunning creates Cognition's primary social and psychic Convergence. When both legitimately govern an Influence Check or Influence Save, Cognition receives both the Favored Attribute and Favored Specialty Affinity pools. This represents deliberate control of tone, intent, emotional pressure, deception, interrogation, command, social manipulation, and resistance to outside attempts to seize behavioral control.

**SIGNATURE CONVERGENCE · PERCEPTION** — Intelligence + Perception creates Cognition's primary investigative Convergence. When both legitimately govern a Perception Check or Perception Save, Cognition receives both Affinity pools. This represents reading emotional tells, identifying deception, noticing contradictions, extracting meaning from fragmented information, recognizing psychic interference, and determining what another mind is attempting to conceal.

**WHAT COGNITION DOES NOT FAVOR** — Cognition has native access to Evasion, Recovery, and Capacity, but it does not automatically gain Affinity on Dexterity, Balance, Endurance, Adaptation, Wisdom, or Mental Fortitude. These Paths remain part of Cognition's mechanical vocabulary without being institutional areas of exceptional training.

## Authority Profile

Cognition Inquisitors command access to classified information networks, psy-link systems, and intelligence archives. Their natural empathy and refined perception grant unique privileges when interacting with higher authority figures — making them ideal mediators, interrogators, or internal auditors. They can extract or decode sensitive data directly from individuals far beyond their official clearance level.

**Advancement** — Cognitive advancement depends on accuracy, subtlety, and reliability in information exchange. Each member aligns with a Dominion — an intelligence collective that trades in knowledge and secrets. The more efficiently an Inquisitor delivers requested analyses or psychological profiles, the faster their Authority Rank rises.

## Authority Ranks · Designation Progression

| Rank | Title | Role | Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seeker | New recruit exploring the boundaries of perception | Basic archive access; begins Dominion affiliation |
| 2 | Auditor | Field operative using emotional and informational feedback | Can request classified reports and conduct independent extractions |
| 3 | Empath | Coordinator overseeing intelligence teams or psychic task forces | Can override data locks and analyze high-security transmissions |
| 4 | Overviewer | Senior interrogator or network influencer choosing a Cognitive Path | Oversight authority to inspect, correct, or dismantle corrupted systems |

### Rank 4 Specialization Fields

**Forensic Auditor** — Internal investigations, truth analysis, and deception breakdown. Most forensic work resolves through Perception, especially when identifying false data, concealed intent, or contradictory behavior.

**Internal Auditor** — Oversees thought-networks and psy-link integrity. Commonly engages Influence to resist intrusive control and Capacity when maintaining or correcting complex psychic networks.

## Combat Specialty

Predominantly support or tactical. Cognition Inquisitors manipulate morale, focus, and perception. They exploit opponents' weaknesses before combat begins, altering the flow of engagement through Influence, Perception, and predictive cognitive control rather than direct Power or Density specialization.

**Style** — Keen, quick, and precise. They anticipate rather than react, reading patterns and emotional tells to determine the most efficient move — or word — to end a conflict.

## Specialties · Genera Domains

**Eldritch Energy** — Channels and weaponizes psychic or emotional energy, allowing invasive mental projection or "mindfire."

**Null Energy** — Creates zones of calm — voids that suppress psychic or emotional influence, including fear, panic, or rage.

These domains define their mental polarity: total immersion in thought or the complete absence of it.

## Interactions & Philosophy

Where others act, the Cognition Paradigm understands. They teach that control without comprehension is chaos — that strategy and reason triumph where brute force fails. Yet their greatest strength is also their flaw: the line between empathy and manipulation often blurs.

**Allies** — Science (data processing), Simulation (neural mapping), Evolution (neural integration research)

**Tensions** — Remnant and Warfare (differing views on morality, truth, and control of information)
