# Crystilian

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Crystilian">Crystilian</span></h2>
<p><i>* Crystilians, the Diamond Beings, are a remarkable race that originated from the dying geodes on the planet Alteria. The unique chemical composition of Alteria's atmosphere fostered the evolution of life in an extraordinary manner, resulting in the formation of the Crystilian race. These beings possess the ability to manipulate their bodies, transforming them into diamond-like objects.</i>
</p>
<h2><span id="Skill.E2.80.99s_and_attributes"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Skill’s_and_attributes"><i>Skill’s and attributes</i></span></h2>
<p><i>* Diamond Form: Crystilians can reshape their bodies, assuming a crystalline form reminiscent of diamonds. In this state, their bodies become incredibly resilient and durable, capable of withstanding immense pressure and even deflecting powerful attacks. The hardness and sharpness of their diamond-like skin make them formidable in combat.</i>
</p><p><i>* Crystal Manipulation: Crystilians possess the innate ability to manipulate and control crystalline structures. They can shape and mold crystals at will, creating intricate objects, constructs, or even weapons. This power allows them to adapt to various situations, whether for offensive purposes or practical uses like crafting.</i>
</p><p><i>* Enhanced Strength: The crystalline nature of Crystilians grants them enhanced physical strength. Their diamond-like bodies provide a solid foundation for powerful strikes and physical feats beyond that of ordinary beings. They can shatter obstacles, deliver devastating blows, and exhibit impressive feats of strength.</i>
</p><p><i>* Energy Absorption: Crystilians have the unique ability to absorb and harness energy from their surroundings. They can draw upon ambient energy sources, such as light, heat, or even geothermal energy, to replenish their own reserves or utilize it for offensive or defensive purposes.</i>
</p>
