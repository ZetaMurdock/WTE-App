<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>Construct SubDermin began as necessity and became philosophy. Deep-dwelling SubDermin communities with access to geological ore deposits and salvaged technology discovered that biological limits could be addressed not by genetic modification but by direct engineering. The first Constructs were emergency cases -- workers who lost limbs to geological hazards and rebuilt with what was available. Subsequent generations internalized the methodology: identify limitation, engineer solution, install. Modern Constructs are architects of themselves, treating their bodies as ongoing projects rather than fixed biological facts.
</p>
