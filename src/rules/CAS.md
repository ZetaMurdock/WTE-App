<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Contextual_Authorization_System">Contextual Authorization System</span></h1>
<p>In <b>Wonderland of the Enigma</b>, Inquisitors always possess their full suite of abilities. Nothing is ever "unlocked" through traditional progression. However, the world only <b>permits</b> certain expressions of those abilities within specific contexts, determined by your <b>Designation Rank</b> and the <b>Area Classification</b> of your current environment.
</p><p>Power exists innately within you.
</p><p><b>Authorization</b> is granted—or denied—by the machinery that surrounds you.​
</p>
<hr>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="5._The_Philosophical_Core">5. The Philosophical Core</span></h2>
<p><b>Higher Designation:</b>
</p>
<ul><li>Reduces Deviation severity across all zones</li>
<li>Converts catastrophic backlash into manageable consequences</li>
<li>Allows you to "bend" zone restrictions rather than shatter them</li>
<li>Makes unauthorized actions less likely to trigger audits</li></ul>
<p><b>Lower Designation:</b>
</p>
<ul><li>Can theoretically do <b>anything</b></li>
<li>Every unauthorized action risks <b>unraveling</b></li>
<li>Constant tension between capability and consequence</li>
<li>Perfect for paranoid, desperate play</li></ul>
<p><b>Eminence interaction:</b>
</p>
<ul><li>High Eminence softens backlash (you're a "valued asset")</li>
<li>Low Eminence amplifies it (you're a "known liability")</li></ul>

<h2><span id="6._Party_Dynamics_.26_Trust"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="6._Party_Dynamics_&amp;_Trust">6. Party Dynamics &amp; Trust</span></h2>
<p>Inquisitors with mismatched Designations experience the <b>same world differently</b>:
</p><p>This creates natural tension:
