# Photonic Genus

> "Everything emits light. We just decide where and how it moves next."

The Photonic Genus is the domain of pure radiance — acceleration, projections, light, and disruptions. Where other domains impose simple effects on the world, Photonic works with what the world is already doing, amplified it further, and further amplifying the radiance that exists deep within everyone. Photonic practitioners are the fastest in WTE: the most movement options, the most escape tools, and the greatest potential for momentum and light based devastation.

*Redirected from Kinetic Genus.*

## Photonic Abilities · 20 Total

All Photonic abilities are available to any Inquisitor with Photonic domain access. Abilities are not gated by rank — they are gated by SS pool capacity.

### Photonic Swing

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Classification | Enhancement / Emission |
| SS Cost | 4 SS |
| Activation | Active (15 sec) |
| Range / Area | Self |
| Target | Self — applies to next melee/physical attack |

Applies a brief Photonic enhancement to the next melee swing — concentrating prismed photons at the moment of impact. The next melee attack deals +1 damage die of the weapon's type, ignores 2 points of physical damage reduction, along with +2 to the attack's damage roll. If the attack already has advantage: also add +1d10 radiance damage. If the attack also triggers a knock-back effect, distance is doubled.

**LIMIT:** Unlimited; once per action

### Luminance Overload

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| SS Cost | 8 SS |
| Activation | Active (1:30 minutes) |
| Range / Area | Cone within 10 feet (Or touch) |

| Classification | Enhancement / Trans-modification |
| SS Cost | 4 SS |

### Reverse Reaction

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Classification | Trans-modification / Enhancement |
| SS Cost | 5 SS |
| Activation | Reaction (Instant) or while moving |
| Range / Area | Self or single target within 20 ft |
| Target | Creature (self or other) |

Reverses the momentum of the target — a creature moving in one direction is instantly moved at equal speed in the opposite direction. On self: escape a rush/charge situation instantly or revert to their pre-movement position as if they never moved (Photonic ghost recall). On others: the Inquisitor makes a Physical Check — Power contested by the target's Physical Save — Recovery; on success, the target's current movement direction is forcibly reversed for that action. Variant (On others): Swaps the velocity and direction vectors of two moving objects or creatures. If used on an unwilling target/animate, the target makes a Physical Save — Evasion (DV 13) or has the friction of their movement removed for 2 rounds.

**LIMIT:** Twice per encounter

| Classification | Trans-modification |

| Activation | Active (Instant) |
