<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Beast">The Beast</span></h2>
<p><b>Jack Reverase</b> was a well-known and highly regarded combat specialist, admired for both his unmatched skill in battle and his deep love for animals—particularly lions, tigers, and bears. He was fascinated by their raw power and primal instincts. Jack spent much of his time bonding with these creatures, play-fighting with them despite countless injuries. Over time, his connection with the animals grew deeper, forging a bond that went beyond mere companionship. He began to see the world not just through human eyes, but through the perspective of a predator, a beast.
</p><p>The more he immersed himself in the wild instincts of his animal companions, the more they trusted him. During one of his deployments, Jack took the bold and controversial step of bringing his three animals into the field. This decision unsettled many of his fellow soldiers and even the commanding officers, but Jack had unwavering faith in his beasts. When they hit the battlefield, the results were undeniable. Jack and his animal companions tore through enemy lines with devastating efficiency, their unity becoming stronger with every drop of blood spilled.
</p><p>By the time they had reached their 100,000th kill together, something extraordinary happened. Jack began to hear voices in his head—the voices of his beasts. At that moment, a mark appeared on his skin, representing each of the creatures he had bonded with. Alongside this mark, Jack’s own body began to change, exhibiting physical traits and instincts akin to the beasts he had tamed. When Jack returned from his mission, many feared him, but his heroic deeds could not be ignored. He had saved countless lives, including an entire platoon with just a single surviving soldier. For his valor, Jack was hailed as a hero, awarded a prestigious medal, and given the title <b>"Beast Captain of the 52nd Platoon."</b> As he rose through the ranks, eventually becoming a Fleet Commander, Jack ensured his platoon stayed by his side, encouraging each member to bond with their own beasts. His influence turned the 52nd Platoon into a legendary unit, renowned for their unity with their animal companions. However, one day, Jack encountered an enemy with the power to control animals, a foe who sought to sever the connection Jack had with his beasts. The unthinkable happened: his bond was broken, and he was forced to kill his beloved creatures to protect himself and his comrades. As their blood soaked into the ground and his body, Jack was overcome with grief. Yet, in his darkest moment, he heard their voices once more.
</p><p>A radiant beam of light descended from the heavens, and the animals he had lost were revived, their forms now more powerful than ever. Jack himself underwent a transformation, becoming the <b>First Beast</b>—the ultimate apex predator. His strength was unmatched, and he effortlessly annihilated his enemy with minimal effort. Following this event, Jack shared his newfound powers with his platoon, spreading the <b>Beast Archetype</b> throughout the army. He trained countless soldiers, turning them into warriors capable of forging bonds with creatures and harnessing the primal strength of predators. Jack went on to become a General, leading an army of beast-bound warriors who embodied his teachings.
</p><p>To this day, <b>General Jack Reverase</b>, the First Beast, continues to command respect and awe, his legend living on as both a hero and a symbol of the unbreakable bond between man and beast.
</p>
