# Lineage

<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Lineage_System">Lineage System</span></h1>
<p><b>Lineage</b> represents the genetic and ancestral roots of your character within the world of *Wonderland of the Enigma (W.T.E.)*. After selecting your <b>Template Species</b>, you will construct your character’s lineage by creating two primary <b>Progenitors</b> — the ancestral sources of your character’s genetic traits. Optionally, you may also include <b>Grand Progenitors</b> if you wish to expand your character’s genetic detail.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Overview">Overview</span></h2>
<p>Your Lineage defines which species traits, abilities, and physical aspects have been passed down to your character. Each Progenitor contributes unique genetic features based on their template species and individual background.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Creating_a_Genetic_Profile">Creating a Genetic Profile</span></h2>
<p>Each Progenitor requires a completed <b>Genetic Profile</b>. This profile captures crucial biological and historical details that shape your character’s heritage.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Step_1:_Determine_Sex">Step 1: Determine Sex</span></h3>
<p>Begin by selecting whether the Progenitor is **male** or **female**.  Sex plays a direct role in the genetic dominance and recessiveness calculations later in the creation process.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Step_2:_Dominance_and_Recessiveness">Step 2: Dominance and Recessiveness</span></h3>
<p>Once sex is determined, apply the following modifiers:
</p>
<ul><li>**Male Progenitor (same species as female):** +10 to <b>Dominance</b></li>
<li>**Female Progenitor (same species as male):** −10 to <b>Recessiveness</b></li></ul>
<p>If the Progenitors belong to different species, standard trait weight values are used without these modifiers.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Step_3:_Select_Physical_Traits">Step 3: Select Physical Traits</span></h3>
<p>Each species features a selection of physical traits it can manifest. During Lineage creation, you may select traits from these available options.
</p><p>Every trait includes a <b>Dominance Weight</b>:
</p>
<ul><li>**Light:** 10 points</li>
<li>**Medium:** 20 points</li>
<li>**Heavy:** 30 points</li></ul>
<p>Traits determine how strongly certain features appear in the resulting character. Players can apply creative flavor to these traits — some may manifest fully, while others remain recessive or partial, depending on the inheritance results.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Step_4:_Assign_Background_and_Origin">Step 4: Assign Background and Origin</span></h3>
<p>After establishing traits, give each Progenitor both a **Background** and a **Planet of Origin**.  These details add cultural, environmental, and historical texture to your lineage, shaping how and why certain traits evolved or persisted.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Finalizing_the_Lineage">Finalizing the Lineage</span></h2>
<p>Once both Progenitors are complete, review their Genetic Profiles and ensure all selections align with your character’s Template Species.  Your completed Lineage forms the genetic foundation of your character — blending visual, biological, and cultural aspects from both ancestral lines.
</p>
<hr><p><i>Example:</i>  A player selects the Template Species *Aetherian.* They design two Progenitors: one male (dominant) and one female (recessive). After assigning traits, backgrounds, and planets, the resulting character inherits a mix of light and medium-weight features from both sides, forming a unique and balanced genetic profile.</p>
