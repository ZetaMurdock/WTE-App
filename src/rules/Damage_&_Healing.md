# Damage & Healing

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><hr>


<h1><span id="Damage_.26_Healing_System"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Damage_&amp;_Healing_System">Damage &amp; Healing System</span></h1>
<p>Damage and healing in <i>Wonderland of the Enigma</i> are <b>contextual, layered, and consequence-driven</b>.
</p><p>HP is not a single “health bar,” and damage is not a single roll.
</p><p>Every hit matters <b>because of where it lands, how it lands, and what follows after</b>.
</p><p>This system is designed to feel <b>cinematic, dangerous, and earned</b>, without devolving into instant lethality.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span id="Hit_Points_.28HP.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Hit_Points_(HP)">Hit Points (HP)</span></h2>
<p>Hit Points represent the <b>total physical, mental, and spiritual strain</b> an Inquisitor can withstand before collapse, incapacitation, or death.
</p><p>HP in W.T.E interacts directly with:
</p>
<ul><li>Wounds</li>
<li>Fatigue</li>
<li>Defensive Hit Points</li>
<li>Specialties</li>
<li>Environmental context</li>
<li>Curator adjudication</li></ul>
<p>HP is not just “meat.” It is <b>durability under pressure</b>.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span id="Defensive_Hit_Points_.28DHP.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Defensive_Hit_Points_(DHP)">Defensive Hit Points (DHP)</span></h2>
<p>Defensive HP represents <b>buffer durability</b>, not true bodily damage.
</p><p>DHP may come from:
</p>
<ul><li>Shields</li>
<li>Armor</li>
<li>Force barriers</li>
<li>Magical reinforcement</li>
<li>Temporary effects</li></ul>
<p>Rules:
</p>
<ul><li>Damage is applied to DHP <b>first</b></li>
<li>DHP regenerates faster than true HP</li>
<li>Losing DHP does <b>not</b> usually cause Wounds</li>
<li>Overflow damage carries into HP</li></ul>
<p>DHP exists to allow <b>cinematic exchanges</b> without immediate mutilation.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="HP_States">HP States</span></h2>
<p>Your remaining HP determines your <b>combat state</b>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Peak_Condition">Peak Condition</span></h3>
<p>High HP and bodily stability may grant:
</p>
<ul><li>Increased impact force</li>
<li>Temporary bonuses</li>
<li>Faster movement</li>
<li>Cleaner Quicktime responses</li></ul>
<p>This is momentum in mechanical form.
</p>
<hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Critical_Condition">Critical Condition</span></h3>
<p>Low HP causes cascading penalties:
</p>
<ul><li>Impaired movement</li>
<li>Reduced attack power</li>
<li>Specialty checks become harder</li>
<li>Slower Quicktime reactions</li>
<li>Increased Wound severity</li></ul>
<p>Exact effects are always <b>contextual</b> and ruled by the Curator.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Layered_Damage">Layered Damage</span></h2>
<p>Damage in W.T.E <b>does not occur all at once</b>.
</p><p>Each damaging event may trigger <b>multiple layers</b> based on what actually happens.
</p>
<hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Example:_Layered_Damage_Resolution">Example: Layered Damage Resolution</span></h3>
<p>Jim is struck in the chest by a powerful punch.
</p>
<ol><li><b>Primary Damage</b>
<ul><li>Force damage (1d10)</li>
<li>Strength modifiers apply</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Environmental Follow-Up</b>
<ul><li>Jim is launched into a brick wall</li>
<li>Crushing damage (1d40)</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Specialty Resolution</b>
<ul><li>Breath Control check</li>
<li>Success → reduced suffocation</li>
<li>Failure → Suffocating damage (2d15)</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Additional Consequences</b>
<ul><li>Winded</li>
<li>Bleed</li>
<li>Stagger</li>
<li>Potential Wound</li></ul></li></ol><p>
Damage is always:</p><blockquote><p><b>Impact + Context + Specialty Outcome</b></p></blockquote>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Damage_Rolls">Damage Rolls</span></h2>
<p>Damage dice represent <b>potential harm</b>, not guaranteed injury.
</p><p>Damage may be:
</p>
<ul><li>Reduced</li>
<li>Converted</li>
<li>Redirected</li>
<li>Layered</li>
<li>Escalated</li></ul>
<p>Based on:
</p>
<ul><li>Armor</li>
<li>Conditions</li>
<li>Positioning</li>
<li>Momentum</li>
<li>Specialty results</li></ul>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Critical_Hits">Critical Hits</span></h2>
<p>A Critical Hit occurs when <b>all</b> of the following apply:
</p>
<ol><li>You roll the <b>maximum</b> on the damage die  <b>OR</b></li>
<li>You roll the <b>even number closest to the maximum</b></li>
<li>Your <b>Attack Power exceeds</b> the target’s defensive value</li></ol>
<p>Critical Hits may:
</p>
<ul><li>Increase damage dice</li>
<li>Add secondary damage types</li>
<li>Trigger a Wound</li>
<li>Cause instant stagger</li>
<li>Grant Action Priority advantage</li>
<li>Enable Quicktime follow-ups</li>
<li>Allow extra Specialty rolls</li></ul>
<p>Criticals are <b>situational amplifiers</b>, not flat multipliers.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span id="Saving_Throws_.26_Damage_Interaction"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Saving_Throws_&amp;_Damage_Interaction">Saving Throws &amp; Damage Interaction</span></h2>
<p>Saving Throws are rolled when:
</p>
<ul><li>External effects target the Inquisitor</li>
<li>Damage types impose conditions</li>
<li>Environmental hazards apply</li>
<li>Body or mind is stressed</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Saves">Core Saves</span></h3>
<ul><li>Strength → physical overpowering</li>
<li>Endurance → fatigue, poison, radiation</li>
<li>Dexterity → dodging, avoidance</li>
<li>Wisdom → illusion, panic</li>
<li>Charisma → fear, domination</li>
<li>Action Priority → real-time evasion</li></ul>
<hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Specialty_Saves">Specialty Saves</span></h3>
<p>Specialties are <b>damage translators</b>, not just resistances:
</p>
<ul><li>Breath Control → suffocation, crushing, winded</li>
<li>Balance → instability, knockdowns</li>
<li>Adaptation → disease, toxins, corruption</li>
<li>Control → internal disruption, magic-like effects</li>
<li>Precision → narrow avoidance</li></ul>
<p>Damage and saves are <b>intertwined</b>, not separate steps.
</p>

<h3><span id="Vertice_Damage_.28Special_Rule.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Vertice_Damage_(Special_Rule)">Vertice Damage (Special Rule)</span></h3>
<p>Vertice damage targets <b>structural failure</b>, not surface harm.
</p><p>Roll 1d100:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Below 50</b>
<ul><li>Crushing + Suffocating</li></ul></li>
<li><b>50 or Above</b>
<ul><li>True Bleed + Piercing</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>Vertice damage <b>always risks Wounds</b> and requires Curator interpretation.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span id="Resistance.2C_Vulnerability_.26_Immunity"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Resistance,_Vulnerability_&amp;_Immunity">Resistance, Vulnerability &amp; Immunity</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Resistance">Resistance</span></h3>
<ul><li>Take <b>half damage</b></li>
<li>Comes from traits, abilities, adaptation, or context</li></ul>
<hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Vulnerability">Vulnerability</span></h3>
<ul><li>Take <b>double damage</b></li>
<li>Often caused by:
<ul><li>Exposed organs</li>
<li>Failed specialties</li>
<li>Existing Wounds</li></ul></li></ul>
<hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Immunity">Immunity</span></h3>
<ul><li>Take <b>no damage</b></li>
<li>Extremely rare</li>
<li>Usually supernatural, archetypal, or long-term adaptation</li></ul>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Healing">Healing</span></h2>
<p>Healing is <b>layered</b>, just like damage.
</p><p>Healing may restore:
</p>
<ul><li>Defensive HP</li>
<li>True HP</li>
<li>Specialty stability</li>
<li>Action Priority consistency</li>
<li>Fatigue</li>
<li>Wound severity</li></ul>
<hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Healing_Types">Healing Types</span></h3>
<ul><li><b>Immediate Healing</b>  Restores Defensive HP first</li>
<li><b>Recovery Healing</b>  Restores true HP</li>
<li><b>Specialty-Based Healing</b>  Breath Control, Balance, Control, Adaptation</li>
<li><b>Environmental Healing</b>  Clean air, rest, holy zones, sunlight</li>
<li><b>Curator-Given Healing</b>  Contextual regeneration or narrative recovery</li></ul>
<p>Healing is <b>earned</b>, not automatic.
</p>
