<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_SoulBinder">Archetype: <i>SoulBinder</i></span></h1>
<p><i>The SoulBinder — One with the Essence of Change</i>
</p><p><i>The SoulBinder is an archetype defined by uniqueness in its approach to power. Rather than growing through conventional means, the SoulBinder advances by extracting, inheriting, and refining aspects of the souls of other creatures. This path grants immense flexibility and depth, but demands patience, restraint, and careful cultivation.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  <i>The SoulBinder is the slowest-growing archetype. Its power must be accumulated and refined over time, requiring deliberate progression before its full potential can be realized.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  <i>The SoulBinder has no fixed combat rhythm. It begins slow and deliberate but compounds in strength as soul aspects are gathered. Over time, it can accelerate dramatically depending on its collected aspects.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  <i>The SoulBinder is highly vulnerable to Soul damage and, in rare cases, Force damage. Severe disruption can cause the loss or destabilization of stored soul aspects within the SoulVial.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  <i>The SoulBinder is gifted with the <b>SoulVial</b>, an interdimensional relic capable of absorbing aspects of the souls of fallen creatures. These aspects empower the bearer in unique and evolving ways.</i></li></ul>
<p><i>The SoulBinder’s playstyle is deeply personal and varies drastically between users. It is a highly dynamic archetype, capable of fulfilling nearly any role, but all power paths are centralized through the SoulVial.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetry:_SoulBinder_Action_Economy">Asymmetry: <i>SoulBinder Action Economy</i></span></h1>
<p><i>Upon choosing <b>SoulBinder</b>, your Actor gains its <b>Asymmetry</b>, defining the <b>Action Economy</b>, <b>mechanical philosophy</b>, and soul-centric manipulation.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy">Action Economy</span></h2>
<ul><li><b><i>2</i> Standard Action(s)</b>  <i>Used for direct actions, soul invocations, or SoulVial interactions.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>X</i> True Opportunity Attack(s)</b>  <i>Granted or modified by acquired Soul Aspects.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>X</i> Free Action(s)</b>  <i>Generated through passive soul effects, bindings, or completed soul conditions.</i></li></ul>
<p><i>The SoulBinder’s Action Economy is not fixed and scales entirely based on the Soul Aspects housed within the SoulVial.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_SoulVial">Aspect: <i>SoulVial</i></span></h1>
<p><i>All <b>SoulBinder</b> Actors begin with the <b>SoulVial</b>, representing dominion over lingering essence and post-mortem potential.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function"><i>Base Function</i></span></h2>
<p><i>The SoulVial is an interdimensional object existing within a pocket dimension known as the <b>Limbus Intersect</b>. All SoulVials are connected through this space.</i>
</p><p><i>When a creature is killed by the SoulBinder, its soul is drawn into the Limbus Intersect, where it wanders for one day before dissipating. Before dissipation, the soul presents the bearer with a set of choices, offering fragments, traits, or conditions tied to its essence.</i>
</p><p><i>The SoulVial is semi-sentient, levitates autonomously, possesses its own priority, and may activate abilities independently or through the bearer.</i>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b>  <i>All forms of physical damage or external manipulation, unless the interference occurs directly within the soul dimension.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b>  <i>Internal effects and conflicts that occur within the Limbus Intersect.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b>  <i>Death</i></li></ul>
