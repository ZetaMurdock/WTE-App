# Enigmatica

<h1><span id="Enigmatica_.28Background.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Enigmatica_(Background)">Enigmatica (Background)</span></h1>
<p>Not everyone is marked by collapse, experimentation, or shared dreams.
</p><p>Some people simply <b>come from somewhere</b>.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p>The <b>Enigmatica</b> background represents characters with a clear, defined origin: a <b>specific planet</b> with its own history, cultures, conflicts, and way of life.
</p><p>Each planet is a complete world — shaped by its environment, technology, beliefs, and past events. Some are stable. Others are fractured, isolated, or on the brink of change.
</p><p>Unlike other backgrounds, Enigmatica does not assume trauma, taint, or alteration. Your story begins with <b>place</b>, not catastrophe.
</p><p>You are not defined by what happened to you — but by <b>where you come from</b>.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Planet_of_Origin">Planet of Origin</span></h2>
<p>When choosing this background, select a <b>planet of origin</b>.
</p><p>Each planet provides:
</p>
<ul><li>A brief history</li></ul>
<ul><li>Cultural descriptors</li></ul>
<ul><li>Environmental traits</li></ul>
<ul><li>Common beliefs or conflicts</li></ul>
<p>Using these elements, you <b>fully create your backstory</b>. Your character’s upbringing, values, and worldview should reflect the world they come from.
</p><p>If a planet does not yet exist, you may <b>work with the Director to create one</b>.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="1._Backstory">1. Backstory</span></h2>
<p>Using your chosen planet’s lore, describe:
</p>
<ul><li>Your life on that world</li></ul>
<ul><li>What caused you to leave</li></ul>
<ul><li>What you carry with you from home</li></ul>
<p>Then explain how you met the party and why you now travel with them.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="2._Allies">2. Allies</span></h2>
<p>List <b>1–3 Allies</b>, such as:
</p>
<ul><li>Family or mentors from your homeworld</li></ul>
<ul><li>Travelers or off-world contacts</li></ul>
<ul><li>Representatives of planetary factions</li></ul>
<p>Allies often remain tied to your planet of origin.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="3._Enemies">3. Enemies</span></h2>
<p>List <b>1–3 Enemies</b>, such as:
</p>
<ul><li>Rival factions</li></ul>
<ul><li>Personal grudges</li></ul>
<ul><li>Those connected to planetary conflicts</li></ul>
<p>Your enemies may never leave your world — or may follow you beyond it.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span id="4._Strengths_.26_Weaknesses"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="4._Strengths_&amp;_Weaknesses">4. Strengths &amp; Weaknesses</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Goals">Goals</span></h3>
<p>Choose <b>1–3 goals</b>, such as:
</p>
<ul><li>Returning home</li></ul>
<ul><li>Changing your planet’s future</li></ul>
<ul><li>Exploring beyond your world</li></ul>
<ul><li>Escaping your past</li></ul>
<hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Bonuses">Bonuses</span></h3>
<p>Choose one of the following:
</p><p><b>Standard Bonuses</b>
</p>
<ul><li>+2, +2, +1, +1</li></ul>
<p><b>Focused Bonuses</b>
</p>
<ul><li>Three bonuses total</li></ul>
<ul><li>One bonus may be <b>+4</b></li></ul>
<ul><li>Remaining bonuses must be <b>+2 or lower</b></li></ul>
<hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Weaknesses">Weaknesses</span></h3>
<p>Choose <b>at least one Weakness</b>, such as:
</p>
<ul><li>Cultural limitations</li></ul>
<ul><li>Naivety about the wider galaxy</li></ul>
<ul><li>Strong ties pulling you back home</li></ul>
<ul><li>Difficulty adapting to other worlds</li></ul>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Starting_Transportation">Starting Transportation</span></h2>
<p>Enigmatica is the <b>only background</b> that grants access to <b>temporary transportation</b>.
</p>
<ul><li>You begin play with a <b>limited means of travel</b> appropriate to your planet or campaign.</li></ul>
<ul><li>This transportation is <b>short-lived</b>, restricted, or unstable.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Its duration, reliability, and eventual loss are determined by the Director.</li></ul>
<p>This transportation exists to move the story forward — not to bypass it.
</p>
<hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Player_Tip">Player Tip</span></h3>
<p>Enigmatica is ideal if you want a <b>grounded starting point</b> with maximum creative freedom.
</p><p>Your story is not about what changed you.
</p><p>It’s about where you started.
</p>
