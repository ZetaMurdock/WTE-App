<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>The Annunaki claim to remember when the Shadow Plane was not a plane -- when it was the primary substrate of existence and normal space was the thin film overlaid atop it. This is either the deepest racial memory ever documented or the most elaborate species-wide mythology in the Enigma. Scholars lean toward the latter. The Annunaki themselves are uninterested in the debate. They have access to shadow-matter depths that make the distinction between philosophy and fact feel less urgent than it does to shorter-lived species. They move through modern Stygian society like geology moves through weather -- present, foundational, and largely unconcerned with the surface turbulence above them.
</p>
