<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Zealot">Archetype: <i>Zealot</i></span></h1>
<p><i>Before my eyes, all is seen.</i>
</p><p>The <b>Zealot</b> is an archetype of control and manipulation, but unlike conventional controllers, their authority does not come from force, magic, or preparation. It comes from <b>observation</b>. Through the Zeal—a veil bound over their eyes—the Zealot perceives the world beyond its surface sequence, peering into the Inbetween to witness events as frames rather than moments. What they see, they can isolate. What they isolate, they can alter.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  The Zealot functions as a <b>reactive manipulator</b>, positioned just behind frontliners rather than alongside casters or long-form preparatory archetypes. Their role is to stabilize live combat by correcting mistakes as they occur—interrupting fatal sequences, misaligning enemy actions, and repurposing hostile momentum. While capable of acting independently, Zealot excel when responding to others, adapting their pace to match the speed of the battlefield itself.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  The Zealot operates in <b>short, precise interventions</b>. They observe, isolate a sequence, and intervene at the exact point where the outcome matters most. Their turns are rarely explosive but frequently decisive, shifting encounters through rewinds, cuts, and reapplications of force. Over time, a Zealot begins to control not space or damage, but <b>the order in which things are allowed to happen</b>.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  Zealot are heavily dependent on <b>perception and continuity</b>. Anything that blinds, scrambles timelines, disrupts cognition, or denies observational certainty can severely limit their effectiveness. They struggle against effects that do not possess a discernible sequence, origin, or frame—pure randomness, conceptual voids, or entities acting outside linear progression.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  <b>Z-Sigil</b> — a sigil bound to the Zealot eyes, anchoring their perception to the Inbetween. Through it, the Zealot can extract, manipulate, and reapply observed sequences of events within a controlled temporal window.</li></ul>
<p><i><b>Playstyle:</b></i> <i>Surgical control, frame manipulation, reactive dominance.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetry:_Zealot_Action_Economy">Asymmetry: <i>Zealot Action Economy</i></span></h1>
<p>Upon choosing <b>Zealot</b>, your Actor gains its <b>Asymmetry</b>, reflecting a style built around reactions, interrupts, and layered micro-actions rather than direct offense.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy">Action Economy</span></h2>
<ul><li><b><i>1</i> Standard Action</b>  Used to initiate a major Zeal effect, lock a target sequence, or establish control over a timeline segment.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>4</i> True Opportunity Attacks</b>  Triggered when enemies act within your perception window, alter momentum, or attempt to complete an observed sequence you have marked.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>7</i> Bonus Actions</b>  Used for minor Zeal manipulations: marking frames, shifting impacts, copying force, or adjusting sequence alignment.</li></ul>
<p><i>Zealot actions are fast but conditional. Mastery comes from knowing when <b>not</b> to act until the correct frame presents itself.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Z-Sigil">Aspect: <i>Z-Sigil</i></span></h1>
<p>All <b>Zealot</b> actors begin with the <i><b>Z-Sigil</b></i>, a metaphysical sigil that exists partially in the Inbetween and partially upon the bearer.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function">Base Function</span></h2>
<p>The Z-Sigil manifests as a <b>fluorescent, warping eye-veil</b>, making it impossible for observers to fully discern where or how the Zealot is looking. When the Zealot focuses on a creature, object, or phenomenon, the Sigil reveals a <b>one-hour timeline</b> of that target’s recent actions.
</p><p>From this hour, the Zealot may isolate a <b>60-second interval</b> and apply one of the following operations:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Rewind</b>  The selected 60 seconds is rewound. The target must succeed on a Wisdom DC against the Zealot. On failure, their body dematerializes for 60 seconds and reappears altered according to the rewound state, creating a temporal blip in their sequence.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Cut</b>  The selected 60 seconds is removed entirely. If this cut would significantly affect the target’s continuity, they must succeed on a Wisdom DC. On failure, the target goes limp, disappears, and returns affected by the absence of that interval.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Copy</b>  The Zealot copies the <b>impact</b> of an action that occurred during the interval (force, momentum, effect), storing it as a transferable frame.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Paste</b>  The stored impact is applied to a new target or location. For supernatural effects, the Zealot must succeed on an Inspiration DC 16 to apply secondary phenomena such as levitation, displacement, or metaphysical force.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Translate</b>  Converts the nature of an observed effect (e.g., physical force into kinetic pressure, motion into displacement).</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Transpose</b>  Swaps the positional or directional outcome of an observed action.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Reconstruct</b>  Reassembles a fragmented sequence into a new, stable outcome within the Zealot control.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b>  Physical damage, conventional anti-magic, or illusion disruption.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b>  Perception denial, timeline erasure, void-based effects, and entities without linear continuity.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b>  The Zealot releases focus, the interval exceeds its temporal window, or the Director determines the sequence no longer exists.</li></ul>
