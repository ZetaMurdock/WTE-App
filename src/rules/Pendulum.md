<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Pendulum">The Pendulum</span></h2>
<p>The Pendulum was constructed by Adondis and has been used by countless mathematicians throughout history. However, its true power was only ever fully harnessed by one individual—someone who understood the Pendulum more deeply than anyone else. This person was Herales Boze.
</p><p>Herales not only mastered the Pendulum, but he also revolutionized Codexian Math, intertwining it with the Pendulum in ways never seen before. He created and mass-produced intricate formulas that even Sovereign King Adomas could not replicate. Through the Pendulum, Herales gained access to a power beyond even the strongest, tapping into the ancient magics that existed before the Great Reset. Herales Boze is, and will forever be, regarded as the greatest Codexian user of this era. There is no other who rivals his mastery over Codexian Mathematics. in which case he became the first to turn it into an archetype and eventually taught other how to do the same with other Pendulums.
</p>
