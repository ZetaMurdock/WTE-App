<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p>After the Oriyu lost their physical forms, they did not retreat from the galaxy.
</p><p>Instead, they shared their <b>technology</b>.
</p><p>Working alongside emerging civilizations, the Oriyu helped stabilize societies, resolve conflicts, and build systems meant to <b>facilitate peace</b> rather than dominance. For a time, it worked. Many sectors prospered under this exchange.
</p><p>But not all regions accepted cooperation.
</p><p>In the <b>Southern and Western Sectors</b>, the Oriyu were no longer treated as allies.
</p><p>They were treated as <b>resources</b>.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Nexus_Prisons">The Nexus Prisons</span></h2>
<p>Oriyu were captured and suspended inside <b>massive glass bulbs</b>, their incorporeal forms anchored by their <b>Nexus</b>. These Nexuses were siphoned continuously, used as living power sources to fuel cities, fleets, and weapons.
</p><p>The glass did not protect them.
</p><p>It contained them.
</p>
