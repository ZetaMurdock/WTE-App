<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Sitheldich">Archetype: <i>Sitheldich</i></span></h1>
<p><i>Reality is nothing but a dream</i>
</p><p><i>The Sitheldich is an archetype that weaponizes illusion, perception, and fractured reality. Rather than overwhelming foes through force, it erodes certainty—turning what is seen, felt, and remembered into unreliable tools that can be rewritten at will.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  <i>Battlefield control through illusion, perception distortion, and reality overlays that mislead enemies, redirect actions, and destabilize decision-making.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  <i>Fast and reactive. The Sitheldich acts frequently through Free Actions, layering illusions and adjustments in real time while enemies struggle to identify what is real.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  <i>Direct confrontation. The Sitheldich relies on misdirection and perception; enemies immune to illusion, with heightened clarity, or capable of overwhelming force can break through its control.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  <i>Yjorn’s Eye — a remote, omnipresent illusion conduit that allows the Sitheldich to project, manipulate, and overwrite perception across any distance.</i></li></ul>
<p><i>The Sitheldich excels at confusion, redirection, and control. It thrives when enemies hesitate, second-guess, or act on false information, turning certainty itself into a liability.</i>
</p>
<hr>

<ul><li><b><i>1</i> Standard Action(s)</b>  <i>Used to establish major illusions, reality overlays, or vision-based effects.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>1</i> True Opportunity Attack(s)</b>  <i>Triggered when an enemy acts based on false information or interacts with an illusion.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>4</i> Free Action(s)</b>  <i>Used to adjust, shift, layer, or redirect illusions already in play.</i></li></ul>
<p><i>The Sitheldich’s power comes from constant, subtle manipulation rather than singular decisive actions.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span id="Aspect:_Yjorn.E2.80.99s_Eye"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Yjorn’s_Eye">Aspect: <i>Yjorn’s Eye</i></span></h1>
<p><i>All <b>Sitheldich</b> actors begin with the <b>Yjorn’s Eye</b>, representing omnipresent perception and the collapse of objective reality.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function"><i>Base Function</i></span></h2>
<p><i>Yjorn’s Eye is an extradimensional object that the Sitheldich may summon to project illusions, manipulate perception, and fabricate illusionary objects.</i>
</p><p><i>The Eye may manifest in any location the user has seen or previously occupied. Illusions created through Yjorn’s Eye function regardless of distance and may affect creatures that view them directly or indirectly.</i>
</p><p><i>Illusions may be formed from objects the user is holding, observing, or perceiving through another conduit.</i>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b>  <i>Physical damage or conventional interaction</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b>  <i>Perception cleansing, anti-illusion effects, or forced clarity abilities</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b>  <i>Dismissed by the user or the Sitheldich is incapacitated</i></li></ul>
