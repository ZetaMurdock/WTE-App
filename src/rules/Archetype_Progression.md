<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Progressional_Archetyping">Progressional Archetyping</span></h2>
<p><i>Progressional Abilities</i> are long-term character growth mechanics based on action-driven evolution. Each Archetype and its Sublets develop through narrative and mechanical triggers called <b>Progressional Pillars</b>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Progressional_Pillars">Progressional Pillars</span></h3>
<p>Each Sublet is associated with 1 or more Progressional Pillars. These are core verbs or themes that define how the Archetype becomes stronger:
</p>
<ul><li>Create</li>
<li>Creation</li>
<li>Absorb</li>
<li>Siphon</li>
<li>Steal</li>
<li>Devour</li>
<li>Destroy</li>
<li>Kill</li>
<li>Eat</li>
<li>Grow</li>
<li>Warp</li>
<li>Transfusion</li></ul>
<p>Each Pillar is assigned:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Level:</b> Ranges from 1 to 5, increasing with repeated use.</li>
<li><b>Priority:</b> Indicates importance to the Sublet’s core identity.</li></ul>
<p><i>Note: Fulfillment of a Progressional Pillar is tracked by both narrative and mechanical evidence during gameplay.</i>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Director_Prompts">Director Prompts</span></h3>
<p>The GM (Director) monitors the player’s progression and provides narrative prompts when a Pillar levels up. This keeps growth immersive and story-bound.
</p><p>Example: <i>"You feel the chains in your chest tighten. You’ve siphoned too much this cycle. You are progressing in Siphon (Lv. 2)."</i>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Additives">Additives</span></h3>
<p><b>Additives</b> are special modifiers earned through progression. They can:
</p>
<ul><li>Alter ability outcomes</li>
<li>Change visual or conceptual themes</li>
<li>Introduce new effects or interactions</li></ul>
<p>Additives come in tiers:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Minor:</b> Slight change (e.g., color, elemental shift)</li>
<li><b>Major:</b> Mechanical change (e.g., fireball splits into three)</li>
<li><b>Core:</b> Redefines ability logic entirely (e.g., burn converts into ice explosion if used in a cold zone)</li></ul>
<p><i>Additives are chosen or earned through Director approval, Pillar mastery, or unique story milestones.</i>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Synergy_Effects">Synergy Effects</span></h3>
<p>If a player fulfills multiple Progressional Pillars at once, a <b>Synergy Effect</b> may trigger, evolving existing abilities or unlocking hybrid powers.
</p><p>Example: <i>Devour + Grow</i> may create an ability that absorbs biomass and rapidly mutates the body in combat.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Node_Permeation">Node Permeation</span></h3>
<p>Once a Pillar reaches Level 5, the player may gain a Permeated Node—a passive ability representing mastery of that Progressional theme.
</p><p>Example: <b>"Hollow Metabolism"</b> (Devour): You regain health equal to 10% of anything you consume.
</p>
