# Combat

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;padding:28px 32px;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;font-family:'Segoe UI',sans-serif;color:#dedede;max-width:960px;margin:0 auto;"><div style="font-family:monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:3px;color:#4e7fa5;margin-bottom:22px;">
<p><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Wonderland_of_The_Enigma_Wiki">WTE WIKI</a> · <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Mechanics">MECHANICS</a> · COMBAT
</p>
</div><div style="font-size:26px;font-weight:700;color:#cf4f4f;letter-spacing:1px;border-bottom:2px solid #a03535;padding-bottom:12px;margin-bottom:24px;">
<p>COMBAT
</p>
</div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:4px solid #a03535;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:24px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#a03535;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">DESIGN PHILOSOPHY</div>Combat in Wonderland of the Enigma is <span style="color:#a03535;">not a loop of rolls.</span>
It is a <b>dynamic exchange of intent, positioning, pressure, and consequence.</b> Every exchange is treated as a <b>cinematic beat</b>, not a turn cycle.<div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:repeat(5,1fr);gap:8px;"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:8px 10px;text-align:center;font-size:11px;color:#7ab2d8;letter-spacing:1px;">ACTION PRIORITY</div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:8px 10px;text-align:center;font-size:11px;color:#7ab2d8;letter-spacing:1px;">POSITIONING &amp; THREAT</div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:8px 10px;text-align:center;font-size:11px;color:#7ab2d8;letter-spacing:1px;">FATIGUE &amp; WOUNDS</div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:8px 10px;text-align:center;font-size:11px;color:#7ab2d8;letter-spacing:1px;">CURATOR NARRATION</div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:8px 10px;text-align:center;font-size:11px;color:#c09a30;letter-spacing:1px;">QUICKTIME MOMENTS</div></div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:16px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#a03535;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">01 · ENGAGEMENT</div>Combat begins when <b>hostile intent</b> is declared, implied, or triggered. Engagement does <b>not</b> require an attack to be made.<div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr;gap:12px;"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:12px 14px;"><div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:8px;">TRIGGERS</div><div style="font-size:12.5px;color:#909090;line-height:1.7;">
<p>› Direct aggression
</p><p>› Threat escalation
</p><p>› Sudden environmental danger
</p><p>› Curator-triggered events
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:12px 14px;"><div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:8px;">ONCE ENGAGED</div><div style="font-size:12.5px;color:#909090;line-height:1.7;">
<p>› Scene becomes context-restricted
</p><p>› Timing, distance, and movement become mechanically relevant
</p><p>› Action Priority may immediately be contested
</p>
</div></div></div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:16px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#a03535;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">02 · MOVEMENT &amp; POSITIONING</div>Movement in WTE is <b>not grid-based</b>. It is measured through <b>threat, proximity, and narrative positioning</b>.<div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:3px solid #c09a30;padding:12px 16px;margin-bottom:10px;"><div style="font-size:11px;font-weight:700;color:#c09a30;letter-spacing:1.5px;margin-bottom:8px;">THREAT RADIUS — ZONE OF INFLUENCE (ZoI)</div>Every hostile creature exerts a <b>30-foot Zone of Influence</b>. Entering, exiting, or repositioning within this zone may trigger a <b>Reaction Check</b>.<div style="font-size:12px;color:#565656;line-height:1.7;">Possible reactions: No response · Shift position · Block movement · Grapple · Knock prone · Strike · Initiate Quicktime · Any contextually reasonable response</div></div><div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr;gap:10px;margin-bottom:10px;"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:2px solid #3a8050;padding:12px 14px;"><div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#3a8050;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:6px;">OUTSIDE ZoI (30+ ft)</div><div style="font-size:12.5px;color:#909090;">Movement is free and unpressured.</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:2px solid #a03535;padding:12px 14px;"><div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#a03535;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:6px;">INSIDE ZoI</div><div style="font-size:12.5px;color:#909090;">Movement is restricted and reaction-sensitive. Repositioning is always a <b>risk decision</b>.</div></div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:3px solid #4e7fa5;padding:12px 16px;"><div style="font-size:11px;font-weight:700;color:#4e7fa5;letter-spacing:1.5px;margin-bottom:6px;">OFF-TURN MOVEMENT</div>An Inquisitor may move during another creature's turn as a <b>Bonus Action</b>, but they forfeit their next Bonus Action. The Curator may trigger reactions, shift combat flow, or initiate Quicktime.
<p>Represents sudden dives, lunges, retreats, or desperation movement.
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:16px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#a03535;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">03 · MAKING AN ATTACK</div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:3px solid #c09a30;padding:12px 16px;margin-bottom:12px;">
<p>Attacks in WTE hit by default.
</p><p>To avoid being hit, a creature must <b>actively respond</b>. Avoidance requires Action Priority, Dexterity, or an appropriate stat — with Curator context ruling. If an attack is ruled unavoidable (point-blank, restrained, from behind, mid-animation), it <b>automatically lands</b>.
</p>
</div><div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr 1fr;gap:10px;"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:2px solid #7ab2d8;padding:12px 14px;"><div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#7ab2d8;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:8px;">RANGED</div><div style="font-size:12px;color:#909090;line-height:1.65;">
<p>Always hit unless:
</p><p>› Target has cover
</p><p>› Target Dodges
</p><p>› Another creature intercepts
</p><p>› Environmental interference
</p><p><span style="color:#c09a30;">Creates Quicktime Window</span> if fired from elevation or target is mid-movement.
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:2px solid #6e50a0;padding:12px 14px;"><div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#6e50a0;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:8px;">AERIAL</div><div style="font-size:12px;color:#909090;line-height:1.65;">
<p>Airborne creatures gain:
</p><p>› <span style="color:#3a8050;">+1 Action Priority</span> (momentum)
</p><p>› <span style="color:#a03535;">−1 Balance</span> (instability)
</p><p>If struck mid-air: roll Balance Check. Failure causes fall, crash, spiral, or stall. Bonus damage based on dive angle and mass.
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:2px solid #a03535;padding:12px 14px;"><div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#a03535;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:8px;">MELEE</div><div style="font-size:12px;color:#909090;line-height:1.65;">
<p>Most stable combat form.
</p><p>› Auto-hit unless Dodged or Parried
</p><p>› Entering range (5–10 ft) may trigger reactions
</p><p>› Parry uses Strength or Weapon Proficiency
</p><p>› <span style="color:#c09a30;">Frequently generates Quicktime</span>: clashes, grapples, disarms, stagger checks
</p>
</div></div></div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:16px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#a03535;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">04 · ANCHORED COMBAT</div><b>Anchored</b> describes any state where movement is severely limited or removed due to conditions, terrain, injury, or effects. Anchoring is <b>never voluntary</b>.<div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr;gap:12px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:12px 14px;"><div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#a03535;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:8px;">CAUSES</div><div style="font-size:12px;color:#909090;line-height:1.7;">
<p>Grappled or restrained · Rooted or pinned · Trapped or buried · Immobilized by terrain · Severely wounded (legs, spine, core) · Fatigue at critical levels · Balance drops to 0 · Psychic or magical immobilization · Bound, chained, or locked
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:12px 14px;"><div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#a03535;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:8px;">EFFECTS</div><div style="font-size:12px;color:#909090;line-height:1.7;">
<p><b>Movement:</b> Cannot move more than 5 ft; movement attempts trigger reactions
</p><p><b>Defense:</b> −1 Action Priority; dodging disadvantaged or impossible
</p><p><b>Combat:</b> Momentum abilities may fail; reliance on Quicktime increases
</p>
</div></div></div><div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr;gap:12px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:12px 14px;"><div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#c09a30;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:8px;">SEVERITY TIERS</div><div style="font-size:12px;color:#909090;line-height:1.7;">
<p><b>SOFT ANCHORED</b> — Can pivot or reach. Cannot relocate.
</p><p><span style="color:#565656;font-style:italic;">Examples: webbing, frost, knee wounds, psychic pressure</span>
</p><p><b>HARD ANCHORED</b> — No movement possible.
</p><p><span style="color:#565656;font-style:italic;">Examples: roots, burial, restraints, tether failure, petrification</span>
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:12px 14px;"><div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#3a8050;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:8px;">BREAKING FREE</div><div style="font-size:12px;color:#909090;line-height:1.7;">
<p>› Spend an Action on a <b>Break Check</b>
</p><p>› Use a skill, ability, or item
</p><p>› Receive ally assistance
</p><p>› Exploit a Quicktime window
</p><p>Break Check uses Strength, Dexterity, or relevant specialty. Difficulty set by anchoring source.
</p>
</div></div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:3px solid #c09a30;padding:12px 16px;"><div style="font-size:11px;font-weight:700;color:#c09a30;letter-spacing:1.5px;margin-bottom:6px;">ANCHORED &amp; QUICKTIME</div>Anchored creatures automatically gain Quicktime chances when attacked in melee. They may use Quicktime to escape, reverse grapples, redirect attacks, survive lethal moments, or change the scene entirely.
</div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:16px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#a03535;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">05 · ENVIRONMENTAL COMBAT STATES</div><div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr;gap:12px;"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:2px solid #4e7fa5;padding:14px 16px;"><div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:8px;">UNDERWATER COMBAT</div><div style="font-size:12.5px;color:#909090;line-height:1.7;">
<p>› Movement halved (unless aquatic)
</p><p>› Ranged attacks fail beyond 10 ft
</p><p>› Fire/lightning altered
</p><p>› Sound amplified
</p><p>› Slashing becomes bludgeoning (unless specialized)
</p><p>› Dodging disadvantaged (unless aquatic)
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:2px solid #6e50a0;padding:14px 16px;"><div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#6e50a0;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:8px;">SPACE COMBAT</div><div style="font-size:12.5px;color:#909090;line-height:1.7;">
<p>› No resistance or gravity
</p><p>› Movement continues until halted
</p><p>› Push/pull effects amplified
</p><p>› Dodging easier, control harder
</p><p>› Every action creates a <b>drift vector</b>
</p><p>› Anchoring restores Balance
</p>
</div></div></div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:3px solid #c09a30;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:24px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#c09a30;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">06 · QUICKTIME COMBAT — SIGNATURE MECHANIC</div>A <b>Quicktime moment</b> suspends the normal flow of action and opens a window for reactive decision-making. It is the defining mechanic of WTE combat — the moment when consequence becomes personal.<div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:12px 16px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#c09a30;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:8px;">TRIGGERS</div><div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:repeat(3,1fr);gap:6px;font-size:12px;color:#909090;"><div>› An animation exposes vulnerability</div><div>› An attack visibly builds up</div><div>› Two attacks clash simultaneously</div><div>› A projectile is dodgeable</div><div>› Sudden movement occurs</div><div>› The Curator calls for tension</div></div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:12px 16px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:10px;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#c09a30;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:10px;">RESOLUTION SEQUENCE</div><div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:auto 1fr;gap:6px 14px;font-size:12.5px;color:#909090;align-items:start;">
<p><span style="color:#c09a30;font-weight:700;">1.</span><span>Curator declares <b>"Quicktime Moment"</b></span> <span style="color:#c09a30;font-weight:700;">2.</span><span>All involved roll <b>Action Priority</b></span> <span style="color:#c09a30;font-weight:700;">3.</span><span>Outcome determines the micro-result</span>
</p>
</div></div><div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:repeat(5,1fr);gap:8px;"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:10px 12px;text-align:center;"><div style="font-size:10px;font-weight:700;color:#c09a30;margin-bottom:4px;">CLASH</div><div style="font-size:11.5px;color:#909090;">→ Disarm</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:10px 12px;text-align:center;"><div style="font-size:10px;font-weight:700;color:#3a8050;margin-bottom:4px;">DODGE</div><div style="font-size:11.5px;color:#909090;">→ Clean evade</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:10px 12px;text-align:center;"><div style="font-size:10px;font-weight:700;color:#4e7fa5;margin-bottom:4px;">REDIRECT</div><div style="font-size:11.5px;color:#909090;">→ Environmental hit</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:10px 12px;text-align:center;"><div style="font-size:10px;font-weight:700;color:#7ab2d8;margin-bottom:4px;">PARRY</div><div style="font-size:11.5px;color:#909090;">→ Punish opening</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:10px 12px;text-align:center;"><div style="font-size:10px;font-weight:700;color:#a03535;margin-bottom:4px;">FUMBLE</div><div style="font-size:11.5px;color:#909090;">→ Wound / misfire</div></div></div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:24px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:12px;">SEE ALSO</div><div style="font-size:12.5px;color:#7ab2d8;line-height:1.9;">
