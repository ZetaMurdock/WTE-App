</div><div style="background:#121212;border-left:4px solid #285070;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:18px;"><div style="font-size:1.6em;font-weight:bold;color:#4890b0;letter-spacing:0.06em;font-family:monospace;">VOAULTON</div><div style="color:#407090;font-size:0.85em;margin-top:4px;font-style:italic;">Group: Humanity — Technologically integrated species with a complex history of parasitism and machine-symbiosis.</div></div><div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #101828;border-left:3px solid #285070;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;color:#90b0d0;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>Voaulton have a layered ancestral history. Their lineage includes a parasitic phase that modern Voaulton have culturally and biologically distanced themselves from. The three main variant lineages — Droid, Cyborg, and N-T1 — represent three different responses to that ancestry: transcendence, sublimation, and corruption.
</p><p>Their high Dom / low Rec values mean Voaulton traits rarely recede in hybrid offspring. Their history is directly tied to the Tauron-87 / First Ruina catastrophe through the Cyborg and N-T1 variant lineages, which makes them politically significant in ways that most Voaulton navigate carefully.
</p>
