<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>Called the death that learns. Cerioisk are living anomalies -- they cannot be killed by conventional means (only sealing stops them), they evolve physically through consumption, and they reproduce by converting other living things into more of themselves. Created during the Witching Moon, a celestial event that fused the essence of a dying Seed with a decaying Kinba. Their existence is considered an existential-tier classification of threat by most governing bodies. The Insectoid tolerate them the way one tolerates a force of nature: carefully, with distance, and with the awareness that tolerance is not the same as control.
</p>
