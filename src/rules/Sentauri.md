# Sentauri

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Sentauri">Sentauri</span></h2>
<p><i>* The Sentauri, enigmatic beings hailing from a distant and mysterious universe, possess a haunting aura that strikes fear into the hearts of those who encounter them. Although the name of their origin universe remains undocumented, their presence is undeniably awe-inspiring and often terrifying. Towering above others, their immense height ranges from 10 to 50 feet, making them truly colossal creatures.</i>
</p><p><i>* What sets the Sentauri apart is their sheer physical prowess, which surpasses the boundaries of imagination. Their strength is likened to the force unleashed by 15 atomic bombs detonating simultaneously, a staggering display of power. Magic is unknown to them, but it is their formidable physical abilities that truly shine. They possess a remarkable flexibility, allowing them to contort their bodies into seemingly impossible shapes, resembling ethereal noodles in tight spaces. This unnerving agility can lead to spine-chilling encounters with these beings, leaving lasting impressions on those who witness their eerie maneuvers.</i>
</p><p><i>* The Sentauri instill fear and awe with their presence, embodying some of the most horrifying and otherworldly creatures ever encountered. Approaching them requires utmost caution, as their immense strength and eerie flexibility make them formidable adversaries in any realm or universe but Long after, a cataclysmic event in their home universe led to the Sentauri voluntarily or involuntarily sealing away a significant portion of their power. This sealing mechanism could be tied to a cosmic artifact, an ancient ritual, or even a collective decision by their elder council. Facing the Primordials , the Sentauri sacrificed their strength to ensure the survival of their race and maintain peace.</i>
</p>
