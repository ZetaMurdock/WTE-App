<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Step_Three:_Unlocking_Your_Heritage">Step Three: Unlocking Your Heritage</span></h2>
<p>The final aspect of your <b>Inherence</b> is <b>Heritage</b>.
</p><p>Heritage is the most daunting—and often the least immediately relevant—component of character creation. While every part of Inherence matters, Heritage is not something you begin the game with. Instead, it represents <b>deep ancestral divergence</b> that only emerges at the height of a character’s journey.
</p><p>Heritage does not define who you are at the start.
</p><p>It defines what you become when pushed to your absolute limits.
</p>
<hr>
<h3><span id="What_Is_Heritage.3F"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="What_Is_Heritage?">What Is Heritage?</span></h3>
<p>Heritage represents a <b>biological and existential deviation</b> beyond your Template Species and Lineage.
</p><p>It is not a refinement of what you already are, but an <b>awakening of dormant ancestry</b>—something buried deep enough that it only surfaces through extreme experience, contradiction, or transformation.
</p><p>Mechanically and narratively, Heritage functions as a <b>late-stage inheritance</b>.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Heritage_Unlock_Conditions">Heritage Unlock Conditions</span></h2>
<p>Heritage is not chosen during initial character creation.
</p>
<ul><li>Heritage becomes available between <b>Levels 17–20</b>, at the Director’s discretion.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Heritage unlocks in the same way <b>Asymmetries (Incepts)</b> do:</li></ul>
<ul><li>Through a <b>High Point</b> (a moment of growth, triumph, or realization)</li></ul>
<ul><li>Or a <b>Low Point</b> (collapse, loss, contradiction, or crisis)</li></ul>
<p>When Heritage unlocks:
</p>
<ul><li>Your lineage percentages may shift</li></ul>
<ul><li>Your <b>chance to express Heritage Traits increases</b></li></ul>
<ul><li>Heritage traits enter your potential <b>Racial Trait Pool</b></li></ul>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Heritage_Selection_Rules">Heritage Selection Rules</span></h2>
<p>Heritage follows the same structural rules as Template Species and Lineage, with additional restrictions.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Racial_Class_Requirement">Racial Class Requirement</span></h3>
<ul><li>Your Heritage <b>must belong to a different Racial Class</b> than your Template Species.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Racial Classes represent broad biological or existential categories.</li></ul>
<ul><li>This ensures Heritage feels <b>transformative</b>, not redundant.</li></ul>
<p>Heritage does <b>not</b> replace your Template Species or Lineage—it overlays them.
</p>
<hr>
<h3><span id="Nature_.26_Balance"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Nature_&amp;_Balance">Nature &amp; Balance</span></h3>
<ul><li>Heritage carries its own <b>Innate Nature</b></li></ul>
<ul><li>It is subject to the <b>Rule of Even</b>, just like Lineage</li></ul>
<ul><li>Opposing Natures introduced by Heritage must still be balanced, unless overridden by the Director</li></ul>
<p>Because Heritage emerges late, its Nature influence is often <b>subtle but powerful</b>, shaping long-term behavior rather than immediate instinct.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Heritage_Progression">Heritage Progression</span></h2>
<p>Once Heritage is unlocked, progression begins at <b>Level 20</b>.
</p><p>At each applicable level, you gain <b>one</b> of the following (Director-approved):
</p>
<ul><li><b>One Heritage Trait (Incept)</b>  A potent racial expression tied to your awakened ancestry</li></ul>
<p><b>OR</b>
</p>
<ul><li>A <b>Stat Additive</b>:</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>+1</b>, <b>+1</b>, or <b>+5</b> to any stats</li></ul>
<p>Heritage traits follow the same inheritance logic as Lineage traits:
</p>
<ul><li>They are <b>not guaranteed</b></li></ul>
<ul><li>Expression is influenced by narrative pressure, percentage weighting, and character development</li></ul>
<ul><li>They may remain latent until conditions are met</li></ul>
<hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Why_Heritage_Is_Delayed">Why Heritage Is Delayed</span></h3>
<p>Heritage is intentionally late-game because it represents:
</p>
<ul><li>Irreversible change</li></ul>
<ul><li>Existential contradiction</li></ul>
<ul><li>Evolution beyond original design</li></ul>
<p>Not every character will fully realize their Heritage—and that is by design.
</p>
<hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Final_Note">Final Note</span></h3>
<p>Heritage is not required to “complete” a character.
</p><p>It is the answer to a question the story asks much later:
</p><p><b>What are you, when everything you were meant to be is no longer enough?</b>
</p>
