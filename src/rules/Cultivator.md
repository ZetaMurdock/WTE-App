<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Cultivator">Archetype: <i>Cultivator</i></span></h1>
<p><i>Through butchering and cultivating, I supersede.</i>
</p><p>The <b>Cultivator</b> is a proactive manipulator who exerts control through <b>harvest, preparation, and adaptation</b>. Where other archetypes manipulate time, code, or perception, the Cultivator manipulates <b>biology, matter, and consequence</b>. Every kill, capture, or wound is an opportunity. Every battlefield is a resource field.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  The Cultivator is a <b>frontline manipulator</b> who shapes combat by harvesting enemies and converting them into tools, traits, and advantages. Through hunting, capturing, butchering, fermenting, and implementation, the Cultivator steadily alters the flow of battle. Over extended engagements, they grow stronger—not through levels alone, but through what they extract and refine in real time.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  Cultivators thrive in <b>extended fights</b>. Early combat is about positioning, tagging targets, and securing viable specimens. Mid-fight, the Cultivator begins processing—harvesting parts, triggering growths, deploying stored effects. Late combat sees the Cultivator fully adapted, wielding battlefield-specific advantages shaped by prior decisions.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  Cultivators require <b>material continuity</b>. Enemies that leave no remains, environments hostile to biological manipulation, or effects that destroy bodies outright can limit their growth. They are also decision-heavy; poor harvesting choices can leave them underprepared later in a fight. - Specify particular aspects</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  <b>Cleaver &amp; Frigidarium</b> — tools that define the Cultivator’s entire philosophy: one to harvest, one to preserve.</li></ul>
<p><i><b>Playstyle:</b></i> <i>Proactive control, battlefield adaptation, long-form dominance.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetry:_Cultivator_Action_Economy">Asymmetry: <i>Cultivator Action Economy</i></span></h1>
<p>Upon choosing <b>Cultivator</b>, your Actor gains its <b>Asymmetry</b>, reflecting a style built around <b>frequent actions</b> and layered preparation rather than singular high-impact turns.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy">Action Economy</span></h2>
<ul><li><b><i>6</i> Standard Actions</b>  Used for attacks, harvesting, implanting, cultivating growths, or processing stored material.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>2</i> True Opportunity Attacks</b>  Triggered when enemies attempt to disengage, are reduced below a threshold, or expose biological vulnerabilities.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>0</i> Free Actions</b>  Cultivator actions are deliberate; every decision costs time.</li></ul>
<p><i>Cultivators overwhelm encounters through volume and planning rather than speed.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span id="Aspect:_Cleaver_.26_Frigidarium"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Cleaver_&amp;_Frigidarium">Aspect: <i>Cleaver &amp; Frigidarium</i></span></h1>
<p>All <b>Cultivator</b> actors begin with the <i><b>Cleaver &amp; Frigidarium</b></i>, representing <b>harvest and preservation</b>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function">Base Function</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Cleaver">Cleaver</span></h3>
<p>The Cleaver is a specialized harvesting weapon used to extract viable biological material.
</p>
<ul><li>When you deal the finishing blow to a creature or incapacitate it, you may make a <b>Harvest Roll</b>:</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Harvest Roll:</b> Strength or Dexterity + Versatility vs a Director-set DC based on target complexity.</li></ul>
<ul><li>On success, you extract <b>Cultivation Material</b> (organs, tissues, growths, fluids).</li></ul>
<ul><li>On failure, the material is damaged but still usable with reduced potency.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Frigidarium">Frigidarium</span></h3>
<p>The Frigidarium is a portable preservation system that halts decay and stabilizes harvested material.
</p>
<ul><li>Stores a limited number of <b>Cultivation Slots</b> (scales with level).</li></ul>
<ul><li>Preserved materials do not degrade over time.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Materials can be:</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Implanted</b> into yourself or other</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Fermented</b> into consumables</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Deployed</b> as environmental hazards or allies</li></ul>
<h3><span id="Processing_.26_Implementation"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Processing_&amp;_Implementation">Processing &amp; Implementation</span></h3>
<ul><li>Processing harvested material requires <b>Standard Actions</b> during combat or <b>Passive Time</b> outside combat.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Each implementation requires a <b>Cultivation Check</b> (attribute varies by effect).</li></ul>
<ul><li>Failed checks may result in mutations, instability, or backlash effects.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b>  Conventional weapon damage, environmental decay.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b>  Anti-biological effects, void exposure, reality sterilization, or timeline erasure.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b>  Materials are consumed, destroyed, or overwritten by new cultivation.</li></ul>
