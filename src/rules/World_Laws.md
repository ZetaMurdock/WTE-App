<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Energy_Types_and_Their_Effects_on_Atoms_and_Subatomic_Particles_in_Enigmatica">Energy Types and Their Effects on Atoms and Subatomic Particles in Enigmatica</span></h3>
<ol><li><b>Neutral Energy</b>  Neutral energy is made up of protons, neutrons, and electrons.  and there are These particles, referred to as <i>Untrons</i>, possess a unique <i>Qusai effect</i> in the world of Enigmatica. This effect allows them to grow, adapt, and change based on the environment they occupy. The nucleus, known as the <i>Nexus</i>, has the ability to absorb and generate new information, which is then used to modify the Untrons. This adaptive nature enables the particles to continuously evolve to fit their surroundings.</li>
<li><b>Eldritch Energy</b>  Eldritch energy consists of neutrons, electrons, and Untrons, with a Nexus replacing the traditional nucleus. The Nexus continuously generates new information based on environmental conditions and charges the neutrons. This process imparts a form of sentience to the neutrons, which are able to absorb the information from the Nexus, itself influenced by the <i>Augo Gland</i>. The charged neutrons can then act on this information, but only in the form of movement or division—never multiplication or addition. The limited scope of actions reflects the energy’s unpredictable nature.</li>
<li><b>Null Energy</b>  Null energy is composed exclusively of neutrons and operates with both a Nexus and a nucleus. The nucleus plays a unique role, filtering and overriding information from surrounding particles. This overridden data is then transmitted to the Nexus, which subsequently sends it to the neutron. As a result, the neutron gains sentience and is compelled to act on its new sense of identity, specifically in a corruptive manner, seeking to infect other particles with this overriding information.</li>
<li><b>Elemental Energy</b>  Elemental energy is made up of protons, electrons, a Nexus, and a nucleus. The nucleus of elemental energy functions similarly to that of Null energy by seeking out neutral particles and energy. It then absorbs them and creates a copy by dividing the energy received by the original particle. This copy is sent to the Nexus, which overrides it, granting it new properties. The resulting charged protons and electrons will move in either a smooth or erratic manner depending on the type of information stored within them, creating distinct elemental behaviors.</li>
<li><b>Kinetic Energy</b>  Kinetic energy consists solely of Untrons and a Nexus. This energy type collects information, stores it within the Nexus, and redistributes it instantly through the Untrons. The transfer of information creates dynamic movement and change, with the kinetic energy rapidly shifting based on the stored data, leading to fast-paced and fluid transformations in matter.</li></ol>

<li>The <b>Nexus</b> acts as an <b>information processor</b>, modifying Untrons dynamically. This is consistent across energy types.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Nexus and Augo Gland Relationship</b>
<ul><li>The <b>Nexus absorbs environmental information</b> and translates it into changes in Untrons.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Energy Types &amp; Their Logic</b>
<ul><li><b>Neutral Energy:</b> Works like a standard atomic structure, but with adaptable Untrons. Their behavior mimics natural atomic interactions while allowing for supernatural adaptations.</li>
<li><b>Eldritch Energy:</b> Since its neutrons are sentient and reactive to the Nexus’s information processing, its limitation (only movement or division) aligns with how real particles can behave without breaking physical consistency.</li>
<li><b>Null Energy:</b> The nucleus acts as a “data override” system, enforcing a corruption-like spread. The <b>sentience in neutrons</b> makes sense if it is due to continuous information feedback, similar to machine learning in AI.</li>
<li><b>Elemental Energy:</b> By absorbing and duplicating neutral energy, it logically follows real-world fission/fusion behaviors but with a specialized transformation step through the Nexus.</li>
<li><b>Kinetic Energy:</b> Since it consists only of <b>Untrons</b>, it behaves fluidly, making sense as a rapid information-transfer mechanism akin to wave-particle interactions.</li></ul></li></ul>

<h3><span id="Subatomic_Particle_Roles_.26_Their_Necessity_in_Different_Energy_Types"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Subatomic_Particle_Roles_&amp;_Their_Necessity_in_Different_Energy_Types">Subatomic Particle Roles &amp; Their Necessity in Different Energy Types</span></h3>
<ol><li><b>Untrons (Primary Adaptive Particle)</b>
<ul><li>Untrons serve as <b>Enigmatica’s fundamental building blocks</b> of energy, similar to how quarks form protons and neutrons.</li>
<li>They possess a <b>quasi-effect</b>, meaning they can <b>grow, adapt, and change</b> based on environmental conditions.</li>
<li><b>Why Are They Necessary?</b>
<ul><li>They are the <b>primary energy carriers</b> for forms that require <b>constant evolution</b> (e.g., Kinetic Energy).</li>
<li>Their adaptability allows them to bond with other particles or act as standalone units of force.</li>
<li>They ensure energy transitions remain <b>fluid</b> rather than rigid (e.g., how kinetic shifts instantly based on information flow).</li></ul></li></ul></li></ol>
<ol><li><b>Protons (Charge &amp; Stability Agents)</b>
<ul><li>Protons in Enigmatica function similarly to real-world protons in atomic nuclei, but with an added role: <b>charge modulation and energy cohesion</b>.</li>
<li><b>Why Are They Necessary?</b>
<ul><li><b>Elemental Energy requires protons</b> because they stabilize atomic structures while also influencing <b>how energy interacts with the physical world</b>.</li>
<li>Without protons, energy would <b>disperse</b> too quickly, making long-lasting elemental effects (like controlled fire, ice, or electricity) impossible.</li>
<li>Protons provide a method for <b>duplicating</b> neutral particles, allowing Elemental Energy to spread without losing integrity.</li></ul></li></ul></li></ol>
<ol><li><b>Neutrons (Energy Storage &amp; Sentience)</b>
<ul><li>Neutrons serve two primary functions: <b>energy storage</b> and, in some cases, <b>sentience</b>.</li>
<li>Unlike protons, they do not carry a charge but instead act as <b>buffers</b> or <b>conduits</b> for energy transfer.</li>
<li><b>Why Are They Necessary?</b>
<ul><li><b>Eldritch Energy is reliant on neutrons</b> because its energy structure is unstable; neutrons absorb <b>informative energy</b> from the Nexus and act as its carriers.</li>
<li>The process of charging neutrons with information gives them <b>semi-conscious decision-making</b>, making Eldritch Energy unpredictable.</li>
<li><b>Null Energy requires neutrons</b> because it uses them to <b>overwrite existing information</b>, forcing energy into a corrupted state.</li>
<li>Without neutrons, these energy types would either <b>collapse under their own instability</b> (Eldritch) or <b>fail to spread their corruptive influence</b> (Null).</li></ul></li></ul></li></ol>
<ol><li><b>Electrons (Fluidity &amp; Energy Transfer)</b>
<ul><li>Electrons govern <b>how energy moves and interacts</b> between particles, making them essential for highly <b>reactive</b> energy types.</li>
<li><b>Why Are They Necessary?</b>
<ul><li><b>Elemental Energy requires electrons</b> to dictate whether energy moves <b>smoothly or erratically</b>, influencing how it behaves in different conditions.</li>
<li><b>Eldritch Energy uses electrons</b> to determine <b>movement patterns</b> but cannot influence charge directly, meaning it always acts in a semi-chaotic way.</li>
<li>Without electrons, energy movement would be rigid, preventing Elemental Energy from flowing naturally or Eldritch Energy from having its <b>erratic but functional</b> nature.</li></ul></li></ul></li></ol>
<ol><li><b>Nexus (Information Processing &amp; Adaptation Center)</b>
<ul><li>The <b>Nexus</b> acts as the <b>core intelligence</b> of all energy types, processing and redistributing information received from the Augo Gland.</li>
<li><b>Why Is It Necessary?</b>
<ul><li>Without a Nexus, energy would <b>lack a central structure</b>, making it either too unstable or too rigid to be functional.</li>
<li><b>Kinetic Energy relies solely on a Nexus</b>, as it doesn’t use protons, neutrons, or electrons—only Untrons, which need a processor to ensure <b>fluidity of movement</b>.</li>
<li><b>Eldritch and Null Energies need a Nexus</b> to properly interpret external information, otherwise, they would be purely chaotic forces with no direction.</li></ul></li></ul></li></ol>
<ol><li><b>Nucleus (Filtering &amp; Control Center)</b>
<ul><li>The <b>Nucleus</b> exists in certain energy types as an added layer of control, filtering or reinforcing information.</li>
<li><b>Why Is It Necessary?</b>
<ul><li><b>Null Energy needs a nucleus</b> to <b>override external influences</b>, ensuring its corruptive nature remains <b>dominant</b> over foreign data.</li>
<li><b>Elemental Energy needs a nucleus</b> to <b>replicate neutral energy</b>, as duplication would be unstable without a strict filtering mechanism.</li>
<li>The <b>absence of a nucleus</b> in Eldritch and Kinetic Energy ensures they remain more <b>free-flowing and unpredictable</b>, aligning with their chaotic nature.</li></ul></li></ul></li></ol>

<ul><li><b>Untrons = Fundamental adaptive energy particles, critical for dynamic movement (Kinetic Energy).</b></li>
<li><b>Protons = Charge controllers &amp; stabilizers, crucial for Elemental Energy.</b></li>
<li><b>Neutrons = Storage &amp; sentience granters, key to Eldritch and Null Energies.</b></li>

<li>Example: A passive ability that must be manually activated once per hour despite being low-cost.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Adaptation &amp; Possibility Constraints</b>

<li>If the ability requires <b>specific environmental conditions</b>, the bacteria may take longer to adjust or even <b>fail</b> if those conditions aren’t met.</li>
<li>Example: A lightning-based ability will adapt faster in a storm but may struggle in an energy-deprived area.</li></ul></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Interaction with the Nexus &amp; Nucleus</b>

<li>In energy types <b>with a nucleus</b> (e.g., Null or Elemental Energy), the bacteria must also bypass the <b>nucleus’s filtering process</b>, which may delay or alter the ability’s final effect.</li></ul></li></ul>
