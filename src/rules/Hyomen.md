Progenitor of most modern civilizations.</div></div><div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #302010;border-left:3px solid #705028;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;color:#c0a070;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>The most widely distributed species in WTE. Hyomen drove many early civilizations and serve as the progenitor stock of multiple variant lineages. Their high Dominance value means Hyomen traits frequently assert in hybrid offspring — a lineage that shapes its descendants even when mixed.
</p><p>Their core vulnerability is institutional: the Nigraldi can subjugate standard Hyomen, making them systemically controllable in ways that their variants — Spatians and Neo-Humans in particular — have evolved around or escaped entirely.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="HYBRID_INTERACTION">HYBRID INTERACTION</span></h2>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #302010;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:16px;color:#c0a070;font-size:0.9em;">
<p>Hyomen Dom 45 / Rec 10 — their traits assert strongly in hybrid offspring and rarely recede. In hybrids with lower-Dom species, Hyomen traits typically dominate the expression pattern. Their broad Asymmetry access (Peak Evolution) is often retained even in mixed lineage.
