# Stynigi

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Stynigi">Stynigi</span></h2>
<p>The Creatures of Darkness formed from the want of Power and the want of obsolete control. The Stynigi hail from the Nigraldi and the Stygians this race of people was made to bring victory to the Stygians and the Nigraldi within the war unfortunately this race was never used for war. ￼
</p>
<h2><span id="Skill.E2.80.99s_and_attributes"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Skill’s_and_attributes"><i>Skill’s and attributes</i></span></h2>
<p>True Darkness: Stynigi's can Envelope an area In Pure Darkness that cannot be countered with any ability except for Action Priority based movement and The Null Path this Darkness De-buffs and applies Sleepy along with Blighted to whoever finds themself lost within this darkness. ￼
</p><p>Forgotten Void: The Stynigi cracks the air and a Hole into their original Universe gushes out a Black Liquid that transforms into beasts ranging from 2 feet beasts to 15 feet beasts this ability cannot be countered any attempt to come into contact with the hole in space time will result in atomic disintegration
</p><p>Forced Reproduction: The Stynigi uses their hand to bring an Unsuspecting Victim to them uses their tendrils and forcibly makes an Offspring with them that the Stynigi produces from their Center this can be countered. ￼
</p><p>Final Eclipse: the Stynigi takes on a Monstrous form and forces a Solar Eclipse everything that the darkness touches become Blighted up to 8 and are afflicted with the curse status up to 8 then afterwards the Stynigi is reverted to a smaller form from before and forced to sleep Indefinitely
</p>
