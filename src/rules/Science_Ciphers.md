<div style="color:#dedede;font-size:11.5px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:12px">The Science Paradigm distributes its Ciphers to empower its scientists, researchers, and even interns, assisting them in their pursuit of discovery and innovation. These Ciphers are not tools of war, but <b>instruments of understanding</b> — designed to push the boundaries of what can be known, built, or altered.</div>
<div style="color:#909090;font-size:11px;line-height:1.7;margin-bottom:12px">As an Inquisitor aligned with the Science Paradigm, it is in your best interest to uphold its values through proactive curiosity, rational investigation, and theoretical advancement. However, how you interpret and embody those values remains your own intellectual freedom — the Paradigm merely provides the foundation upon which you may build.</div>
<div style="color:#909090;font-size:11px;line-height:1.7;margin-bottom:14px">The Ciphers range from facilitating basic chemical manipulation to enabling metaphysical experimentation that questions the very structure of existence. Across all categories, their purpose is consistent: to refine perception, control, and comprehension of the world around you.</div>
