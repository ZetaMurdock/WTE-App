# Science Ciphers

WTE · MECHANICS · PARADIGM CIPHER · SCIENCE

## Science Paradigm Overview

The Science Paradigm distributes its Ciphers to empower its scientists, researchers, and even interns, assisting them in their pursuit of discovery and innovation. These Ciphers are not tools of war, but instruments of understanding — designed to push the boundaries of what can be known, built, or altered.

As an Inquisitor aligned with the Science Paradigm, it is in your best interest to uphold its values through proactive curiosity, rational investigation, and theoretical advancement. How you interpret and embody those values remains your own intellectual freedom — the Paradigm merely provides the foundation upon which you may build.

The Ciphers range from basic chemical manipulation to metaphysical experimentation that questions the very structure of existence. Across all categories, their purpose is consistent: to refine perception, control, and comprehension of the world around you.

**CALIBRATION STANDARD** — Every Science Cipher is calibrated to instrument tolerances: effects resolve exactly as listed, damage resolves through its listed dice, and no calibration permits an outcome that ends a life outside those dice. Alterations target unattended matter unless an entry states otherwise — anything worn or held by a creature resists modification through its bearer.

**SCIENCE ROLL PROFILE** — Science Ciphers natively operate through Physical Saves, Mental Checks, and Mental Saves. Science does not natively call for Physical Checks. Effects inherited from weapons, copied abilities, environmental processes, or external systems retain their original Roll Path.

## Offline Ciphers · TL ≤ 2.0 · Fixed / Unregulated

### Light Weight

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 35 |
| Action | Science Cipher |
| Rank | Researcher |
| Component | Physical object (non-biological) |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — The Cipher alters the physical makeup of a non-biological object, drastically reducing its weight. Objects affected can shift downward by one to two weight classes, depending on the Cipher's calibration.

EFFECT — The object becomes significantly lighter — but only for the user who activated it. Upon picking up or holding the object, the Inquisitor gains limited control over its dimensional properties. As a bonus action, the user can enlarge or shrink the object by up to two size categories in either direction. If an object is reduced beyond its minimum stable size, it defaults to its smallest possible safe configuration.

**LIMIT:** One dimensional adjustment per Bonus Action; calibration holds only while the activating user carries the object.

### Malliation

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 15 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Intern |
| Component | Inanimate Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — Alters the physical properties of the bonded object, allowing it to transition between states of extreme hardness and softness at the Inquisitor's command, its density shifting in response to direct mental or tactile input.

EFFECT — The bonded object can be hardened or softened with exceptional precision, ranging from the density of cushioned fabric to the structural integrity of refined diamond. Hard State: resists cutting, crushing, or piercing damage up to Tier-3 materials; may be used offensively (as a weapon of its Tier, standard dice). Soft State: reduces mass and rigidity, absorbs kinetic energy, enables stealth compression.

**LIMIT:** Switching states requires one Reaction or Bonus Action; each full material shift consumes a minor Cipher charge, replenishable upon rest.

### Visiation

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 25 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Intern |
| Component | Animate Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — Designed for direct neural integration, embedding itself into the user's ocular nerve — a painless bond marked only by a faint geometric shimmer across the eye when active. Once bonded, the Cipher permanently synchronizes with the Inquisitor's visual cortex.

Passive: grants +2 to Mental Checks — Capacity reliant on visual or chemical analysis.

### Chemical Alter

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 45 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Researcher |
| Component | Inanimate Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When affixed to an object, induces a controlled destabilization effect, disrupting the object's molecular cohesion. The object becomes a mutable framework, allowing additional Ciphers to modify or extract its physical attributes.

EFFECT — A destabilized object can be deconstructed into its fundamental physical aspects, extracted as tangible particle blocks held in the Inquisitor's hand. Extracted aspects include: Size, Mass, Density, Viscosity, Friction, Force. Each block is a condensed representation of a single property. Two or more blocks can be combined to form composite materials, or applied to existing objects to alter their characteristics. Modified material requires stabilization to prevent further disintegration.

**KEYWORD — DESTABILIZED:** a destabilized object remains mutable until Stabilized. Unattended objects only — matter worn or held by a creature resists destabilization through its bearer.

### Stabilize

|---|---|

### Bipartition

|---|---|
| SS | 50 |
| Action | Bonus Action |

### Masking

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 25 |
| Action | Prepared Action |
| Rank | Intern |
| Component | Inanimate Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When the Cipher is affixed to an object, the object's structure shifts and reforms into a face mask — a smooth, contoured piece that fits over the wearer's face while retaining faint traces of the original material, adjusting its form to match the user's features.

EFFECT — When activated while worn, allows the user to comprehend and imitate the thinking patterns and outward behavior of another species they are observing or directly interacting with — intuitively understanding how that species tends to react, move, and communicate. Over time in the same encounter the mask enables the user to mirror their actions or language, reproducing basic speech patterns, tone, and simple phrases closely enough to be treated as a familiar presence rather than an outsider.

**RESOLUTION:** when an observer of the imitated species actively scrutinizes the imitation, the wearer makes a Mental Check — Capacity contested by the observer's Mental Save — Perception. Behavioral inconsistencies win ties for the observer.

## Special Ciphers · Off-Network · Granted to Exceptional Inquisitors

### S1 — Absolute Zero

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 80 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Distinguished Scientist |
| Component | Ambient thermal energy |

EFFECT — Drops temperature within a 30-ft radius to absolute zero over 2 rounds. All molecular motion ceases — objects become structurally perfect but completely brittle; living creatures make a Physical Save — Recovery (DV 18) each round or take 3d10 cold damage and lose 1 Action. Sustained up to 3 rounds. Releasing stored thermal energy detonates it — 5d10 fire damage in the same radius; affected creatures make a Physical Save — Evasion for half damage. Special Ciphers are never Online-logged.

**KEYWORD — DEEP FREEZE:** the cold incapacitates rather than executes — a creature dropped to 0 HP inside the field is frozen unconscious and stable until thawed.

#### Actions

- Cost: 80 SS
- Save: Physical Save — Recovery, DV 18
- Fail: Damage: 3d10 Cold
- Fail: Ruling: the creature also loses 1 Action; the Save repeats each round the field is sustained, up to 3 rounds
- Save: Physical Save — Evasion
- Damage: 5d10 Fire, half on success
- Ruling: the detonation resolves only when the stored thermal energy is released; DEEP FREEZE — a creature dropped to 0 HP inside the field is frozen unconscious and stable until thawed

## Science Paradigm Perk — Unclassified Paradigms

The Science Paradigm grants five additional Unclassified Paradigm slots to exceptional Inquisitors — reserved for scholars whose obsession with discovery transcends standard methodologies. These recipients seek to understand not just what the world is, but how it chooses to evolve.
