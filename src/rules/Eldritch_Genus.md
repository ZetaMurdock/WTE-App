# Eldritch Genus

> "Entropy is not destruction — it is the natural state of everything. We merely accelerate the inevitable."

The Eldritch Genus is the domain of entropy, destabilization, and psychic disruption. Eldritch energy is the force of breakdown given form — the corruption of coherence, the unmaking of reliable cause-and-effect. Practitioners disrupt, distort, and dismantle, excelling at denying enemy capability and imposing catastrophic failure at the worst possible moment.

## Domain Profile

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Identity | Entropy, destabilization, psychic disruption, forced failure. Eldritch practitioners unravel the coherence of enemies rather than destroying them outright. |
| Paradigm Access | Cognition Paradigm · Evolution Paradigm |
| SNR Status | No Standard Null Ruling. Eldritch abilities resolve in normal initiative order and can be intercepted by standard Reactions. |

## Eldritch Abilities · 18 Total

All Eldritch abilities are available to any Inquisitor with Eldritch domain access. Abilities are not gated by rank — they are gated by SS pool capacity.

### Lark

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Classification | Emission / Trans-modification |
| SS Cost | 5 SS |
| Activation | Active (5 min) |
| Range / Area | Multi-target / 10-ft rectangle within 30 ft (shapeable) |
| Target | Creatures, Objects |

Fires scattered bursts of entropic energy in erratic patterns — unpredictable like a bird's flight. Each creature in the rectangle makes a Physical Save — Recovery or takes 1d40 Spirit Damage. Uncontrolled pattern — the user must succeed on a Mental Check — Capacity (DV 12) or catch an edge of their own effect (1d4 damage, no Save).

**LIMIT:** Once per Synaptic Focus
