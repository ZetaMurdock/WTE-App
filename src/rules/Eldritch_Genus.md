<p>The <b>Eldritch Genus</b> is the domain of entropy, destabilization, and psychic disruption. Eldritch energy is the force of breakdown given form — the corruption of coherence, the unmaking of reliable cause-and-effect. Practitioners disrupt, distort, and dismantle, excelling at denying enemy capability and imposing catastrophic failure at the worst possible moment.
