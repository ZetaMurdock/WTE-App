# Kinetic Weapon

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #3a2010;padding:8px 14px;margin-bottom:12px;font-family:monospace;font-size:0.82em;color:#7a6040;">
<p><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Weapons_Guide">Weapons</a>&nbsp;› <strong class="mw-selflink selflink">Kinetic Weapon</strong>
</p>
</div><div style="background:#121212;border-left:4px solid #804020;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:18px;"><div style="font-size:1.6em;font-weight:bold;color:#c07040;letter-spacing:0.06em;font-family:monospace;">KINETIC WEAPON</div><div style="color:#9a8060;font-size:0.85em;margin-top:4px;font-style:italic;">Physical force. Ballistic, impact, gauss, bladed. The oldest and most universal weapon type.</div></div><div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #3a2010;border-left:3px solid #804020;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;color:#c0a070;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>Kinetic weapons operate on the oldest principle in warfare: mass and velocity applied to a target. They require no power source, generate no detectable energy signature, and function reliably in sealed CAS zones where Energy and Exotic weapons are flagged or suppressed. In a world defined by CAS surveillance and Eminence tracking, a Kinetic weapon is a statement — loud, physical, and impossible to deny.
</p><p>Kinetic weapons leave evidence. Shell casings. Impact craters. Structural damage. That is both their weakness and their honest brutality.
</p><p>Inspired by the ordnance of Trench Crusade, the bolt weapons of WH40k, and WTE's own industrial pre-Collapse manufacturing tradition.
</p>
</div>
