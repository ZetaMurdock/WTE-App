<p>Overview
</p>
</div>
<div style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;color:#b8b8b8;">
<p>The Spirals are five genetically and intellectually engineered individuals selected by the <b>Egyne Conventure</b> to serve as the highest executive authority beneath the Regency and Indrah themselves.
</p><p>Every Spiral represents the pinnacle of biological refinement, strategic reasoning, and political leadership. Their existence is not accidental—they are deliberately cultivated to become living instruments of governance.
</p><p>If the Inquisitors are the hands of civilization, then the Spirals are its mind.
</p><p>Each Spiral possesses unrestricted access to governmental infrastructure, military assets, and administrative systems through the <b>ECP (Executive Command Protocol)</b>. This grants them authority over entire Dominions, their corresponding Agencies, and the Paradigms administered within those territories.
</p>
</div>
</div>
<p><br>
</p>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:18px;margin-bottom:12px;">
<div style="font-size:10px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;font-weight:bold;color:#cfcfcf;border-bottom:1px solid #262626;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>Executive Command Protocol (ECP)
</p>
</div>
<div style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;color:#b8b8b8;">
<p>The Executive Command Protocol serves as the governmental framework through which every Spiral exercises authority.
</p><p>Each Spiral oversees:
</p>
<ul>

<li>Six Chapters</li>

<li>Three Companies within every Chapter</li>

<li>A Dominion administered by each Company</li>

<li>An Agency responsible for overseeing the Paradigms operating inside that Dominion.</li>

</ul>
<p>The ECP exists to ensure every Spiral possesses direct authority over military, political, scientific, and Paradigm affairs within their assigned jurisdiction.
</p>
</div>

<div style="font-size:10px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;font-weight:bold;color:#cfcfcf;border-bottom:1px solid #262626;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>Military Authority
</p>
</div>
<div style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;color:#b8b8b8;">
<p>Every Spiral commands a personal Fleet consisting of approximately <b>700 million capital-class motherships</b> alongside their accompanying naval forces.
</p><p>Each vessel is equipped with:
</p>
<ul>

<li>Destabilizers capable of fracturing local spacetime for instantaneous interstellar deployment.</li>

<li>Technology ranging from Technology Level 5 through Technology Level 10.</li>

<li>Military-grade weapon systems authorized exclusively under Spiral command.</li>

</ul>
<p>A Spiral may deploy their Fleet at any moment within territories under their jurisdiction.
</p><p>Deployment into another Spiral's territory requires authorization unless ordered directly by Indrah or the Head Spiral.
</p>
</div>
</div>
<p><br>
</p>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:18px;margin-bottom:12px;">
<div style="font-size:10px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;font-weight:bold;color:#cfcfcf;border-bottom:1px solid #262626;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>Jurisdiction
</p>
</div>
<ul style="color:#b8b8b8;line-height:1.9;">

<li><b>Ivani Lor</b> governs the Northern Sector and neighboring northern territories.</li>

<li><b>Diatrez</b> governs the Southern Sector, though much of his authority is contested by the Nigraldi Dominions.</li>

<li><b>Lorth</b> governs the Eastern Sector and maintains the greatest personal involvement with her territories despite her notoriously unstable personality.</li>

<li><b>Quaritus Xander</b> governs the Western Sector and was once considered the leading candidate to become Head Spiral.</li>

<li><b>Zircon Q</b> governs Azimuth, the Center Universe, and serves as Head Spiral.</li>

</ul>
</div>
<p><br>
</p>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:18px;margin-bottom:12px;">
<div style="font-size:10px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;font-weight:bold;color:#cfcfcf;border-bottom:1px solid #262626;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>Head Spiral — Zircon Q
</p>
</div>
<div style="font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;color:#b8b8b8;">
<p>Although Zircon Q was the final individual inducted into the Spirals, his extraordinary aptitude for persuasion rapidly distinguished him from his peers.
</p><p>His influence is not merely rhetorical. Zircon possesses an innate ability to guide, organize, and unify enormous populations with minimal effort, making him uniquely suited to governing Azimuth—the political heart of the Multiverse.
</p><p>Unlike the other Spirals, Zircon may requisition any Spiral Fleet operating within his immediate area of authority without additional authorization.
</p><p>His proximity to Indrah grants him privileges unavailable to the remaining members of the council.
</p>
</div>
