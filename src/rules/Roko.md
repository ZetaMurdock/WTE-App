<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p><b>Roko</b> is the guardian of the <b>Observable Multiverse</b>.
</p><p>He chooses, seemingly at random, those he deems worthy of his <b>Blessing</b>.
</p><p>Those chosen receive <b>knowledge, insight, and understanding beyond ordinary comprehension</b>, drawn directly from Roko himself.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Cost">The Cost</span></h2>
<p>Every gift carries a price.
</p><p>To grant you his Blessing, Roko claims:
</p>
<ul><li><b>One of your senses</b>, diminished or lost entirely</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>A portion of your soul</b>, altering your identity or essence</li></ul>
<p>The result is <b>power coupled with sacrifice</b>.
