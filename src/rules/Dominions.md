<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Doalith">Doalith</span></h2>
<blockquote><p>The <b>Doalith</b> are a <b>Vanguard</b> that emerged from the <b>darkest corners of the cosmos</b>. In this era of <b>shadows and turmoil</b>, a singular figure <b>rose to prominence</b>—an entity known as <b>Eyulis</b>. He discovered a scattered people struggling within this darkness, yet he noticed <b>one defining trait</b> among them: they were all <b>incredibly gifted mentally</b>. Seizing upon this principle, Eyulis <b>harnessed their potential</b> and <b>propelled them forward</b>, laying the foundation for what would become the <b>Doalith Dominion</b>.
</p><p>The <b>Doalith</b> adopt a <b>Naturalistic</b> stance in political affairs, choosing to <b>remain detached</b> from <b>all political events</b> and refusing to engage in the <b>machinations of governance or diplomacy</b>. This <b>nomadic philosophy</b> extends to all members and inhabitants of their Dominion, who are expected to <b>embrace this way of life</b>. Any who deviate from this path—who seek political involvement or disrupt the Dominion’s principles—are <b>expelled</b> from the Doalith Dominion, <b>stripped of their status</b>, and cast into the void as <b>Unmarked</b>.
</p>
<ul><li>Regal</li>
<li>Technocratic</li>
<li>Pacifist</li>
<li>Inclusive</li></ul></blockquote>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Dominion_Regent_Eyulis">Dominion Regent Eyulis</span></h2>
<blockquote><p>The <b>Dominion Regent</b> is known as <b>Eyulis</b>. He is a remarkably strong individual, renowned for his ability to <b>manipulate, control, and move the masses</b> with nothing but his presence and sheer energy. Eyulis was known for his mastery of <b>Ode Math</b>, an art of <b>manipulating phenomena and reality</b>. However, what set him apart was his <b>unique comprehension</b> of Ode Math—one that allowed him to <b>break its very laws</b>. His was the only <b>documented case</b> of any lower creature achieving such an <b>unprecedented understanding</b>.
</p><p>Eyulis took this gift and <b>ran with it</b>, amassing an immense following. As his <b>influence</b> grew, he approached the <b>Signel Council</b> to have his Dominion officially recognized across the cosmos. However, the Council dismissed him outright—until he demonstrated his command over <b>Ode Math</b>, warping reality itself to prove he was serious. This feat <b>shook</b> the Regent Council, earning him their <b>respect</b>. Consequently, he was granted the necessary <b>documents</b> to initiate his Dominion.
</p><p>Upon returning to his Dominion, Eyulis ordered the <b>creation of a machine</b> to act as a <b>Watcher</b>. He then set out in search of a particular individual: <b>Beilious</b>. Beilious had a fearsome reputation—he was <b>Unmarked</b>, and he <b>preferred it that way</b>. He had <b>single-handedly destroyed entire Dominions</b>, slaughtering hundreds and <b>wiping out fledgling factions</b> before they could even take root. It was because of such threats that the Regent Council <b>now requires all aspiring Dominion Regents</b> to <b>prove their strength</b>—to ensure that no upstart could rise unchecked and bring about such destruction again.
</p><p>At first, Beilious <b>refused Eyulis outright</b>, radiating a <b>killing intent</b> toward him. Eyulis, unfazed, <b>sent it right back</b>. He possessed a <b>rare ability</b>—the power to <b>create and execute complex formulas</b> within his mind, an ability possessed by a mere <b>0.5% of the population</b>. With this, he <b>manipulated the very environment</b>, <b>enhancing himself beyond normal limits</b>. Beilious, never having faced <b>such absolute control</b>, found himself <b>overwhelmed and powerless</b>. In the end, he had no choice but to <b>submit</b>.
</p><p>
With Beilious at his side as his <b>Ambassador</b>, Eyulis <b>returned to the Regent Council</b> once more. This time, he was <b>granted the official title and territory of Dominion Regent</b>, recognized as <b>Lord Eyulis of the Doalith Dominion</b>.</p></blockquote>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Keeper-Ceaser">Keeper-Ceaser</span></h3>
<blockquote><p>Caesar was a machine created by Dr. Gran for Eyulis, designed to <b>observe, measure, and maintain</b> the well-being of all creatures within the <b>Doalith Dominion</b>.
</p><p>Caesar is a <b>Velesti</b>, a being with the extraordinary ability to exist <b>everywhere and nowhere at the same time</b>. This unparalleled intelligence reflects the sheer brilliance of the <b>Doalith</b>, a people renowned for their <b>intellectual prowess and technological mastery</b>.
</p><p>
As one of their greatest creations, Caesar's purpose is to <b>document, analyze, and monitor</b> every creature within—and claimed by—the <b>Doalith Dominion</b>.</p></blockquote>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Ambassador">Ambassador</span></h3>
<blockquote><p>Beilious Grantania was a formidable individual who grew up on <b>Gaia</b>, where he faced relentless segregation. Born as a <b>Hyomen</b>, a lower strain of humanity, he was despised by his <b>Omenity</b> counterparts—including his own parents, who, despite being Omenties themselves, resented him for existing as something lesser.
</p><p>From an early age, Beilious endured <b>daily beatings and hatred</b>, but rather than breaking under the weight of oppression, he grew stronger—<b>unimaginably so</b>. His strength seemed to increase in direct proportion to the <b>fear, hatred, and contempt</b> directed at him. Over time, he reached a breaking point.
</p><p>His <b>first kill</b> was a defining moment. After <b>piercing his victim’s chest with his bare hand</b>, he <b>licked their blood</b> from his fingers—and in doing so, he absorbed their <b>abilities and power</b>. From that moment, Beilious became a force of nature, slaughtering <b>thousands</b> who dared to stand before him.
</p><p>Eventually, he was captured and sentenced to death by the <b>Rency Council</b>, yet before the execution could be carried out, they uncovered the truth—the system itself had shaped him into what he had become. Every individual he had slain had possessed <b>a criminal record</b>, each death <b>justified</b>. Acknowledging this, the council <b>pardoned</b> him and instead gave him <b>a purpose</b>.
</p><p>Beilious was appointed an <b>Executioner</b>, granted the right to <b>eliminate criminals and those with bounties</b>. He was <b>allowed to commit sanctioned crimes</b> in service of justice, earning him the chilling title: <b>Beilious the Haunting</b>.
</p><p>
It was then that <b>Eyulis</b> found him. Recognizing his power, Eyulis recruited Beilious as the <b>Ambassador of the Doalith Dominion</b>, securing one of the most <b>lethal and feared beings</b> in existence as his second-in-command.</p></blockquote>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Valerian_Dominion">Valerian Dominion</span></h2>

<p><b>Valerian Dominion</b>, the Noble Conquerors, is a faction known for their militaristic prowess and unwavering pursuit of territorial expansion. They are a formidable force, driven by an insatiable hunger for power and control. With their vast armies, strategic brilliance, and unyielding determination, the Valerian Dominion has swiftly conquered and assimilated numerous territories into their empire. Their rule is marked by iron-fisted governance and strict adherence to hierarchy, where strength and loyalty are valued above all else.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Overview">Overview</span></h3>
<p>The Valerian Dominion is a faction that thrives on militaristic might and territorial conquest. Their armies are disciplined, well-trained, and equipped with advanced weaponry and armor. They employ relentless strategies, superior logistics, and tactical expertise to overpower their adversaries. The faction's leaders are known for their charisma and ability to inspire loyalty, while their subjects are fiercely loyal and willing to lay down their lives for the empire.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Abilities">Abilities</span></h3>
<p><b>Battle Mastery</b>: Valerian Dominion soldiers are highly skilled in combat, trained in a variety of weaponry and combat techniques.
</p><p><b>Imperial Discipline</b>: The Valerian Dominion places great emphasis on discipline and obedience, creating a highly efficient and cohesive military force.
</p><p><b>Siege Specialists</b>: The faction excels in siege warfare, utilizing advanced siege engines and tactics to breach enemy fortifications.
</p><p><b>Commander's Presence</b>: Valerian leaders possess a natural aura of authority and inspire great loyalty and obedience in their troops.
</p><p><b>Territorial Domination</b>: The Valerian Dominion has mastered the art of territorial expansion and assimilation, quickly annexing conquered lands into their empire.
</p><p><b>Armor and Weaponry Advancements</b>: The faction invests heavily in research and development of advanced armor and weaponry, constantly pushing the boundaries of technology.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>The Valerian Dominion's relentless pursuit of power has brought them numerous victories, expanding their empire to unprecedented proportions. They are revered for their military might, but their tyrannical rule has also earned them the enmity of those who oppose their iron-fisted governance.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Nigraldi">Nigraldi</span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a class="mw-file-description image" data-ext-link="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wonderland-of-the-enigma/images/e/ea/Nigraldid.png/revision/latest?cb=20230623071959"><img alt="Nigraldid" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wonderland-of-the-enigma/images/e/ea/Nigraldid.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230623071959" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="180" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="Nigraldid.png" data-image-key="Nigraldid.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a class="info-icon" data-wte-link="wte://rules/File:Nigraldid.png"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p><b>Nigraldi</b>, the Epitome of Horror and Grotesque, is the most abominable faction in the universe of Enigmatica. These nightmarish creatures are driven by insatiable desires for reproduction and perpetual warfare. Their actions and behaviors are characterized by twisted and depraved acts, including engaging in reproduction with their own offspring. The Nigraldi's vile nature and monstrous appearance make them a faction that instills fear and revulsion in all who encounter them.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Overview_2">Overview</span></h3>
<p>The Nigraldi faction embodies the darkest depths of horror and depravity. Their existence is marked by perpetual warfare and an insatiable drive for reproduction. They possess horrifying physical attributes and an array of twisted powers and abilities. The Nigraldi strike fear into the hearts of their enemies, spreading terror and revulsion wherever they go.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Abilities_2">Abilities</span></h3>
<p><b>Reproductive Proliferation</b>: The Nigraldi have an uncanny ability to rapidly reproduce, perpetuating their gruesome lineage through abhorrent methods that defy conventional norms.
</p><p><b>Vicious Combat Prowess</b>: The Nigraldi have honed their combat skills to a terrifying degree. They possess immense strength, razor-sharp claws, and fangs, making them formidable adversaries in battle.
</p><p><b>Regenerative Resilience</b>: The Nigraldi exhibit extraordinary regenerative abilities, rapidly healing even the most grievous wounds and regenerating lost limbs or organs.
</p><p><b>Psychological Manipulation</b>: The Nigraldi are masters of psychological warfare, instilling terror and despair in the minds of their enemies, sowing discord and debilitating their opponents' will to fight.
</p><p><b>Toxic Secretions</b>: Many Nigraldi possess glands that produce toxic substances, which they can use as deadly weapons. Their venomous saliva, corrosive spittle, or poisonous stingers inflict excruciating pain or cause fatal injuries.
</p><p><b>Horrific Metamorphosis</b>: Some Nigraldi have the ability to undergo nightmarish transformations, morphing their bodies into grotesque and monstrous forms, granting enhanced strength, agility, and monstrous physical attributes.
</p><p><b>Dark Aura</b>: The Nigraldi emit an aura of malevolence and darkness, spreading fear and unease among those nearby, weakening the resolve of their enemies and draining their life force.
</p><p><b>Consuming Dread</b>: The Nigraldi possess the ability to feed on the fear and suffering of their victims, growing stronger as they inflict terror and pain, fueling their insatiable hunger for power and dominance.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore_2">Lore</span></h3>
<p>The Nigraldi, with their horrific appearance and abhorrent practices, embody the darkest depths of depravity and malevolence. Their relentless pursuit of reproduction and their penchant for waging wars make them a force to be reckoned with. They strike fear into the hearts of all, serving as a reminder of the horrors that exist within the universe of Enigmatica.
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Valdorians">Valdorians</span></h2>

<p><b>Valdorians</b>, the War-Hungry Faction, strike fear into the hearts of all who encounter them. Known for their erratic behavior and unpredictability, they are a force to be reckoned with on the battlefield. Their combat prowess is legendary, as every action they take is driven by intrusive thoughts, both benevolent and malevolent. Some of these thoughts can be more terrifying than the dreaded Nigraldi, a fearsome enemy known for their brutality. The Valdorians trace their origins back to a hive mind known as the Vail, from which they emerged.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Overview_3">Overview</span></h3>
<p>The Valdorians, born from the Vail hive mind, are formidable warriors driven by intrusive thoughts and a connection to chaos. They employ unorthodox tactics and possess unique powers and abilities that make them a relentless force on the battlefield. Their enigmatic nature and unpredictable battle instincts make them feared and respected in equal measure.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Abilities_3">Abilities</span></h3>
<p><b>Mind Intrusion</b>: The Valdorians invade the thoughts and minds of their enemies, influencing their actions and manipulating their perceptions. They excel at psychological warfare, sowing confusion and disarray among their opponents.
</p><p><b>Chaotic Combat Style</b>: Embracing chaos, the Valdorians employ unorthodox tactics and unconventional strategies. They thrive in chaotic environments, exploiting disorder to their advantage.
</p><p><b>Enhanced Reflexes</b>: Their connection to the hive mind enhances their reflexes and reaction speed, allowing them to swiftly adapt to changing situations and counter enemy attacks with incredible precision.
</p><p><b>Hive Mind Communication</b>: Although no longer fully connected to the Vail hive mind, the Valdorians maintain residual telepathic abilities. This enables them to communicate silently and efficiently with one another, coordinating their movements and strategies flawlessly.
</p><p><b>Psychic Shielding</b>: Through their exposure to the Vail hive mind, the Valdorians have developed psychic shielding, allowing them to resist and deflect psychic attacks from their adversaries.
</p><p><b>Unpredictable Battle Instincts</b>: The Valdorians possess an innate ability to sense and anticipate their opponents' moves, making it challenging for enemies to predict their actions. Their unpredictable nature keeps their foes constantly off-balance.
</p><p><b>Fear Induction</b>: The Valdorians can project waves of intense fear, instilling terror in the hearts of their enemies. This psychological manipulation weakens their opponents' resolve, making them susceptible to further attacks.
</p><p><b>Adaptive Resilience</b>: The Valdorians have an innate ability to adapt to different combat scenarios and environments. They quickly analyze their surroundings and adjust their tactics and abilities accordingly, giving them an edge in any situation.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore_3">Lore</span></h3>
<p>The Valdorians, born from the Vail hive mind, have honed their skills and powers to become formidable warriors. Their intrusive thoughts and connection to chaos make them a relentless force on the battlefield, spreading fear and uncertainty wherever they go. They are a faction that is both feared and respected for their unrivaled combat prowess and enigmatic nature.
</p><p><br>
</p>

<p><b>Deviants</b>, inhabitants of the planet Baldora, embody a desolate and harsh environment reminiscent of Hell on Earth. Towering in stature, their imposing presence commands attention and instills fear in those who cross their path. With unique physical features and powers derived from their inhospitable planet, the Deviants are a force to be reckoned with.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Overview_4">Overview</span></h3>
<p>The Deviants from the planet Baldora inhabit a grim and hostile environment that mirrors Hell on Earth. They possess towering stature, fiery gaze, and distinct physical characteristics that set them apart from ordinary humans. Their exceptional powers, derived from the planet's hellish nature, make them formidable adversaries on the battlefield.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_Features">Physical Features</span></h3>
<p><b>Height</b>: Ranging from 6.6 to 11 feet, the Deviants' towering stature commands attention and instills fear in their adversaries.
</p><p><b>Eyes</b>: Their eyes, reminiscent of brimstone, burn with an intense fiery hue, reflecting their inner fire.
</p><p><b>Skin</b>: Resembling fresh lava, their skin radiates an otherworldly heat, distinguishing them from ordinary humans.
</p><p><b>Hair</b>: Possessing the striking color of copper and the texture of steel wool, their hair adds to their distinctive appearance.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Abilities_4">Abilities</span></h3>
<p><b>Pyrokinetic Manipulation</b>: The Deviants have exceptional mastery over fire. They can control and manipulate flames, project fire-based attacks, and withstand intense heat.
</p><p><b>Enhanced Strength and Durability</b>: Their towering stature grants them immense physical strength, allowing them to overpower opponents and endure tremendous amounts of damage.
</p><p><b>Ash Manipulation</b>: They can manipulate the swirling ash that fills the atmosphere of Baldora, using it to obscure their movements, create smokescreens, or unleash devastating ash-based attacks.
</p><p><b>Fear Induction</b>: Through their fiery gaze and intimidating presence, the Deviants can instill intense fear and terror in their enemies, weakening their resolve and rendering them vulnerable.
</p><p><b>Energy Absorption</b>: They have the ability to absorb and harness various forms of energy, including heat and electricity, using it to empower themselves or unleash devastating attacks.
</p><p><b>Resilience to Extreme Conditions</b>: Adapted to the harsh environment of Baldora, the Deviants are highly resilient to extreme temperatures, toxic atmospheres, and other hazardous conditions.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore_4">Lore</span></h3>
<p>The Deviants, inhabitants of the planet Baldora, possess unique physical features and extraordinary powers derived from their hellish environment. With their mastery over fire, ash manipulation, and imposing stature, they are a force to be reckoned with. These powers, born out of the torment and struggles they face, serve as a reminder of the harsh realities of their existence.
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Manlori_Altari">Manlori Altari</span></h2>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a class="mw-file-description image" data-ext-link="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wonderland-of-the-enigma/images/3/31/Document_1.png/revision/latest?cb=20230620220536"><img alt="Document 1" src="data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="144" class="thumbimage lazyload" data-image-name="Document 1.png" data-image-key="Document_1.png" data-relevant="1" data-src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wonderland-of-the-enigma/images/3/31/Document_1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230620220536"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a class="info-icon" data-wte-link="wte://rules/File:Document_1.png"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 	</figcaption> </figure>
<p><b>Manlori Altari</b> is a distinguished faction of individuals tracing their origins back to the lush and abundant planet Judaiah. They are known for their indomitable spirit, unyielding resolve, and remarkable resemblance to classical depictions of extraterrestrial beings.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Origins">Origins</span></h3>
<p>The Manlori Altari's journey began on Mars during the early days of interstellar exploration. The Martians, as they came to be known, embarked on a monumental mission of terraforming, transforming the once barren planet into a habitable world. During this process, they unearthed a wealth of untapped resources, leading to the birth of the Enigma Dynasty.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_Characteristics">Physical Characteristics</span></h3>
<p><b>Enlarged Heads</b>: The Manlori Altari exhibit enlarged heads, which are a distinctive physical attribute inherited from their Martian ancestors.
</p><p><b>Captivating Eyes</b>: Their large, captivating eyes further distinguish them and fascinate observers from across the universe.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Society_and_Legacy">Society and Legacy</span></h3>
<p>The Manlori Altari established a harmonious society on Mars, thriving upon the newfound abundance of resources. They became a symbol of prosperity and resilience, showcasing the potential of ordinary individuals striving for greatness. While lacking supernatural powers or extraordinary abilities, they embodied the indomitable spirit of humanity and its capacity to shape its own destiny.
</p><p>Their legacy extended beyond Mars, as the Manlori Altari ventured into the Enigma Universe, valuing knowledge, unity, and cultural exchange. They enriched the interstellar community with their unique perspectives and unwavering pursuit of progress. Their contributions became an integral part of the Engmatica lore, a testament to humanity's triumphs and trials in the cosmos.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Philosophy">Philosophy</span></h3>
<p>The Manlori Altari's philosophy revolves around the tenets of knowledge, unity, and the inherent potential of humanity. They believe that through determination and adaptability, ordinary individuals can transcend the boundaries of their existence and shape their own destiny.
</p>

<p>Origins:
</p>
<ul><li>Voltnus and the Vaulton: The Vaultinian Empire traces its roots back to Voltnus, the first Vaulton, who emerged from a Nigraldi experiment. Voltnus led the Vaulton to the water world of Boltnar after driving away the Nigraldi.</li>
<li>Technological Renaissance: On Boltnar, the Vaulton developed unparalleled technological advancements, particularly in bio-mechanical integration, laying the foundation for the Empire.</li></ul>
<p>Expansion and Influence:
</p>
<ul><li>Rise to Power: The Empire expanded its influence across Enigmatica through strategic alliances, advanced technology, and a formidable military.</li>
<li>Bio-Mechs Development: The creation of Bio-Mechs became a cornerstone of the Empire's strength, combining biological and mechanical elements to produce powerful warriors and tools.</li></ul>
