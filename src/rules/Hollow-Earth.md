<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p>Beneath Earth once existed a vast cavernous expanse — a <b>Hollow Earth</b> where gravity shifted, light bent unnaturally, and life developed in isolation.
</p><p>The first discovery occurred in <b>2132</b>, dismissed and buried.
</p><p>In <b>2407 (Present Day)</b>, governments officially confirmed its existence.
</p><p>By then, it was too late.
</p><p>The original inhabitants — <b>your people</b> — were not welcomed. They were <b>studied</b>.
</p><p>Every individual was tagged, implanted, and cataloged under a single designation:
</p><p><b>Hollowth.</b>
</p><p>Homes were seized.
</p><p>The opening was sealed.
</p><p>The people were removed.
</p><p>Hollowth were scattered across <b>Aulorn</b>, sent to distant worlds with no coordination, no preservation, and no intention of return.
</p><p>Most died.
</p><p>Others survived — but lost their customs, lineage, and memory, becoming hollow in truth as well as name.
</p><p>You are one of the few who remain.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Hollowth_Legacy">The Hollowth Legacy</span></h2>
<p>Each Hollowth originated from a <b>different region of the opening</b>.
</p><p>Because of this:
</p>
<ul><li>No two Hollowth cultures are the same</li></ul>
<ul><li>Traditions fractured instead of unifying</li></ul>
<ul><li>Customs survived only where individuals chose to remember</li></ul>
<p>With this background, you may choose to <b>follow and preserve your Hollow-Earth customs</b>.
</p><p>These customs are <b>player-created</b>.
</p><p>They may include:
</p>
<ul><li>Beliefs about gravity, direction, or space</li></ul>
<ul><li>Rituals tied to the cavern’s regions</li></ul>
<ul><li>Oral histories, markings, or taboos</li></ul>
<ul><li>Rules for survival in inverted or unstable environments</li></ul>
<p>What you remember is a choice.
</p>
