<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_String_Theorist">Archetype: <i>String Theorist</i></span></h1>
<p><i>“The world is my canvas; I’ll treat it as such.”</i>
</p><p>The <b>String Theorist</b> is a master of perception and manipulation, able to see the world as a network of strings connecting all matter and energy. By controlling these strings, they can influence the environment, affect creatures, and create complex conditions to dominate combat and narrative situations.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  <i>Excels in slow-developing scenarios, setting up advantages before taking rapid, precise actions.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  <i>Slow initial setup followed by high-impact manipulation; thrives on controlling conditions rather than brute force.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  <i>Cannot immediately act with full efficacy; setup time is required. Vulnerable to forceful, chaotic disruptions (especially elemental bursts like fire or kinetic shock).</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature Ability:</b>  <i>The Ward: manifests in the ocular nerve of the actor’s species, allowing the String Theorist to perceive and replace material objects with string-based representations, enabling manipulation.</i></li></ul>
<p><b>Playstyle:</b>
</p><p>Maintain distance while subtly manipulating minor strings in the environment and on creatures. Causes indirect afflictions and “Virtice Damage” (damage over time/conditional damage tied to string manipulation).
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetry:_String_Theorist_Action_Economy">Asymmetry: <i>String Theorist Action Economy</i></span></h1>
<p>Upon choosing <b>String Theorist</b>, your Actor gains its <b>Asymmetry</b>, defining <b>action economy</b>, <b>mechanical philosophy</b>, and <b>visualized string manipulation</b>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy">Action Economy</span></h2>
<ul><li><i><b>4 Standard Actions</b></i>  <i>Primarily used to deploy abilities, manipulate strings, or set up environmental control.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><i><b>1 True Opportunity Attack (T.O.A.)</b></i>  <i>Triggers when a target interacts with your controlled strings or violates your manipulated conditions.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><i><b>1 Free Action</b></i>  <i>Used to facilitate setup, adjust string visualization, or activate minor manipulations.</i></li></ul>
<p><b>Note:</b> Standard actions are the backbone of the String Theorist’s strategy, the free action aids in fluid control, and T.O.A ensures actions succeed even in reactive situations.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span id="Aspect:_Ward_.E2.80.93_Manifestation_.26_String_Visualization"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Ward_–_Manifestation_&amp;_String_Visualization">Aspect: <i>Ward – Manifestation &amp; String Visualization</i></span></h1>
<p>All <b>String Theorist</b> actors begin with the <b>Ward – Manifestation String Visualization</b>, representing <b>inner understanding of invisible theoretical structures</b>.
</p>
<h2><span id="String_Visualization_.E2.80.94_Base_Function"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="String_Visualization_—_Base_Function"><i>String Visualization — Base Function</i></span></h2>
<p>The String Theorist can alter ocular nerves and retinal perception to see “strings” instead of objects. Physical objects retain tactile properties, but strings overlay them for manipulation.
</p>
<ul><li>Strings <b>glow proportionally</b> to influence magnitude.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Material objects are <b>replaced visually with string-line equivalents</b>.</li></ul>
<p><b>Clarifications:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b>  <i>Requires concentration; if ocular nerves are damaged, abilities halt immediately.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b>  <i>Excessive force or overwhelming visual data can cause 3d8 Psychic Damage.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b>  <i>Deactivated or forcibly halted.</i></li></ul>
