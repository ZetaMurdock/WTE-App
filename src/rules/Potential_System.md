# Potential System

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr">

<h1><span id="Potential_System_in_.22Wonderland_of_the_Enigma.22_.28W.T.E.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Potential_System_in_&quot;Wonderland_of_the_Enigma&quot;_(W.T.E)">Potential System in "Wonderland of the Enigma" (W.T.E)</span></h1>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Overview">Overview</span></h2>
<p>The Potential system is an invisible stat for players that adds an additional layer of mystery and depth to their actions and interactions within the game world. This stat, unknown to the players, is determined by the Director and influences the outcomes of various scenarios, particularly when players interact with key NPCs or complete significant quests.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="How_the_Potential_System_Works">How the Potential System Works</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Initial_Setup">Initial Setup</span></h3>
<ol><li><b>Determining Potential:</b>
<ul><li>At the beginning of the campaign or when a new player joins, the Director secretly rolls a 1d100 for each player to determine their Potential score. This score is kept hidden from the players and can be adjusted by the Director as the story progresses.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Influence on Gameplay:</b>
<ul><li>The Potential score can affect various aspects of gameplay, including rewards, quest opportunities, NPC interactions, and unexpected boons. The higher the Potential score, the more favorable and impactful the outcomes.</li></ul></li></ol>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Activation">Activation</span></h3>
<ol><li><b>Director's Role:</b>
<ul><li>The Director monitors the players' actions and determines when to consider their Potential score. This can be during key moments such as completing a significant quest, interacting with important NPCs, or discovering hidden secrets.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Scenario Examples:</b>
<ul><li><b>Quest Completion:</b>
<ul><li>When players complete a challenging quest, the Director rolls a hidden Potential check to determine the additional rewards or opportunities. For example, instead of just receiving gold, a player with high Potential might be given a mystical sword with immense power.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>NPC Interactions:</b>
<ul><li>When players interact with a crucial NPC, the Director uses the Potential score to influence the NPC's response. A player with high Potential might receive a secret quest, valuable information, or an alliance offer.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Discovery:</b>
<ul><li>When players explore and discover hidden areas or items, the Potential score can determine the significance and rarity of what they find. High Potential might lead to discovering ancient artifacts or powerful relics.</li></ul></li></ul></li></ol>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Potential_Scenarios_and_Outcomes">Potential Scenarios and Outcomes</span></h3>
<p><b>Scenario: Turning in Boss Drops to a Merchant</b>
</p>
<ol><li><b>Low Potential (1-30):</b>
<ul><li>The merchant gives the player a standard reward (gold) and a simple thank you.</li>
<li>The merchant may offer a minor quest, such as delivering a message.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Medium Potential (31-70):</b>
<ul><li>The merchant recognizes the player's capability and offers a valuable item in addition to the gold.</li>
<li>The merchant may offer a more challenging quest with better rewards, such as recovering a stolen artifact.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>High Potential (71-100):</b>
<ul><li>The merchant sees great potential in the player and gifts them a mystical sword with powers rivaling that of the king.</li>
<li>The merchant may introduce the player to influential figures, such as the king, leading to significant story arcs and major quests.</li></ul></li></ol>
<p><b>Scenario: Meeting a Random NPC on the Street</b>
</p>
<ol><li><b>Low Potential (1-30):</b>
<ul><li>The NPC offers basic information or a small task, such as finding a lost pet.</li>
<li>The interaction is brief and uneventful.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Medium Potential (31-70):</b>
<ul><li>The NPC provides useful information, a decent reward, or a side quest with moderate difficulty.</li>
<li>The interaction may lead to discovering a hidden location or a minor subplot.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>High Potential (71-100):</b>
<ul><li>The NPC reveals themselves to be a disguised noble or a powerful mage, offering a quest of great importance or a rare item.</li>
<li>The interaction significantly impacts the main story, opening new paths and opportunities.</li></ul></li></ol>
<h3><span id="Director.27s_Guidelines"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Director's_Guidelines">Director's Guidelines</span></h3>
<ol><li><b>Balancing Potential:</b>
<ul><li>Ensure that the Potential system enhances the narrative without overshadowing player agency. Use it to add depth and surprise, not to control the story entirely.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Adjusting Potential:</b>
<ul><li>Potential scores can be adjusted based on player actions, significant achievements, or character development. For example, completing a heroic deed might increase a player's Potential score.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Keeping it Secret:</b>
<ul><li>The Potential score and its mechanics should remain hidden from the players to maintain the element of mystery and unpredictability.</li></ul></li></ol>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Conventional_Routes">Conventional Routes</span></h3>
<ul><li>Players can still obtain information and rewards through conventional methods such as asking the right questions, conducting research, and interacting with the world in creative ways. The Potential system simply provides an additional layer of opportunity and depth, allowing for unexpected and enhanced outcomes based on the unseen Potential of the players.</li></ul>
