</div><div style="background:#121212;border-left:4px solid #704828;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:18px;"><div style="font-size:1.6em;font-weight:bold;color:#c09060;letter-spacing:0.06em;font-family:monospace;">ORIYU</div><div style="color:#806050;font-size:0.85em;margin-top:4px;font-style:italic;">Group: Omenity — Energy-attuned species with intrinsic control over personal energy fields.</div></div><div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #281808;border-left:3px solid #704828;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;color:#c09060;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>Oriyu have one of the cleanest energy-ability suites in the species roster. Their four innate abilities form a coherent loop: absorb incoming energy, store it, project it outward, and shield against further incoming damage. This makes them self-reinforcing in energy-dominant engagements — the more energy directed at them, the more they can redirect.
</p><p>Their high Dominance value (40) means Oriyu energy traits reliably express in hybrid offspring. Their dual Civilized/Feral Eminence (+20 / +5) reflects functional integration into most social structures — they're trusted in institutions, but their raw energy output generates low-level institutional flags.
</p><p>No variants are currently documented.
</p>
</div>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="HYBRID_INTERACTION">HYBRID INTERACTION</span></h2>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #281808;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:16px;color:#c09060;font-size:0.9em;">
<p>Oriyu Dom 40 / Rec 10 — strong Dominance with moderate Recessiveness. Oriyu energy traits reliably carry forward in hybrid offspring, though the full ability loop (Absorb → Store → Project → Shield) may not express completely.
