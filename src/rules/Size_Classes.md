# Size Classes

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr">

<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Size_Classes">Size Classes</span></h1>
<p>Size Classes in WTE determine physical scale, combat reach, equipment capacity, movement ranges, and interaction rules between creatures of different sizes. Every species has a <b>Default Size Class</b>, though Variants and Wryde mutations can shift a creature one class up or down.
</p><p>There are <b>6 Size Classes</b>, calibrated from Minute sub-creatures to world-scale entities. The anchor class is <b>Moderate</b>, which represents most adult Hyomen and is the design baseline for all equipment and rules.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Detailed_Size_Profiles">Detailed Size Profiles</span></h2>
<h3><span id="TINY_.28Under_1_ft_.2F_Under_5_lbs.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="TINY_(Under_1_ft_/_Under_5_lbs)">TINY (Under 1 ft / Under 5 lbs)</span></h3>
<p>Tiny creatures operate on a fundamentally different scale. Not currently a default class for any PC species, but may be encountered as NPCs, companion creatures, or creatures affected by Size-reducing abilities.
</p><p><b>Combat Rules:</b>
</p>
<ul><li>Cannot be targeted by weapons not designed for their scale (Director discretion)</li>
<li>Take half damage from area-of-effect attacks (size makes them hard to hit in a splash)</li>
<li>Deal 1/4 weapon damage (scale factor)</li>
<li>Grapple against non-Tiny creatures: automatic failure unless special ability</li>
<li>Can enter spaces occupied by larger creatures without triggering threatened space rules</li></ul>
<p><b>Equipment:</b> 8 slots; all items must be Minute-scale; Minute-scale weapons deal 1d4 maximum damage regardless of type.
</p>
<h3><span id="SMALL_.281.E2.80.934_ft_.2F_5.E2.80.9360_lbs.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="SMALL_(1–4_ft_/_5–60_lbs)">SMALL (1–4 ft / 5–60 lbs)</span></h3>
<p><b>Combat Rules:</b>
</p>
<ul><li>Disadvantage on attacks against Huge or Colossal targets unless using reach weapons or special abilities</li>
<li>Advantage on attacks against Huge or larger if attacking from below or flanking</li>
<li>Grapple initiated by a Large or larger creature against a Small creature: Large has advantage; Small has disadvantage</li>
<li>Can move through spaces occupied by Moderate or larger creatures (squeeze mechanics apply)</li></ul>
<p><b>Equipment:</b> 13 slots; standard equipment can be used but must be resized (20% cost premium at vendors; Paradigm-issued equipment is auto-scaled).
</p><p><b>Environmental:</b> Access to vents, crawlspaces, and tight passages; +1 Stealth in non-open environments; +1 to grapple escape against Moderate or larger opponents.
</p>
<h3><span id="MODERATE_.284.E2.80.937_ft_.2F_60.E2.80.93350_lbs.29_.E2.80.94_BASELINE"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="MODERATE_(4–7_ft_/_60–350_lbs)_—_BASELINE">MODERATE (4–7 ft / 60–350 lbs) — BASELINE</span></h3>
<p>Moderate is the design standard. All rules, equipment sizes, and environmental assumptions default to Moderate. No bonuses or penalties apply.
</p><p><b>Equipment:</b> 20 slots (baseline); all standard equipment is Moderate-sized by default.
</p>
<h3><span id="LARGE_.287.E2.80.9312_ft_.2F_350.E2.80.931.2C500_lbs.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="LARGE_(7–12_ft_/_350–1,500_lbs)">LARGE (7–12 ft / 350–1,500 lbs)</span></h3>
<p><b>Combat Rules:</b>
</p>
<ul><li>Advantage on attacks against Small or Tiny targets</li>
<li>Reach: 10 ft (can attack creatures up to 10 ft away)</li>
<li>Occupies a 10-ft × 10-ft space (vs. 5-ft × 5-ft for Moderate)</li>
<li>+1d4 damage on melee attacks (mass advantage)</li>
<li>Area-of-effect attacks deal +1d4 against Large targets (larger surface area)</li></ul>
<p><b>Equipment:</b> 27 slots; standard equipment for Large costs 40% more; Large weapons gain +1 damage die; Large armor grants +2 DHP.
</p><p><b>Environmental:</b> Cannot access standard vents without squeeze rules; Squeeze at half speed through Moderate-sized spaces; −1 to Stealth in open environments.
</p>
<h3><span id="HUGE_.2812.E2.80.9325_ft_.2F_1.2C500.E2.80.9315.2C000_lbs.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="HUGE_(12–25_ft_/_1,500–15,000_lbs)">HUGE (12–25 ft / 1,500–15,000 lbs)</span></h3>
<p>Huge creatures are rare as PCs and primarily encountered as major NPCs or apex creatures.
</p><p><b>Combat Rules:</b>
</p>
<ul><li>Advantage against any target Moderate or smaller</li>
<li>Reach: 15 ft; occupies 15-ft × 15-ft space</li>
<li>+2d6 damage on melee attacks</li>
<li>Stomp action: target in same or adjacent space makes Dexterity Save (DC = 10 + Strength Modifier) or takes 2d8 crushing damage</li>
<li>Moderate creatures and smaller cannot Parry a Huge creature's melee attacks — only Avoid or Endure</li></ul>
<p><b>Equipment:</b> 35 slots; most standard equipment non-functional — Huge-scale fabrication required.
</p>
<h3><span id="COLOSSAL_.2825.2B_ft_.2F_15.2C000.2B_lbs.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="COLOSSAL_(25+_ft_/_15,000+_lbs)">COLOSSAL (25+ ft / 15,000+ lbs)</span></h3>
<p>Colossal creatures are essentially environmental features — faction-level threats or ancient entities. No PC species defaults here.
</p><p><b>Scale Tiers:</b>
</p>
<ul><li>Colossal I: 25–50 ft / 15,000–100,000 lbs</li>
<li>Colossal II: 50–100 ft / 100,000–500,000 lbs</li>
<li>Colossal III: 100+ ft (effectively geological)</li></ul>
<p>Standard combat mechanics break down at Colossal scale. Encounters with Colossal entities are Director-designed phase events, not standard combat rounds.
</p>
