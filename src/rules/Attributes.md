# Attributes

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;padding:28px 32px;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;font-family:'Segoe UI',sans-serif;color:#dedede;max-width:960px;margin:0 auto;"><div style="font-family:monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:3px;color:#4e7fa5;margin-bottom:22px;">
<p><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Wonderland_of_The_Enigma_Wiki">WTE WIKI</a> · <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Character_Creation">CHARACTER CREATION</a> · ATTRIBUTES
</p>
</div><div style="font-size:26px;font-weight:700;color:#7ab2d8;letter-spacing:1px;border-bottom:2px solid #4e7fa5;padding-bottom:12px;margin-bottom:24px;">
<p>ATTRIBUTES
</p>
</div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:4px solid #4e7fa5;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:24px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">OVERVIEW</div><b>Attributes</b> are the seven foundational statistics of every Inquisitor in WTE. Rolled at character creation (<b>1d20+5 each, capped at 20</b>), they represent innate physical and cognitive capacity. Attributes feed directly into <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Derived_Statistics">Derived Statistics</a> and contribute <b>Attribute Modifiers</b> to the <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Pressure_Engine">AAV formula</a>.
Each Attribute has a <b>threshold of 15</b>. Above 15, it contributes positively to the AAV roll. Below 15, it drags the roll down.<div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:12px 16px;font-family:monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#c09a30;">
<p>If Attribute ≥ 15: Modifier = Attribute − 15 (positive)
</p><p>If Attribute &lt; 15: Modifier = −(15 − Attribute) (negative)
</p><p>If Attribute = 15: Modifier = 0 (neutral)
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:24px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:16px;">THE 7 CORE ATTRIBUTES</div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:3px solid #a03535;padding:12px 16px;margin-bottom:10px;"><div style="font-size:12px;font-weight:700;color:#a03535;letter-spacing:1.5px;margin-bottom:6px;">STRENGTH</div>Raw physical might. Governs melee power, load capacity, and checks involving lifting, breaking, or forcing.<div style="font-size:12px;color:#909090;line-height:1.7;">
<p><span style="color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;">Feeds into:</span> Attack Power (derived stat), grapple checks, Concussive Saves
</p><p><span style="color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;">Key interaction:</span> Strength is pure offensive upside — no mechanical penalty to any derived stat.
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:3px solid #3a8050;padding:12px 16px;margin-bottom:10px;"><div style="font-size:12px;font-weight:700;color:#3a8050;letter-spacing:1.5px;margin-bottom:6px;">DEXTERITY</div>Agility, coordination, and speed. Governs dodging, precision movement, and fine physical tasks.<div style="font-size:12px;color:#909090;line-height:1.7;">
<p><span style="color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;">Feeds into:</span> Movement, Evasion, Action Density
</p><p><span style="color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;">Key interaction:</span> High Dexterity conflicts with Weight investments through the Movement reduction formula. You cannot fully maximize both.
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:3px solid #6e50a0;padding:12px 16px;margin-bottom:10px;"><div style="font-size:12px;font-weight:700;color:#6e50a0;letter-spacing:1.5px;margin-bottom:6px;">WISDOM</div>Perception, intuition, and arcane/dimensional sense. The awareness attribute — reading the world as it is, not calculating what it will become.<div style="font-size:12px;color:#909090;line-height:1.7;">
<p><span style="color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;">Feeds into:</span> Perception Range, Neuronal Capacity
</p><p><span style="color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;">Social use:</span> Governs insight checks, reading intent, and resistance to mental manipulation. Distinct from Intelligence (analysis).
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:3px solid #c09a30;padding:12px 16px;margin-bottom:10px;"><div style="font-size:12px;font-weight:700;color:#c09a30;letter-spacing:1.5px;margin-bottom:6px;">ENDURANCE</div>Physical durability, stamina, and resilience. How long the body can sustain punishment and maintain function.<div style="font-size:12px;color:#909090;line-height:1.7;">
<p><span style="color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;">Feeds into:</span> DHP, Recovery Rate
</p><p><span style="color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;">Key saves:</span> Endurance Saves are the most common defensive roll against physical and environmental hazards.
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:3px solid #7ab2d8;padding:12px 16px;margin-bottom:10px;"><div style="font-size:12px;font-weight:700;color:#7ab2d8;letter-spacing:1.5px;margin-bottom:6px;">CHARISMA</div>Social presence, command authority, and persuasion capacity. The public-facing attribute.<div style="font-size:12px;color:#909090;line-height:1.7;">
<p><span style="color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;">Feeds into:</span> Influence (derived stat)
</p><p><span style="color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;">Paradigm interaction:</span> Governs the Authority Score. Critical for Cognition and Warfare Paradigm social operations.
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:3px solid #4e7fa5;padding:12px 16px;margin-bottom:10px;"><div style="font-size:12px;font-weight:700;color:#4e7fa5;letter-spacing:1.5px;margin-bottom:6px;">INTELLIGENCE</div>Cognitive processing, analytical capacity, and learning speed. The mind's raw computational power — distinct from Wisdom (awareness) and Mental Fortitude (psionic endurance).<div style="font-size:12px;color:#909090;line-height:1.7;">
<p><span style="color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;">Feeds into:</span> Synaptic Space, Cipher Slots, Genus Slot Count
</p><p><span style="color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;">Design note:</span> Wisdom = reading what <i>is</i>. Intelligence = calculating what <i>will be</i>. They are separate axes.
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:3px solid #c09a30;padding:12px 16px;"><div style="font-size:12px;font-weight:700;color:#c09a30;letter-spacing:1.5px;margin-bottom:6px;">ACTION PRIORITY</div>Speed of response, reaction window, and situational awareness in combat. Replaces traditional initiative systems.<div style="font-size:12px;color:#909090;line-height:1.7;">
<p><span style="color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;">Feeds into:</span> Action Density, Movement
</p><p><span style="color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;">Mechanic:</span> AP is not rolled for initiative — it <i>is</i> the initiative value. Higher AP acts first every round. Ties broken by Priority Specialty, then Director call.
</p>

</div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:24px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:12px;">THE 12 SPECIALTIES</div>Specialties are trained skills distributed from a <b>75-point pool</b> at character creation. </p>

</div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:4px solid #a03535;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:24px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#a03535;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">TAX BURDEN</div>Four Specialties generate Tax Burden: <b>Weight, Precision, Control, and Weapon Mastery</b>. Tax Burden reduces your Complexity Modifier — the core driver of creative AAV ceiling.<div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:12px 16px;font-family:monospace;font-size:12.5px;color:#c09a30;margin-bottom:12px;">
<p>Tax Burden = Σ floor(each reducing specialty ÷ 3)
</p><p>Complexity Modifier = Inspiration − Tax Burden
</p>
</div>Example: Weight 30 + Weapon Mastery 24 → Tax Burden = 10 + 8 = 18. If Inspiration = 15, Complexity Modifier = −3. High AAV must be earned through creative play, not stat accumulation alone.
</div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:24px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:16px;">BUILD ARCHETYPES</div>These are not classes — they are optimization patterns that emerge naturally from the specialty system.<div style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr;gap:12px;"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:2px solid #a03535;padding:12px 14px;"><div style="font-size:11px;font-weight:700;color:#a03535;letter-spacing:1.5px;margin-bottom:6px;">KINETIC</div><div style="font-size:11.5px;color:#909090;line-height:1.65;">Weight + Weapon Mastery. High DHP and raw Attack Power. Heavy Tax Burden. Needs strong Inspiration to stay positive on Complexity. Pair with Endurance + Strength.</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:2px solid #3a8050;padding:12px 14px;"><div style="font-size:11px;font-weight:700;color:#3a8050;letter-spacing:1.5px;margin-bottom:6px;">GHOST</div><div style="font-size:11.5px;color:#909090;line-height:1.65;">Precision + Cunning + Dexterity. High Evasion, Action Density, Perception Range. Low Tax Burden. Reduced raw Attack Power. Excellent AAV on creative low-element approaches.</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:2px solid #6e50a0;padding:12px 14px;"><div style="font-size:11px;font-weight:700;color:#6e50a0;letter-spacing:1.5px;margin-bottom:6px;">PSIONIC</div><div style="font-size:11.5px;color:#909090;line-height:1.65;">Mental Fortitude + Control + Adaption. Massive Synaptic Space. Moderate Tax from Control. Reduced Evasion and Action Density — compensate with Dexterity or Cunning.</div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:2px solid #4e7fa5;padding:12px 14px;"><div style="font-size:11px;font-weight:700;color:#4e7fa5;letter-spacing:1.5px;margin-bottom:6px;">ANALYTICAL</div><div style="font-size:11.5px;color:#909090;line-height:1.65;">Intelligence + Versatility + Adaption. High Cipher Slot count and Genus Slot capacity. Low Tax Burden. Pairs well with any Paradigm relying on active ability management.</div></div></div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:24px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:12px;">SEE ALSO</div><div style="font-size:12.5px;color:#7ab2d8;line-height:1.9;">
