<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>The Droids built the Mundus Machines -- world-scale constructs of enormous engineering complexity. They are actively feared by the Nigraldi, one of few species that registers as a systemic threat to that power structure rather than a manageable population. Their removal of parasitic ancestry was not a natural evolution -- it was a designed choice. They looked at what they came from and decided to be something else entirely. That deliberateness defines Droid culture: they are perhaps the most intentional species in the setting. Everything about their existence is a choice. Whether that makes them freer or more constrained depends on who you ask.
</p>
