<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Zen_Practioner">The Zen Practioner</span></h2>
<p>Muen The Silent was a girl who never seemed to connect with others. She kept to herself, always immersed in books and rarely leaving the safety of her home. Her days were spent reading, until one day, she stumbled upon a book that revealed something from long ago—an ancient practice from the 20th century. The book described movements designed to alleviate stress and banish negative emotions and spirits from the body.
</p><p>Curious, Muen began practicing the techniques. She became so skilled that she could hear and feel her own heartbeat, something that fascinated her. Eventually, one day, someone saw her practicing. A boy began picking on her, as he usually did, but instead of retreating as she typically would, she found herself unable to let his negativity linger. Instinctively, she zoned out, focusing on the rhythmic beat of her heart.
</p><p>Without thinking, she thrust her fist forward, sending a punch straight to his center mass. The shockwave from her heartbeat reverberated through his body, leaving him incapacitated and sending him into a coma, a state that could last indefinitely. Terrified by what she had just done, Muen fled before anyone could find her. The realization of what had happened—and what she was capable of—left her shaken. She feared she might be one of those rare individuals who accidentally developed an archetype. Stories said that these people rarely lived long lives, as they were either exploited for their powers or hunted by the government.For years, Muen hid her abilities, growing stronger in secret. But eventually, she was forced to use her power again, this time to save a young girl in danger. In doing so, she began to teach the girl the same techniques that had once been her solace. And thus, the cycle continued.
</p>
