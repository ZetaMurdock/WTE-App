<p>The Director (also called the <b>Curator</b>) is the architect of pressure, not the opponent of the players. The Director does not win or lose. The Director ensures that the world responds with appropriate stakes to whatever the Inquisitors do.
</p>

</div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#6e50a0;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ High Points and Low Points</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;margin-bottom:12px;line-height:1.7;">High Points and Low Points are deliberate structural beats placed by the Director — moments of earned triumph or forced confrontation that shift the emotional register of the campaign. Use them intentionally, not reactively.</div>
<table style="width:100%;border-collapse:separate;border-spacing:8px;">
<tbody><tr>
<td style="width:50%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:2px solid #3a8050;border-radius:5px;padding:14px 16px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#3a8050;margin-bottom:8px;font-weight:700;">High Points — When to Use</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.8;">
<ul><li>After a sequence of meaningful player victories — earned, not consolation</li>
<li>When a long-held mystery resolves and the payoff should land fully</li>
<li>When a character breakthrough (Incept expression, Reclassification) deserves a narrative beat</li>
<li>To refresh player energy before a hard arc stretch</li></ul>
</div>
</td>
<td style="width:50%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:2px solid #a03535;border-radius:5px;padding:14px 16px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#a03535;margin-bottom:8px;font-weight:700;">Low Points — When to Use</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.8;">
<ul><li>When the party has been winning cleanly — real stakes require real cost</li>
<li>When an NPC death, capture, or betrayal needs weight</li>
<li>When the institutional system closes around the Inquisitors</li>
<li>Never use Low Points as punishment — they are structural pressure, not Director retaliation</li></ul>
</div>
</td></tr></tbody></table>
</div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ Reclassification Events</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;margin-bottom:12px;line-height:1.7;">A Reclassification Event marks a structural shift in the campaign — a faction changes alignment, a Paradigm collapses, a major NPC is removed, or the political context the party has been operating in is fundamentally altered. Prepare before running one:</div>
<table style="width:100%;border-collapse:separate;border-spacing:6px;">
<tbody><tr>
<td style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:2px solid #4e7fa5;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;padding:10px 14px;vertical-align:top;font-size:12px;"><span style="color:#4e7fa5;">01.</span> <span style="color:#909090;">Define what changes — be specific about what the world looks like before and after</span>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:2px solid #4e7fa5;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;padding:10px 14px;vertical-align:top;font-size:12px;"><span style="color:#4e7fa5;">02.</span> <span style="color:#909090;">Identify which NPCs are affected and in what direction — not all changes harm the party</span>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:2px solid #4e7fa5;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;padding:10px 14px;vertical-align:top;font-size:12px;"><span style="color:#4e7fa5;">03.</span> <span style="color:#909090;">Check player characters' active relationships — a Reclassification that touches none of them is flavor, not stakes</span>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:2px solid #4e7fa5;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;padding:10px 14px;vertical-align:top;font-size:12px;"><span style="color:#4e7fa5;">04.</span> <span style="color:#909090;">Seed the event two to three sessions before it hits — players should feel they might have been able to prevent it, even if they couldn't</span>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:2px solid #4e7fa5;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;padding:10px 14px;vertical-align:top;font-size:12px;"><span style="color:#4e7fa5;">05.</span> <span style="color:#909090;">After the event: document the new faction/world state in your session notes. Reclassifications are permanent.</span>
</td></tr></tbody></table>
</div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ Background Active Triggers</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;margin-bottom:12px;line-height:1.7;">Player Backgrounds carry narrative conditions that, when encountered in play, can activate additional mechanical or story effects. The Director should review all party Backgrounds before each session and identify whether the planned material might contact any of them.</div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #262626;border-radius:4px;padding:10px 14px;font-size:12px;color:#565656;">
<p><b>Examples:</b> A player with the Null Background encounters a Keno artifact directly — trigger the Background's weakness rules. A player with Genesis Collapse origin encounters evidence of the Collapse event — this is a High Point candidate. A player with The Derived Background meets another Derived individual — their identity conflict becomes active.
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ Narrative Mechanics Integration</div><div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:10px;">
<p>A WTE session is a <b>sequence of encounters</b> connected by a Director-maintained world state. Between encounters, the Director advances ongoing threads — faction positions shift, NPC relationships develop, the institutional network logs what just happened. This is not optional flavor: the world changes because of what Inquisitors do, and the Director is responsible for making those changes visible.
</p>
</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.8;">
<p>Each session should end with the Director privately noting: what changed this session, what threads are now closer to a Reclassification, which EAC counts increased, and whether any Backgrounds were triggered. That record becomes the foundation of the next session's opening state.
