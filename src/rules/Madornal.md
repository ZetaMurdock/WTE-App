<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Madornal">Archetype: <i>Madornal</i></span></h1>
<p><i>Through Forceful Transformation comes Advancement in Creation</i>
</p><p><i>Madornal is the archetype of extreme control and biological manipulation. It operates on the strangest levels of existence, being responsible for the transformation of flesh and organic life—and the conversion of inorganic matter into living flesh—thereby allowing complete manipulation of anything it touches.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  <i>Transforming the environment to gain additional actions. The more flesh present in an area, the more influence and control the Madornal exerts, becoming a living infestation that spreads and reshapes the battlefield.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  <i>Madornal is a Support/Manipulation-focused archetype. While capable of dealing damage, it primarily remains in the backline, manipulating enemies, controlling terrain, and providing biological healing or debilitation.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  <i>Madornal’s greatest weakness is its strongest offensive output: Pathogenic damage. This damage type is highly effective against other Madornal and similar organic-based entities.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  <i>Vitalis is the defining Aspect of the Madornal, manifesting as a living Organic Heart. This heart may be implanted into any object, converting it into organic matter. Once transformed, the object remains organic even if the Vitalis is removed.</i></li></ul>
<p><i>The Madornal playstyle relies heavily on allies to keep enemies at bay while it prepares the battlefield. By seeding flesh throughout the environment, it compensates for its limited mobility and capitalizes on sustained engagements.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetry:_Madornal_Action_Economy">Asymmetry: <i>Madornal Action Economy</i></span></h1>
<p><i>Upon choosing <b>Madornal</b>, your Actor gains its <b>Asymmetry</b>, defining its unique <b>Action Economy</b>, <b>mechanical philosophy</b>, and grotesque, biological manipulation.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy">Action Economy</span></h2>
<ul><li><b><i>5</i> Standard Action(s)</b>  <i>Primarily used for flesh manipulation, environmental transformation, healing, infection, and control-based abilities.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>0</i> True Opportunity Attack(s)</b>  <i>Madornal does not react instinctively; its power comes from preparation rather than sudden retaliation.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>2</i> Free Action(s)</b>  <i>Used to maintain flesh constructs, reposition organic growths, or sustain ongoing biological effects.</i></li></ul>
<p><i>Madornal trades reactive combat potential for overwhelming control through preparation and environmental dominance.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Vitalis">Aspect: <i>Vitalis</i></span></h1>
<p><i>All <b>Madornal</b> Actors begin with the <b>Vitalis</b>, representing the living core of biological authority and forced evolution.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function"><i>Base Function</i></span></h2>
<p><i>Madornal, the seeker and stitcher of flesh, gains access to <b>Vitalis</b>, a sentient organic heart. When manifested, Vitalis demands a suitable sacrifice at the beginning of each day. If this requirement is not met, Vitalis will not submit to the user.</i>
</p><p><i>Upon successful activation, Vitalis grants the Madornal an additional Free Action. If this action is not used, it may be stored, stacking up to <b>three</b> stored actions. Upon reaching three stored actions, the Madornal gains <b>one True Opportunity Action</b>.</i>
</p><p><i>If the True Opportunity Action is not used within three turns, stored actions reset back to three Free Actions.</i>
</p><p><i>Anything within <b>10 feet</b> of Vitalis must make an <b>Endurance + Adaptation</b> save against the Madornal’s DC. On a failure, all organic matter within range becomes manipulable—capable of being stretched, infected, grown, ruptured, or weaponized through organic means.</i>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b> <i>All external, non-biological forces</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b> <i>Pathogenic Damage</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b> <i>The incapacitation of the user</i></li></ul>
