# Warfare

> "War is here. War is coming. We will be prepared."

The Warfare Paradigm embodies strength, order, and strategy. In a fractured world torn by endless conflict, its members endure as living weapons and commanders of structure. They believe peace is merely the space between wars — and that survival belongs to those who prepare without hesitation. Victory through mastery: conflict is inevitable and eternal, and only the disciplined and ready can shape its outcome.

## Paradigm Identity

**Who Joins** — Soldiers, tacticians, strategists, veterans, or engineers drawn from every field of combat. Many are driven by loyalty, structure, or necessity, finding purpose in the order of militarization.

**Culture** — Resolute, honorable, and relentless. Warfare members respect hierarchy, efficiency, and calculated action. Emotion is secondary to execution; victory, its own justification. War is not chaos — it's a system. "Discipline wins battles. Unity wins wars."

**Archetype Feel** — Fighter / Barbarian. The Warfare Inquisitor is the axis around which every battle turns — not just a combatant but a force multiplier who turns a skirmish into an operation.

## Roll Axis Profile

Warfare occupies 5 of the 7 universal Roll Paths. Its native mechanics emphasize direct physical force, compressed execution, bodily avoidance, physical endurance, and tactical cognitive operation. Warfare Ciphers do not natively specialize in Perception or Influence, though Warfare Inquisitors may still make those rolls normally whenever the situation calls for them.

| Roll Path | Native | Direction | Warfare Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power | ● | Check | Strike, overpower, breach defenses, weaponize objects, and impose force through disciplined combat technique. |
| Density | ● | Check | Compress weapon transitions, repositioning, coordinated attacks, reloads, formation changes, and other physical operations into narrow tactical windows. |
| Evasion | ● | Save | Avoid attacks, reposition under fire, break dangerous lines of engagement, and preserve battlefield positioning. |
| Recovery | ● | Save | Endure trauma, exhaustion, suppression, hostile environments, prolonged engagements, and physical battlefield consequences. |
| Capacity | ● | Check | Issue operational commands, coordinate units, maintain tactical plans, seize control of systems, process battlefield states, and execute structured military doctrine. |
| Perception | — | Check / Save | Not native to Warfare mechanics; investigation and resistance to deception remain universally available. |
| Influence | — | Check / Save | Not native to Warfare mechanics; persuasion, intimidation, social pressure, and resistance to coercion remain universally available. |

**COMMAND IS CAPACITY** — Warfare command effects are treated as tactical cognitive execution, not ordinary social persuasion. When a Warfare Cipher attempts to impose an order, seize operational control, or enforce battlefield authority, it normally uses a Capacity Check. A resisting creature may defend itself through its natural Influence Save when the effect attempts to override intent. This preserves Warfare as a doctrine of command structure rather than making it a second Influence-focused Paradigm.

**PROFILE STANDARD** — Warfare's Roll Profile describes the Paths its native Paradigm mechanics are built around. It does not prevent a Warfare Inquisitor from making Perception or Influence rolls during ordinary play, and it does not overwrite the natural Roll Path of weapons, Genera, copied abilities, environmental effects, or other external systems.

## Warfare Paradigm Affinity

Warfare does not favor every statistic contained inside its Roll Profile. Its Affinities represent the capabilities military doctrine trains to exceptional reliability: force production, weapon mastery, action compression, and precise execution under battlefield pressure.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Favored Attributes | Strength · Action Priority |
| Favored Specialties | Weapon Mastery · Precision |

Strength represents the force behind trained aggression. Action Priority represents the ability to execute meaningful combat operations inside limited tactical windows. Weapon Mastery reinforces trained force application through armaments. Precision reinforces the exact timing and coordination necessary for dense combat execution.

| Designation Rank | Favored Attribute | Favored Specialty |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | +1d5 | +1d10 |
| 3–5 | +2d5 | +2d10 |
| 6–8 | +3d5 | +3d10 |
| 9 | +4d5 | +4d10 |

**SIGNATURE CONVERGENCE · POWER** — Strength + Weapon Mastery creates Warfare's primary offensive Convergence. When both legitimately govern a Power Check, Warfare receives both the Favored Attribute and Favored Specialty Affinity pools. This represents disciplined application of force through trained armament: strikes, breaches, weaponized objects, heavy attacks, close-quarters domination, and other direct physical impositions.

**SIGNATURE CONVERGENCE · DENSITY** — Action Priority + Precision creates Warfare's primary execution Convergence. When both legitimately govern a Density Check, Warfare receives both Affinity pools. This represents compressing coordinated attacks, weapon swaps, reloads, movement, counters, formation changes, and other precise physical operations into a single tactical opportunity.

**WHAT WARFARE DOES NOT FAVOR** — Warfare has native access to Evasion, Recovery, and Capacity, but it does not automatically gain Affinity on Dexterity, Balance, Endurance, Adaptation, Wisdom, or Mental Fortitude. These Paths remain part of Warfare's mechanical vocabulary without being institutional areas of exceptional training.

## Authority Profile

Warfare Inquisitors gain special privileges within military command centers, armories, and government installations. Once an Inquisitor's Authority Rank and Designation Rank both surpass 3, they may override security levels, issue operational commands, and temporarily assume control of lower-ranked personnel or systems.

**Advancement** — Authority increases through mission success, leadership efficiency, and tactical performance. Each completed military or strategic operation contributes to rank elevation. Tactical decisions that conserve resources or secure objectives amplify recognition within the paradigm's hierarchy.

## Authority Ranks · Designation Progression

| Rank | Title | Role | Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Private | Entry-level soldier gaining basic combat field experience | Access to standard weapons and training facilities |
| 2 | Corporal | Field operative leading small squads or support teams | Authorization to conduct independent combat maneuvers |
| 3 | Captain | Tactical commander or division leader | Can issue field orders, requisition military gear, and command operations |
| 4 | Specialist / Director | High-authority commander choosing a specialization path | Controls advanced systems, heavy equipment, and full combat divisions |

### Rank 4 Specialization Fields

**Infantry** — Frontline strategy, formation combat, and small-unit command. Commonly emphasizes Power for offensive force, Density for coordinated execution, and Capacity for tactical command.

**Engineering** — Siege design, fortification, and resource optimization. Commonly engages Capacity for planning and systems operation, while battlefield deployment may engage Density when rapid coordinated execution matters.

**Cybersecurity** — Information warfare and digital combat networks. Most active network warfare resolves through Capacity when operating, seizing, or coordinating systems; Perception remains available when the task is specifically investigative.

## Combat Specialty

Warfare Inquisitors are typically frontline combatants or tactical commanders. They represent physical strength, leadership, and unyielding focus — capable of adapting between offensive and defensive postures depending on field specialization. Their native mechanics most strongly reward Power, Density, and Capacity, supported by Evasion and Recovery.

**Style** — Commanding, disciplined, and forceful. Their fighting style emphasizes overwhelming strategy, controlled aggression, and precision through structure.

## Specialties · Genera Domains

**Combat Training** — Focused on advanced weapons mastery and unit coordination. Direct armament use most strongly reinforces Power, while coordinated multi-step combat execution most strongly reinforces Density.

**Neutral Energy** — Balances physical and energy combat tactics; adapts armaments for dynamic battlefield conditions. Depending on application, these effects commonly interact with Power, Density, Evasion, or Recovery.

**Photonic Energy** — Channels raw force and momentum. These effects commonly interact with Power when imposing force, Density when controlling recoil or chained actions, and Recovery when enduring physical battlefield stress.

## Interactions & Philosophy

To the Warfare Paradigm, war is not chaos — it's a system. Every choice, every command, every casualty forms a pattern that can be studied, repeated, and perfected. They define justice through dominance and believe power, when wielded through strategy, ensures order in a collapsing world.

**Allies** — Remnant (joint combat operations), Science (equipment contracts and supply lines), Evolution (augmented soldier programs)

**Tensions** — Simulation (fluid, manipulative methods challenge the Warfare ideal of direct confrontation), Cognition (information control vs. direct command)
