<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>Xeno are what Stygians become when they stop treating the Shadow Plane as a resource and start treating it as home. The process is not intentional. No Stygian decides to become partially extradimensional. It happens over time, through repeated and deep shadow-integration, until the boundary between the Stygian and the shadow they inhabit becomes more concept than fact. Xeno communities exist in permanent liminal space -- settlements built across both planes simultaneously, structures that are partially present in normal space and partially visible only through shadow-sensitive perception. Outsiders find Xeno settlements deeply disorienting. Xeno find it difficult to explain why.
</p>
