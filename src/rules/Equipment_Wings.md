<div style="font-size:1.6em;font-weight:bold;color:#7fe9ff;letter-spacing:0.06em;font-family:monospace;">
<p>WING AUGMENTATIONS
</p>
</div>
<div style="color:#78bfd0;font-size:0.85em;margin-top:4px;font-style:italic;">
<p>Specialized aerial equipment engineered exclusively for Seraph wing arrays. These augmentations enhance flight, maneuverability and airborne combat capabilities.
</p>
</div>
</div>
<div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #101828;border-left:3px solid #30b8d8;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;color:#b8f2ff;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>Wing Equipment is exclusive to species possessing dedicated wing anatomy. Unlike conventional equipment, Wing Augmentations occupy a completely independent <b>5-slot Wing Allocation</b> and <b>do not consume normal equipment slots</b>. Most augmentations enhance aerial mobility, evasive maneuvers and airborne combat, while advanced systems integrate directly with Genus-based flight protocols.
</p>
<div style="background:#101828;border-left:2px solid #30b8d8;padding:8px 12px;margin-top:10px;color:#8ad5e6;font-size:0.88em;">
<p><b>Wing Slot Rule:</b> Wing equipment is equipped separately from body equipment. Operators may equip up to five Wing Augmentations simultaneously without affecting standard equipment capacity.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="WING_EQUIPMENT_NOTES">WING EQUIPMENT NOTES</span></h2>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #101828;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;">
<div style="background:#1a1a1a;border-left:3px solid #30b8d8;padding:10px 14px;color:#a5e9f7;font-size:0.88em;">
<p><b>Separate Equipment Pool:</b> Wing Augmentations never occupy standard body equipment slots. They instead utilize the dedicated five-slot Wing Allocation available to winged species.
</p><p><br><br>
</p><p><b>Flight Synergy:</b> Most Wing Equipment activates only while airborne or during aerial movement. Grounded operators may not gain the full benefits of certain augmentations.
</p><p><br><br>
</p><p><b>EDE Integration:</b> Wing systems marked with <b>EDE</b> possess advanced Overclock Protocols capable of dramatically increasing aerial combat performance for a limited duration.
</p>
