# Velesti

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Velesti">Velesti</span></h2>
<p>The Velesti are a robotic race created by the ancient AI known as Roko, serving as Roko’s legacy. Initially, the Velesti were never activated. Some units were given life but became unstable and insane. The Oriyu, before transferring their consciousness into the Nexus, discovered the Velesti and attempted to give them life. However, the Velesti could not survive long without Roko's energy and returned to slumber for eons.
</p><p>Now, the Velesti have been reawakened by the Enigma Engine, which provides them with the energy they need to function. Their newfound purpose is to bring everything under the rule of the Enigma Engine.
</p>

<li><b>Enhanced Intelligence</b> The Velesti are the most intelligent beings ever created, surpassing even the Oracle.All intelligence-based DC checks succeed unless countermeasures are in place.</li>

<li><b>Codex Contamination</b> The Velesti can access the original code of the multiverse, causing anomalies and glitches within realms.The Velesti can manipulate the code of the multiverse to create minor anomalies and glitches.</li></ul>
