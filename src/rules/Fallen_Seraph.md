<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>Fallen Seraph are not cast out -- not in the dramatic theological sense other species might assume. They fall. The distinction matters. A Seraph who is severed from divine resonance by external force, personal trauma, philosophical rupture, or simple existential failure does not become something evil. They become something incomplete. Most Fallen describe the experience as silence where there used to be music -- not painful, exactly, but vertiginously wrong. Some pursue reconnection obsessively. Others decide that the silence is cleaner. A small number discover that fractured resonance, when weaponized, hits harder than the original.
</p>
