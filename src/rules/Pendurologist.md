<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Pendurologist">Archetype: <i>Pendurologist</i></span></h1>
<p><i>The Pendulum does not swing at random. It obeys equations, remembers waves, and enforces balance.</i>
</p><p>The <b>Pendurologist</b> is a master of encoded reality—an Actor who translates wavelengths, vibrations, and metaphysical motion into executable equations. Where others cast spells or strike steel, the Pendurologist <b>writes reality</b>, using the Pendulum as both compiler and execution engine.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  Battlefield control, reality manipulation, and high-skill expression through wave-based equations.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  Deliberate but explosive. The Pendurologist prepares equations over time, then executes them in rapid succession, often altering the flow of combat mid-turn.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  High cognitive load, preparation dependency, and vulnerability when deprived of time, focus, or sensory input.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  The <b>Pendulum</b> — a metaphysical construct that inscribes, calculates, and enacts equations through wavelength manipulation.</li></ul>
<p><i>The Pendurologist rewards foresight, planning, and mastery. In the hands of a novice it is fragile; in the hands of an expert it is catastrophic.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetry:_Pendurologist_Action_Economy">Asymmetry: <i>Pendurologist Action Economy</i></span></h1>
<p>Upon choosing <b>Pendurologist</b>, your Actor gains its <b>Asymmetry</b>, reflecting the archetype’s equation-heavy, execution-focused philosophy.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy">Action Economy</span></h2>
<ul><li><b>4 Standard Actions</b>  Used to inscribe equations, swing the pendulum, execute wave logic, or maintain active calculations.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>1 True Opportunity Attack</b>  Triggered when energy, magic, or metaphysical motion passes within the Pendulum’s influence.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>1 Free Action</b>  Used to adjust variables, toggle equations, or shift pendulum states.</li></ul>
<p><i>In practice, the Pendurologist often acts multiple times within a single combat window, chaining preparation and execution into a single decisive sequence.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Pendulum">Aspect: <i>Pendulum</i></span></h1>
<p>All <b>Pendurologist</b> actors begin with the <b>Pendulum</b>, the physical-metaphysical interface through which all equations are written and resolved.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function">Base Function</span></h2>
<p>The Pendulum may manifest from any point of the Actor’s body, soul, or metaphysical tether. Once manifested, it shares the user’s senses and may act independently within defined limits.
</p><p>The Pendulum inscribes <b>equations composed of wavelengths</b>, converting variables into executable effects. These equations may target physical matter, energy flow, conceptual states, or probability fields.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b> Mundane force, conventional magic, or non-coded interference</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b> Anti-wave phenomena, entropy fields, forced desynchronization</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b> Dismissed, overwritten, or the user loses consciousness</li></ul>
<hr>
<h1><span id="Equation_.26_Wave_System_.28Foundational_Rule.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Equation_&amp;_Wave_System_(Foundational_Rule)">Equation &amp; Wave System (Foundational Rule)</span></h1>
<p>Before an equation can be executed, its <b>wave components must be understood</b>.
</p>
<ul><li>Waves are categorized as:  <b>Base (B), Conceptual (C), Quantum (Q), Exotic (E), Kinetic (K), Extra (X)</b></li></ul>
<ul><li>Each wave possesses variables such as:  <b>Amplitude, Frequency, Phase, Range, Power</b></li></ul>
<ul><li>Waves must be <b>researched, experienced, or observed</b> before use.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Equations scale in <b>Action Point cost</b>, <b>risk</b>, and <b>instability</b> based on complexity.</li></ul>
