    <div style="margin-top:14px;color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;">
<p>The Multiversal Cosmology Framework is a comprehensive model describing the physical, metaphysical, and causal organization of the greater reality continuum. It operates as a self-regulating cosmological engine built upon dark energy, inflationary theory, and quantum superposition—scaling real-world physics into mythic proportions. This framework defines how universes originate, interconnect, rupture, and evolve across the grand lattice that binds all known planes of existence.
</p>

    <div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>The genesis of the continuum relies on a pre-cosmic meta-singularity known as **The Spiral Engine**. Its primary function is the generation of extreme-density Dark Energy, establishing the baseline conditions for physical reality to emerge. 
</p>
</div>
    <div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>The First Act of Creation began when the Spiral Engine produced the First Universe, an unstable reality with over-saturated dark energy density. Its uncontrollable inflation caused a catastrophic structural failure on a cosmic scale, known historically as **The Pop Event** (or The First Rupture).
</p>
