<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>The Nullfield is the other side of the Radiant threshold -- the inversion. Where Radiants become energy made flesh, Nullfields become the negation of energy made flesh. They are not cold so much as they are absence. Standing near one feels like watching colors drain from a painting. Energy-based sensors misread them. Lights flicker and dim in their presence not from any dramatic power surge but from quiet, constant consumption. Among Oriyu society, Nullfields occupy an uneasy position: respected for their utility in suppression operations, kept at a social distance by those who find their presence draining.
</p>
