<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Beastbound">Archetype: <i>Beastbound</i></span></h1>
<p><i>“The primal within awakens; every wound fuels the beast.”</i>
</p><p>The <b>Beastbound</b> archetype channels the raw potential of animals, primal instincts, and shapeshifting abilities through a <b>Ward of Transformation</b>. This ward manifests as the ability to partially or fully transform, granting enhanced strength, resilience, and instinctive combat prowess. Beastbound actors thrive in chaotic combat, leveraging ferocity, adaptability, and protective instincts.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  <i>Transform into beasts or utilize primal instincts to amplify attacks, defense, and battlefield control.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  <i>Builds power as the battle progresses; gains momentum as health decreases or conditions trigger.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  <i>Slower to react initially; relies on transformations or thresholds to access full potential.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  <i>Ward of Transformation allows partial or full-body shifts into beasts, enhancing stats, abilities, and sensory perception.</i></li></ul>
<p><i>Beastbound excels in survival, direct confrontation, and controlling space with intimidation and adaptive forms.</i>
</p>

<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Ward_of_Transformation">Aspect: <i>Ward of Transformation</i></span></h1>
<p><i>All Beastbound actors begin with the <b>Ward of Transformation</b>, which allows the manifestation of primal abilities and forms.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function"><i>Base Function</i></span></h2>
<ul><li><b>Partial Transformation:</b> Gain enhanced speed, strength, and defensive abilities for 1–2 turns.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Full Transformation:</b> Gain maximum stats and abilities, including instinctive perception of unseen threats.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Adaptive Combat:</b> Effects and buffs scale with current health, feral states, or active abilities.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b> <i>Conventional control; ward is tied to life force.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b> <i>Severe mental interference or dispelling effects.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b> <i>Deactivated, fully dispelled, or the Actor is incapacitated.</i></li></ul>
