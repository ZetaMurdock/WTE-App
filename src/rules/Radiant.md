<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>Radiants emerged from a threshold that most Oriyu never cross -- the point where the energy field stops being something the body produces and starts being something the body IS. They do not generate energy so much as they ARE energy given biological form. The practical consequences are significant: they glow constantly, they cannot be invisible, and in environments where energy is sparse, they feel it like hunger. In environments of rich energy output -- industrial zones, active battlefields, unstable dimensional seams -- they are terrifying.
</p>
