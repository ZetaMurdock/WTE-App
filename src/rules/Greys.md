<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>The Greys did not design their collective architecture. It emerged from necessity -- Stygian communities in hostile environments where shadow resources were scarce discovered that pooling shadow-resonance across multiple individuals was more efficient than individual accumulation. What began as a resource-sharing adaptation became something more fundamental over generations: a genuinely distributed mind where the boundaries of individual consciousness became negotiable. Greys do not lose their individual identity in the network. They gain a second one. Outsiders interacting with a Grey network for the first time often describe the unsettling experience of being read by something that is simultaneously one thing and several people.
</p>
