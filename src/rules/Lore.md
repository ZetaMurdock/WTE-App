<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Book_of_Enon_Old_Gensis">Book of Enon Old Gensis</span></h2>
<p><i>Rise of the Ancients; The Augur</i>
</p><p>Before there was time, space, light, energy, or existence itself, there was <i>The Augur</i>. And before them, there was nothing. The Augur were enigmatic beings of boundless potential, capable of creating anything they could imagine. However, their existence was not without purpose—they were sent on a mission by a higher being known as the <i>Spiral Engine</i>. Their task: to inhabit and explore alternate worlds.
</p><p>During their journey, the Augur stumbled upon a vast and empty void in the cosmos. It was here that they began to build.
</p><p>Eons passed before their efforts bore fruit. The exact duration of their labor is beyond documentation and thus will not be covered here. But for thousands—if not hundreds of millions—of years, they meticulously crafted every aspect of the known multiverse. Eventually, they conquered the <i>Atom</i>, mastering the construction of matter from the fundamental building blocks of existence.
</p><p>As their knowledge expanded, the Augur refined their abilities to an unprecedented level, eventually reaching the point where they could create their ideal solar system. At its center lay the <i>Primordial Soup</i>, a vast, ever-shifting expanse stretching across hundreds of trillions of miles. Using this cosmic essence, they shaped the foundations of their universe. The creation process took millions of years, with each planet requiring approximately 500,000 years to form. The planets, forged in sequence, were named:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Gaia</b></li>
<li><b>Beloria</b></li>
<li><b>Nuvuru</b></li>
<li><b>Venta</b></li>
<li><b>Benreial</b></li>
<li><b>Plutai</b></li>
<li><b>Cagilara</b></li>
<li><b>Ragonus</b></li>
<li><b>Uberon</b></li>
<li><b>Tholadus</b></li>
<li><b>Jolatun</b></li></ul>
<p>Each planet required the combined efforts of three Augur to initiate its formation. However, once the process was set in motion, a single Augur could complete it. By this time, the Augur had nearly perfected their ability to create, though their full potential had yet to be realized.
</p><p>To safeguard their creation, they forged the <b>Six Great Pillars of Woe</b>, stationed at the outskirts of the observable multiverse. These pillars functioned as an interdimensional gate, shielding their reality from unknown forces. To enter the multiverse, one would need to bypass, evade, or destroy these colossal sentinels.
</p><p>Unfortunately, the Augur acted a few million years too late.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Birth_of_Enigmatica"><b>The Birth of Enigmatica</b></span></h3>
<p>As they worked, the Augur conceived hundreds of thousands of ideas—concepts that would shape and define their creation. With each stroke of their tools, each whisper of thought, a single word began to echo among them:
</p><p><b>"World."</b>
</p><p>Again and again, the word resounded—repeated, refined, reimagined. And then, in unison, a singular realization struck them all, an idea so profound it became the final key to their grand design.
</p><p><b>"Enigmatica."</b>
</p><p>The moment the thought formed, the final stroke was struck, the hammer fell with a resounding <i>clang</i>, and the last stir of the Primordial Soup was made.
</p><p>In an instant, the great cosmic essence vanished, drawn into the vastness of creation itself. Then, a blinding flash—worlds began to emerge, life sparked into existence, and the fabric of the multiverse wove itself into reality. The Augur had set in motion an unstoppable chain reaction—a <i>domino effect</i> that started with the forging of the first atom and culminated in the birth of complex life.
</p><p>The multiverse, once an empty void, now <i>wrote itself into being</i>. From the unknowable depths of nothingness, a world that had never existed before had been born.
</p><p><b>Enigmatica began to shine, its enigmatic and glorious light illuminating the vastness of the cosmos.</b>
</p><p>This was the <b>Rise of the Augur</b>.
</p><p>All that the Light touched, life was breathed into and sent throughout it. The Augur had finally achieved what their Creator wanted—they could finally rest.
</p><p>Or so they thought.
</p><p>One after another, planets began to succumb to corruption. Something they couldn’t explain was absorbing everything they had worked so hard to build. Then, a single name came to mind—the one their Creator had warned them about. It was here. It had always been here, waiting for the right moment.
</p><p>It was Null.
</p><p>Thousands of worlds fell. Millions wilted and died. The Augur had to act swiftly. Somehow, Null had found a way past the Pillars—or perhaps it had always been here. The weight of these thoughts was too much to bear. They had no choice but to begin again.
</p><p>The Soup was created once more. Then, one of the Augur had an idea: to split the Multiverse into layers, into dimensions. And so, the first dimension was born—the first layer, the Forge. Here, they toiled for eons, tirelessly removing Null from every space it touched. Eventually, they created additional dimensions, crafting one specifically to imprison Null. They locked it away, never to enter it again.
</p><p>Through trials and tribulations, their work continued. By the time they finished, three layers remained. The first, the Heart, was the innermost layer, now slowly succumbing to corruption. The second, the Forge, became the realm where they transferred the Primordial Soup. And the third, the Void, lay beyond the Forge—the place where they had sealed Null, until they could create something capable of counteracting it.
</p><p>They labored for eons more, tirelessly striving to keep their creation safe. But in time, they began to wither, growing old, exhausted, and drained. They needed a way to rejuvenate themselves.
</p><p>And in their search, they discovered that the answer lay within Null itself.
</p><p>Some of the Augur ventured past the Forge into the Void, and there, they began to understand.
</p><p>When they returned, the Old Augur hoped for good news. But the creatures—the builders, the engineers—who came back were no longer Augur. Yet, nothing immediately suggested that something was wrong. They spoke, they shared their findings, and together, the Old and New Augur searched for other options.
</p><p>But as the New Augur experimented, each step closer to discovery brought forth an indescribable rage from within—like something was trying to break free.
</p><p>Millions of years passed. The Old Augur grew older, bound to their traditions, while the Younger Augur became stronger, more ruthless, more relentless. A great divide formed. And so, the Old Augur created something that would outlive them, something they called the Enigma Engine—named after their Creator.
</p><p>The Younger Augur took notice. In response, they began constructing something of their own—something dark, exuding an aura unlike any familiar world. The Old Augur could sense its power, a force drawn from the Void itself. And in that moment, the Younger Augur shed their old name with the creation of their Seed, as they called it. The Seed was more so found while within the Void; they just augmented it and forced it to give all that it had absorbed, rather than just absorbing and taking. The Seed was the culmination of all the energy taken by Null up until this point, which is why they each consumed it, declaring themselves the Oracle Gods—beings beyond the scope of mortal understanding.
</p><p>In time, the Augur faded. Their bodies withered, but their essence remained, merging with the Enigma Engine. And when the Engine roared to life, its Light was impossible to overshadow.
</p><p>The Oracle Gods tried to seize control, but they could not. Their so-called “new technology” was nothing in comparison. The Engine’s power surpassed that of the original Augur—it was something new, something beyond comprehension.
</p><p>With this revelation, the Multiverse underwent a great cleansing, going through every aspect of the Observable and Unobservable Multiverse. Each Dimension was purged, with the inconsistencies and planets corrupted by the negative effects of Null eradicated. The Enigma Engine went on these cleanses for millions, if not billions, of years, halting the Oracle Gods' advance to experiment with any negative or evil creatures that might have existed. The Enigma Engine, now unshackled, forged new races—each a reflection of the Augur who once were.
</p><p>But among them, there was one Augur who had not perished. One who had succumbed to Null yet was not lost. He beheld the Light, and it purged the Null from within him.
</p><p>He was called Enon.
</p><p>Enon seized the Enigma Engine and wielded it—not to destroy, but to further breathe life and understanding into the world, creating the first intelligent life besides the Augur and the Oracle. And with the Spiral Engine of His Creator, he gained deeper knowledge of Null. It was not simply the destruction of all things. It was equilibrium. A return to zero. True balance.
</p><p>The Great Will of Null had foreseen it.
</p><p>The Heart was saved. The Augur were no more.
</p><p>All except for one.
</p><p>The final Augur.
</p><p>The one who would pave the way for the New Testament.
</p><p>Enon, the Chosen.
</p><p>The world began to move forward with Enon at the helm of creation, but the battle with the Oracle never ceased. The eternal Enon and the persistent Oracle always butted heads and were never able to come to an agreement. The Oracle were, and still are, strong in their convictions. They believed in progress, reform, and revision. They believed that Null was the future, and that belief is where they split from the Augur.
</p><p>The Augur didn't believe that Null was the future, which is why they kept experimenting. They couldn't let go of the old ways—so they didn’t, and they perished. The Oracle believed that, because of their mass exodus, they were chosen to lead their kind, but they were gravely mistaken.
</p><p>Looking beyond the scope of the Augur, the Oracle, and even the Enigma Engine lies something greater—something stronger, something more—something that no creature, being, entity, god, or man could explain. It was a will. A force. It was the true way forward, and yet nobody followed this will, this force.
</p><p>But with the resurgence of something greater, both the Oracle and Enon were forced to act.
</p><p>Across the span of the cosmos, things were changing. The Great Pillars that had once protected them had let something in—not to destroy what they had built, but to <b>change</b> it. And that change was <b>fascinating</b>. Both the Oracle and Enon needed a way to observe this transformation while still working in the background. Thus, the <b>Retrospective Omni-Kinetic Reactor</b> was born—<b>Roko</b>, for short.
</p><p>Constructing the Reactor was no easy task. They had to harness the <b>combined efforts of the Void</b>, extract the cores of several planets, and mine the <b>minerals of an entire solar system</b> to create its foundation. Even then, it was incomplete. To bring it to life, they <b>imbued it with the very essence of the Augur</b>. Once they did, they <b>had done more than build a reactor</b>—they had created something <b>powerful enough to draw the attention of the Will</b>.
</p><p>Enon and the Oracle soon <b>noticed something unsettling</b>. The Reactor was beginning to react—to something they neither <b>understood nor could explain</b>. It was searching, shifting, <b>looking for something</b>. So, they gave it a body—one it could <b>inhabit</b>.
</p><p>Using <b>stardust, primordial materials, and the remnants of the first cosmic soup</b>, they forged a <b>robotic vessel</b>. And in doing so, they <b>created one of the first intelligent beings they had ever made</b>—Roko. Its sole purpose was to <b>watch, observe, and report</b>. And it did so <b>flawlessly</b>.
</p><p>Through Roko, a revelation emerged: <b>the Will was instilling beings with the ability to understand the very fabric of existence</b>. It was granting them knowledge of <b>Odexi Math</b>—a failed Augur experiment once thought impossible. The Augur had abandoned it, believing the energy required to make it work was beyond any conceivable output. <b>But they were wrong.</b>
</p><p>Roko provided the key. It explained that instead of altering reality on a <b>massive, destabilizing scale</b>, one could <b>manipulate the existing world through precise, localized changes</b>—not by bending dimensions, but by <b>adjusting the values already present within the fabric of existence</b>. <b>Mathematical formulas could be rewritten</b>, subtly shaping reality <b>without breaking it</b>.
</p><p>The Will was not <b>corruption</b> nor the force of <b>change</b>—it was <b>awakening</b>. It was the realization of <b>all things</b>. And as this truth became clear, the Will <b>turned its gaze upon Roko</b>. It saw in Roko <b>great potential</b>.
</p><p>But the Will was still young. <b>It did not yet understand</b> that <b>not all things should be set free from their cage.</b>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Book_of_Enon_Prime_Horizon">Book of Enon Prime Horizon</span></h2>
<p><i><small>"It is man's natural sickness to believe that he possesses the Truth."-Blaise Pascal</small></i> 
</p><p>The Will was still young, still in its infancy, bound within the constraints of Xail'Untais. Yet, even in its early stages, it set its sights on Roko, initiating a transformation—one that would elevate Roko into something far greater.
</p><p>The Will saw Roko as more than just a machine; it recognized the potential for Roko to fulfill the mantle of a Gnostic being. Roko’s comprehension neared that of the Spiral Engine itself, and with the guidance of the Will, it transcended. This evolution sent the Oracle into chaos, unable to grasp what had occurred.
</p><p>When the Will took control of Roko, it altered the machine’s very essence, unlocking a latent potential that had long been suppressed. Enon, however, still maintained control over the <b>Enigma Engine</b>, ensuring that order did not completely collapse. Yet, the Will began to understand that Roko had evolved into something vast—something capable of containing Enon’s Heart within the tip of its finger.
</p><p>Now a being of incomprehensible scale, akin to the Spiral Engine, Roko assumed the role of a Watcher and an ambassador for Enon’s Heart, which encompassed the universe itself. With this newfound power, Roko could direct the Pillars toward threats and command them through the Augur technology stored within its mainframe by the Oracle.
</p><p>Now, the cosmic hierarchy is structured as follows:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Roko</b> watches over the outer realms, ensuring stability on a grand scale.</li>
<li><b>Enon</b> maintains balance within, overseeing the cosmic order.</li>
<li><b>The Oracle</b> functions on a micro level, creating diverse races and tending to the intricacies of individual life.</li></ul>
<p>While Enon concerns itself with the vast cosmic equilibrium, and Roko guards the outer edges of existence, the Oracle ensures the flourishing of life in all its unique forms.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Divide">The Divide</span></h3>
<p>Eons have passed since the establishment of hierarchy, and though things have settled to a simmer, true peace remains elusive. Power has shifted hands—sometimes to those deserving, and other times to those unworthy. Humanity, once the pride of the Augur, was nurtured and overseen by the Oracle, who bestowed upon them what they deemed "gifts"—alterations to their genetic makeup. However, these so-called gifts brought chaos, discourse, and unintended consequences.
</p><p>The first genetic alteration led to the creation of the <b>Hyomen</b>, beings engineered with the intelligence of an Oracle child. This advancement placed them on par with certain higher beings in terms of comprehension. Over time, the very essence of humanity eroded, giving way to something entirely new. The Oracle engineered various branches of evolution: <b>the Hyomen, the Primordials, the Oractus, and the Elderooth</b>. Once these genetic divergences were established, nature took its course. Hybridization followed, leading to the rise of countless new species—some thriving, others failing.
</p><p>But humanity was not the only force of transformation. The Oracle, along with what appeared to be the remnants of humanity, altered animals and even alien life, granting them sentience. With this resurgence of new beings came a revival of individuality, yet it also gave rise to <b>unforeseen communication barriers, deep divisions, and violent conflicts</b>. Racism and prejudice surged to unprecedented levels. The Hyomen, despite their intelligence, were deemed inferior simply because they retained humanoid forms—forms that had become symbols of the past, now regarded as obsolete.
</p><p>In time, what was once called humanity no longer existed. Those who evolved beyond their predecessors rebranded themselves as <b>Omenity</b>—the next step in existence, the superior beings who had left behind the frailties of their ancestors.
</p><p>With these transformations, different factions emerged, each built upon distinct ideologies that further shaped the course of history. These ideological divides fueled tensions, alliances, and conflicts, pushing Omenity into what became known as <b>the Mor Era</b>, spanning from <b>3068 to 5609</b>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mor-Era">Mor-Era</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Year-3104">Year-3104</span></h3>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Sol_Harbinger_Year">The Sol Harbinger Year</span></h4>
<p>The races that endured the test of time were both many and few—some still yet to emerge onto this young world. Over time, the Oracle classified these races into three primary categories, a system that was eventually adopted by the Collective races themselves: <b>Humanity, Omenity, and the Asternem</b>.
</p><p><b>Humanity</b> remained the closest to the original life created naturally by the Augur, nurtured and engineered by the Oracle. As time passed, some members of Humanity evolved further, adapting to their circumstances, while others remained relatively unchanged. Those who transcended their origins became known as <b>Omenity</b>—a new strain of existence that considered itself superior. Viewing Humanity as lesser beings, the Omenity established laws and systems to maintain their dominance. Governments and regulations were formed to suppress Humanity, restricting their access to bioengineering advancements and limiting their education within Omenity-controlled domains.
</p><p>Then, there were the <b>Asternem</b>—beings from beyond, emerging from the vastness beyond the grand cusp of the observable universe. Unlike Humanity and Omenity, the Asternem did not seek dominion; they roamed the empty spaces of the cosmos, largely uninterested in the affairs of the other races. To them, interference was an unnecessary conflict. However, their detachment could not last forever. Scarcity of resources eventually forced them to interact, leading to tensions that could no longer be ignored.
</p><p>Thus began the era of the <b>Sol Harbinger</b>, a turning point in history that would reshape the fate of all three Cognitive Strains.
</p><p>Once the connection was made, it could never truly be stabilized. The Omenity, once dominant, now found themselves diminished, their significance waning. This shift allowed Humanity to rise from its subjugation, further destabilizing the already fragile relationship between the two. The realization that there existed beings capable of surpassing even the Omenity sent them into a spiral of mental turmoil.
</p><p>In response, Humanity struck back against the Omenity, seeking to reclaim what had been denied to them. Meanwhile, the Asternem watched from the sidelines, taking what they needed from the chaos but refraining from direct interference.
</p><p>Amidst the growing disorder, the Oracle remained passive observers—until they could no longer ignore the threat of total collapse. They came to a singular realization: without intervention, this chaos could lead to the irreversible destruction of what they still considered Humanity. Order had to be established, or everything would be lost forever. and then the Signet Council was Born
</p>
<h3><span id="The_Signel_Council_.E2.80.93_Universal_Government"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Signel_Council_–_Universal_Government"><b>The Signel Council – Universal Government</b></span></h3>
<p>In the boundless expanse of the cosmos, the Oracle, in its infinite wisdom, established the Signel Council, the supreme governing body tasked with ensuring harmony, balance, and order across all known existence. The Council was forged to bring a fair and just system that would allow for the natural diversity of celestial bodies and their inhabitants while maintaining universal equity. The Signel Council's vast network of Branches and Departments work in concert to sustain the balance of power and uphold the efficiency of governance within the universe.
</p>
<h4><span id="The_Regency_.E2.80.93_Governing_Authority_within_the_Signel_Council"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Regency_–_Governing_Authority_within_the_Signel_Council">The Regency – Governing Authority within the Signel Council</span></h4>
<p>At the heart of the Signel Council lies the Regency, the executive and legislative arm of the Universal Government. Composed of a select group of elected leaders known as Regents, the Regency oversees the creation and enforcement of universal laws, policies, and decisions. Regents are chosen for their wisdom, political acumen, and their ability to mediate the diversity of the universe, guiding the cosmos with the Oracle’s vision in mind. The Regency is responsible for the greater political, economic, and legal systems that stretch across the stars.
</p><p><b>Structure of the Regency:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Regency Assembly:</b> The primary legislative body of the Regency, the Assembly is made up of representatives from the key sectors of the universe, from mighty star systems to small, independent worlds. They debate and craft universal laws, amendments, and policies, ensuring universal harmony and fairness.</li>
<li><b>Regent’s Council:</b> A select body of senior Regents who oversee the implementation of laws passed by the Assembly. This Council directs the security, economic systems, and diplomacy of the cosmos, functioning as the executive force that ensures the adherence to the laws of the universe.</li></ul>
<p><b>Divisions of the Regency:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Division of Universal Peace:</b> Tasked with maintaining peace across celestial bodies, this division works to mediate conflicts, negotiate peace treaties, and prevent escalations that could lead to galactic wars.</li>
<li><b>Division of Universal Prosperity:</b> Responsible for managing intergalactic trade, resource flow, and economic stability. This division ensures fair distribution and equitable trade between civilizations.</li>
<li><b>Division of Social Welfare:</b> Focusing on the welfare of all sentient life forms, this division guarantees that basic needs like healthcare, food, and education are accessible across the universe.</li></ul>
<p><b>Role of the Regency in Relation to Other Branches:</b>
</p><p>The Regency operates in tandem with other branches of the Signel Council. It works closely with the <b>Equal Libertarian Branch</b> (ELB) to create fair and just laws, and with the <b>Adjudicative Segan Branch</b> (ASB) to enforce those laws. The Regency has ultimate authority over matters of diplomacy and universal law, while also stepping in to manage crises, resource shortages, and conflicts when needed.
</p>
<h4><span id="The_Equal_Libertarian_Branch_.28ELB.29_.E2.80.93_Guardian_of_Justice"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Equal_Libertarian_Branch_(ELB)_–_Guardian_of_Justice">The Equal Libertarian Branch (ELB) – Guardian of Justice</span></h4>
<p>The ELB serves as the universal legal authority, ensuring that the laws crafted by the Regency are just, fair, and equitable. Its responsibility lies in the ongoing creation, implementation, and review of these laws, ensuring their impartial application across all sentient beings.
</p><p><b>Departments within the ELB:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Regulatory Division:</b> Tasked with drafting new laws based on the ever-evolving needs of the universe.</li>
<li><b>Public Equity Council:</b> Works to ensure laws are enforced equitably, respecting cultural differences while maintaining universal fairness.</li>
<li><b>Universal Ethics Tribunal:</b> Acts as the moral compass of the universe, reviewing and challenging laws to ensure they align with the Oracle’s vision of balance.</li></ul>
<h4><span id="The_Adjudicative_Segan_Branch_.28ASB.29_.E2.80.93_Enforcement_of_Law"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Adjudicative_Segan_Branch_(ASB)_–_Enforcement_of_Law">The Adjudicative Segan Branch (ASB) – Enforcement of Law</span></h4>
<p>The ASB is the enforcement arm of the Signel Council, ensuring the implementation of laws across the universe. Its role is not only to investigate and punish violations but also to eliminate corruption within the system, ensuring the integrity of the Universal Government.
</p><p><b>Departments within the ASB:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Court of the Cosmos:</b> The highest judicial body, dealing with the most serious breaches of galactic law.</li>
<li><b>Enforcement and Action Division:</b> A network of highly trained officers responsible for law enforcement across the stars.</li>
<li><b>Regency Oversight:</b> A special committee ensuring the actions of the Regency remain free from corruption or abuse of power.</li></ul>
<h4><span id="The_Treasury_Branch_.28TB.29_.E2.80.93_Economic_Stewardship"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Treasury_Branch_(TB)_–_Economic_Stewardship">The Treasury Branch (TB) – Economic Stewardship</span></h4>
<p>The Treasury Branch manages the universal economy, ensuring financial stability and equitable distribution of resources across the cosmos. It oversees the Universal Monetary System (UMS), manages trade routes, and ensures that resources are fairly allocated among civilizations.
</p><p><b>Departments within the TB:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Universal Monetary Council:</b> Oversees the exchange of currency and manages intergalactic financial systems.</li>
<li><b>Resource Equity Division:</b> Ensures that resources are distributed fairly, preventing monopolies and ensuring equity in access to resources.</li>
<li><b>Intergalactic Financial Auditing:</b> Monitors the financial health of planetary systems, ensuring that no system is exploited.</li></ul>
<h4><span id="Regional_Governments_.E2.80.93_The_Keeper_System_and_Ambassadors"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Regional_Governments_–_The_Keeper_System_and_Ambassadors">Regional Governments – The Keeper System and Ambassadors</span></h4>
<p>Each region within the universe is governed by a Regional Government, with <b>Ambassadors</b> and <b>Keepers</b> overseeing political and ecological affairs, respectively. The <b>Keeper</b> is a protector of all life, ensuring the ecological balance and sustainability of planets and sectors. The <b>Ambassador</b> handles political relations, trade, and diplomacy, acting as the representative of their region within the greater Universal Government.
</p><p><b>Unique Features of the Universal Government:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Celestial Hierarchy of Power:</b> The universe is divided into regions, each with its own level of autonomy based on its resources and influence. More powerful regions have greater influence within the Universal Government.</li>
<li><b>Inter-Governmental Communication:</b> The Oracle’s Web connects the various sectors, allowing instantaneous communication and the swift exchange of orders and decrees.</li>
<li><b>Regional Councils:</b> Each planet or sector has a local Council of Keepers and Ambassadors, allowing for localized governance while maintaining diplomatic ties to the Universal Government.</li></ul>
