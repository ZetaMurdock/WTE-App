# Monster Types

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr">

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Monster_Types">Monster Types</span></h3>
<hr>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Beast">Beast</span></h4>
<p>Explanation: Natural animals or monstrous versions of animals. They rely on physical strength and agility.
</p><p>Effective Damage Types:
</p>
<ul><li>Slashing (1d8): Effective against their flesh and hide.</li>
<li>Piercing (1d6): Useful for penetrating their vital organs.</li>
<li>Bludgeoning (1d6): Useful for breaking bones and crushing organs.</li></ul>
<p>Immunities:
</p>
<ul><li>Poison (1d6)</li>
<li>Bleed (1d4 per round)</li>
<li>Crushing (1d40)</li></ul>
<hr>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Humanoid">Humanoid</span></h4>
<p>Explanation: Creatures with human-like forms, such as humans, elves, orcs, and goblins. They are often balanced in strength, agility, and intelligence.
</p><p>Effective Damage Types:
</p>
<ul><li>Piercing (1d6): Effective for precise attacks on vital points.</li>
<li>Slashing (1d8): Good for general combat and causing bleed damage.</li>
<li>Bludgeoning (1d6): Effective against armor and for breaking bones.</li></ul>
<p>Immunities:
</p>
<ul><li>Poison (1d6)</li>
<li>Radiation (1d12)</li></ul>
<hr>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Reptile">Reptile</span></h4>
<p>Explanation: Cold-blooded creatures like lizardfolk, dragons, or giant snakes. They often have tough scales.
</p><p>Effective Damage Types:
</p>
<ul><li>Cold (1d8): Exploits their cold-blooded nature.</li>
<li>Slashing (1d8): Useful for cutting through scales.</li>
<li>Fire (1d10): Effective for creatures with cold-blooded physiology.</li></ul>
<p>Immunities:
</p>
<ul><li>Poison (1d6)</li>
<li>Bleed (1d4 per round)</li>
<li>Winded (1d8)</li></ul>
<hr>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Construct">Construct</span></h4>
<p>Explanation: Artificial beings such as golems, animated statues, and robots. They are typically made of inorganic materials.
</p><p>Effective Damage Types:
</p>
<ul><li>Bludgeoning (1d6): Effective for smashing rigid bodies.</li>
<li>Lightning (1d8): Can disrupt internal systems.</li>
<li>Force (1d10): Penetrates and disrupts structural integrity.</li></ul>
<p>Immunities:
</p>
<ul><li>Poison (1d6)</li>
<li>Necrotic (1d10)</li>
<li>Psychic (1d8)</li></ul>
<hr>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Elemental">Elemental</span></h4>
<p>Explanation: Beings composed of fundamental forces like fire, water, air, and earth. They are embodiments of their respective elements.
</p><p>Effective Damage Types:
</p>
<ul><li>Force (1d10): Effective against all elemental forms.</li>
<li>Radiant (1d10): Can disrupt their elemental nature.</li>
<li>Magical (1d10): Bypasses elemental resistances.</li></ul>
<p>Immunities:
</p>
<ul><li>Poison (1d6)</li>
<li>Bleed (1d4 per round)</li>
<li>Necrotic (1d10)</li></ul>
<hr>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Fae">Fae</span></h4>
<p>Explanation: Enchanted creatures from mystical realms, such as fairies, dryads, and sprites. They are often tied to nature and magic.
</p><p>Effective Damage Types:
</p>
<ul><li>Cold (1d8): Can disrupt their magical energy.</li>
<li>Iron (traditional, typically bludgeoning or piercing): Known weakness in many fae lore.</li>
<li>Radiant (1d10): Counters their mystical nature.</li></ul>
<p>Immunities:
</p>
<ul><li>Poison (1d6)</li>
<li>Psychic (1d8)</li></ul>
<hr>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Descended">Descended</span></h4>
<p>Explanation: Creatures with lineage tracing back to divine or infernal beings. Examples include tieflings and aasimar.
</p><p>Effective Damage Types:
</p>
<ul><li>Radiant (1d10): For infernal-descended beings.</li>
<li>Necrotic (1d10): For celestial-descended beings.</li>
<li>Psychic (1d8): Effective against their often powerful minds.</li></ul>
<p>Immunities:
</p>
<ul><li>Poison (1d6)</li>
<li>Bleed (1d4 per round)</li></ul>
<hr>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Extraterrestrial">Extraterrestrial</span></h4>
<p>Explanation: Beings from other planets or dimensions. They may have unknown anatomies and abilities.
</p><p>Effective Damage Types:
</p>
<ul><li>Radiation (1d12): Disrupts alien physiology.</li>
<li>Explosive (2d6): Effective against unknown biological structures.</li>
<li>Psychic (1d8): Can target their possibly advanced minds.</li></ul>
<p>Immunities:
</p>
<ul><li>Poison (1d6)</li>
<li>Necrotic (1d10)</li>
<li>Bleed (1d4 per round)</li></ul>
<hr>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Parasite">Parasite</span></h4>
<p>Explanation: Organisms that live off or inside a host, such as mind-controlling worms or body-infesting insects.
</p><p>Effective Damage Types:
</p>
<ul><li>Poison (1d6): Targets their biology directly.</li>
<li>Fire (1d10): Purges infestations.</li>
<li>Acid (1d8): Effective for dissolving them.</li></ul>
<p>Immunities:
</p>
<ul><li>Psychic (1d8)</li>
<li>Bludgeoning (1d6)</li></ul>
<hr>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Eldritch">Eldritch</span></h4>
<p>Explanation: Beings from beyond the known universe, often with incomprehensible forms and powers. Inspired by Lovecraftian horror.
</p><p>Effective Damage Types:
</p>
<ul><li>True (1d12): Cuts through their alien existence.</li>
<li>Psychic (1d8): Can damage their often powerful but unstable minds.</li>
<li>Radiant (1d10): Counters their dark, chaotic nature.</li></ul>
<p>Immunities:
</p>
<ul><li>Poison (1d6)</li>
<li>Bleed (1d4 per round)</li>
<li>Necrotic (1d10)</li></ul>
<hr>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Ascended">Ascended</span></h4>
<p>Explanation: Beings who have transcended mortal limitations, often gaining god-like abilities. Examples include ascended mages or demigods.
</p><p>Effective Damage Types:
</p>
<ul><li>Force (1d10): Penetrates their elevated defenses.</li>
<li>Radiant (1d10): Counters their heightened power.</li>
<li>Necrotic (1d10): Can drain their life force.</li></ul>
<p>Immunities:
</p>
<ul><li>Poison (1d6)</li>
<li>Bleed (1d4 per round)</li></ul>
<hr>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Psionic">Psionic</span></h4>
<p>Explanation: Creatures with powerful mental abilities, capable of telepathy, telekinesis, and other mind-based powers.
</p><p>Effective Damage Types:
</p>
<ul><li>Psychic (1d8): Directly counters their mental powers.</li>
<li>Sonic (1d8): Disrupts their concentration.</li>
<li>Force (1d10): Effective against their possibly weaker physical forms.</li></ul>
<p>Immunities:
</p>
<ul><li>Poison (1d6)</li>
<li>Bleed (1d4 per round)</li>
<li>Necrotic (1d10)</li></ul>
<hr>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Amorphous">Amorphous</span></h4>
<p>Explanation: Shapeless, fluid-like beings such as oozes, slimes, and gelatinous creatures. They can change form and move fluidly.
</p><p>Effective Damage Types:
</p>
<ul><li>Fire (1d10): Can cause them to evaporate or solidify.</li>
<li>Acid (1d8): Corrosive substances can disrupt their bodies.</li>
<li>Cold (1d8): Can freeze and shatter their fluid form.</li></ul>
<p>Immunities:
</p>
<ul><li>Poison (1d6)</li>
<li>Piercing (1d6)</li>
<li>Slashing (1d8)</li></ul>
