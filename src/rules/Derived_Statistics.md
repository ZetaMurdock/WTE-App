<div style="font-size:1.6em;font-weight:bold;color:#5090c0;letter-spacing:0.06em;font-family:monospace;">DERIVED STATISTICS</div>
<div style="color:#406080;font-size:0.85em;margin-top:4px;font-style:italic;">Mechanics — Calculated values defining a character's combat and operational capacity.</div>
</div>
<div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #182840;border-left:3px solid #2a5080;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;color:#8090b0;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>Derived Statistics are computed from base attributes and specialty investments at character creation, updating whenever those inputs change.
</p><p><b>Scaling formula:</b> All derived stats begin at <b>Base +5</b>, gaining <b>+2 per 10 points</b> invested in contributing specialties.
</p><p><b>Reduction rate:</b> Reducing specialties subtract <b>1 per 3 points</b> invested.
</p>
</div>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="TAX_BURDEN">TAX BURDEN</span></h2>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #182840;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:16px;">
<div style="background:#101828;border-left:3px solid #2a5080;padding:10px 14px;color:#8090b0;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;margin-bottom:10px;">
<p>Tax Burden is the total cost of a build's reductions. It is used by the <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Pressure_Engine">Pressure Engine</a> to calculate the Complexity Modifier.
</p>
</div>
<pre style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #182840;padding:10px 14px;color:#70a0c8;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.8;">Tax Burden         = sum of (specialty ÷ 10) for every specialty that creates reductions
Complexity Modifier = Inspiration specialty − Tax Burden
</pre>
<div style="color:#507090;font-size:0.85em;margin-top:10px;line-height:1.7;">
<p>A build heavy in Weight and Precision gains physical and tactical power but loses complexity ceiling. A lean, Inspiration-forward build with few reducing specialties maximizes creative impact through the Pressure Engine.
</p><p><b>Example:</b> Precision 20 and Weight 30 → Tax Burden = (20÷10) + (30÷10) = 2 + 3 = <b>5</b>. If Inspiration is 18, Complexity Modifier = 18 − 5 = <b>+13</b>.
</p>
</div>
