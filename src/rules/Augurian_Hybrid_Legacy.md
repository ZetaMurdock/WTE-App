<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p>The <b>Augur</b> were not conquerors or gods — they were <b>experimenters</b>.
</p><p>Through their work, entire civilizations were created, altered, and erased. Their goal was never domination, only understanding: to see how far life could be shaped before it broke.
</p><p>You are a consequence of that work.
</p><p>Those tied to Augurian experimentation are known as <b>Syliu</b> — a word that directly translates to <b>“Empty.”</b>
</p><p>Empty soul.
</p><p>Empty origin.
</p><p>Empty purpose.
</p><p>The Augur no longer exist in the present age. Their disappearance did not end their influence. Their experiments remain, scattered across worlds as common inhabitants of reality.
</p><p>In their absence, a new organization has risen — one that openly supports <b>Hybridization Research</b>, even at the cost of life.
</p><p>You are proof that the work never truly ended.
</p>
