# Movement & Positioning

Movement in W.T.E is **not grid-based**. It is measured through threat,
proximity, and narrative positioning. There are no squares to count — there is
only how close you are to something that can hurt you.

---

### Threat Radius — the Zone of Influence

Every hostile creature exerts a **30-foot Zone of Influence (ZoI)**.

Entering, exiting, or repositioning within that zone may trigger a **Reaction
Check**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Outside ZoI** (30+ ft) | Movement is free and unpressured. |
| **Inside ZoI** | Movement is restricted and reaction-sensitive. Repositioning is always a risk decision. |

---

### Reactions

When a Reaction Check fires, the creature whose zone you disturbed may answer
with any of:

*   **No response** — they let it happen
*   **Shift position** — reposition rather than punish
*   **Block movement** — deny the path outright
*   **Grapple**
*   **Knock prone**
*   **Strike**
*   **Initiate Quicktime**
*   **Any contextually reasonable response** the Curator allows

The list is deliberately open. A Zone of Influence is not an attack of
opportunity — it is a standing threat that a thinking creature spends however it
wants.

---

### What this changes at the table

Because there is no grid, distance is a **Curator ruling**, not a measurement.
Ask "am I in their zone or not?" and play from the answer. The only number that
matters is 30 feet, and it exists so that the answer is consistent between
tables rather than a matter of taste.

Two things follow from this that are easy to miss:

*   **Reach and ZoI are different numbers.** A creature's
    [size class](wte://rules/Derived_Statistics) sets its innate *reach* — 5 ft
    for a Moderate body, 25 ft for a Colossal one — which is how far it can
    physically touch you. Its ZoI is 30 ft regardless. A Tiny creature with 0
    reach still projects a full Zone of Influence; it simply has to do something
    other than hit you with it.
*   **Some creatures widen the zone.** An Inderi **Aeor** projects a
    40-foot Zone of Influence rather than the standard 30, and contests energy
    that enters it.

---

### Where the zone already applies

The ZoI is referenced across existing equipment and abilities, and all of it
reads against this rule:

*   **Optic Camouflage** drops the moment you enter a hostile creature's ZoI.
*   **Forearm blades** are built for fighting *inside* one — high Dexterity to
    strike within restricted melee, with Evasion boosted so the operator can
    survive being there.
*   **Diagnostic gear** reads vitals and status conditions on anything inside a
    30-foot ZoI.
*   Several **species innates** use the ZoI as their area of effect rather than
    a separate radius.
