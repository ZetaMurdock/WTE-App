# Premise

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"> <div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:8px;padding:24px 36px;margin-bottom:16px;"><div style="font-family:monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:3px;color:#4e7fa5;margin-bottom:4px;"><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Wonderland_of_The_Enigma_Wiki">HOME</a> / <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Rulebook_Index">RULEBOOK INDEX</a> / PREMISE</div><div style="font-size:26px;font-weight:700;color:#dedede;letter-spacing:1.5px;">Premise</div><div style="color:#565656;font-size:12px;margin-top:6px;font-style:italic;">What WTE is · Why it matters · Who you are inside it</div><div style="background:#181818;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:4px;padding:10px 14px;font-size:13px;color:#c0c0c0;font-style:italic;text-align:center;">
<p>"In a world run by process, the Inquisitor is the question the process cannot answer."
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ High Concept</div><div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:12px;">
<p><b>Wonderland of the Enigma</b> takes place in a civilization defined by efficiency, codified process, and controlled repetition. By Year 3261, the great institutions of the known worlds have solved — or believe they have solved — most variables of social function, resource flow, and interpersonal conflict. What remains unresolved is not mechanical. It is personal. It is psychic. It is the problem of what people do when the system stops telling them who to be.
</p>
</div><div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:12px;">
<p>The world runs on <b>Paradigm energy</b> — six institutionally sanctioned frameworks through which Inquisitors access, process, and project power. These are not just metaphysical — they are bureaucratic. To use a Paradigm is to declare an institutional affiliation and to accept the terms of its oversight. The Designation Rank system tracks your authority and your deviation from sanctioned behavior simultaneously.
</p>
</div><div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;">
<p>Underlying this is a deeper substrate: <b>Eminence</b> and <b>Null energy</b>. Before the institutions, before the Paradigms, there was something older. What the Keno dimension represents, why some individuals express it without training, and what happens when a person approaches the limit of their nature — these are the questions WTE asks across every campaign.
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ Theme and Tone</div>
<table style="width:100%;border-collapse:separate;border-spacing:8px;">
<tbody><tr>
<td style="width:25%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:5px;padding:14px 16px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#7ab2d8;margin-bottom:8px;font-weight:700;">Identity</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">Who are you when your power is institutional? When the authority you carry is also a leash? WTE asks characters to define themselves apart from and against their Paradigm.</div>
</td>
<td style="width:25%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:5px;padding:14px 16px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#7ab2d8;margin-bottom:8px;font-weight:700;">Trust</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">Inquisitors work in teams but report to structures that have their own goals. Every relationship is threaded with the question: is this person your ally, or your handler? Can you tell the difference?</div>
</td>
<td style="width:25%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:5px;padding:14px 16px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#7ab2d8;margin-bottom:8px;font-weight:700;">Wonder</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">The title is not ironic. There are things in Year 3261 that no institution has classified, forces that no Paradigm fully explains. The player-facing experience should include genuine awe alongside the political tension.</div>
</td>
<td style="width:25%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:5px;padding:14px 16px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#7ab2d8;margin-bottom:8px;font-weight:700;">Control</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">The system does not hate you. It has no feelings about you whatsoever. That is the horror. The Mogul's network isn't malicious — it's functional. Resisting a system with no malice requires a different kind of courage.</div>
</td></tr></tbody></table>
<div style="background:#0d0d0d;border-left:2px solid #4e7fa5;padding:12px 16px;margin-top:12px;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.8;font-style:italic;">The emotional register of WTE is <b>eerie, somber, and enigmatic</b>. Sessions can be tense, occasionally terrifying, sometimes transcendent, and rarely comedic. It is not grim for grittiness's sake — it takes its world seriously, and it asks players to do the same.</div></div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ Who You Play</div><div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>You play an <b>Inquisitor</b> — an individual sanctioned by the <b>Indra</b> network to investigate anomalies, enforce Paradigm law, or operate in gray zones where the institutions do not officially act. The word "sanctioned" is doing a lot of work in that sentence.
</p>
</div>
<table style="width:100%;border-collapse:separate;border-spacing:8px;">
<tbody><tr>
<td style="width:33%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:5px;padding:12px 16px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#3a8050;margin-bottom:8px;">Tools</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">Some Inquisitors are genuinely institutionally loyal — they believe in the Paradigm network, trust the Mogul's governance model, and operate within it competently. They are tools of the system and they know it. Their stories are about what happens when the system asks something of them they can't provide.</div>
</td>
<td style="width:33%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:5px;padding:12px 16px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#c09a30;margin-bottom:8px;">Saboteurs</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">Some Inquisitors carry the credentials while working against the network. They attend audits, pass compliance reviews, and then spend their off-hours pulling threads. They live in performance — always managing what the Eminence Audit Counter records about them.</div>
</td>
<td style="width:33%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:5px;padding:14px 16px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#a03535;margin-bottom:8px;">Saviors</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">Some Inquisitors believe they can fix the Mogul's network from inside — or believe that the right action at the right moment can change the trajectory of the known worlds. They are idealists in a world calibrated to exhaust idealism.</div>
</td></tr></tbody></table>
<div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;margin-top:12px;line-height:1.7;">The <b>Indra</b> organization is the institutional frame through which Inquisitors are tracked, ranked, and mobilized. It is not a government — it is something more diffuse, a network of credential-holders that overlap with every major Paradigm institution while claiming loyalty to none specifically. Whether Indra is the resistance or another layer of control is a question WTE deliberately leaves open.</div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ What You Do at the Table</div><div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>WTE sessions typically involve one or more of the following core activities. The Director assembles these into encounters with layered structure — rarely is a scene purely one type.
</p>
</div>
<table style="width:100%;border-collapse:separate;border-spacing:8px;">
<tbody><tr>
<td style="width:50%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:5px;padding:12px 14px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#4e7fa5;margin-bottom:6px;">Exploration and Investigation</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">WTE worlds are mega-systems — orbital stations, underground cities, layered habitation rigs. Inquisitors move through these spaces gathering data, mapping anomalies, and establishing what the standard institutional record has omitted. Discovery is not safe. What you find may not want to be found.</div>
</td>
<td style="width:50%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:5px;padding:12px 14px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#4e7fa5;margin-bottom:6px;">Confrontation</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">Not every conflict is combat. Confronting a corrupt Paradigm officer, auditing a facility against an uncooperative administrator, pushing through a Social Encounter with perfect Pressure rolls — these are confrontations with institutional resistance, not just kinetic enemies.</div>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:5px;padding:12px 14px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#4e7fa5;margin-bottom:6px;">Combat</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">The Pressure Engine resolves kinetic encounters with the same core formula as social and investigation challenges. Combat uses Action Priority, CAS Zones, and the AAV formula directly. It is lethal, fast, and requires resource management — your Synaptic Space, Genus access, and Weapon Slots all matter the moment stakes go kinetic.</div>
</td>
<td style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:5px;padding:12px 14px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#4e7fa5;margin-bottom:6px;">Narrative and Political Maneuvering</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">Your Designation Rank is not just a power level — it determines what authority you can claim, who will talk to you, and what the Eminence Audit Counter tracks against you. Managing your institutional footprint is active gameplay, not background flavor.</div>
</td></tr></tbody></table>
</div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#a03535;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ Conflict and Stakes</div><div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>WTE operates at three levels of conflict simultaneously. Good campaigns have all three active at once.
</p>
</div>
<table style="width:100%;border-collapse:separate;border-spacing:8px;">
<tbody><tr>
<td style="width:33%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:2px solid #a03535;border-radius:0 5px 5px 0;padding:12px 14px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#a03535;margin-bottom:6px;">The Mogul</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">The Mogul is not one person. It is the organizing principle of institutional control in Year 3261 — the belief that any problem can be solved by a sufficiently sophisticated system of ranking, compliance, and resource routing. The Mogul doesn't need villains. The system runs on its own momentum. Fighting it means finding the leverage points — the specific individuals, decisions, and events that the system cannot self-correct around.</div>
</td>
<td style="width:33%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:2px solid #c09a30;border-radius:0 5px 5px 0;padding:12px 14px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#c09a30;margin-bottom:6px;">System vs. Self</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">The Designation Rank tracks your institutional record — every high-profile Pressure roll, every deviation, every audit, every time you pulled on a thread the system wanted left alone. This is not flavor. Accumulated deviation changes how NPCs interact with you, what authority you can claim, and eventually whether you can remain in operation at all. Your character's relationship with the system is mechanical.</div>
</td>
<td style="width:33%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:2px solid #4e7fa5;border-radius:0 5px 5px 0;padding:12px 14px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#4e7fa5;margin-bottom:6px;">Personal and World Stakes</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">WTE campaigns carry both intimate personal stakes — what this Inquisitor needs to resolve, who they can still trust, what they will sacrifice — and world-level stakes — what actually changes if the party succeeds or fails. The two are meant to be inseparable. What happens at the world scale should run through the characters personally.</div>
</td></tr></tbody></table>
</div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#4e7fa5;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ Genre and Inspirations</div><div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>WTE occupies the space between cinematic Sci-Fi and Low Fantasy. Technology is sophisticated and pervasive but not dystopian in a neon-rain way — it is institutional, mundane, deeply embedded in how people live. The fantastical elements (Eminence energy, Paradigm abilities, Keno contact, Anima) emerge from within the world's logic rather than breaking it.
</p>
</div>
