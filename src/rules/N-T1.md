<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Genetic_Profile:">Genetic Profile:</span></h3>
<p>The Novus-Tauron are the accursed descendants and biological echoes of the original Tauron, infamous for the calamity known as <i>Tauron-87</i>. While the Tauron lineage was thought to be erased following the First Ruina, the Novus variants emerged as haunting reminders of that tragedy—beings that retained not only Tauronic abilities but fragments of their genetic memory.
</p><p>Despised by nearly every Dominion and regarded with disgust by most sentient species, the Novus-Tauron live under constant persecution. Even the Voaultons, genetically descended from Tauron experimentation, shun them as malformed remnants of an era that should never have been repeated. Their existence evokes both horror and pity, their biology a synthesizing mix of flesh, nerve, and sentient memory.
</p><p>Physically, they are distorted reflections of their ancestors. Their nervous system exists externally, running along their skin in visible crimson streaks and luminous veins. Around their necks cluster bulbous nodes resembling exposed miniature brains, pulsating gently beneath the surface. To blend with society, most Novus-Tauron shroud themselves in heavy fabrics and armor, concealing these physiological signatures. However, their most feared mutation is their ability to dislodge portions of these neural systems—projecting their nerves outward to ensnare, assimilate, or temporarily override the motor control of other beings. This psychic parasitism has made them synonymous with corruption and loss of free will.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="General_Synopsis:">General Synopsis:</span></h2>
<p>Reviled yet resilient, Novus-Tauron occupy a grim niche in the political and social hierarchy of the Sectors. Their very existence is a reminder of history’s greatest atrocity—an echo of the Tauron’s sins revisited upon their descendants. Considered <i>abominations</i> and <i>failed progeny</i>, many live in isolation, forced underground or into the outer colonies where law and prejudice blur.
</p><p>Yet, amid their suffering, new alliances have begun to form. The Deviants, long advocates for societal outcasts, have extended aid to the Novus-Tauron, fostering a fragile solidarity. Through this connection, they met the Xeno, whose own history of persecution and genetic manipulation forged an unspoken kinship. Together, the Novus-Tauron and Xeno have established a powerful undercurrent—a covert alliance of the marginalized that grows quietly in strength while the great dominions, oblivious to the rising tension, begin to take notice.
</p>
