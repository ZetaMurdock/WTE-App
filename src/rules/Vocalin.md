# Vocalin

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr">

<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Vocalin">Archetype: <i>Vocalin</i></span></h1>
<p><i>Through speech, I dominate.</i>
</p><p>The <b>Vocalin</b> is a manipulator archetype that weaponizes <b>voice, intent, and emotional resonance</b>. Rather than exerting control through force or illusion, the Vocalin forges <b>living bonds</b> through speech—threads of influence that connect minds, emotions, and bodies. Their words do not persuade; they <b>imprint</b>.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  The Vocalin operates as a <b>backline controller and support manipulator</b>, buffing allies, destabilizing enemies, and reshaping combat through emotional and psychological pressure.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  The Vocalin alternates between <b>rapid repositioning and verbal execution</b>. They are capable of sudden bursts of speed or deliberate stillness, depending on the context of the encounter and the number of active bonds.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  Vocalins struggle against <b>illusion-based effects</b>, sound nullification, and <b>non-mental forms of forced manipulation</b>. Their control weakens if bonds are not actively maintained or if the target is severed from emotional influence.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  <b>Vocal Binds</b> — ethereal tethers manifested through speech, linking the Vocalin to the minds and bodies of affected creatures.</li></ul>
<p><i>The Vocalin thrives in chaos, coordination, and prolonged encounters.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetry:_Vocalin_Action_Economy">Asymmetry: <i>Vocalin Action Economy</i></span></h1>
<p>Upon choosing <b>Vocalin</b>, your Actor gains its <b>Asymmetry</b>, defining a playstyle built on <b>rapid verbal commands and layered influence</b>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy">Action Economy</span></h2>
<ul><li><b><i>6</i> Standard Actions</b>  Used to issue commands, reinforce bonds, reposition through influence, or manipulate emotional states.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>0</i> True Opportunity Attacks</b>  The Vocalin does not retaliate through instinct—they dominate proactively.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>3</i> Bonus Actions</b>  Used to maintain Vocal Binds, shift emotional states, or reinforce existing effects.</li></ul>
<p>Vocalins act frequently but lightly—power emerges through <b>accumulation</b>, not single actions.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Vocal_Binds">Aspect: <i>Vocal Binds</i></span></h1>
<p>All <b>Vocalin</b> actors begin with the <i><b>Vocal Binds</b></i>, representing <b>the conversion of speech into tangible emotional and physical influence</b>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function"><i>Base Function</i></span></h2>
<p>Vocal Binds are ethereal tethers extending from the Vocalin’s voice to the <b>back of the neck or core presence</b> of affected creatures. These binds transmit emotion into physical response—what is felt becomes what is done.
</p><p>Through Vocal Binds, the Vocalin may issue <b>Basic Commands</b>:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Sleep</b> — Target makes a <b>Wisdom Save</b> against your DC</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Influence</b> — Target makes an <b>Action Priority Save</b> against your DC</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Anger</b> — Target makes an <b>Endurance Save</b> against your DC</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Avoid</b> — Target makes a <b>Dexterity + Wisdom Save</b> against your combined DC</li></ul>
<p>Commands manifest as emotional compulsions that translate into physical behavior.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b>  Mundane distractions, fear effects, simple pain responses.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b>  Illusions, silence effects, psychic interference, severed line-of-sound or emotional nullification.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b>  The Vocalin releases the bind, loses consciousness, or the bond is forcibly disrupted.</li></ul>

<p>Unlocked after achieving <b>3 Asymmetries</b>.
</p><p>Each subtype reflects a <b>philosophy of vocal dominance</b>, shaping how the Vocalin’s words manifest.
</p>

<p><i>The voice that soothes.</i>
</p><p>The Voice of Vueala focuses on <b>harmony, unity, and reinforcement</b>, turning Vocal Binds into stabilizing forces.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Subtype_Features">Subtype Features</span></h2>
<ul><li><b>Soothing Resonance:</b>  Allies bound by your voice gain resistance to fear, panic, and morale damage.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Harmonic Command:</b>  Commands issued to allies grant minor buffs instead of compulsions.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Shared Breath:</b>  You may redirect emotional damage from allies to yourself through active Vocal Binds.</li></ul>

<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Tone_of_Bale"><span class="new" title="Tone of Bale (page does not exist)" data-uncrawlable-url="L3dpa2kvVG9uZV9vZl9CYWxlP2FjdGlvbj1lZGl0JnJlZGxpbms9MQ==">Tone of Bale</span></span></h1>
<p><i>The voice that fractures.</i>
</p><p>The Tone of Bale embodies <b>discord, rage, and internal collapse</b>, weaponizing emotional instability.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Subtype_Features_2">Subtype Features</span></h2>
<ul><li><b>Rupturing Cadence:</b>  Anger commands inflict escalating penalties if targets fail repeated saves.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Emotional Feedback:</b>  Enemies suffer backlash damage when resisting your Vocal Binds.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Shattered Will:</b>  Targets under multiple emotional states suffer reduced saving throws.</li></ul>

</p><p>This subtype specializes in <b>motion, displacement, and reactive control</b>, using Vocal Binds as vectors of force.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Subtype_Features_3">Subtype Features</span></h2>
<ul><li><b>Directive Pulse:</b>  Commands can forcibly reposition affected creatures.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Echo Step:</b>  You may move instantly toward or away from a bound target as a bonus action.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Resonant Impact:</b>  Movement caused by your commands can trigger environmental effects.</li></ul>

</p>
