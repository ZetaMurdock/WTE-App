    <div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:12px;">
<p>Within the territory of the Mogul, a <b>Chapter</b> is a massive, institutional monolith responsible for governing a foundational aspect of modern civilization and society[cite: 60, 96]. They serve as the administrative buffer between the high-level executive intelligence of the Spirals and the daily execution of law by the Inquisitors[cite: 67, 70, 96]. 
</p>
    </div>
    <div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;">
<p>Each of the five Spirals commands exactly <b>six Chapters</b> under their branch of the Executive Command Protocol (ECP), translating high strategic intent into systemic societal control[cite: 73, 74]. However, a Chapter's philosophical mandates and structural protections exist *only* inside the geofenced borders of the Mogul Territory; outside it, their frameworks are completely void, leaving the government with free rein to act without limitation[cite: 60].
</p>
    </div>
</div>
