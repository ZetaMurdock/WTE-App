<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Reality_warper_-_The_Magus">The Reality warper - The Magus</span></h2>
<p><b>Celesti Bondr</b> was a curious girl, full of life and energy. She loved to experience the world, always outside playing with her friends or exploring the outdoors. She had a happy, normal life—a loving family with a mother and father who cherished her, a golden retriever dog, and a cat. She lived in a beautiful house and went to school just like any other child.
</p><p>One day, while playing with her friends, they dared her to enter a haunted house on the outskirts of town. Though hesitant, Celesti accepted the challenge and entered. Inside, she discovered an ancient amulet that, when touched, bonded itself to her skin. This amulet granted her strange, powerful abilities that terrified her. She began to perceive the world in a way she never had before, seeing the very elements and compounds that made up everything around her.
</p><p>At first, she played with her newfound powers, manipulating the world as she wished—shifting things, bending reality to her will. Excited, she ran out of the house to show her friends. But instead of awe, they reported her to the government, fearing what they didn’t understand.
</p><p>Her once-normal life was shattered. The government seized her, and she was experimented on endlessly. The amulet, having merged with her, disappeared into her being, leaving her power and blood as the only things they wanted. They drained her of blood constantly, attempting to replicate her abilities in soldiers. They succeeded, creating a new breed of soldiers, known as <b>Codexian Mathematicians</b>, who could wield similar powers. Eventually, Celesti managed to escape. She wandered through various sectors, searching for solace. In time, she returned home, only to discover that her family had moved on without her. They were living a life of luxury, with a son who had everything Celesti once had—and more. When she tried to reconnect, they screamed at her, calling the police to have her removed. With no place left to turn, Celesti spiraled into despair. In the end, she took her life, hanging herself in the attic of the very haunted house where it all began.
</p><p>Her lifeless body remains there, and the amulet slowly reconstructed itself, waiting for another soul to awaken it—waiting for the next <b>Magus</b> to rise.
</p>
