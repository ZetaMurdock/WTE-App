<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p>From the darkest reaches of the farthest sectors rises the <b>Nigraldi Dominion</b>—a sprawling, cancerous power driven by <b>reproduction, body augmentation, and relentless expansion</b>.
</p><p>The Nigraldi are not a single species.
</p><p>They are a <b>composite</b>.
</p><p>Entire peoples are subsumed into the Dominion, their bodies altered, merged, and repurposed. What remains is ravenous and grotesque—forms shaped by function alone, stripped of identity and restraint.
</p><p>Even the <b>Higher Echelons</b> avoid them.
</p><p>So severe is the threat that protections are placed around so-called <i>“Droids”</i>, ensuring they are never exposed to Nigraldi contact. The fate awaiting any conscious mind taken by the Dominion is considered <b>worse than death</b>.
</p>

</p><p>You survived only because you were <b>too young</b>.
