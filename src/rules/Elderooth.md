<p>&gt; The **Elderooth** are an ancient, highly intelligent, and magically adept race, originating from the deepest oceans of early Earth. Renowned for their mastery of **Codexian Magic**, they are both scholars and strategists, having established the foundations for the 14 Enigmatical Orders. Emerging from their aquatic confines, they now influence the surface realms and beyond, bringing wisdom and subtle power to those who encounter them.

<p>1. **Codexian Aptitude**
</p>
<pre>   Elderooth are natural masters of Codexian Magic. They gain a bonus to all Intelligence and Arcana checks and may use Codexian-related abilities with reduced difficulty. This does **not** grant automatic spellcasting or reality-altering power.
</pre>
<p>2. **Aquatic Adaptation**
</p>
<pre>   Elderooth can breathe underwater, withstand extreme pressures, and move efficiently in aquatic environments. On land, they gain moderate movement bonuses in swimming pools, shallow water, or rivers.
</pre>
<p>3. **Enhanced Memory &amp; Learning**
</p>
<pre>   Elderooth can recall vast amounts of information with ease, granting advantage on History, Investigation, and Knowledge-based skill checks. They can learn new languages or systems faster than most other races.
</pre>
<p>4. **Tentacular Dexterity**
</p>
<pre>   Elderooth possess prehensile limbs or tentacle-like appendages, giving them versatility in combat, manipulation, or multi-tasking. They gain advantage on one additional manual action per round, such as grappling, manipulating objects, or performing intricate tasks.
