<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p>The <b>Genesis Collapse</b> background represents characters born from <b>creation and destruction intertwined</b>.
</p><p>You are a <b>construct</b> — or descended from one — shaped by the collapse or rebirth of something greater than yourself. What ended did not simply vanish. Its remnants became <b>you</b>.
</p><p>This origin can take two forms:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Cosmic Genesis</b>  Your existence began at a cosmic scale — the collapse of a star, the birth of a black hole, a dimensional rupture, or the death of a celestial body.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Metaphorical Genesis</b> <i>(Curator-Friendly)</i>  Your creation came from the collapse or rebirth of something worldly:  the destruction of an asteroid economy, the eruption of a super-volcano, the fall of a megastructure, or the end of a civilization.</li></ul>
<p>Both interpretations are equally valid.
</p><p>What matters is not the scale — but the <b>impact</b>.
</p>

<p>Your character was <b>externally changed</b> by this event.
