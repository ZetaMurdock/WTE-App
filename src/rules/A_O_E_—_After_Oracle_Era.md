<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p>The <b>After Oracle Era</b> marks the moment reality collapsed inward after the Oracle’s disappearance. No guidance. No prophecy. No certainty.
</p><p>The people prayed — not knowing <i>who</i> they prayed to.
</p><p>It was not <b>Enon</b>, who now held the helm of the multiverse.
</p><p>It was something else.
</p><p>Something neither good nor evil.
</p><p>Something <b>twisted</b>.
</p><p>It answered.
</p><p>They called it <b>the Will</b>.
</p><p>Those who were heard were <b>changed</b>.
</p><p>You are one of them.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Your_Place_in_the_A.O.E">Your Place in the A.O.E</span></h2>
<p>You were born on <b>Earth</b> — untouched by the greater collapse until it reached you.
</p><p>When the Will answered the prayers of the lost, it did not save you.
</p><p>It <b>marked you</b>.
</p><p>You became <b>tainted</b> by its presence — altered in ways you do not fully understand.
</p><p>Soon after, you were <b>captured</b> and taken from Earth, sent to <b>Aulorn</b>, where you survived as a <b>slave</b> in an ever-shifting world that no longer allowed permanence, safety, or easy connection.
</p><p>You learned quickly:
</p>
<ul><li>Trust is fragile</li></ul>
<ul><li>Bonds break</li></ul>
<ul><li>The world changes without warning</li></ul>
<p>And yet, you endured.
</p>
