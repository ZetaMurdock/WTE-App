<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_ChainBound">Archetype: ChainBound</span></h1>
<h3><span id=".E2.80.9CBreak_the_world_before_it_breaks_you..E2.80.9D"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="“Break_the_world_before_it_breaks_you.”"><i>“Break the world before it breaks you.”</i></span></h3>
<p>ChainBound is the Archetype of overwhelming power, explosive burst damage, and <b>reaction-forcing pressure</b>. Its users thrive on aggression, punishing hesitation and forcing combatants into unfavorable decisions. The drawback is intentional — <b>low Action Priority</b>, giving enemies windows for Counterplay.
</p><p>ChainBound is not subtle; it is domination through sheer force.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b> Burst Damage, Forced Reactions</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b> Rushdown, overwhelming pressure</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b> Low Action Priority → enemies get windows to respond</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b> Chains (Linked Objects)</li></ul>
<p>ChainBound Actors break open fights with raw explosiveness, but must choose carefully when to commit, because enemies can exploit their lowered priority.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetry:_Chainbound_Action_Economy">Asymmetry: Chainbound Action Economy</span></h1>
<p>Upon choosing ChainBound, your Actor gains its <b>Asymmetry</b>, which includes the following <b>Action Economy</b>:
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy:">Action Economy:</span></h3>
<ul><li><b>1 Standard Action</b>  Used for primary attacks, abilities, and burst mechanics.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>1 True Opportunity Attack</b>  Triggered by enemy actions; cannot be negated unless stated.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>1 Free Action</b>  Reserved for small manipulations or activating chain effects.</li></ul>
<p>This tightly focused action suite reinforces ChainBound’s role:
</p><p><b>Strike hard. Force reactions. Punish movement.</b>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span id="Aspect:_Chains_.28Linked_Object.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Chains_(Linked_Object)">Aspect: Chains (Linked Object)</span></h1>
<p>All ChainBound Actors begin with the <b>Chains Aspect</b>, representing a metaphysical connection between objects, materials, and targets.
</p>
<h3><span id="Chains_.E2.80.94_Base_Function"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Chains_—_Base_Function">Chains — Base Function</span></h3>
<ul><li>You gain the ability to <b>Link objects together</b>, but only if they share a <i>constructive category</i>:</li></ul>
<ul><li>Cup → Mug → Vase (all hollow containers)</li></ul>
<ul><li>Iron rod → Iron gate → Iron spear (all ferrous constructs)</li></ul>
<ul><li>Etc.</li></ul>
<p>A <b>Chain</b> is:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable</b> by physical means</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent</b> unless voluntarily dismissed</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Still affected by external forces</b> (gravity, magical displacement, reality shifts, etc.)</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sentient_Wyrd">Sentient Wyrd</span></h3>
<p>Your Asymmetry automatically grants:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Sentient Wyrd</b>  The Linked Object becomes semi-alive, gaining:</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Its own True Opportunity Attack</b></li></ul>
<ul><li>The ability to reposition within 5ft (or equivalent space)</li></ul>
<ul><li>Reactive awareness of threats</li></ul>
<p>The Sentient Wyrd is treated mechanically as:
</p>
<ul><li>An extension of you</li></ul>
<ul><li>A separate reactive entity</li></ul>
<ul><li>A “living chain” that interprets danger</li></ul>
<p>This allows ChainBound Actors to <b>dominate the battlefield through intelligent control of space</b>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Warden"><span class="new" title="Warden (page does not exist)" data-uncrawlable-url="L3dpa2kvV2FyZGVuP2FjdGlvbj1lZGl0JnJlZGxpbms9MQ==">Warden</span></span></h2>
<p><i>“Bind the world so nothing escapes.”</i>
</p><p>Warden expands the concept of chains from constructed objects to <b>natural materials</b>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Warden_Features">Warden Features</span></h3>
<ul><li>Link <b>elements</b>:</li></ul>
<ul><li>Lightning</li></ul>
<ul><li>Ice</li></ul>
<ul><li>Fire</li></ul>
<ul><li>Air</li></ul>
<ul><li>Water</li></ul>
<ul><li>Link <b>raw earth materials</b>: stone, metal, crystal, sand</li></ul>
<ul><li>Gain access to <b>Essence Linking</b>, allowing you to:</li></ul>
<ul><li>Target creatures with <b>lower Ode levels than you</b></li></ul>
<ul><li>Sever, restrict, or weaken abilities</li></ul>
<ul><li>Clamp down on spellcasting, mobility, or regenerative functions</li></ul>
<p>Warden is battlefield control through <b>environmental mastery</b>.
</p>

<ul><li>Create <b>linked living entities</b> you can:</li></ul>
<ul><li>Position</li></ul>
<ul><li>Move</li></ul>
<ul><li>Command</li></ul>
<ul><li>Reshape</li></ul>
<ul><li>Use as weapons or shields</li></ul>
<p>This subtype is powerhouse <b>puppeteering</b> and mental domination.
</p>

<p><i>“You may run. You won’t get far.”</i>

</p>

<ul><li>Suffer <b>stacking penalties</b> if they fail</li></ul>
<ul><li>Create <b>reaction-based traps</b> that trigger when creatures move</li></ul>
<ul><li>Specializes in:</li></ul>
<ul><li>Movement lockdown</li></ul>
<ul><li>Escape prevention</li></ul>
<ul><li>Grappling and snaring</li></ul>
<ul><li>“Zone imprisonment”</li></ul>
<p>Prisoner embodies the <b>inescapable captor</b>.
</p>
