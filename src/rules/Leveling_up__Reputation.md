<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Leveling">Leveling</span></h2>
<p>Levels can be gained directly by leveling up your Reputation, With Sub-Archetype Levels you gain a point every 5 levels up to level 60, they will allow you to allocate and unlock other Sub-Archetype abilities of the same branch. With Archetypes you gain 3 Levels and Level 100 and will allow you to allocate them in Archetype skills within the same branch. +1 WP Level every 5 levels.
</p>
