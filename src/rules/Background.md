<h2><span id="Background_.26_Origin_Overview"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_&amp;_Origin_Overview">Background &amp; Origin Overview</span></h2>
<p>Background in <i>Wonderland of the Enigma</i> ties character creation directly into the setting’s history.
</p><p>It is about <b>who you were before the story starts</b> and <b>when</b> in the world’s fractured timeline you truly belong.​
</p><p>Backgrounds are:
</p>
<ul><li>~80% narrative: personality, scars, culture, social position.</li>
<li>~20% mechanical: starting knowledge, edge cases, and targeted bonuses.​</li></ul>
<p>Each character’s <b>Origin</b> has two parts:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Origin Planet</b> – where you came from.</li>
<li><b>Background</b> – when and how you lived before the campaign.</li></ul>
<hr>
<h2><span id="Time.2C_Eras.2C_and_Perspective"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Time,_Eras,_and_Perspective">Time, Eras, and Perspective</span></h2>
<p><i>Wonderland of the Enigma</i> is set in the year <b>3261</b>—the 32nd century. Across this span, roughly <b>25 wars</b> and multiple technological rises and collapses have reshaped civilization.​
</p><p>The timeline is divided into two experiential eras:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Surman</b> – the <b>Oracle Period</b>
<ul><li>Sometimes referenced as <b>O.E.</b> (Oracle Era).</li>
<li>Runs from <b>Year 00 to 2079</b>.</li>
<li>Age of rapid expansion, prophetic systems, and high‑pressure experimentation.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Layman</b> – the <b>Post‑Oracle Period</b>
<ul><li>Sometimes referenced as <b>A.O.E.</b> (After Oracle Era).</li>
<li>Runs from <b>2080 to the present year 3261</b>.</li>
<li>Age of slowed innovation, fragmented knowledge, and long consequences.​</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>In play:
</p>
<ul><li>“<b>Past</b>” usually means <b>Surman</b>.</li>
<li>“<b>Present</b>” usually means <b>Layman</b>.</li></ul>
<p>Your Background chooses a <i>position</i> in or relative to these eras, which changes what your character takes for granted.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="What_Background_Does_in_Play">What Background Does in Play</span></h2>
<p>Narratively, Background answers:
</p>
<ul><li>What part of the Oracle or post‑Oracle world shaped you.</li>
<li>How many wars, collapses, or renaissances you personally lived through or inherited.</li>
<li>Where your character’s instincts, fears, and assumptions come from.​</li></ul>
<p>Mechanically, Background may:
</p>
<ul><li>Shift what information your character already knows (history, factions, tech).</li>
<li>Bias certain skill checks (e.g., advantage on Surman lore if you are directly from that era).</li>
<li>Give small, focused bonuses or starting resources tied to your era and circumstances.​</li></ul>
<p>Backgrounds are <b>interchangeable</b> with Origin Planets in the sense that one can be chosen first and the other second, but together they define:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Where</b> you came from.</li>
<li><b>When</b> you came from.</li>
<li><b>How</b> you arrived at the current moment.</li></ul>
<hr>
<h2><span id="Era-Based_Knowledge_.26_Focus"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Era-Based_Knowledge_&amp;_Focus">Era-Based Knowledge &amp; Focus</span></h2>
<p>Different Backgrounds emphasize different <b>knowledge baselines</b>:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Surman‑rooted Backgrounds</b>
<ul><li>You may understand lost Oracle technologies, myths, or protocols more intuitively.</li>
<li>You might lack practical context for certain Layman social norms or patched systems.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Layman‑rooted Backgrounds</b>
<ul><li>You are more familiar with current factions, patchwork infrastructure, and “how people survive now.”</li>
<li>Ancient Oracle systems may feel alien, half‑remembered, or reduced to superstition.​</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>Some Backgrounds bridge the eras (e.g., descendants of Surman survivors, archivists, relic communities), giving a <b>mixed perspective</b> and flexible information focus.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="How_to_Read_This_Chart">How to Read This Chart</span></h2>
<ul><li><b>Core Theme</b>: The emotional and narrative identity of the Background</li>

<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Design_Intent">Design Intent</span></h2>
<p>No Background is stronger than another.
</p><p>They differ by:
</p>
<ul><li>Tone</li>
<li>Narrative weight</li>
<li>Degree of mystery</li>
<li>Level of Curator involvement</li></ul>
<p>Choose the one that best supports the <b>story you want to play.</b>
</p>
