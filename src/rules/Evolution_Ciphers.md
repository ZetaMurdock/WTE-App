# Evolution Ciphers

WTE · MECHANICS · PARADIGM CIPHER · EVOLUTION

## Evolution Paradigm Overview

The Evolution Paradigm distributes its Ciphers to Inquisitors aligned with the doctrine of seamless union between flesh and technology. Their work is not modification for its own sake, but the relentless pursuit of an optimized form — one in which biology and constructed system become indistinguishable from each other.

The Evolution Paradigm lives by the philosophy of Nomadic Freedom — the belief that no organism should be constrained by the limitations of its birth-form. Every cell is a candidate for refinement. Every genetic sequence is a draft awaiting revision. The Paradigm invests in the future of adaptation as aggressively as any field commander invests in their troops — and holds one revision sacred: its instruments refine life, they do not end it. Biology under the knife always keeps its vital floor.

Oversight of the Evolution Paradigm is maintained by the Shawchun Corporation, whose technological expertise and biological research infrastructure form the backbone of the Paradigm's operations. Inquisitors of the Evolution Paradigm are among the most biologically capable in the field — capable of modifying themselves or others mid-mission, inducing temporary physiological transformations, or permanently reshaping cellular architecture. An unwilling subject's biology always fights the needle: unless an entry states otherwise, hostile application is resisted with a Physical Save — Recovery.

**SHAWCHUN CORPORATION OVERSIGHT** — The Shawchun Corporation does not merely sponsor the Evolution Paradigm — it shapes it. Every Cipher distributed through Evolution channels bears Shawchun's encoded signature, and its data is accessible to the Corporation's research division. This arrangement is known, accepted, and considered the price of access to the Paradigm's most advanced biological tools.

## Offline Ciphers · TL ≤ 2.0 · Fixed / Unregulated

### Genetic Model

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 25 |
| Action | Prepared Action |
| Rank | Tester |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an animate subject, scans the subject's full genetic architecture and renders it as a precise data model stored within the Cipher's memory — a complete biological blueprint including species markers, trait expressions, latent mutations, and structural anomalies.

EFFECT — The Inquisitor can reference and share this model at any time. It functions as a baseline against which all future modifications are measured. If modifications are applied to the subject without a stored Genetic Model, reverting to the original form becomes significantly more difficult or impossible. Models can be transmitted to other Inquisitors with compatible Evolution Ciphers, enabling coordinated biological operations.

### Gene Edit

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 50 |
| Action | Prepared Action |
| Rank | Modificator |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an animate subject (with or without an existing Genetic Model), performs a direct edit to one or more genetic sequences. The scope and complexity of the edit are determined by a Mental Check — Capacity and the Inquisitor's Modificator rank, as well as the Curator's assessment of biological plausibility.

EFFECT — Allows precise permanent or temporary modifications to a subject's biology: enhancing strength, speed, or endurance; suppressing or activating dormant traits; removing inherited conditions; or introducing synthetic sequences. Modifications without a Genetic Model risk unpredictable cascades — the Curator rolls secretly to determine whether side effects emerge. With a stored Model, precision is guaranteed within the bounds of the Cipher's scope.

**RESOLUTION:** unwilling subjects resist with a Physical Save — Recovery; edits never sever vital function — biology under the knife keeps its vital floor.

### Modify

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 45 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Modificator |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an animate subject, induces a rapid but controlled morphological shift. The modification targets phenotypic expression rather than the underlying sequence — altering visible or functional traits in real time without permanently rewriting the genetic foundation.

EFFECT — The subject undergoes a temporary change to their physical form: shifting size, altering skin density, extending limb proportions, modifying surface texture, or adjusting metabolic rate. Duration is based on a Mental Check — Capacity. On expiration, the subject returns to their baseline unless a Gene Edit was applied to lock the modification in place. Repeated use on the same subject without rest can cause trait fatigue — diminishing returns on modification potency.

**RESOLUTION:** unwilling subjects resist with a Physical Save — Recovery.

### Develop

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 35 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Modificator |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an animate subject, triggers a precise acceleration of cellular development in a targeted tissue group or organ system. The affected region undergoes compressed growth and functional maturation far beyond natural timelines.

EFFECT — The targeted area heals, regenerates, or grows at accelerated rate. Wounds close over one to three rounds depending on severity. Damaged organs recover partial or full function. Atrophied systems regain strength. Can also be used to mature developing or immature tissue — enabling rapid physical growth in a controlled region. Excessive use without recovery time risks overgrowth or cellular instability in the affected area.

### Induce

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 50 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Modificator |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an animate subject, introduces a synthetic or natural biochemical compound directly into the target's biological system, bypassing standard absorption barriers and interacting directly with cellular receptors.

EFFECT — The Inquisitor selects one of three induction types: Stimulant (heightens physical performance, granting a temporary bonus to Physical Checks — Power and Density and Physical Saves — Evasion and Recovery), Suppressant (dampens metabolic activity, reducing the subject's ability output and initiative), or Adaptive (primes the subject's biology for a specific modification, reducing the SS cost of the next Evolution Cipher applied to them by half). Duration varies by compound type and subject's constitution.

**RESOLUTION:** unwilling subjects resist with a Physical Save — Recovery; compounds meter themselves — no induction reaches lethal concentration.

### Specia

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 35 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Modificator |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an animate subject, identifies and activates a Specialty Trait — a dormant or semi-expressed biological feature unique to the subject's species or lineage that has not yet expressed at full functional capacity.

EFFECT — The activated Specialty Trait becomes fully functional for the duration. Examples include: heightened sensory acuity, latent venom production, limited regenerative tissue, bioluminescent communication, or pressure-resistant physiology. If the subject has no dormant traits in accessible range, the Cipher may instead enhance one already-active trait to perform at peak expression. The Curator determines available Specialty Traits based on the subject's species background.

### Inter-Speci

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 70 |
| Action | Prepared Action |
| Rank | Xenobiologist |
| Component | Two Animate Subjects |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to two animate subjects simultaneously, creates a temporary genetic bridge between them. Compatible genetic sequences are identified, isolated, and prepared for cross-species integration. The process requires at least one Genetic Model for each subject to be established before activation.

EFFECT — Transfers one selected Specialty Trait or modified attribute from one subject to the other for the duration — or permanently, if a Gene Edit is applied to anchor it. The receiving subject temporarily experiences physiological stress as their biology adapts (Physical Save — Recovery required; failure indicates temporary incapacitation during integration). Incompatible species may experience cascading rejection responses if the Curator determines biological conflict — rejection expels the graft rather than the patient. Reserved exclusively for Xenobiologist-rank Inquisitors due to the procedure's extreme complexity.

### Temp HP

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 35 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Modificator |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an animate subject, rapidly stimulates redundant tissue growth along high-damage-exposure areas — joints, torso, skull, and exposed muscle groups. The new tissue is not permanent; it forms over the existing structure as a biologically reactive buffer, disposable by design.

EFFECT — Grants the subject temporary Hit Points equal to 2d8 + the Inquisitor's Neuronal Capacity Modifier, lasting until expended or until the scene ends. Temporary HP absorbs incoming damage before the subject's actual HP is reduced. If unused, the tissue reabsorbs harmlessly within the hour. Can be stacked with other recovery effects but cannot exceed the subject's maximum HP when combined.

### Genetic Heritage

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 50 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Modificator |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an animate subject, reaches beyond the subject's active genome and into their evolutionary history — accessing dormant ancestral sequences that pre-date the subject's current species form, encoded but no longer expressed.

EFFECT — Selectively reactivates one ancestral trait from the subject's genetic heritage for the duration. The available traits are determined by the Curator based on the subject's species lore. Examples include prehistoric endurance, environmental resistance, heightened predatory instincts, or primordial regenerative capacity. The awakened trait operates at the peak of its ancestral expression — often exceeding the power of the modern equivalent. Ancestral traits cannot be made permanent without extreme biological intervention.

## Online Ciphers · TL 3.0+ · Regulated / Upgradeable / Logged

### Rapid Adaptation

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 40 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Tester |
| Component | Animate target |

EFFECT — The target's biology rapidly adapts to current environmental conditions: in extreme cold, develops thermal insulation; in low-oxygen environments, increases blood oxygen efficiency; in high-radiation zones, cellular repair mechanisms activate. The adaptation is temporary (lasts 1 hour) but provides resistance to environmental damage. Cannot adapt to supernatural or Null-based threats.

### Evolutionary Fork

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 55 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Modificator |
| Component | Animate target |

EFFECT — Target expresses two distinct evolutionary adaptations at once for 2 rounds — gaining benefits from both at 60% efficacy of each. After 2 rounds, the body collapses back to its dominant path, and the target makes a Physical Save — Recovery (DV 13) or takes 1d8 damage from the cellular strain.

#### Actions

- Cost: 55 SS
- Save: Physical Save — Recovery, DV 13
- Fail: Damage: 1d8
- Ruling: the target expresses two adaptations at once for 2 rounds at 60% efficacy of each; the Save is made when the body collapses back to its dominant path

### Genetic Lock

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 35 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Tester |
| Component | Animate target |

**RESOLUTION:** an unwilling target resists the Lock with a Physical Save — Recovery.

## Special Ciphers · Off-Network · Granted to Exceptional Inquisitors

### S1 — New Species

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 100 |
| Action | Prepared Action (24 hours) |
| Rank | Distinguished Evolutionist |
| Component | Willing animate subject |

EFFECT — Guides the complete evolutionary transformation of a willing subject into a new species variant — a genuine biological new form. The new form is determined collaboratively by Inquisitor and Director. The subject becomes a new entry in WTE's species catalogue — with their own Dom/Rec values, Eminence Nature defaults, and innate abilities. Irreversible without counterpart Null procedure.

### S2 — Genesis

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 120 |
| Action | Prepared Action (72 hours) |
| Rank | Distinguished Evolutionist |
| Component | Collected genetic material from multiple sources |

EFFECT — Creates a completely new living organism from collected and synthesized genetic material. The new life form has: a basic genome designed by the Inquisitor; a species designation; and initial behavioral programming (not full consciousness). Shawchun Corporation authorization required. The most significant creation capability in WTE.

### S3 — The Apex

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 85 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Distinguished Evolutionist |
| Component | Self |

EFFECT — Triggers the Inquisitor's own evolution to a temporary Apex form for 5 minutes. In Apex form: one size category larger; all physical stats +3; natural attack deals 2d10 + Strength and resolves through Physical Check — Power; resistance to all non-Null damage; one unique ability from their deepest lineage (Director determines). After Apex ends: mandatory 1 full rest. Each use permanently expresses one Wryde mutation. Maximum 3 uses before the biological cost becomes life-threatening.

### S4 — Genetic Heritage: Complete

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 75 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Distinguished Evolutionist |
| Component | Self |

EFFECT — Simultaneously expresses all variant and sub-species traits available in the Inquisitor's lineage for 3 rounds. For each round: gain the benefits of up to 3 different Variant trait sets simultaneously. After 3 rounds: Physical Save — Recovery (DV 16) or the Inquisitor is unable to fully revert to base form for 24 hours (stuck between expressions).

### S5 — Life Weave

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 110 |
| Action | Prepared Action (48 hours) |
| Rank | Distinguished Evolutionist |
| Component | Two or more living organisms (willing) |

EFFECT — Permanently merges the biological traits of two or more organisms into each of the participants — all gain access to the other's most significant biological capabilities. Each participant gains: one innate ability from each other participant; shared resistance to conditions the others are naturally resistant to; and a combined Wryde expression. All participants must be willing. The bond cannot be reversed.
