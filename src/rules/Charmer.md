<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Charmer">The Charmer</span></h2>
<p>Alex Vandrew was an esteemed entertainer with a unique gift—he could understand and manipulate people to get whatever he wanted, whenever he wanted, and by any means necessary. Alex moved from bar to bar, pub to pub, learning the intricacies of human behavior and how to influence others. He was exceptional at what he did and loved every moment of it. The people loved him too, at first.
</p><p>But that love quickly turned to resentment. His actions throughout the sector began to spread like wildfire—"Look out for Alex, you might be his next victim." People grew tired of being used for his gain, whether it was for money or his own twisted pleasures.
</p><p>
Eventually, a manhunt was launched. Alex was captured and set to be executed. But just as all hope seemed lost, a girl appeared, and a contract materialized before him. With no other options, he signed it.As soon as he did, the world around him began to melt and shift. Suddenly, he could perceive the emotions of others—he could feel what they felt, understand why they felt it, and even sense their feelings toward him, if they had any. This newfound ability became his obsession, and over time, he cultivated it. He became the first Charmer.Later, Alex discovered that by sharing blood with others, they too could experience what he saw—the emotions and feelings of those around them. As for the girl who had given him the contract, she disappeared from his life, never to be seen again.Now, Alex lives a different life, one where he uses his gift to help others, rather than manipulate them. He may never understand why she helped him, but he’s grateful for the second chance she gave him.</p><blockquote></blockquote>
