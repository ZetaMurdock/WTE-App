# Exotic Weapon

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #200a30;padding:8px 14px;margin-bottom:12px;font-family:monospace;font-size:0.82em;color:#604080;">
<p><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Weapons_Guide">Weapons</a>&nbsp;› <strong class="mw-selflink selflink">Exotic Weapon</strong>
</p>
</div><div style="background:#121212;border-left:4px solid #3a1458;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:18px;"><div style="font-size:1.6em;font-weight:bold;color:#7040a0;letter-spacing:0.06em;font-family:monospace;">EXOTIC WEAPON</div><div style="color:#604080;font-size:0.85em;margin-top:4px;font-style:italic;">Void, dimensional, temporal, reality-bending. Operates outside standard physical laws.</div></div><div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #200a30;border-left:3px solid #3a1458;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;color:#a080c0;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>Exotic weapons are the rarest category in WTE's arsenal. Most are pre-Collapse recovered artifacts, Paradigm-classified ordnance, or dimensional constructs that require Exotic Varna to wield safely. They do not draw energy signatures readable by standard CAS scanners — but they trigger deep-spectrum alerts in Sanction Zoned or higher areas. Using one in public is not just illegal; it's evidence of something CAS has no category for.
</p><p>These weapons feel wrong. They exist slightly out of phase with the moment you fire them. A Void Needle doesn't cut through armor — it punctures through the dimensional membrane keeping the target cohesive. A Reality Fracture Cannon doesn't destroy a building; it makes the building uncertain about whether it exists.
</p>
Translated from WH40k's psycannon, force weapons, and vortex grenades — and from Trench Crusade's relic armaments — through WTE's Null/Void framework.<div style="background:#180a28;border-left:2px solid #3a1458;padding:8px 12px;margin-top:10px;color:#8060a0;font-size:0.88em;">
<p><b>CAS Warning:</b> Exotic weapons in Managed zones add +1 Audit risk per scene. In Sanction Zoned areas, using an Exotic weapon triggers immediate Audit.
</p>
</div></div>
