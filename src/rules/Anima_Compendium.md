# Anima Compendium

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"> <div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:8px;padding:24px 36px;margin-bottom:16px;"><div style="font-family:monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:3px;color:#a03535;margin-bottom:4px;"><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Wonderland_of_The_Enigma_Wiki">HOME</a> / ANIMA</div><div style="font-size:26px;font-weight:700;color:#dedede;letter-spacing:1.5px;">Anima</div><div style="color:#565656;font-size:12px;margin-top:6px;font-style:italic;">Celestial manifestations · Sin-anchored entities · Director-facing compendium</div><div style="background:#1a0808;border:1px solid #3a1212;border-radius:4px;padding:12px 16px;font-size:13px;color:#c0c0c0;font-style:italic;text-align:center;">
<p>"They are not born. They are not summoned. They are what happens when an emotion accumulates enough celestial energy to become real."
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#a03535;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ The Nature of Anima</div><div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:12px;">
<p>Anima are manifestations of celestial energy — the raw substrate threading through the Enigma's cosmology — given form by concentrated emotional resonance. They are not creatures in any conventional sense. They have no goals, no memories, no preferences. They have a <b>direction</b>. An Anima of Wrath destroys because Wrath destroys. An Anima of Gluttony consumes because Gluttony consumes. The behavior is not chosen. It is the emotion, expressed as action.
</p>
</div><div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:12px;">
<p>Celestial energy responds to emotion. In the Enigma's cosmology, emotion generates measurable resonant signatures in the celestial field. Under the right conditions — areas of concentrated psychic output, catastrophic events, prolonged dimensional instability — that resonance nucleates into a physical form.
</p>
</div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border-left:2px solid #a03535;padding:10px 14px;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;font-size:12px;color:#909090;">
<p>The resulting entity expresses its source emotion <b>completely</b>, without restraint, and without the moderating influence of a self. This is what makes Anima dangerous even at low tiers. A creature that is angry can choose to stop. An Anima of Wrath cannot.
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#a03535;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ Tiers of Manifestation</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;margin-bottom:12px;">Anima are observed at three intensity levels, reflecting how much celestial energy they have absorbed and how fully the source emotion has expressed itself.</div>

<p>Stalkers — scavengers and opportunists who work in celestial-energy-rich environments — are among the most frequently exposed to active Anima. Over time, this exposure has consequences. The celestial field begins to respond to the Stalker's own dominant emotional resonance. They begin to change.
</p>
</div><div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;">
<p>This process is called <b>Alter Anima</b> — the point at which a Stalker's inner self begins to manifest physically. Greed becomes physical accumulation. Hatred becomes a weaponized form. Fear becomes something that hunts in the dark. See <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Alter_Anima">Alter Anima</a> for full mechanics and statblocks.
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#a03535;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ Engaging Anima — Director Reference</div><div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:12px;">
<p>Anima cannot be negotiated with. They can be disrupted, suppressed, redirected, or destroyed. Certain Incepts and Genera abilities interact specifically with celestial energy, making them more effective against Anima than conventional attacks.
</p>
</div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #262626;border-radius:4px;padding:10px 14px;font-size:12px;color:#565656;">
<p><b>Director Note:</b> Killing an Anima at Manifested or Apex tier does not necessarily destroy the underlying celestial resonance — it disperses the form. If the emotional conditions persist in the area, a new Anima may coalesce. Dispersal should be treated as a temporary solution in any scenario where the source resonance has not been addressed.
</p>
