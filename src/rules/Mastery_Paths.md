<td>Drawing upon the primal forces of volcanic activity, the practitioner commands the destructive power of eruptions to devastate enemies and shape the battlefield. This ability taps into the raw energy and elemental fury of volcanoes, unleashing cataclysmic effects.
<ul><li><b>Lava Surge</b>: The practitioner summons a surge of molten lava, inflicting <b>Fire Damage</b> (1d10) per round to enemies within the affected area. The area of effect grows each turn, covering a wider radius with each subsequent round.</li>
<li><b>Pyroclastic Blast</b>: Once per encounter, the practitioner unleashes a devastating blast of volcanic ash and rock fragments, inflicting <b>Fire Damage</b> (1d10) and <b>Bludgeoning Damage</b> (1d6) to all enemies within a specified area. This blast continues to burn and smolder, dealing residual fire damage over time.</li>
<li><b>Environmental Manipulation</b>: The volcanic eruption reshapes the battlefield, creating hazardous conditions such as unstable ground, ash clouds, and falling debris. These environmental hazards impose penalties on enemy movement and actions.</li>
<li><b>Elemental Imbuement</b>: The practitioner's attacks and abilities are imbued with volcanic energy, enhancing their damage output with <b>Fire Damage</b> (1d10). This fire damage increases by one die step for each consecutive turn the ability remains active, up to a maximum of 3d10.</li></ul>

<li><b>Fortification</b>: The practitioner can fortify their own defenses by encasing themselves in a protective shell of solidified magma. This shell absorbs incoming damage and reflects a portion of it back to attackers as <b>Fire Damage</b> (1d10).</li>
<li><b>Magma Burst</b>: Once per encounter, the practitioner can trigger a sudden eruption of magma beneath their enemies, causing <b>Fire Damage</b> (2d10) and <b>Bludgeoning Damage</b> (1d6) to all targets in the area of effect. The eruption creates a temporary crater filled with molten lava, hindering movement and causing residual fire damage to enemies caught within.</li></ul>

</td>
<td>25

<td>The practitioner channels the relentless fury of volcanic eruptions to empower their attacks and wreak havoc upon enemies.
<ul><li><b>Eruption Cascade</b>: By invoking volcanic fury, the practitioner triggers a cascading series of eruptions around them. Each eruption deals <b>Fire Damage</b> (1d10) and <b>Bludgeoning Damage</b> (1d6) to nearby enemies, with the damage increasing exponentially with each successive eruption (1d10, 2d10, 4d10, and so on).</li>
<li><b>Ash Storm</b>: The practitioner summons a swirling storm of volcanic ash and debris, impairing enemy vision and causing <b>Bludgeoning Damage</b> (1d6) and <b>Fire Damage</b> (1d10) per round to enemies caught within the storm. The ash storm persists for several rounds, disrupting enemy actions and defenses.</li>
<li><b>Magma Surge</b>: As a pinnacle of volcanic fury, the practitioner can unleash a massive surge of molten magma that engulfs a large area, inflicting <b>Fire Damage</b> (3d10) and <b>Bludgeoning Damage</b> (2d6) to all enemies within range. The magma surge creates a zone of intense heat and fire that persists, dealing ongoing fire damage to enemies who remain within its reach.</li></ul>

<td>Volcanic Cataclysm
</td>
<td>110
</td>
<td>Embodying the cataclysmic power of volcanic eruptions, the practitioner becomes a harbinger of destruction and chaos on the battlefield. They command the full fury of volcanoes to unleash devastating attacks and wreak havoc upon their enemies.
<ul><li><b>Eruption Nova</b>: The practitioner triggers a massive volcanic eruption at a designated location, causing a catastrophic release of molten lava and volcanic debris. This eruption deals <b>Fire Damage</b> (2d10) and <b>Bludgeoning Damage</b> (2d6) to all enemies within a wide radius. The eruption's force knocks back enemies and leaves behind hazardous terrain that continues to burn, dealing residual fire damage each round.</li>
<li><b>Pyroclastic Storm</b>: Once per encounter, the practitioner summons a swirling storm of superheated ash and rock fragments. This storm inflicts <b>Fire Damage</b> (2d10) and <b>Bludgeoning Damage</b> (2d6) per round to enemies caught within its area of effect. The storm persists for several rounds, disrupting enemy actions and causing continuous damage as it moves unpredictably across the battlefield.</li>
<li><b>Magma Tsunami</b>: Channeling the volatile energy of a volcanic tsunami, the practitioner unleashes a wave of molten magma that surges across the battlefield. Enemies caught in the path of the magma tsunami suffer <b>Fire Damage</b> (3d10) and <b>Bludgeoning Damage</b> (3d6) as they are engulfed by the searing heat and crushing force of the magma flow. The magma tsunami leaves behind a trail of scorched earth and molten rock, creating impassable terrain and dealing ongoing fire damage to enemies attempting to navigate through it.</li>
<li><b>Volcanic Rebirth</b>: As a master of volcanic cataclysm, the practitioner can invoke a surge of volcanic energy to rejuvenate themselves and allies. This ability restores health and provides temporary resistance to fire and physical damage, empowering allies to withstand enemy assaults and continue fighting with renewed vigor.</li></ul>
