<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Machinarch">Archetype: <i>Machinarch</i></span></h1>
<p><i>“Wielder of Machines, Builder of Legend”</i>
</p><p><i>The Machinarch does not cast spells nor swing fate—they <b>author mechanisms</b>. Where others see matter, the Machinarch sees <b>instruction, structure, and recursion</b>. Every creation is deliberate. Every failure catastrophic.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  <b>Architect of Innovation.</b> The Machinarch creates machines, constructs, intelligences, and systems—ranging from handheld devices to legendary engines—through deliberate study, reconstruction, and transfiguration of matter using their Station.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  <b>Preparation-locked, payoff-heavy.</b> The Machinarch is the slowest archetype to activate but one of the most adaptable if properly prepared. Entering combat without prior setup is lethal; entering combat prepared makes the Machinarch terrifyingly versatile.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  <b>Technological manipulation and disruption.</b> EMP-like effects, code corruption, AI interference, forced desynchronization, or hostile control systems can cripple or even turn Machinarch creations against them.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  <b>Mechanix Metal &amp; the Calloux Intersect.</b> A soul-bound metal that allows reconstruction of matter at the quark-level—but only within the Machinarch’s personal cognitive fabrication space known as their Station.</li></ul>
<p><i>The Machinarch does not react. They deploy.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Playstyle_Overview">Playstyle Overview</span></h2>
<p>The Machinarch is a <b>logistics-first archetype</b>.
</p><p>You do not ask “what can I do this turn?”
</p><p>You ask “what did I prepare three scenes ago?”
</p><p>They reward:
</p>
<ul><li>Foresight</li></ul>
<ul><li>Planning</li></ul>
<ul><li>Resource mapping</li></ul>
<ul><li>Environmental awareness</li></ul>
<p>And punish:
</p>
<ul><li>Improvisation</li></ul>
<ul><li>Panic</li></ul>
<ul><li>Rushed creation</li></ul>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetry:_Machinarch_Action_Economy">Asymmetry: <i>Machinarch Action Economy</i></span></h1>
<p><i>Upon choosing <b>Machinarch</b>, your Actor gains its Asymmetry, representing deliberate execution over reflex.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy">Action Economy</span></h2>
<ul><li><b><i>1</i> Standard Action</b>  Used exclusively to <b>deploy, activate, or command</b> prepared creations or enter the Station.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>5</i> Bonus Actions</b>  Used for:</li></ul>
<ul><li>Adjusting machine parameters</li></ul>
<ul><li>Issuing sub-commands</li></ul>
<ul><li>Redirecting power</li></ul>
<ul><li>Swapping modular components</li></ul>
<ul><li>Running micro-calculations</li></ul>
<p><i>The Machinarch does not “attack” often. Their turn is an orchestration phase.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Mechanix_Metal">Aspect: <i>Mechanix Metal</i></span></h1>
<p><i>All <b>Machinarch</b> actors begin with <b>Mechanix Metal</b>, representing <b>Creation Through Absolute Understanding</b>.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function">Base Function</span></h2>
<p><b>Mechanix Metal (Mechanix-M)</b> is a soul- and blood-bound material gifted by a Machinarch’s mentor. It serves as:
</p>
<ul><li>A <b>universal construction catalyst</b></li></ul>
<ul><li>A <b>reality-interface material</b></li></ul>
<ul><li>A <b>key</b> to the Calloux Intersect (Station)</li></ul>
<h3><span id="The_Station_.E2.80.94_Calloux_Intersect"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Station_—_Calloux_Intersect">The Station — Calloux Intersect</span></h3>
<p>The Station is a <b>mental fabrication space</b> manifested through Mechanix-M. Inside it, the Machinarch may:
</p>
<ul><li>Study materials</li></ul>
<ul><li>Simulate outcomes</li></ul>
<ul><li>Reconstruct atomic and subatomic structures</li></ul>
<ul><li>Design Artificial Languages, AIs, or Engines</li></ul>
<ul><li>Assemble Machines of Legend</li></ul>
<p>Time conversion rules apply <b>only within the Station</b>.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span id="Material_Study_.26_Density_Rule"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Material_Study_&amp;_Density_Rule">Material Study &amp; Density Rule</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Study_Rules">Study Rules</span></h3>
<ul><li>Every material has an <b>Information Density</b>, ranging from <b>0.1 to 10.1</b>.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Study Time Scales Linearly</b> with density:</li></ul>
<table class="fandom-table">
<tbody><tr>
<th><b>Action Type</b>
</th>
<th><b>Time per 0.1 Density</b>
</th></tr>
<tr>
<td>Passive (out of combat)
</td>
<td>10 seconds
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td>Active (in combat)
</td>
<td>15 seconds
</td></tr></tbody></table>
<ul><li><b>Formula:</b></li></ul>
<ul><li>Passive Time = Density × 100 seconds</li></ul>
<ul><li>Active Time = Density × 150 seconds</li></ul>
<p><b>Examples:</b>
</p>
<ul><li>Marble (Density ~2.7) → 2.7 × 100 = 270 seconds passive, 2.7 × 150 = 405 seconds active</li></ul>
<ul><li>Diamond (Density ~3.52) → 352 seconds passive, 528 seconds active</li></ul>
<ul><li>Extremely dense material (Density 10.1) → 1,010 seconds passive, 1,515 seconds active</li></ul>
<p><b>Note:</b> During combat, these times reflect <b>Active Time</b>. Studying a material in advance (outside combat) allows you to significantly speed up deployment during engagements.
</p><p>Once study completes, the Machinarch may choose one operation:
</p>
<hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Operations">Operations</span></h3>
<ul><li><b>Transfigure</b>  Change form while preserving material identity  <i>(metal → articulated plates, liquid alloy, nanoframe)</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Reconstruct</b>  Break down and rebuild at atomic/quark level  <i>(metal → machine, AI core, kinetic engine)</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affexte</b>  Convert matter into <b>pure energy</b></li></ul>
<ul><li>Requires containment</li></ul>
<ul><li>Failure results in:</li></ul>
<ul><li>Explosion</li></ul>
<ul><li>Neutralization</li></ul>
<ul><li>Reality backlash  <i>(Director determines severity via roll)</i></li></ul>
<hr>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b>  Physical force, mundane destruction, theft.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b>  Tech domination, forced AI override, reality suppression, hostile machine logic.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b>  Death of the Machinarch or complete soul severance.</li></ul>
