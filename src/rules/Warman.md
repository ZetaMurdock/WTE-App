<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Warman">Archetype: <i>Warman</i></span></h1>
<p><i>Through Trial and Combat, I Reign</i>
</p><p><i>The Warman is the Empire’s blade made flesh—an unyielding frontline warrior who bends fate through steel, vows, and repetition of violence. They dominate space, punish movement, and carve inevitability into every fight.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  <b>Frontline damage dealer and zone controller.</b> The Warman exists to occupy space, threaten lethal retaliation, and break enemies through sustained pressure.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  <b>Relentless and reactive.</b> The Warman strikes on their own turn and during others’, chaining attacks through positioning, opportunity, and momentum.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  <b>Vulnerable to supernatural manipulation.</b> Mind control, reality distortion, and non-physical damage bypass their strengths and challenge their philosophy.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  <b>The Ward.</b> A living oath forged into weaponry, allowing the Warman to manifest any sanctioned Imperial weapon as an extension of their will.</li></ul>
<p><i>A straightforward but brutally effective archetype. The Warman rewards positioning, timing, and commitment—simple tools used with overwhelming intent.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetry:_Warman_Action_Economy">Asymmetry: <i>Warman Action Economy</i></span></h1>
<p><i>Upon choosing <b>Warman</b>, your Actor gains its <b>Asymmetry</b>, defining its relentless tempo and battlefield dominance.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy">Action Economy</span></h2>
<ul><li><b><i>2</i> Standard Actions</b>  Used primarily for <b>weapon attacks</b>, stance shifts, or pressure-based maneuvers.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>2</i> True Opportunity Attacks</b>  Triggered when enemies <b>move recklessly, disengage, attack allies nearby, or expose themselves through failed actions</b>.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>2</i> Bonus Actions</b>  Used for <b>weapon morphing, bracing, marking targets, or reinforcing position</b>.</li></ul>
<p><i>The Warman is designed to act constantly. Even when it is not their turn, the battlefield must respect their presence.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Ward">Aspect: <i>Ward</i></span></h1>
<p><i>All <b>Warman</b> actors begin with the <b>Ward</b>, representing <b>Imperial Authority, Oathbound Steel, and Right of Armament</b>.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function">Base Function</span></h2>
<p><i>The Ward is bestowed by the Empire and grants the Warman access to its sanctioned arsenal.</i>
</p>
<ul><li>The Ward may <b>transform into any single-person weapon</b> the Empire recognizes.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Transformation requires <b>1 full turn</b> to fully manifest.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Once manifested, the weapon is <b>indistinguishable from a real weapon</b> in function, weight, and presence.</li></ul>
<ul><li>If wielded by another creature, it behaves as a normal weapon with no special properties.</li></ul>
<ul><li>The Warman may <b>dismiss or recall</b> the Ward at any time, causing it to manifest in their possession or revert to its sigil form.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b>  Mundane damage, disarm attempts, or non-Imperial weapon suppression.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b>  Anti-empire effects, oath-breaking conditions, high-tier reality or authority negation.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b>  The Warman breaks their vow, defects from their Empire, or is formally stripped of rank.</li></ul>
