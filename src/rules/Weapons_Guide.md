# Weapons Guide

<p>All Inquisitors carry up to <b>4 weapon slots</b>. You may draw 2 weapons simultaneously; switching to a stowed weapon costs 1 Action. Weapons are divided into four categories — Kinetic, Energy, Exotic, and Hybrid — each with distinct CAS zone behavior, Paradigm access rules, and aesthetic identity.
</p>

<p>Physical force delivery: ballistic, impact, gauss, railgun, and bladed mechanisms. Kinetic weapons function in <b>Sealed CAS zones</b> where Energy and Exotic weapons are suppressed — the only category that works everywhere. Cannot be detected by energy scanners. <i>Heavy, mechanical, loud.</i> They leave shell casings, impact craters, and structural damage.
</p>

Significantly more damaging than equivalently sized Kinetic weapons. Neural-keyword variants can target <b>MHP directly</b>. Flagged by CAS scanners in Managed zones and above — creating Audit risk Kinetic weapons do not. <i>Precise, clinical, devastating.</i>
