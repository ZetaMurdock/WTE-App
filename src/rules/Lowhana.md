# Lowhana

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Lowhana">Lowhana</span></h2>
<p>The Lowhana are a variant of Mirage that became permanently genetically tainted after a freak accident. This incident left a singular Mirage with the ability to transform only into dead creatures. In a desperate attempt to survive the cold, it transformed into a wolf, but unfortunately, it still perished in that form. Scientists later discovered the wolf and brought it to a lab run by a scientist named Valin. He preserved the wolf and hung it in a test tube, realizing that it was a Mirage—specifically, a defective one. Valin decided to meld the Mirage's tissue with that of his daughter, who suffered from a condition that would soon lead to her death. Her cells struggled to divide, and the old cells had difficulty decaying, leaving her as a slowly growing, cancerous, tumorous mass.
</p><p>Valin thought that perhaps he could kickstart her cellular process by giving her the genetic DNA of a Mirage. He strapped her to a table and began drilling into her bone marrow to administer the first injection, followed by an injection into her spine. As this happened, the Mirage's DNA accelerated the process that had been slowed down. However, since the Mirage was defective and could only transform into beasts, the same fate awaited her—only worse.
</p><p>She grotesquely transformed into a cancerous, malnourished, and sickly wolf, with elongated arms and fingers, and extremely sharp claws that could almost cut through anything. Her eyes were as black and deep as the abyss, and her fur coat, while beautiful, was terrifying, filled with fleshy appendages. In this feral state, she devoured her father. Afterward, she transformed back and now lives with an alternate being within her body that calls itself the Hyena. As she killed her father, another entity grew from his body, gaining abilities similar to his daughter's and also taking on the name Hyena. They became linked, and the Hyena manifested within her, leading to the creation of a new species known forever as the Lowhana, the Undeath Bringers.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Skills_and_Attributes">Skills and Attributes</span></h2>
<ul><li>InterPerona Each Lowhana possesses a deep-seated alternate persona known as the <b>Hyena</b>, a manifestation of their subconscious desires unbound by morality or ethics. This entity can materialize by overtaking the original body, initiating a transformation that takes <b>one minute of Active Time</b>. During this transformation, all enemies within <b>30 feet</b> gain <b>Fear Stacks</b> equal to the transformation duration (1 stack per second), and for each Fear Stack, all their rolls suffer <b>x2 Disadvantage</b>. Once fully transformed, the Lowhana experiences a surge of power, receiving a <b>+10 bonus to all base Ability Scores</b>, plus an additional boost equal to their <b>Contribution score</b>. Their movement speed and attack range <b>double</b>, and all <b>Action Priority rolls become instant successes</b>, eliminating the need for a roll check. If they successfully win <b>Action Priority</b>, they gain a <b>True Opportunity Attack</b>, though this attack cannot be a <b>Critical Success</b> unless the target is <b>Fatally Wounded</b>, in which case it becomes a <b>Guaranteed Critical Hit</b>, instantly killing the target.</li>
<li>InterhEX The Lowhana’s ability to morph its body mid-action allows them to shift their anatomy during an attack, optimizing their strikes in real time. This transformation is <b>instantaneous</b>, requiring no preparation or additional action cost. By activating <b>InterhEX</b>, the Lowhana gains <b>Advantage</b> on both <b>Action Priority</b> and <b>Priority Rolls</b>, ensuring they can act before their opponent. Additionally, the attack’s damage increases by an amount equal to their <b>Attack Power</b> modifier, making each strike devastatingly effective.</li>
<li>Heyna Bind When a Lowhana kills a creature, they can resurrect it as a <b>Lowhana Husk</b>, a zombie-like thrall that follows their commands. These Husks act as their servants, mindless yet loyal, carrying out their master’s will without question. If the Lowhana <b>dies</b>, they can consume the life force of one of their Husks, immediately reviving at its location. Each time they resurrect in this way, they gain a <b>permanent +2 bonus to a stat of their choice</b>, slowly growing stronger with every cycle of death and rebirth.</li>
<li>Roar of the Dark Beast With a monstrous roar that echoes with the essence of death, the Lowhana forces all creatures within <b>60 feet</b> to make a <b>Wisdom + Mental Fortitude Dice Challenge</b> against a <b>DC 50</b>. Those who fail are <b>forced to submit</b>, unable to act offensively, and are afflicted with <b>every Negative Status Effect</b> for <b>20 minutes of Passive Time</b>. After this time has passed, the afflicted become <b>mentally marked</b>, a lingering imprint of the Lowhana’s dominance. If the Lowhana dies while a marked creature exists, they can <b>manifest a clone of that being</b>, using its mental state and knowledge as a template. However, the clone is left physically drained, suffering from the <b>Winded Condition</b> upon creation.</li></ul>
