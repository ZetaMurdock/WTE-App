<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Orialia">Orialia</span></h2>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore_Backstory">Lore Backstory</span></h2>
<p><small><i>In the far reaches of the Enigmatica Universe, hails an enigmatic race known as the Orialia. Emerging from the outer Realms, these extraordinary beings possess a physiology that defies mortal comprehension. Their bodies embody the epitome of perfection, devoid of any imperfections, and have the wondrous ability to shape-shift at will. To behold an Orialia is to witness ethereal beauty incarnate, for their skin emanates a celestial golden glow, their teeth gleam with the purity of pearls, and their flowing hair cascades like silken strands of moonlight.</i></small>
</p><p><small><i>Cloaked in an air of mystique, the Orialia are the closest beings in existence that embody the essence of the Oracle. Often veiled in folklore and legend, little is known about their origins, yet tales speak of their unparalleled strength, boundless knowledge, and the wellspring of ancient wisdom that resides within them. They stand as paragons of perfection, unrivaled by any other, and are regarded as the epitome of excellence in wielding the profound art of Codexian Magic.</i></small>
</p><p><small><i>Within the realms of the Universe, the Orialia shine as beacons of absolute power and reverence. Their adeptness in employing the intricate language of Codexian Magic is unmatched, rendering them among the most formidable practitioners of this arcane craft. Their mastery over the Codexian language grants them unprecedented control over the very fabric of reality itself. They command the forces of creation and manipulation, transcending the boundaries of existence as bestowed upon them by the divine hand of God.</i></small>
</p><p><small><i>While the extent of their powers remains a mystery, it is known that the Orialia possess extraordinary vision, a gift beyond comprehension. They possess the sight of gods, perceiving the intrinsic attributes of all they gaze upon, delving deep into the depths of souls and unraveling the intricate tapestry of auras that surround each living being. This supernatural ability grants them insights and understanding beyond mortal comprehension, empowering them to discern hidden truths and unseen energies. Legends speculate that their unparalleled racial skills stem from this divine sight, an innate connection to the very essence of all existence.</i></small>
</p><p><small><i>Enigmatic and inscrutable, the Orialia find solace within their ethereal homes, the magnificent Star Villages, vast organic starships that traverse the cosmos. These enigmatic structures, numbering a staggering 100 thousand, serve as sanctuaries for the Orialia, shielding them from the prying eyes of the Universe. The true extent of the Orialia population remains shrouded in mystery, an enigma that eludes all who dare to fathom it. To challenge the Orialia in warfare would be an exercise in futility, for their might is immeasurable, and their mastery of power borders on the divine.</i></small>
</p><p><small><i>Beware, for the echoes of a fateful paradox that once unfolded with King Adomas linger in the annals of time. Should one dare provoke the Orialia, the cataclysmic consequences that shook the Universe may be unleashed once more. To cross paths with these enigmatic beings is to dance on the precipice of destiny, where the very fabric of reality can be reshaped by their whims. The Orialia, guardians of the sublime and harbingers of perfection, stand as testaments to the immeasurable wonders that lie beyond mortal comprehension.</i></small>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Abilities">Abilities</span></h2>
<p>Celestial Shapeshifting: The Orialia possess the extraordinary ability to shape-shift at will, seamlessly transforming their physical form into any desired shape or appearance. They can alter their appearance, size, and even mimic other beings or objects with impeccable accuracy.
</p><p>Divine Sight: With their godlike vision, the Orialia can perceive the intrinsic attributes of all they gaze upon. They can delve deep into the depths of souls, unravel the complex tapestry of auras surrounding living beings, and gain insights into hidden truths and unseen energies. Their divine sight empowers them with unparalleled understanding and discernment. Roll Control and Perception DCs of 15 or higher total to Gain Information about Objects in a Wide area Determined by your movement speed.
</p><p>Star Village Guardianship: As the guardians of the magnificent Star Villages, the Orialia have honed their skills in protecting their ethereal homes. They possess exceptional defensive capabilities and are adept at utilizing advanced technologies and defenses to safeguard their sanctuaries from external threats. Gain advantage while within on or around the starship as long as it is within 2000 feet of you gain Plus 2 for each member aboard or within the Starship as long as they're again aboard or within 1000 feet of the starship.
</p><p>Star Transference: as the Guardian of the Starship, you have the ability to harness the power that the starship endows within not just its but within the environment as well. Manipulate the Reverberations of the Starship to change and rewrite the environment and world around you By Dismantling and Reconstructing Cosmic Alteration Phenomena.
</p><p>Paradox Unraveling: The Orialia have an innate ability to navigate and unravel paradoxes and intricate temporal anomalies. They can perceive the subtlest fluctuations in the fabric of time and space, allowing them to traverse temporal rifts, mend paradoxes, and maintain the delicate balance of the universe. Their skills in paradox management make them invaluable guardians of cosmic stability.
</p>
