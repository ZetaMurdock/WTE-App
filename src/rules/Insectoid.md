</div><div style="background:#121212;border-left:4px solid #285030;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:18px;"><div style="font-size:1.6em;font-weight:bold;color:#488060;letter-spacing:0.06em;font-family:monospace;">INSECTOID</div><div style="color:#408060;font-size:0.85em;margin-top:4px;font-style:italic;">Group: Omenity — Multi-form chitinous species with exoskeletal biology and regenerative capacity.</div></div><div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #102018;border-left:3px solid #285030;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;color:#80b090;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>Insectoids have an unusually high Recessiveness value (40) relative to their Dominance (24) — their traits frequently recede in hybrid offspring, making them one of the most genetically "submissive" species in mixed lineages. This is a practical reality for a species that navigates a complicated relationship with its own variants.
</p><p>The Archnida were originally engineered as Hyomen military weapons from Insectoid genetic material. After Tauron-87 disrupted the command structures that controlled them, the Insectoid have since reclaimed partial authority — but Archnida that serve the Nigraldi represent the faction that was never reclaimed. The Cerioisk are something else entirely: a living anomaly born during the Witching Moon, classified at existential-tier threat level by most governing bodies.
</p>
</div>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="HYBRID_INTERACTION">HYBRID INTERACTION</span></h2>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #102018;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:16px;color:#80b090;font-size:0.9em;">
<p>Insectoid Dom 24 / Rec 40 — the highest Recessiveness value among main species and well above their Dominance. Insectoid traits frequently recede in hybrid offspring, making them one of the most genetically passive species in the setting. The Eyeless trait (non-visual sensory navigation) is among the most persistent when it does express, as it functions as a full sense replacement rather than a supplement.
</p>
