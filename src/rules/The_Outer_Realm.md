<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p>The <b>Outer Realm</b> is the domain of the <b>Spiral Engine</b>—a being existing <b>outside all things</b>, beyond space, time, and understanding.
</p><p>Nothing and no one can commune with the Spiral Engine directly—<b>except its children</b>.
</p><p>You are a <b>distant, diluted descendant</b> of these children. Through your lineage, you carry a <b>direct connection</b> to the Spiral Engine itself.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Your_Role">Your Role</span></h2>
<p>Through this connection, you gain the ability to <b>see possibilities yet to occur</b>—visions of events that <b>have not yet passed</b>, informed by the Spiral Engine’s perception of creation, both direct and indirect.
</p><p>The exact manifestation of this ability is <b>up to you and your Director</b>, allowing full narrative flavoring:
</p>
<ul><li>You might receive visions as fleeting images, whispers, or emotions.</li></ul>
<ul><li>You might interpret possibilities symbolically or literally.</li></ul>
<ul><li>The ability may influence your intuition, foresight, or decision-making in unique ways.</li></ul>
