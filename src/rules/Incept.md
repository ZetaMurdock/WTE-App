# Incept

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"> <div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:8px;padding:24px 36px;margin-bottom:16px;"><div style="font-family:monospace;font-size:10px;letter-spacing:3px;color:#6e50a0;margin-bottom:4px;"><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Wonderland_of_The_Enigma_Wiki">HOME</a> / <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Asymmetries">ASYMMETRIES</a> / INCEPT</div><div style="font-size:26px;font-weight:700;color:#dedede;letter-spacing:1.5px;">Incept</div><div style="color:#565656;font-size:12px;margin-top:6px;font-style:italic;">Ancestral asymmetry · Director Splitting · Wryde manifestation · Identity through inheritance</div><div style="background:#181818;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:4px;padding:12px 16px;font-size:13px;color:#c0c0c0;font-style:italic;text-align:center;">
<p>"What emerges is not always what you could have been — but what your character has become."
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#6e50a0;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ What is an Incept?</div><div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:12px;">
<p>An <b>Incept</b> is an Asymmetry that represents a hidden truth of a character's <b>Inherence</b> — a latent ancestral trait, biological asymmetry, or existential echo that emerges through narrative progression.
</p>
</div><div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:12px;">
<p>Incepts do not directly affect the external world. They exclusively reshape the <b>character</b>: body, instincts, internal mechanics, perception, and identity. Incepts are not chosen freely. They are <b>discovered</b>, <b>filtered</b>, and <b>manifested</b> through play.
</p>
</div><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#565656;margin-bottom:8px;">Each Incept Gives the Character:</div>

</td>
<td style="width:25%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:2px solid #6e50a0;border-radius:5px;padding:10px 12px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#6e50a0;margin-bottom:5px;">Incept Effect</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;">A permanent mechanical expression</div>
</td>
<td style="width:25%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-top:2px solid #6e50a0;border-radius:5px;padding:10px 12px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#6e50a0;margin-bottom:5px;">Director Splitting</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;">A recalibration of potential</div>
</td>

</div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#6e50a0;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ Dominance and Recessiveness</div><div style="color:#c0c0c0;font-size:13px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:12px;">Every Incept expresses itself through two values. These govern whether an Incept strengthens, mutates, becomes unstable, or is suppressed in future progression.</div>
<table style="width:100%;border-collapse:separate;border-spacing:8px;">
<tbody><tr>
<td style="width:50%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:2px solid #6e50a0;border-radius:0 5px 5px 0;padding:14px 16px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#6e50a0;margin-bottom:8px;font-weight:700;">Dominance</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">How strongly the Incept asserts itself within the character's Inherence. Derived from Species Elderooth Values, Lineage composition, and Heritage influence (if unlocked).</div>
</td>
<td style="width:50%;background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:2px solid #565656;border-radius:0 5px 5px 0;padding:14px 16px;vertical-align:top;"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1.5px;color:#565656;margin-bottom:8px;font-weight:700;">Recessiveness</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.7;">How suppressed, unstable, or dormant the expression is. A high Recessiveness value may prevent an Incept from manifesting or cause it to mutate unpredictably.</div>
</td></tr></tbody></table>
<div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #262626;border-radius:4px;padding:8px 12px;margin-top:10px;font-size:11px;color:#565656;">
<p><b>These values are not static.</b> They may shift due to narrative High Points or Low Points, gaining additional Incepts, or Director Splitting.
</p>
</div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#6e50a0;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ Director Splitting — The Mandatory Process</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;"><b>Director Splitting</b> is the mandatory process used <i>every single time</i> a character obtains an Incept. It ensures internal biological and existential consistency, prevents redundant or identical expressions, and controls the evolution of the character's trait potential.</div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:4px;padding:10px 14px;margin-bottom:8px;display:flex;gap:12px;align-items:flex-start;"><div style="color:#6e50a0;font-family:monospace;font-size:16px;font-weight:700;flex-shrink:0;width:24px;">1</div><div><div style="color:#dedede;font-weight:600;font-size:12px;margin-bottom:4px;">Compile the Potential Incept Pool</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.6;">The Director gathers all Incepts the character could possibly manifest based on Template Species, Lineage, Heritage (if applicable), and current narrative state. This forms the <b>Potential Incept Pool</b>.</div></div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:4px;padding:10px 14px;margin-bottom:8px;display:flex;gap:12px;align-items:flex-start;"><div style="color:#6e50a0;font-family:monospace;font-size:16px;font-weight:700;flex-shrink:0;width:24px;">2</div><div><div style="color:#dedede;font-weight:600;font-size:12px;margin-bottom:4px;">Value Comparison</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.6;">The Director compares the Dominance and Recessiveness values of every Incept in the pool. If two or more Incepts have <b>identical Dominance and Recessiveness values</b>, only one may remain. All others with matching values are omitted. Omitted Incepts are not necessarily lost — but cannot manifest at this time.</div></div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:4px;padding:10px 14px;margin-bottom:8px;display:flex;gap:12px;align-items:flex-start;"><div style="color:#6e50a0;font-family:monospace;font-size:16px;font-weight:700;flex-shrink:0;width:24px;">3</div><div><div style="color:#dedede;font-weight:600;font-size:12px;margin-bottom:4px;">Value Shifting</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.6;">After omissions, the Director applies <b>value shifts</b> to the remaining Incepts. Shifting represents the character's Inherence reorganizing itself to accept change. Shifts may increase or decrease Dominance or Recessiveness, destabilize certain expressions, or suppress future manifestations. Must be applied consistently.</div></div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:4px;padding:10px 14px;margin-bottom:8px;display:flex;gap:12px;align-items:flex-start;"><div style="color:#6e50a0;font-family:monospace;font-size:16px;font-weight:700;flex-shrink:0;width:24px;">4</div><div><div style="color:#dedede;font-weight:600;font-size:12px;margin-bottom:4px;">Trait Pool Reset</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.6;">The character's <b>Incept Trait Pool is reset</b>. All previously manifested Incepts remain active. Future potential traits are recalculated. Suppressed or omitted expressions may no longer be available. This reset only affects <i>future Incepts</i>, never those already gained.</div></div></div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:4px;padding:10px 14px;display:flex;gap:12px;align-items:flex-start;"><div style="color:#6e50a0;font-family:monospace;font-size:16px;font-weight:700;flex-shrink:0;width:24px;">5</div><div><div style="color:#dedede;font-weight:600;font-size:12px;margin-bottom:4px;">Incept Manifestation</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;line-height:1.6;">One Incept from the adjusted pool manifests. The character gains the Incept's <b>Memory</b>, gains the <b>Incept Effect</b>, and the final Dominance and Recessiveness values are recorded. A <b>Wryde mutation</b> immediately appears.</div></div></div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-radius:6px;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:12px;"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#6e50a0;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:14px;">▸ Wryde Mutation — Visual and Behavioral Expression</div><div style="color:#909090;font-size:12px;margin-bottom:12px;line-height:1.7;">Whenever an Incept manifests, it <b>automatically produces a Wryde mutation</b>. A Wryde is a visible, behavioral, or sensory alteration tied to the Incept's expression. Wrydes affect only the character; they never alter the external world directly. The Wryde type is determined at the moment the Incept is gained.</div>
