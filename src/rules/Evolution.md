# Evolution

> "The genes and information are there — we simply need to harness them."

The Evolution Paradigm studies the mutable boundaries of life. Its members view genetic code not as a constraint but as a toolkit — a living library to be rewritten, refined, and repurposed. They pursue transformation without fear, believing adaptation is both nature's oldest weapon and its ultimate path forward. Survival through transformation: all things evolve, but only those who understand biology's code control what they become.

## Paradigm Identity

**Who Joins** — Genetic scientists, bio-engineers, hybrid soldiers, and symbiotic hosts who see the body as a machine open for optimization. Some are idealistic innovators; others, self-made monstrosities seeking perfection.

**Culture** — Intensely curious and morally flexible. They view mutation not as corruption but as creation. Their culture encourages experimentation — often to extremes — and they regard ethics as a form of evolutionary stagnation. The line between human and monster is a false dichotomy.

**Archetype Feel** — Biotech / Primal Adaptation. The Evolution Inquisitor is a living proof of concept — their body itself is the experiment and the result simultaneously.

## Roll Axis Profile

Evolution occupies 6 of the 7 universal Roll Paths. Its native mechanics emphasize bodily force, compressed physical execution, avoidance, recovery, active perception, and preservation or projection of intent through transformation. Evolution Ciphers do not natively specialize in Capacity, though Evolution Inquisitors may still make Capacity rolls normally whenever the situation calls for them.

| Roll Path | Native | Direction | Evolution Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power | ● | Check | Impose force through enhanced musculature, natural weapons, altered mass, reinforced anatomy, or direct biological aggression. |
| Density | ● | Check | Coordinate rapid bodily changes, natural weapons, movement sequences, and multiple physical adaptations inside a limited Action Priority window. |
| Evasion | ● | Save | Avoid threats through adaptive movement, altered posture, reactive anatomy, balance, or instantaneous bodily correction. |
| Recovery | ● | Save | Resist mutation failure, disease, poison, exhaustion, hostile environments, biological corruption, and physical destabilization. |
| Capacity | — | Check | Not native to Evolution mechanics; complex cognitive operation remains universally available. |
| Perception | ● | Check / Save | Read biological signals, identify bodily abnormalities, detect environmental or genetic threats, and resist false or corrupted sensory information. |
| Influence | ● | Check / Save | Project instinct, dominance, biological presence, or transformed identity, and preserve personal intent through radical physical alteration. |

**PROFILE STANDARD** — Evolution's Roll Profile describes the Paths its native Paradigm mechanics are built around. It does not prevent an Evolution Inquisitor from making Capacity rolls during ordinary play, and it does not overwrite the natural Roll Path of weapons, Genera, copied abilities, environmental effects, or other external systems.

## Evolution Paradigm Affinity

Evolution does not favor every statistic contained inside its Roll Profile. Its Affinities represent the capabilities its doctrine trains most aggressively: physical force, bodily endurance, biological adaptation, and physical stability during transformation.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Favored Attributes | Strength · Endurance |
| Favored Specialties | Adaptation · Balance |

Strength represents the force produced by an optimized or transformed body. Endurance represents the body's ability to survive stress, mutation, damage, and repeated biological alteration. Adaptation reinforces survival through changing biological conditions. Balance reinforces bodily stability, reactive posture, and control of a form that may no longer obey ordinary anatomy.

| Designation Rank | Favored Attribute | Favored Specialty |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | +1d5 | +1d10 |
| 3–5 | +2d5 | +2d10 |
| 6–8 | +3d5 | +3d10 |
| 9 | +4d5 | +4d10 |

**SIGNATURE CONVERGENCE · RECOVERY** — Endurance + Adaptation creates Evolution's primary fixed Convergence. When both legitimately govern a Recovery Save, Evolution receives both the Favored Attribute and Favored Specialty Affinity pools. This represents the Paradigm at its purest: surviving mutation, poison, disease, exhaustion, regenerative strain, environmental exposure, assimilation, and biological destabilization by changing faster than the threat can destroy the body.

**FAVORED POWER · STRENGTH** — Strength is Favored whenever it legitimately governs a Power Check, granting Evolution its Attribute Affinity pool for direct physical force. This reflects enhanced muscle, altered mass, natural weapons, reinforced anatomy, or other biological modifications that increase the body's ability to impose force. Evolution does not automatically gain Specialty Convergence on Power unless another rule provides a Favored Specialty that governs that resolution.

**FAVORED EVASION · BALANCE** — Balance is Favored whenever it legitimately governs an Evasion Save, granting Evolution its Specialty Affinity pool for physical stability and reactive bodily correction. This reflects altered centers of mass, additional limbs, shifting anatomy, instinctive posture changes, and the ability to remain functional in a body that may transform during movement.

**WHAT EVOLUTION DOES NOT FAVOR** — Evolution has native access to Density, Perception, and Influence, but it does not automatically gain Affinity on Action Priority, Precision, Intelligence, Perception, Charisma, or Cunning. These Paths remain part of Evolution's mechanical vocabulary without being institutional areas of exceptional training.

## Authority Profile

Members hold prioritized clearance to government biolabs, gene modification centers, and augmentation facilities. They receive preferential treatment in supply chains involving biological or medical technologies — effectively granting discounts or early access to bio-components, especially in emergencies.

**Advancement** — Research-driven and results-based. Inquisitors must log genetic experiments — on subjects, biomass, or themselves — and submit verified reports or papers to registered labs. These submissions advance Authority Rank, reinforcing credibility across research networks.

## Authority Ranks · Designation Progression

| Rank | Title | Role | Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tester | Novice researcher experimenting with isolated modifications | Access to basic biotechnical equipment and genetics interfaces |
| 2 | Modificator | Field specialist performing adaptive enhancement procedures | Authorized self-modification and local specimen collection rights |
| 3 | Xenobiologist | Head of bio-teams or mutation research divisions | Can command mobile labs and request live subject samples |
| 4 | Mutagenist | Senior authority on biological transformation | Full access to high-tier augmentation facilities; can authorize new genetic lines or public adaptation programs |

**Note on Rank 4** — The Mutagenist title encompasses the full authority of the Evolution Paradigm. Sub-specializations (biotic strain focus, xenobiological classification, surgical modification) are selected internally and are not formally designated.

## Combat Specialty

Evolution Inquisitors excel in adaptive combat roles. They manipulate their biology mid-conflict — increasing muscle density, regrowing tissue, or converting biomass into weapons or armor as needed. Their native mechanics span Power, Density, Evasion, Recovery, Perception, and Influence, with Recovery serving as their strongest fixed Affinity Convergence.

**Style** — Brutal, reactive, and self-sustaining. Some fight as towering monstrosities; others as living swarms of muscle and intent. All embody evolution's ruthless efficiency: adaptation without hesitation.

## Specialties · Genera Domains

**Elemental Energy** — Harnesses biological adaptations tied to natural forces — fire-resistant tissues, hydro-reactive cells, shock-conduits built into the body. These effects most commonly interact with Recovery when enduring exposure and Power or Evasion when the adaptation is used physically.

**Eldritch Energy** — Explores mutations beyond physical evolution through parasitic or extradimensional DNA patterns — the body reaching past what nature intended. Such alterations commonly pressure Recovery and may also engage Influence or Perception when identity, instinct, or sensory interpretation is altered.

These domains symbolize Evolution's expanding canvas — from nature's frameworks to cosmic re-engineering.

## Interactions & Philosophy

The Evolution Paradigm believes that the line between human and monster is a false dichotomy — one drawn by those afraid of growth. Where others see horror, they see possibility. The only question that matters is not should we evolve, but have we evolved enough?

**Allies** — Science (data sharing), Cognition (neural integration), Warfare (enhanced soldiers and augmented combat units)

**Tensions** — Remnant (views self-alterations as desecrations of the human condition), Simulation (opposing views on what constitutes real vs. constructed change)
