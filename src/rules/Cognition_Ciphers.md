# Cognition Ciphers

WTE · MECHANICS · PARADIGM CIPHER · COGNITION

## Cognition Paradigm Overview

The Cognition Paradigm distributes its Ciphers to Inquisitors who specialize in mental navigation, spatial manipulation, and psychological extraction. These tools are not weapons in the conventional sense — they are precise instruments that engage, reshape, and disassemble the architecture of the mind.

The Cognition Paradigm operates on a distinct Philosophy of Duality — a doctrine that defines its members as either numb and unyielding, or deeply empathetic and precisely controlled. Every Inquisitor who carries a Cognition Cipher must choose a side of this duality and embody it fully. There is no middle ground. The Paradigm's power lies in that extremity — those who feel nothing and strike without hesitation, and those who feel everything and channel that sensitivity into something devastating.

Cognition Ciphers operate through cognitive penetration, emotional amplification, and structural memory alteration. The Cipher holder must understand the mind as intimately as a surgeon understands anatomy — probing, incising, and restructuring with intentional care or overwhelming force. And like a surgeon's, their instruments are bounded by the anatomy itself: a mind can be steered, softened, or silenced, but the Surgeon's Rule holds — no Cognition Cipher can compel a subject to end or mutilate itself, and every hold breaks at the moment it would.

**COGNITIVE RESONANCE** — An advanced mental state achievable only by Special Cipher holders. In Resonance, the Inquisitor's consciousness partially merges with a target's cognitive architecture — perceiving their thoughts, memories, and decision pathways as an extension of their own awareness. The experience is often described as disorienting or transcendent depending on the Inquisitor's duality alignment.

## Offline Ciphers · TL ≤ 2.0 · Fixed / Unregulated

### Mind Tweak

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 25 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Seeker |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an animate subject, the Cipher embeds itself into the target's cognitive signal pathways, establishing a passive monitoring link. From this point, the Inquisitor can read emotional flux and sense surface-level intention without triggering active psychic detection.

EFFECT — The Inquisitor gains the ability to subtly influence the target's thinking process — amplifying doubt, suppressing aggression, or nudging a decision toward a preferred outcome. These alterations are small and non-invasive, undetectable by normal means. Larger modifications resolve through the Inquisitor's Mental Check — Capacity contested by the target's Mental Save — Influence. If the target fails, they comply with one minor suggestion before recovering. On a Critical Success by the Inquisitor against their will, the target believes the influenced thought originated from themselves.

**SURGEON'S RULE:** suggestions that would drive the subject to harm itself or betray its core identity collapse on contact — the mind's own architecture rejects them.

### Detection

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 15 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Seeker |
| Component | Inanimate Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When affixed to an object, the Cipher synchronizes with the object's material composition and electromagnetic signature, allowing it to detect incoming signals, vibrations, or cognitive emissions within range.

EFFECT — The Inquisitor becomes aware of any cognition-based Cipher or mental ability being used within the object's detection radius. Upon detection, the Inquisitor can identify the ability type, the approximate origin point, and whether the emitter is aware of being detected. They may then attempt to trace the cognitive link back to its source.

### Brain Wash

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 40 |
| Action | Prepared Action |
| Rank | Auditor |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an animate subject, establishes deep cognitive contact. The Cipher lowers the target's mental resistance through repeated micro-pulses over the activation period, gradually softening the structural integrity of their memory architecture.

EFFECT — The Inquisitor can selectively erase, overwrite, or implant specific memories within the target's conscious recall — one memory block per activation. Implanted memories feel authentic. Erased memories leave no awareness of absence. The Inquisitor makes a Mental Check — Capacity contested by the target's Mental Save — Influence; outside parties may make a Mental Check — Capacity to identify inconsistencies within the altered memory. On Critical Failure, the target's entire recent memory block is compromised, leaving the subject temporarily amnesiac and highly suggestible.

**SURGEON'S RULE:** structural identity — name, bonds, core self — resists the pulses; a mind stripped of a block rebuilds toward its baseline over the following Downtime unless re-washed.

Hostile cognition-based Ciphers targeting the protected subject automatically fail unless the originating Check exceeds the Filter's established threshold by 10 or more. ### Arithmetic

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 40 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Empath |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an animate subject, generates a cognitive thread linking their logical processing centers to an external framework driven by the Inquisitor's own mental architecture — remote influence over how the target weighs data, makes decisions, and resolves equations.

EFFECT — The Inquisitor can inject logical constructs — inescapable paradoxes, self-contradicting belief sequences, or mathematical impossibilities — directly into the target's decision-making process. This causes the target to become locked in involuntary analytical loops, unable to act until their cognition resolves the injected construct (Curator determines resolution time). Particularly complex injections can subtly alter the target's priority hierarchy — unnoticed until a critical moment, and unwinding naturally at the target's next Downtime.

**RESOLUTION:** a mind that resolves a construct is inoculated — immune to further injections from the same Inquisitor for the rest of the scene.

### Diffuse

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 35 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Empath |
| Component | Inanimate Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When affixed to an object, causes it to emit a field of diffuse cognitive interference — low-level white noise targeting the neural signal layer of all animate beings within range, not strong enough to cause harm but sufficient to muddy perception and reaction clarity.

EFFECT — All animate subjects within the interference radius (excluding the Inquisitor and chosen allies) suffer disadvantage on Mental Checks — Capacity and Mental Saves — Perception while inside the field. Complex decision-making slows. Cognitive Ciphers used by affected subjects have their effectiveness reduced. The field is invisible and non-threatening in appearance — targets may not immediately identify the source of their deteriorating cognition.

### Algorim

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 55 |
| Action | Prepared Action |
| Rank | Empath |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an animate subject, embeds a predictive cognitive algorithm deep within their behavioral response matrix. This algorithm silently catalogs patterns — reaction times, decision tendencies, emotional triggers, and combat rhythms — building a behavioral model unique to the target.

EFFECT — The Inquisitor can access this behavioral model to anticipate the target's next action. Once per encounter, the Inquisitor may declare a Predicted Action before the target takes their turn. If the declaration is correct, the Inquisitor may immediately respond as if they had already prepared that countermeasure — gaining advantage on the next Check or Save made directly against that predicted action. The algorithm grows more accurate with extended exposure, improving from 50% match probability at first use to near-certainty after three successful encounters with the same subject.

### Resonant Field

|---|---|
| SS | 45 |
| Action | Bonus Action |

| Component | Animate (self or target) |

## Online Ciphers · TL 3.0+ · Regulated / Upgradeable / Logged

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 30 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Seeker |
| Component | Animate (self + ally) |

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 55 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Auditor |
| Component | Animate target |

The erasure is accurate — no false memories replace the gaps, just absence. Structural identity remains untouchable under the Surgeon's Rule. Online: Paradigm archives a record of all wiped content for accountability.

The download is instantaneous — the Inquisitor gains the target's level of expertise in the designated domain for 24 hours. Target retains their knowledge — it is copied, not removed.

### Dream State

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 40 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Auditor |
| Component | Animate target |

EFFECT — Target makes a Mental Save — Perception (DV 13). On failure, they enter a semi-aware state: they believe they are experiencing a vivid dream. They continue basic functions but cannot make complex decisions, cannot use abilities requiring Intent, and perceive their surroundings with surreal distortion. The dreaming mind still guards its body — it drifts away from obvious hazards. They will fail the first Roll that they are given, then be brought out of the state if targeted by anything that requires a roll. Duration: until the target takes damage or succeeds a repeated Mental Save — Perception (retry each round at +1 cumulative bonus).

## Special Ciphers · Off-Network · Granted to Exceptional Inquisitors

### S1 — Omniscient Moment

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 70 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Distinguished Cognitioneer |
| Component | Self |

EFFECT — For exactly 1 round, the Inquisitor knows: the precise intended action of every creature within 60 ft before that creature has taken it; the exact current HP and active conditions of all creatures in range; and the optimal defensive and offensive decision for this round. This is not prophecy — it is extreme predictive modeling at Cipher fidelity. After the Omniscient Moment ends: Downtime for 1 round.

### S2 — Soul Stitch

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 60 |
| Action | Prepared Action |
| Rank | Distinguished Cognitioneer |
| Component | Two willing subjects |

EFFECT — Creates a permanent cognitive bond between two willing subjects. Both are always aware of: the other's physical location (accurate to 30 ft); their current emotional state; and whether they are conscious. While bonded: both gain +1 to all Checks when within 60 ft of each other. The Stitch is permanent and does not require SS to maintain.

### S3 — The Empty

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 80 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Distinguished Cognitioneer |
| Component | Target creature |

EFFECT — Target makes a Mental Save — Influence (DV 18). On failure, their personality, will, and memory are temporarily suspended — leaving a blank, obedient biological shell. The Empty subject: follows any direct instruction its body can survive — the autonomic core is the one thing suspension cannot reach, and orders that would end or mutilate the shell simply halt it in place; retains full physical capability; has no emotional or moral capacity; does not retain memory of their time Empty. Duration: 1 hour. Paradigm considers unauthorized use one of its most serious ethical violations.

### S4 — Cognitive Cascade

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 90 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Distinguished Cognitioneer |
| Component | Multiple animate targets |

EFFECT — Algorim chains through up to 4 targets simultaneously — the Inquisitor puppets all of them in real time. Each target's movements are individually controllable. Targets resist with a Mental Save — Influence (DV 15 + Neuronal Capacity Modifier) each round. A puppet's self-preservation cannot be overridden — a directive that would destroy the puppet snaps the chain for that target. While Cascade is active: the Inquisitor cannot move but can channel all of their abilities through any of the Cascaded subjects. The cognitive strain deals 1d6 psychic damage to the Inquisitor per active puppet per round.

### S5 — Enlightenment

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 100 |
| Action | Prepared Action (10 min meditation) |
| Rank | Distinguished Cognitioneer |
| Component | One mystery, object, situation, or entity |

EFFECT — The Inquisitor understands the complete truth of the designated subject with absolute clarity — no ambiguity, no deception, no incomplete information. For an object: its full history, composition, and purpose. For a situation: its causes, current state, and most likely consequences. For an entity: their true nature, motivations, and all known capabilities. For a mystery: the accurate answer. Director confirms accuracy.
