<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Broker">Archetype: <i>Broker</i></span></h1>
<p><i>The Money Is There — Just for Me to Take</i>
</p><p><i>The Broker is greed refined into infrastructure. Where others fight, the Broker invests. Where others act, the Broker acquires. Wealth is not a resource to them—it is leverage, information, and inevitability.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  <b>Master Manipulator and System Controller.</b> The Broker dominates encounters through leverage, transactions, predictive analysis, and purchased advantages, capable of flexing into tank, skirmisher, or support roles when properly funded.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  <b>Escalatory and inevitable.</b> The longer the Broker observes a conflict, the more information, assets, and opportunities they accumulate—turning extended encounters decisively in their favor.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  <b>Direct physical pressure.</b> Rushdown enemies, sudden violence, and scenarios with no time to analyze or transact severely limit the Broker’s effectiveness.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  <b>B.A.C. — Business Analytical Console.</b> A personal, holographic HUD that tracks wealth, value, risk, assets, and opportunity in real time—fueling every Broker ability.</li></ul>
<p><i>The Broker is not fast, flashy, or fair. They win by ensuring the fight was decided before it began.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetry:_Broker_Action_Economy">Asymmetry: <i>Broker Action Economy</i></span></h1>
<p><i>Upon choosing <b>Broker</b>, your Actor gains its <b>Asymmetry</b>, defining its transaction-heavy, multi-step manipulation style.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy">Action Economy</span></h2>
<ul><li><b><i>4</i> Standard Actions</b>  Used for <b>analysis, transactions, asset activation, summoning, deal execution, or purchased abilities</b>.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>1</i> True Opportunity Attack</b>  Triggered when a creature <b>breaks a deal, violates an agreement, or acts against their own financial interest</b>.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>1</i> Prepared Action</b>  Used to <b>access B.A.C., mark value, finalize micro-transactions, or update risk models</b>.</li></ul>
<p><i>The Broker acts constantly, but rarely directly. Their turns resemble negotiation phases rather than attack sequences.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span id="Aspect:_Businessman.E2.80.99s_Analytical_Console_.28B.A.C..29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Businessman’s_Analytical_Console_(B.A.C.)">Aspect: <i>Businessman’s Analytical Console (B.A.C.)</i></span></h1>
<p><i>All <b>Broker</b> actors begin with the <b>B.A.C.</b>, representing <b>Greed as Intelligence, Value as Truth, and Wealth as Power</b>.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function">Base Function</span></h2>
<p><i>The B.A.C. is a personalized, non-transferable holographic HUD visible only to the Broker.</i>
</p>
<ul><li>Displays <b>total wealth, asset flow, debts, market shifts, creature value, and resale potential</b>.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Acts as the <b>activation interface</b> for all Broker abilities.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Automatically logs <b>kills, transactions, negotiations, and environmental value</b>.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Converts slain enemies into <b>Gold equal to their generated worth</b>.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b>  Physical damage, theft, or non-targeted interference.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b>  Anti-information fields, reality suppression, extreme time distortion.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b>  The Broker is bankrupt, unconscious, or willingly relinquishes their identity as a Broker.</li></ul>
