<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Reality_Shaper">Archetype: <i>Reality Shaper</i></span></h1>
<p><i>“Every problem has a formula; every formula has a consequence.”</i>
</p><p>The <b>Reality Shaper</b> manipulates reality through advanced calculations, equations, and digital constructs. By visualizing, writing, and activating equations, they manipulate energy, matter, and spatial dimensions to control the battlefield. Their playstyle blends rapid problem-solving with precise execution, turning theoretical knowledge into tangible effects.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b>  <i>Write and activate equations to manipulate reality, summon constructs, or generate energy effects, combining offensive and defensive tactics.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b>  <i>Calculative and deliberate; sets up equations, then rapidly executes sequences for maximum efficiency.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b>  <i>Dependent on preparation and focus; disrupted calculations or destroyed computational tools hinder performance.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b>  <i>Ability to manifest a personal computational interface (“the Computer”), which stores, generates, and executes equations in real-time, transforming theoretical mathematics into physical or metaphysical effects.</i></li></ul>
<p><i>The Reality Shaper excels in control, disruption, and adaptive combat. They are highly versatile in both offensive and defensive applications, capable of summoning constructs, redirecting energy, or collapsing matter with precision.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetry:_Reality_Shaper_Action_Economy">Asymmetry: <i>Reality Shaper Action Economy</i></span></h1>
<p><i>Upon choosing <b>Reality Shaper</b>, your Actor gains its <b>Asymmetry</b>, defining <b>Action Economy</b>, <b>mechanical philosophy</b>, and equation-based manipulation.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy">Action Economy</span></h2>
<ul><li><b><i>4</i> Standard Action(s)</b>  <i>Used to write, modify, and activate equations, or manifest computational constructs.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>2</i> True Opportunity Attack(s)</b>  <i>Triggered by successful equation activation or environmental interaction.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b><i>1</i> Free Action(s)</b>  <i>Used to adjust equations, prepare equations in advance, or reposition computational constructs.</i></li></ul>
<p><i>The action economy reflects a balance between preparation and execution, rewarding strategic foresight and sequencing of equations.</i>
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Computer">Aspect: <i>Computer</i></span></h1>
<p><i>All Reality Shaper actors begin with the <b>Computer</b>, representing the ability to materialize, store, and execute equations. Equations can create energy manifestations, summon constructs, or manipulate the environment.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Base_Function"><i>Base Function</i></span></h2>
<ul><li><b>Equation Writing:</b> Write equations as actions or free actions. Each equation can manifest in the Physical Plane, creating offensive, defensive, or utility effects.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Equation Activation:</b> Once an equation is written, it can be triggered immediately or stored for later activation.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Physical and Metaphysical Manifestation:</b> Equations can form weapons, barriers, or constructs, translating abstract math into tangible effects.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Unbreakable by:</b> <i>Conventional physical interference.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by:</b> <i>Disruption of the Computer interface or direct interference with stored equations.</i></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b> <i>Deactivation, destruction of the Computer, or incapacitation of the Archetype Holder.</i></li></ul>
