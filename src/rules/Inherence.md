<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Inherence">Inherence</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Overview">Overview</span></h3>
<p><b>Inherence</b> represents the inheritance of your character, deeply rooted in the world of <b>Wonderland of the Enigma</b> and shaped by the player's visions. It encompasses your character's origins, blending genetic, cultural, and spiritual elements into a cohesive identity. Inherence is composed of a <b>Species Lineage</b> and an optional <b>Heritage</b>. By selecting species and lineage, you gain access to <b>Incepts</b> and <b>Genetic Traits</b>, which define your physical and innate characteristics. These elements are crucial for character creation, providing depth and mechanical benefits that evolve through play.
</p><p>Inherence is the foundation of your "Place of Origin," influencing how your character interacts with the world, other beings, and the enigmatic forces at play. All choices should be discussed with your Curator (game master) to ensure balance, thematic fit, and narrative coherence.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Genetic_Traits">Genetic Traits</span></h3>
<p><b>Genetic Traits</b> are afflictions or inherent qualities derived from your species' genetic makeup. Since your chosen species have lived and evolved before your character, their mutations and adaptations bleed into your genetics. These traits are divided into four main sections: <b>Appearance</b>, <b>Bonuses</b>, <b>Mutations</b>, and <b>Polygenics</b>. Each section includes selectable aspects based on your species, presented on a table ranging from <b>Stable</b> (low chance of change) to <b>Mutagenic</b> (high chance of change). You choose traits that align with your species, and they can manifest variably depending on environmental fInquisitors or hybridization.
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Appearance">Appearance</span></h4>
<p>This section defines your character's physical form, determined by genetic markers and evolutions. It covers how your body is structured and perceived, including skin, hair, and other features. Aspects are selected from a stable-to-mutagenic table, allowing for customization while reflecting species-specific traits.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Skin and Skin Type:</b> Describes the texture, color, and composition of your skin (if applicable). For example, a species with reptilian traits might have scaly, iridescent skin that shifts with emotions.</li>
<li><b>Hair:</b> Includes style, color, density, and any unique properties (e.g., luminescent strands or retractable fur).</li>
<li><b>Eyes:</b> Covers shape, color, pupil type, and special abilities (e.g., night vision or hypnotic patterns).</li>
<li><b>Fingers and Toes:</b> Details digit count, flexibility, and adaptations (e.g., webbed for aquatic species or clawed for predatory ones).</li>
<li><b>Other Physical Affections:</b> Any additional features, such as horns, tails, wings, or bioluminescent markings, that affect your overall appearance and genetics.</li></ul>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Bonuses">Bonuses</span></h4>
<p>Bonuses provide slight stat modifications or natural interactions with the world and environment, enhancing your character's innate capabilities. These are tied to your species' evolutionary history and are chosen from the stable-to-mutagenic table.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Stat Modifications:</b> Minor boosts or penalties to base skills (e.g., +1 to Endurance for a hardy species or -1 to Dexterity for a bulky one).</li>
<li><b>Environmental Interactions:</b> How your character naturally adapts or excels in certain settings (e.g., resistance to cold climates or enhanced swimming speed in water).</li>
<li><b>World Interactions:</b> Subtle ways your genetics influence social or survival aspects (e.g., natural camouflage in forests or pheromone-based intimidation).</li></ul>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Mutations">Mutations</span></h4>
<p>Mutations are species-reliant changes that can alter appearance, bonuses, and polygenics. They are strict and tied to your species' lineage, but in cases of <b>hybridization</b> (combining traits from multiple species), mutations can mutate further, blending or transforming based on the involved species. This is determined by your Curator and how you manifest it in play. Select from the stable-to-mutagenic table, where mutagenic options have a higher chance of unpredictable changes.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Effects on Appearance:</b> Mutations might add or modify features (e.g., extra limbs or color-shifting skin).</li>
<li><b>Effects on Bonuses:</b> Alter stat mods or interactions (e.g., a mutation granting fire resistance but reducing speed).</li>
<li><b>Effects on Polygenics:</b> Influence size, composition, or creature interactions (e.g., a mutation making you emit toxic pheromones).</li>
<li><b>Hybridization Rules:</b> If your character is a hybrid, mutations from each species combine and potentially evolve, creating unique traits (e.g., merging avian and aquatic mutations for amphibious flight).</li></ul>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Polygenics">Polygenics</span></h4>
<p>Polygenics determine your character's scale, composition, and biochemical interactions, including how other creatures perceive or react to you. These are highly variable and selected from the stable-to-mutagenic table, reflecting genetic diversity.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Size and Composition:</b> Your overall build, density, and material makeup (e.g., ethereal for ghostly species or metallic for robotic hybrids).</li>
<li><b>Creature Interactions:</b> How animals, monsters, or other beings respond (e.g., attracting predators or repelling insects).</li>
<li><b>Pheromones and Hormones:</b> Biochemical signals you emit, affecting social dynamics or environmental effects (e.g., calming auras or aggressive scents).</li></ul>
<p>Genetic Traits are fully customizable within your species' framework. The stable-to-mutagenic scale ensures variety—stable traits are reliable and predictable, while mutagenic ones introduce risk and potential for dramatic change during adventures.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Incepts">Incepts</span></h3>
<p><b>Incepts</b> are abilities derived from the species within your Inherence, building your <b>Incept Pool</b>. They are obtained through <b>trial and tribulation</b>—earned in-game via challenges, quests, or personal growth rather than selected at creation. Incepts fall under <b>Asymmetries</b>, alongside <b>Genera</b> and <b>Varna</b>, representing metaphysical or innate powers unlocked by overcoming adversity.
</p>
<ul><li><b>How They Work:</b> Each species in your lineage contributes to potential Incepts. For example, a species with adaptive traits might grant Incepts related to environmental survival.</li>
<li><b>Incept Pool:</b> A collection of abilities that grow as you play, providing bonuses, resistances, or unique actions tied to your genetic heritage.</li>
<li><b>Asymmetries Link:</b> Incepts are considered part of your character's asymmetries, enhancing your specialization and Paradigm over time.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Heritage">Heritage</span></h3>
<p><b>Heritage</b> is a distinct species outside your primary racial class, adding exotic depth to your lineage. It can only be selected if your Inherence is of a <b>pure racial class</b>—meaning your <b>Species Template</b> and all selected species in your <b>Lineage</b> must originate from the <b>same racial class</b> (e.g., all mammalian or elemental beings).
</p>
<ul><li><b>Conditions:</b> If your species are from different classes, Heritage is unavailable.</li>
<li><b>Selection:</b> Choose a Heritage species from outside the racial class of your previous 3–5 species (depending on lineage complexity). This might grant unique traits, cultural insights, or spiritual connections not available through standard Inherence.</li>
<li><b>Benefits:</b> Heritage often provides narrative flavor, such as ancestral knowledge or rare abilities, emphasizing your character's blended or distant origins.</li></ul>
<p>Inherence is a deeply personal aspect of character creation, reflecting the enigmatic world's diversity. Use it to craft a backstory that ties into your Specialization, and consult your Curator for hybridization or mutation details. With Inherence complete, your character's origins are set—now, embrace the trials that will unlock your true potential! ```
