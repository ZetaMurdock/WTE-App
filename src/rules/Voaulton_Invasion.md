<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p>In the desolate year of <b>2087</b>, time and life on Earth seemed to <b>stand still</b>.
</p><p>The planet was invaded by a species now known as the <b>Voaulton</b>, though they were once called the <b>Tauron</b>.
</p><p>Their origins remain a mystery. Their technology was unlike anything known—materials not found anywhere in <b>Enon’s Heart</b> (the Multiverse itself). Even today, scientists and Curators alike <b>study remnants</b> of that tech in awe and terror.
</p>
<hr>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Invasion">The Invasion</span></h3>
<p>The invasion was <b>horrific</b>, rivaling even the Nigraldi in scale and grotesquery.
</p><p>Key atrocities include:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Body Hijacking:</b> Voaulton tendrils embedded into the nervous systems and brains of other lifeforms.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Human Extinction:</b> Humans were targeted more than any other species. Massive body hijacking events wiped out the species entirely.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Rapid Adaptation:</b> After humans were gone, the Voaulton adapted their methods for other species, though less efficiently.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Burning Hosts:</b> To stop spread, surviving species often had to <b>incinerate infected bodies</b>, fueling the rapid collapse of occupied regions.</li></ul>
<p>The Voaulton’s strategy was <b>efficient and merciless</b>, leaving the galaxy forever changed.
</p>
