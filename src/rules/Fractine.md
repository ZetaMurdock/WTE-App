<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>Fractine occupy the deepest social stratum of SubDermin settlements -- not because they are feared or despised, but because they simply live deeper. Their preferred environments are places where most other species cannot function: magma-adjacent tunnels, extreme-pressure geological vaults, ancient ore deposits. Their crystalline internal structure makes them extraordinarily difficult to damage through conventional means, but it also means they are slow, highly visible (crystalline protrusions catch light even in total darkness), and dependent on geological environments in a way other SubDermin are not.
</p>
