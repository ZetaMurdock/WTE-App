<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetype:_Mycelian">Archetype: <i>Mycelian</i></span></h1>
<h3><span id=".E2.80.9CThe_world_rots_beautifully.E2.80.94if_you_let_it..E2.80.9D"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="“The_world_rots_beautifully—if_you_let_it.”"><i>“The world rots beautifully—if you let it.”</i></span></h3>
<p>The <b>Mycelian</b> Archetype embodies biological dominance, environmental corruption, and persistent battlefield attrition.
</p><p>Mycelians reshape terrain, infest bodies, and weaponize decay to slowly overwhelm foes. Their strength lies not in bursts, but in <b>inescapable progression</b>—rot that spreads, spores that multiply, and corruption that accelerates until the enemy can no longer fight back.
</p><p>Their drawback is severe: they are <b>extremely vulnerable to elemental effects</b>, especially <b>Fire</b>, which disrupts or destroys their fungal constructs.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Identity">Core Identity</span></h1>
<ul><li><b>Primary Function:</b> Environmental Control, Corruption, Forced Attrition</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Combat Rhythm:</b> Slow buildup → escalating inevitability</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Weakness:</b> Highly vulnerable to elemental statuses (Fire, Lightning, Acid)</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Signature:</b> <b>Biofilm</b>, a persistent fungal medium that anchors all Mycelian manipulation</li></ul>
<p>Mycelians excel when allowed time to grow, corrupt, and spread. A patient player becomes unstoppable; a reckless one burns quickly.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetry:_Mycelian_Action_Economy">Asymmetry: <i>Mycelian Action Economy</i></span></h1>
<p>Upon choosing <b>Mycelian</b>, your Actor gains its Asymmetry, establishing your <b>Action Economy</b>, <b>environmental interaction rules</b>, and <b>fungal corruption behaviors</b>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Action_Economy">Action Economy</span></h3>
<ul><li><b>3 Standard Actions</b>  Used for fungal manipulation, growth, spreading corruption, or direct attacks.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>1 True Opportunity Attack</b>  Triggered when creatures disturb your fungal structures, enter corrupted zones, or attempt to cleanse your terrain.</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>0 Free Actions</b>  Mycelian abilities rarely involve instantaneous commands; their power grows through deliberate action.</li></ul>
<p><b>Design Intent:</b>
</p><p>Mycelians trade reactive speed for overwhelming presence. You act slowly, but everything you create continues acting <i>after</i> you.
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect:_Biofilm">Aspect: <i>Biofilm</i></span></h1>
<p>All Mycelians begin with the <b>Biofilm Aspect</b> the moment their Asymmetry manifests.
</p>
<h3><span id="Biofilm_.E2.80.94_Base_Function"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Biofilm_—_Base_Function">Biofilm — Base Function</span></h3>
<p>A <b>5-foot radius fungal layer</b> spreads around you passively. You can manipulate this layer at any time.
</p><p>Any <b>direct manipulation</b> of the Biofilm grants <b>1 Bonus Action</b> usable only for additional fungal effects.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Core_Rules">Core Rules</span></h3>
<ul><li><b>Zone of Influence:</b>  Any creature within 5ft is affected by your fungal debilitations (Poison, Rot, Slow, Hallucination, etc.).</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Biofilm Is:</b></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Unaffected by</b> external manipulation (wind, gravity, forced movement).</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Affected by</b> elemental attacks targeting <i>you</i>, especially Fire (removes or weakens the Biofilm).</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Persistent Until:</b> You take damage, are afflicted by an elemental debuff, or choose to collapse it.</li></ul>
<h3><span id="Corrupt_.28Innate_Wyrd.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Corrupt_(Innate_Wyrd)">Corrupt (Innate Wyrd)</span></h3>
<p>Your Mycelian Asymmetry grants the <b>Corrupt Wyrd</b>, empowering all fungal matter:
</p>
<ul><li>Anything your Biofilm contacts becomes <b>corrupted</b>, decaying organically over time.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Your corruption intensifies in areas with <b>genetically similar lifeforms</b> (forests, caves, beasts, humanoids of shared ancestry).</li></ul>
<p>This is the foundation of all Mycelian subtypes.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Subtype_Features">Subtype Features</span></h2>

<p>Your attacks deal <b>+2d6 Poison Damage</b> and apply <b>Infused</b>:
</p>
<ul><li>The target suffers <b>–10 on all rolls</b> for <b>35 seconds</b> (active time).</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Poisonous_Infestation">Poisonous Infestation</span></h3>
<p>When your blood touches anything, mushrooms erupt from it.
</p>
<ul><li>A <b>15ft poisonous spore field</b> forms around the affected object or creature.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Spores linger, dealing poison damage over time.</li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Congregation_of_Poison">Congregation of Poison</span></h3>
<p>If your <i>blood</i> is part of an attack:
</p>
<ul><li>The attack deals <b>double damage</b>.</li></ul>
<ul><li>The target suffers <b>+2d12 True Damage</b> from all sources.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Targets hit by poison become <b>Restrained</b>.</li></ul>
<p><b>Hook:</b>
</p><p>AMA Poilodies transforms the battlefield into a toxic shrine—every drop of their blood is a catastrophic event.
