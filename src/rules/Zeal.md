<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Zeal-_the_Zeal_Sighted">The Zeal- the Zeal Sighted</span></h2>
<p><b>Shaveria Vexuin</b> lived a life of quiet simplicity. She was the kind of person who blended into the background—a woman with oversized T-shirts, thick glasses, and a modest job in an office cubicle. Shaveria had no grand ambitions or sense of self-importance. She preferred it that way, finding solace in the small, predictable comforts of her daily routine. Her world was uncomplicated, viewed through the literal and metaphorical lens of her glasses.
</p><p>Despite her unassuming demeanor, Shaveria possessed an exceptional talent for <b>Codexian Math</b>—a highly advanced and enigmatic form of mathematics. She inherited this skill from her father, one of the very first Codexian mathematicians. Yet, her father had always been secretive about his past, refusing to tell Shaveria where her mother came from. Although she suspected there was more to her origins, she never pressed for answers. She chose to remain in her safe, predictable life, using her talent for Codexian Math to solve the occasional puzzle at work, never venturing beyond her comfort zone.
</p><p>That was, until the day <b>Adondis</b> arrived.
</p><p>Adondis, a seemingly ordinary IT worker, walked into the office and noticed Shaveria’s hair. Something about it caught his attention. “Has your hair always been like this?” he asked, smiling in a way that felt almost too knowing. Shaveria nodded absentmindedly. Without another word, Adondis snapped his fingers. The moment he did, Shaveria’s reality shifted. She found herself in a mental space, confronted by an individual who looked exactly like her. The doppelgänger smiled warmly and spoke before Shaveria could.
</p><p>“Finally, I get to see you after all these years,” the figure said. “We don’t have much time. You’re not from this world, and you don’t need to pretend you are. Your father has been lying to you. He comes from a twisted background, but now it’s time for you to see the truth.”
</p><p>Before Shaveria could respond, the figure extended a hand, and <b>blue energy</b> began to flow from her into Shaveria, specifically into her eyes.
</p><p>“This is a power I will unlock for you,” the figure continued, “but you must cultivate it. Become its Archetype. There is another who carries a sliver of this energy, but they will not have the same chance as you. You are the true Progenitor. Awaken, my daughter.”
</p><p>With that, Shaveria snapped back to reality, sitting at her cubicle. But everything had changed. Her vision no longer saw the world in ordinary terms. She now perceived everything in <b>frames</b>—layers of reality unfolding simultaneously. She could see patterns, pathways, and alternate possibilities in a way she could not understand.
</p><p>That same day, Shaveria walked out of her job, overwhelmed by the change. On her way out, she accidentally brushed against a coworker. To her shock, she immediately glimpsed their entire family history—along with alternate versions of that history. The rush of knowledge sent her mind into overload, and she fled the office. Driving home, the world around her fractured even further. As she looked to her left, she saw <b>multiple versions of herself</b> all looking back. One version suddenly appeared in front of her, opening a portal. With no time to react, Shaveria drove through it and, inexplicably, found herself parked in front of her home. She ran inside and locked herself in her room, hiding from a world she no longer understood. For days, Shaveria stayed in her room, trying to make sense of her newfound powers. She discovered that by exuding the strange energy within her, her <b>animals</b>—her beloved pets—began to exhibit similar traits. They glowed faintly with the same blue energy, and she realized she had the ability to <b>share this power</b> with others.
</p><p>In time, Shaveria embraced her new identity as <b>The Zeal-Sighted</b>. Her vision of reality became her strength, and she started experimenting with her powers, mastering her abilities to manipulate alternate possibilities, perceive truths hidden to others, and share her energy with those she trusted. From her newfound power, she created the foundation of a new <b>Archetype</b>, one that would be passed down to others, creating a lineage that tied sight, energy, and truth together.
</p><p>Shaveria Vexuin, once an ordinary office worker, became the progenitor of a legacy that would change the fabric of reality itself.
</p>
