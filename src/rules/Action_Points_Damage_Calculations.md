# Action Points/Damage Calculations

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><p>in this system, the damage dealt by your character's attacks will be scaled based on several factors, including the severity of the attack and the specific placement of the strike. Additionally, the words used to describe the actions will affect the effectiveness of the attack. Detailed and specific descriptions enhance the impact of attacks.
</p><p>For example, instead of a simple statement like "My character attacks the neck," provide a more immersive description like "My character swiftly lunges, aiming a precise strike at the exposed base of the neck. They carefully assess the situation, maintaining awareness of potential counterattacks within their range."
</p><p>By incorporating specific details and showcasing your character's intent, strategy, and situational awareness, attacks will have a greater impact on the outcome of the encounter.
</p>
