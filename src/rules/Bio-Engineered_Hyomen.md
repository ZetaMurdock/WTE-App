<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>Born at biological age 23 with no natural birth process -- each Bio-Engineered Hyomen is a deliberate, individual engineering product. They were created as a controlled population for endurance roles and hazardous labor. Their semi-collective consciousness -- a faint biological signal they share without a formal Hive structure -- makes them appear emotionally restrained and socially self-contained. They are not independently born and cannot be force-bred; every member of the population was someone's deliberate decision to create. That knowledge shapes their culture in complex ways -- some find purpose in designed identity, others spend their lives trying to move beyond it.
</p>
