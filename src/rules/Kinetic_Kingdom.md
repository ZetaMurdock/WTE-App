<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p>The <b>Kinetic Kingdom</b> traces its origin to the <b>Insectoid</b> — a mischievous, clever species whose history begins long before civilization.
</p><p>In Earth’s earliest age, an <b>orb fell from the sky</b>.
</p><p>Within it was a grotesque mass of dark flesh and ink, born of squid-like entities. The mass grew, absorbed, and spread through the oceans, adapting as Earth formed around it.
</p><p>When a meteor struck the planet, the mass was <b>shattered</b>, scattered across the seas into countless fragments.
</p><p>To survive, those fragments had to change.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Birth_of_the_Kinba">The Birth of the Kinba</span></h2>
<p>The original orb was damaged by the meteor’s impact. No longer capable of sustaining a singular mass, it began producing <b>tiny clumps of flesh</b> instead.
</p><p>Too small to consume as before, these clumps adapted by absorbing <b>bacteria from their surroundings</b>, developing a specialized organ known as the <b>Augo Gland</b>.
</p><p>With this adaptation, the <b>Kinba species was born</b>.
</p><p>Clever. Adaptive. Restless.
</p><p>Their early societies formed around movement, learning, and quiet mischief — always evolving, never static.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy_and_Loss">Legacy and Loss</span></h2>
<p>You are one of the <b>original descendants</b> — raised on stories of purpose, survival, and origin.
</p><p>But that legacy was interrupted.
</p><p>Your colony was taken from its homeworld and transported to <b>Aulorn</b> as prisoners. Some Kinba escaped. Others vanished. Not all survived.
</p><p>Those who escaped carved out a new home deep within Aulorn.
</p><p>Those who did not were lost.
</p><p>You come from the fracture between those two outcomes.
</p>
