<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Abomination">The Abomination</span></h2>
<blockquote><p>Life is filled with many wonders and horrors, and today we delve into a story that encapsulates both. Meet Balinx Eradore, a man with a fire to serve his people. He was kind-hearted, but like all men, he had his flaws and insecurities. Unfortunately, those insecurities often got the better of him. When he was sent to war, he was forced to make an unimaginable decision—to kill his own men to survive. Not long after, the army rescued him, but only a day passed. To cover up the shame, he lied about the events, and this dishonesty haunted him for the rest of his life. 
The truth eventually slipped out during a conference, and it spread like wildfire. The media branded him a monster, insane, and an abomination. This led him to isolate himself in his home, never leaving. Yet, despite his self-imposed exile, Balinx was deployed once again. This time, he was sent on a suicide mission to retrieve a stolen artifact. However, the artifact's power was too great. Upon returning, the artifact morphed Balinx into a creature no one could recognize. Instead of being hailed as a hero, he was met with even worse punishment—exile for stealing the artifact. Consumed by rage, his power continued to grow, transforming him into the first Abomination. But he did not let that define him. Balinx managed to find a new home, and there, he built a family of his own. His children began to exhibit signs of his newfound archetype, and Balinx became the first Archetype Progenitor, creating a hereditary archetype that would live on through his descendants.</p></blockquote>
