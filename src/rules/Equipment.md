<div style="font-size:2em;font-weight:bold;color:#c8ccff;letter-spacing:0.08em;font-family:monospace;">
<p>THE OVERCLOCK SYSTEM
</p>
</div>
<div style="margin-top:6px;color:#9da4ff;font-style:italic;font-size:0.92em;">
<p>The neural process through which equipment temporarily exceeds its designed operating limits.
</p>
</div>
</div>
<div style="background:#101018;border:1px solid #202848;border-left:4px solid #6d72ff;padding:16px 18px;margin-bottom:22px;color:#d8ddff;line-height:1.75;">
<p>Overclocking is an advanced combat protocol available to Inquisitors capable of directly manipulating their <b>Neuronal Capacity (NC)</b>. During an Overclock, equipment, Ciphers and Genus temporarily ignore conventional operating restrictions, dramatically increasing performance at the cost of severe neurological strain.
</p><p>Every Overclock follows three mandatory phases:
</p>
<div style="margin-top:10px;">
<p><b>Phase I</b> — Extender Phase<br>
<b>Phase II</b> — Dextender Phase<br>
<b>Phase III</b> — Equalizer Phase
</p>
</div>
</div>
<div style="background:#121212;border-left:5px solid #42b4ff;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:20px;">
<div style="font-size:1.4em;font-weight:bold;color:#8fd5ff;">
<p>Ⅰ • THE EXTENDER PHASE
</p>
</div>
<div style="font-style:italic;color:#7ab6d8;margin-top:4px;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>"Push the system beyond safe operating limits."
</p>
</div>
<p>By channeling their direct <b>Neuronal Capacity</b>, an Inquisitor may Overclock an active capability for a brief duration.
</p>


<h3 style="color:#9fdcff;"><span class="mw-headline" id="Cipher_Overclock">Cipher Overclock</span></h3>
<div style="background:#1a1f2f;border-left:3px solid #42b4ff;padding:12px 14px;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>The distant Operator's synchronization is intentionally severed, granting the player direct physical control of the terminal.
</p><p>• Offline Ciphers may be forced Online, injecting live enhancements.
</p><p>• Online Ciphers may be forced Offline, removing Paradigm monitoring while reducing their Synaptic Space cost by <b>exactly 50%</b>.
</p>
</div>
<h3 style="color:#9fdcff;"><span class="mw-headline" id="Genus_Overclock">Genus Overclock</span></h3>
<div style="background:#1a1f2f;border-left:3px solid #42b4ff;padding:12px 14px;">
<p>Genus abilities may be cast for <b>zero Synaptic Space cost</b> up to the user's <b>Neuronal Capacity Modifier</b>. Upon successful activation, one Domain Effect immediately resolves.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<div style="font-size:1.4em;font-weight:bold;color:#ff9aa4;">
<p>Ⅱ • THE DEXTENDER PHASE
</p>
</div>
<div style="font-style:italic;color:#ffb6bc;margin-top:4px;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>"The nervous system pays the price."
</p>
</div>
<p>Once the Overclock expires, neural saturation forces the system into temporary instability.
</p>
<h3 style="color:#ffc2c7;"><span class="mw-headline" id="Cipher_Backlash">Cipher Backlash</span></h3>
<div style="background:#2a1719;border-left:3px solid #ff5b6a;padding:12px 14px;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>• Synaptic Space cost increases to <b>1.8×</b> normal.
</p><p>• Every opponent immediately gains Action Priority before the Cipher resolves.
</p>
</div>
<h3 style="color:#ffc2c7;"><span class="mw-headline" id="Genus_Backlash">Genus Backlash</span></h3>
<div style="background:#2a1719;border-left:3px solid #ff5b6a;padding:12px 14px;">
<p>• Synaptic Space cost doubles.
</p><p>• Every combatant temporarily overrides your Action Priority during the casting window.
</p>
</div>
</div>
<div style="background:#121212;border-left:5px solid #54d27d;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:24px;">
<div style="font-size:1.4em;font-weight:bold;color:#9af0b3;">
<p>Ⅲ • THE EQUALIZER PHASE
</p>
</div>
<div style="font-style:italic;color:#7fd89d;margin-top:4px;margin-bottom:14px;">
<p>"Restore equilibrium."
</p>
</div>
<p>The Overclock cannot be used again until the equipment's neural pathways are re-attuned.
</p>
<div style="background:#18231c;border-left:3px solid #54d27d;padding:12px 14px;">
<p>The user must either:
</p><p>• Spend a <b>Prepared Action</b> during combat.
</p><p><b>OR</b>
</p><p>• Complete a <b>Recovery Phase</b> outside combat.
</p><p>Both methods fully restore the equipment to its normal operating state.
</p>
</div>
