# Leveling

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr">

<h2><span id="Advancement_.26_Classification_in_Wonderland_of_the_Enigma.E2.80.8B"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Advancement_&amp;_Classification_in_Wonderland_of_the_Enigma​">Advancement &amp; Classification in Wonderland of the Enigma​</span></h2>
<p>In <b>Wonderland of the Enigma</b>, growth is not a reward for victory—it is a reclassification imposed by systems that measure your usefulness, deviation, and survivability. You do not “level up”; you are indexed, re-ranked, and re-optimized as the world decides what you are allowed to become. Advancement increases visibility, pressure, and consequence rather than simple power, aligning progression with the game’s focus on system over self, optimization.​
</p><p>Progression unfolds through two intertwined forces:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Designation Rank</b> – your operational clearance and mechanical evolution</li>
<li><b>Eminence</b> – how aligned or hazardous you are to system logic</li></ul>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="1._Designation_Rank">1. Designation Rank</span></h2>
<p><b>Designation Rank</b> reflects how deeply you are embedded in Wonderland’s operational hierarchy. Higher Ranks grant expanded permissions, authority, and specialized capabilities—but they also bring tighter scrutiny, harsher corrections, and greater vulnerability to Extraction.​
</p>
<ul><li>Typical range: <b>Designation 0–9</b> (the exact ceiling remains flexible for each campaign).</li>
<li>Advancement is triggered when the system concludes that:
<ul><li>You have survived sufficient strain.</li>
<li>You have demonstrated functional value.</li>
<li>You have not yet warranted Extraction.</li></ul></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="What_Designation_Rank_Represents">What Designation Rank Represents</span></h2>
<p>Each new Designation may include:
</p>
<ul><li>Paradigm reconfigurations and updates</li>
<li>Expanded permissions (sealed Stages, restricted technologies, deeper contracts)</li>
<li>Increased mental, physical, and systemic tolerance thresholds</li>
<li>Greater visibility to <b>Indrah</b>, planetary Keepers, and the Mogul’s proxies</li></ul>
<p>Higher Designation means the world has invested more into you as a component—not that you are safely “stronger.”​
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Rank_Advancement_Triggers">Rank Advancement Triggers</span></h2>
<p>Instead of tracking EXP, advancement is driven by <b>Accumulated System Pressure</b>, a measure of how much strain, impact, and deviation you’ve Caused.​
</p><p>You gain Pressure by:
</p>
<ul><li>Resolving high-impact system failures or critical breakdowns</li>
<li>Surviving sanctioned conflicts and high-risk operations</li>
<li>Interacting with core Stages or planetary mega-machinery</li>
<li>Making choices that meaningfully alter optimization outcomes</li>
<li>Being flagged, audited, or reviewed—and surviving the procedure</li></ul>
<p>Pressure is tracked abstractly (segments, clocks, or thresholds) instead of grindable numbers, discouraging repetitive “farming” behavior. Repeating low-impact actions quickly loses value as the system marks them as redundant or statistically irrelevant.​
</p><p>When Pressure crosses a key threshold, the Curator initiates a <b>Reclassification Event</b>, which might result in:
</p>
<ul><li>A promotion to a new Designation</li>
<li>Forced specialization or narrowing of function</li>
<li>Conditional advancement tied to explicit obligations</li>
<li>Partial advancement paired with penalties, oversight, or implanted constraints</li></ul>
<hr>
<h2><span id="2._Eminence_.28System_Alignment_Index.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="2._Eminence_(System_Alignment_Index)">2. Eminence (System Alignment Index)</span></h2>
<p><b>Eminence</b> measures how the machinery of Wonderland interprets you: as an asset, a liability, or an unpredictable anomaly. It ranges from <b>–20 (System Liability)</b> to <b>+20 (System Asset)</b>, beginning at <b>0</b>.​
</p><p>Eminence is not about likability. It is about <b>alignment with optimization logic.</b>
</p>
<ul><li>Preserving a system at human cost tends to increase Eminence.</li>
<li>Creating inefficiency to save lives tends to reduce Eminence.</li>
<li>Clean, optimized outcomes raise your standing.</li>
<li>Instability, ambiguity, and unpredictability erode it.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="How_Eminence_Shapes_Advancement">How Eminence Shapes Advancement</span></h2>
<p>Instead of modifying EXP or raw speed, Eminence alters <b>how</b> advancement manifests:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Advancement Shape</b>
<ul><li>High Eminence:
<ul><li>Clean promotions, expanded permissions, sanctioned Talents and interfaces</li>
<li>“Approved” upgrades that make you a more efficient tool</li></ul></li>
<li>Low Eminence:
<ul><li>Conditional or distorted advancements</li>
<li>Corrupted Talents, intrusive monitoring, failsafes embedded into your growth</li></ul></li></ul></li>
<li><b>Advancement Cost</b>
<ul><li>Positive Eminence: fewer explicit strings, more assumed compliance</li>
<li>Negative Eminence: advancement still occurs, but something is taken
<ul><li>Memories rewritten or extracted</li>
<li>Access revoked or rerouted</li>
<li>Identity markers and records altered or redacted</li></ul></li></ul></li>
<li><b>World Response</b>
<ul><li>High Eminence: systems accommodate you—doors open, protocols flex, agents cooperate.​</li>
<li>Low Eminence: systems adapt against you—locks tighten, surveillance escalates, resistance hardens.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>Progress is never fully blocked; instead, the <b>form</b> of your progression grows more hostile, parasitic, or invasive as Eminence drops.​
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Gaining_and_Losing_Eminence">Gaining and Losing Eminence</span></h2>
<p>Eminence changes are tied to <b>impact</b>, not intent.​
</p><p>Examples:
</p>
<ul><li>Preserving optimization while allowing collateral suffering → Eminence rises.</li>
<li>Sabotaging efficiency to preserve autonomy or life → Eminence falls.</li>
<li>Delivering clean, measurable, and stable outcomes → Eminence rises.</li>
<li>Introducing chaos, ambiguity, or unresolved variables → Eminence falls.</li></ul>
<p>The Curator applies Eminence shifts sparingly and narratively, announcing changes in-fiction, such as:
</p><p>“Your intervention stabilizes the planetary network, but three communities vanish from the registry. The system logs this as a success: <b>+3 Eminence</b>.”
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="How_Advancement_Feels_in_Play">How Advancement Feels in Play</span></h2>
<p>Example: A character qualifies for <b>Designation 4</b>. The system presents options:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Full Promotion:</b>
<ul><li>Gain full Rank benefits and broader permissions.</li>
<li>Lose access to an older identity, community, or unmonitored space.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Partial Promotion:</b>
<ul><li>Gain specific improvements or Talents.</li>
<li>Accept tighter oversight, embedded restraints, or standing directives.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Deferred Promotion:</b>
<ul><li>Remain at a lower Designation with less visibility.</li>
<li>Avoid immediate scrutiny—but flag yourself as anomalous, inviting later correction.</li></ul></li></ul>
<hr>
<h2><span id="Hit_Points_.26_Physical_Stability"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Hit_Points_&amp;_Physical_Stability">Hit Points &amp; Physical Stability</span></h2>
<p>Hit Points in Wonderland of the Enigma represent <b>structural integrity</b>, not heroic toughness.​
</p>
<ul><li><b>Base HP:</b>
<ul><li>All characters begin with <b>10 + (3d4, drop the highest)</b>, modeling inherent stability before system intervention.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>Archetype HP Dice (rolled once at creation):</b>
<ul><li>These represent body modification, mental reinforcement, or systemic redundancy installed as part of your Archetype.</li>
<li>They do <b>not</b> scale directly with Designation in a traditional way; Rank does not simply mean “more HP.”​</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>Higher Designation instead provides:
</p>
<ul><li>Better mitigation tools (fields, redundancies, redirects)</li>
<li>More conditional survivability (failsafes, sacrificial systems, emergency reruns)</li>
<li>Access to external supports rather than raw durability</li></ul>
<p>​
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span id="The_Curator.E2.80.99s_Role_in_Advancement"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Curator’s_Role_in_Advancement">The Curator’s Role in Advancement</span></h2>
<p>The Curator does not <b>grant</b> power—the Curator <b>administers reclassification</b> in the fiction.
</p><p>Their responsibilities include:
</p>
<ul><li>Determining the cost attached to each advancement</li>
<li>Deciding what must be lost, altered, or constrained when power grows</li>
<li>Aligning every growth step with the premise of optimization, identity erosion, and systemic pressure</li>
<li>Preventing clean, consequence-free power fantasies that break the tone​</li></ul>
<p>Core principle:
</p><p><b>Any advancement must increase pressure, visibility, or consequence.</b>
</p>
