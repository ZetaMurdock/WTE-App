# Trait Pools

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr">

<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Trait_Pools">Trait Pools</span></h1>
<p><b>Trait Pools</b> represent the dynamic structure through which a character’s genetic, ancestral, and asymmetrical features are expressed and managed.  They serve as the <b>organizational core</b> for traits gained through <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Inherence">Inherence</a>, <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Lineage">Lineage</a>, <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Heritage">Heritage</a>, and <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Incepts">Incepts</a>, allowing these elements to interact coherently throughout gameplay.
</p><p>While <span class="new" title="Genetic Traits (page does not exist)" data-uncrawlable-url="L3dpa2kvR2VuZXRpY19UcmFpdHM/YWN0aW9uPWVkaXQmcmVkbGluaz0x">Genetic Traits</span> describe <i>what</i> a character can express and <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Incepts">Incepts</a> define <i>how</i> those expressions transform over time, Trait Pools determine <i>when</i> and <i>why</i> those expressions manifest.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Overview">Overview</span></h2>
<p>A Trait Pool is the <b>living record</b> of a character’s biological and metaphysical inheritance.  Instead of immediately granting all possible traits at creation, Trait Pools manage *availability, activation, and evolution.*
</p><p>Each character maintains multiple interconnected pools reflecting different aspects of Inherence:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Genetic Trait Pool</b> – Physical and innate functions derived from species biology.</li>
<li><b>Incept Pool</b> – Dormant or evolving abilities unlocked through adversity and growth.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Function_of_Trait_Pools">Function of Trait Pools</span></h2>
<p>Each pool categorizes traits into three statuses:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Dormant</b> – Traits that exist but remain suppressed or inactive.</li>
<li><b>Active</b> – Traits currently expressed by the character.</li>
<li><b>Suppressed</b> – Traits overridden by new genetic, environmental, or asymmetrical expressions.</li></ul>
<p>Trait Pools help the Director and player determine how traits interact narratively and statistically as a character evolves.
</p><p><b>Example:</b>  If a character’s lineage includes amphibious and avian species, their Trait Pool tracks both adaptations, noting which are active (e.g., gills), dormant (e.g., wings), or suppressed (e.g., redundant breathing traits).
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Pool_Hierarchy">Pool Hierarchy</span></h2>
<p>Each pool maintains its own layering, influenced by the character’s <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Lineage">Lineage</a> structure and <span class="new" title="Dominance &amp; Recessiveness (page does not exist)" data-uncrawlable-url="L3dpa2kvRG9taW5hbmNlXyUyNl9SZWNlc3NpdmVuZXNzP2FjdGlvbj1lZGl0JnJlZGxpbms9MQ==">Dominance &amp; Recessiveness</span> weighting.
</p><p>Each pool informs the next; the Composite Pool becomes the foundation for further calculation, hybridization, or mutation.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Expression_Rules">Expression Rules</span></h2>
<p>Trait Pools operate under the following universal rules:
</p>
<ul><li><b>1. Weighted Manifestation</b> – Traits compete for expression based on Dominance, Recessiveness, and environmental prompting.</li>
<li><b>2. Layered Resolution</b> – Traits from the deepest ancestry manifest last but may persist longest.</li>
<li><b>3. Director Mediation</b> – When conflicting traits arise, the Director determines whether suppression, merger, or mutation occurs.</li>
<li><b>4. Rule of Even</b> – Natures and binaries (such as instinct pairs, light/dark, or calm/chaos alignments) must remain balanced within the Composite Pool unless overridden by the Director.</li>
<li><b>5. Evolution Through Pressure</b> – Narrative tension (High or Low Points) may alter which traits become dominant within any pool.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Managing_Trait_Pools">Managing Trait Pools</span></h2>
<p>Both the Player and Director can keep a structured record of each pool using a simple chart or template.  This ensures all active, dormant, and suppressed traits remain traceable.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Reset_and_Rebalancing">Reset and Rebalancing</span></h2>
<p>Events such as <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Director_Splitting">Director Splitting</a> or major narrative breakthroughs can reset one or more Trait Pools.  When a reset occurs:
</p>
<ul><li>Previously gained active traits remain.</li>
<li>Dominance and Recessiveness scores are recalculated.</li>
<li>The availability of suppressed traits may change.</li>
<li>Heritage or Incept influence may alter genetic weighting.</li></ul>
<p>This system symbolizes continual metamorphosis—characters are never static, only evolving.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Thematic_Purpose">Thematic Purpose</span></h3>
<p>Trait Pools emphasize that identity in *Wonderland of the Enigma* is fluid.  A creature is never a single thing—it is a tapestry of inherited memory, hidden code, and awakened possibility.  The pool is not a list of powers; it is a reflection of ancestry remembered through struggle.
</p><p><i>You do not simply acquire traits.</i>  <i>You inherit potential.</i>
</p>
