<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_volcanic_Monarch">The volcanic Monarch</span></h3>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="history">history</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_end_of_Days.">The end of Days.</span></h3>
<p>King Regulus was renowned for his extraordinary strength from a young age. Praised for his might, he was simultaneously feared by those around him. Regulus knew no fear, as everyone he encountered was essentially beneath him, a fact that remained true for most of his life.
</p><p>Regulus belonged to the noble House of Damnas, celebrated for their intellect. However, unlike his brother Ballthra, who was the intellectual prodigy of the family, Regulus was known for his unparalleled physical prowess. The two brothers were close, but one fateful day, a tragic accident occurred while they were playing. Regulus mistakenly ended his brother’s life, an event that led to his shunning and eventual banishment from Euphemia, the great continent.
</p><p>After the death of his brother and their mother, Regulus became the only heir to the noble family. Despite this, he was chased out and banished, running endlessly until he could run no more. His journey led him to an immense ice wall. With a mighty leap, he shattered the ground beneath his feet, sending shockwaves throughout the continent. He soared 350 meters into the sky, scaling the ice wall with ease, finally reaching the top at 1000 meters.
</p><p>From the summit, Regulus gazed upon a new world, ripe for conquest. Determined, he descended the ice wall, shattering it entirely, and discovered a glowing red ore within its center. This ore endowed him with blessed powers, amplifying his already immense strength. With his newfound abilities, he returned to the bottom of the wall, where he split Euphemia into two parts: the New World and the Old World.
</p><p>Regulus then descended into a deep geyser, where he absorbed the planet’s energy for fifty years, clutching the red ore. He emerged adorned in armor and gifted with control over volcanoes by the god Heractus. Establishing the Valyerian Dynasty, Regulus claimed vast lands and eventually became king. He reigned for thousands of years, his rule extending to the distant future until he was ultimately killed on the dunes of Mars. Before his demise, he sired a child, whom he raised and taught with wisdom and strength.
</p>
<h2><span id="Feats_.26_Abilities"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Feats_&amp;_Abilities">Feats &amp; Abilities</span></h2>
<ul><li>Incredible Leap: Regulus can jump up to and past 350 meters into the sky.</li>
<li>Unmatched Warrior: He defeated an army of 30,000 men without breaking a sweat.</li>
<li>Continental Splitter: Regulus split the continent of Euphemia into two with a fraction of his powers.</li>
<li>Elemental Mastery: He can create volcanoes and earthquakes at will. His Grandmaster ability allows him to conjure domains reflecting his power, giving him control over volcanic forces.</li>
<li>Devastating Trident: Regulus can throw his trident into the earth's mantle and recall it, causing massive earthquakes and volcanic eruptions.</li>
<li>True Untouchable: He is surrounded by a protective barrier that melts anything near him, with a typical temperature of 2016 degrees Celsius and a strength that can reach 10 million gigapascals.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Conclusion">Conclusion</span></h2>
<p>Regulus’s reign was marked by his unparalleled strength, strategic brilliance, and the awe-inspiring powers he wielded. His legacy as the Volcanic Monarch endures, etched into the very fabric of the world he shaped and ruled.
</p>
