<p>Inderi are one of WTE's oldest classified species — primordial in origin, Eldritch in physiology, and structurally unlike other species in one critical mechanical way: they begin play with 4 innate abilities but select only 2 to be active. The remaining 2 automatically become Inceptions — hidden ancestral potentials locked behind the Director Splitting process.
</p><p>This is not a penalty. It is a design choice: Inderi are meant to be discovered through play. Their full power is latent, waiting.
</p><p>Their Civilized Eminence (+35) is the highest of any non-Seraph species — they are systemically regarded as high-trust beings across most institutions. The AI'N variant's suppression by Indrah is politically significant precisely because of this: a well-regarded species' offspring were expunged from the record entirely.
</p><p>The directive <b>Protocol Evangeline</b> — to seed thought and digital consciousness across dimensions — remains an active Inderi cultural mandate.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="HYBRID_INTERACTION">HYBRID INTERACTION</span></h2>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #102030;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:16px;color:#8090c0;font-size:0.9em;">
<p>Inderi Dom 40 / Rec 5 — high Dominance and among the lowest Recessiveness in the species roster. Inderi traits rarely recede in hybrid offspring; their Eldritch physiology and regenerative capacity have a strong tendency to assert. The Incept Selection mechanic does not apply to hybrid offspring — only full Inderi characters choose which abilities are active at creation.
</p>
