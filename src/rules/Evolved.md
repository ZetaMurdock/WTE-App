<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p>The <b>Evolved</b> are the result of natural evolution continuing where the world chose to stop.
</p><p>In an age defined by <b>Fyber</b>, transhuman modification, and rigid class hierarchies, biology is seen as obsolete. Power is purchased. Bodies are replaced. Worth is measured by augmentation and reputation.
</p><p>The Evolved stand apart.
</p><p>Marked by deviation, mutation, or biological variance, the Evolved are often treated as <b>less than</b> — dismissed as primitive for relying on flesh instead of enhancement.
</p><p>Yet while the world optimizes itself into corruption, the Evolved endure.
</p><p>Where others modify, you adapt.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="A_World_Without_Hope">A World Without Hope</span></h2>
<p>Society is stratified.
</p><p>Opportunity is hoarded.
</p><p>Everything worth claiming has already been claimed.
</p><p>Those at the bottom are expected to vanish quietly.
</p><p>The Evolved do not.
</p><p>You come from those forgotten layers — the ones forced to survive without upgrades, without status, and without safety. In a world that no longer believes in growth, you carry it forward.
</p><p>Not through machines.
</p><p>Through life.
</p>
