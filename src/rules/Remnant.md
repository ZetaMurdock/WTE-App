# Remnant

> "Soldiers lost to war. Victors slain and forgotten."

The Remnant Paradigm is the domain of those left behind by war — shattered veterans, survivors, and warriors haunted by what they could not save. Once men and women of purpose, they now channel their pain and discipline into relentless survival. The Remnant does not seek glory or redemption; it endures because it must, driven by instincts honed in fire and loss. Strength born from suffering.

## Paradigm Identity

**Who Joins** — Former soldiers, operatives, or combatants drawn from catastrophic conflicts. Many are recruited after psychological collapse or extraction from battle zones deemed lost. Survivors of the unwinnable.

**Culture** — Stoic, grim, and fiercely loyal to those who have shared their pain. Remnants often distrust leadership and ideals, acting instead from personal codes of honor. They respect action above speech, precision above pride. "If nothing lasts, endure longer than everything else."

**Archetype Feel** — Precision Fighter / Rogue. The Remnant Inquisitor is a ghost who still fights — not for glory, not for duty, but because combat is all they know how to do.

## Roll Axis Profile

Remnant occupies the complete Roll Axis — 7 of 7 universal Roll Paths. Its native mechanics are built around survival through uncertainty: direct force, rapid execution, bodily avoidance, recovery, tactical cognition, perception, and force of intent. This breadth represents adaptability and battlefield experience, not an inherent bonus to every roll.

| Roll Path | Native | Direction | Remnant Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power | ● | Check | Apply direct force, exploit weakened positions, break obstacles, or finish an engagement through physical imposition. |
| Density | ● | Check | Compress practiced battlefield movement, weapon transitions, repositioning, and rapid tactical execution into limited openings. |
| Evasion | ● | Save | Avoid incoming threats, survive ambushes, reposition under pressure, and remain functional in unstable combat spaces. |
| Recovery | ● | Save | Endure trauma, fatigue, hostile conditions, bodily disruption, and the lingering consequences of prolonged conflict. |
| Capacity | ● | Check | Coordinate battlefield information, maintain tactical operations, operate unfamiliar systems, and continue functioning under cognitive pressure. |
| Perception | ● | Check / Save | Read battlefields, detect threats, identify deception, track movement, and recognize changes before they become fatal. |
| Influence | ● | Check / Save | Project hard-earned authority, intimidate through experience, reinforce allied resolve, and preserve personal intent against coercion. |

**FULL AXIS STANDARD** — Remnant's 7/7 Profile grants mechanical breadth, not automatic numerical superiority. Remnant Ciphers may naturally interact with any Roll Path, but a Remnant only receives Affinity Dice when the Attribute or Specialty actually governing the resolution is one of their Favored statistics.

## Remnant Paradigm Affinity

Remnant Affinity reflects survival instinct plus the particular habits a veteran was forced to develop. Unlike more standardized Paradigms, part of a Remnant's Affinity is chosen by the player, representing the battlefield role, trauma, profession, or survival pattern that shaped them before and during recruitment.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Favored Attributes | Dexterity · Choose 1 Additional Attribute |
| Favored Specialties | Adaptation · Choose 1 Additional Specialty |

Dexterity represents the reflexive repositioning and survival movement common to experienced survivors. The second Attribute records what that individual learned to rely upon most. Adaptation represents continued function after conditions change. The second Specialty reflects the practiced technique that kept that Remnant alive.

| Designation Rank | Favored Attribute | Favored Specialty |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | +1d5 | +1d10 |
| 3–5 | +2d5 | +2d10 |
| 6–8 | +3d5 | +3d10 |
| 9 | +4d5 | +4d10 |

**FIELD AFFINITY SELECTION** — When a Remnant is created, choose 1 additional Attribute and 1 additional Specialty to become Favored. These choices become part of that Remnant's Paradigm Affinity and are not casually changed between encounters. The selection represents the specific survival doctrine that individual developed rather than a standardized institutional curriculum.

**CONVERGENCE THROUGH EXPERIENCE** — Remnant does not possess one universal signature Convergence. Instead, its chosen Affinities determine where Convergence forms. Choosing Balance creates a natural Evasion Convergence with Favored Dexterity. Choosing Endurance creates a natural Recovery Convergence with Favored Adaptation. Other combinations may create Power, Density, Capacity, Perception, or Influence Convergences when the chosen Attribute and Specialty legitimately govern the same resolution.

**BREADTH IS NOT A BONUS** — Having all seven Roll Paths in the Remnant Profile does not increase Affinity Dice, grant advantage, or add a universal modifier. Remnant gains the same Rank-scaled Affinity pools as every other Paradigm; its distinction is that one Attribute and one Specialty are individualized.

## Authority Profile

The Remnant Paradigm lacks inherent administrative privileges — no labs, no command posts of their own. Yet their reputation opens doors. Even the most bureaucratic institutions respect the battle-scarred; high-ranking officers and officials often grant Remnant Inquisitors courtesy access, assistance, or supplies out of deference to their experience.

**Advancement** — Authority grows through battlefield merit rather than research or politics. Completing missions, hunting aberrations, and surviving direct combat raise rank and standing. Remnants gain notoriety through deeds, reinforcing their image as hardened survivors.

## Authority Ranks · Designation Progression

| Rank | Title | Role | Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wanderer | Displaced survivor, learning to adapt beyond the battlefield | Minimal access; granted travel and mission rights |
| 2 | Dependent | Contracted operative completing assignments for higher Paradigms | Authorized to undertake combat missions and acquire field gear |
| 3 | Contractor | Field leader commanding small units or mercenary teams | Can coordinate with allied forces and claim combat resources |
| 4 | Chosen / Specialist | Fully recognized Remnant veteran selecting their combat archetype | Significant authority in battle decisions; access to advanced armament lines |

### Rank 4 Specialization Fields

**Juggernaut** — Heavily armored combatant relying on sheer resilience. Commonly emphasizes Recovery for bodily endurance and Power when controlling space through force.

**Skirmisher** — Agile duelist excelling in hit-and-run or close-quarters tactics. Commonly emphasizes Evasion and Density for repositioning, rapid weapon transitions, and compressed physical execution.

**Operator** — Tactical commander or precision striker using combined arms. Commonly emphasizes Capacity for coordination and Perception for battlefield reading and target recognition.

## Combat Specialty

Primarily frontline and tactical. The Remnant excels at physical combat, tactical leadership, and relentless assault. Their combat style is adaptable across many weapon types, blending expertise from a life spent on the battlefield. Unlike narrower Paradigms, Remnant Ciphers may naturally interact with the complete Roll Axis.

**Style** — Quick, brute, tactical precision. They read the flow of battle instinctively and respond with brutal efficiency — conserving movements, exploiting mistakes, and weaponizing endurance.

## Specialties · Genera Domains

**Photonic Energy** — Momentum, acceleration, and light-force applications carried over from the Kinetic rework. Its applications commonly support Power, Density, or Evasion depending on whether the Remnant is imposing, executing, or escaping.

**Neutral Energy** — Balances offense and restraint through equilibrium training. Its applications commonly support Density, Evasion, or Recovery depending on whether the Remnant is executing, avoiding, or enduring.

These domains serve as reflections of the Remnant's duality — brute instinct and calculated control.

## Interactions & Philosophy

Remnants view themselves as living memorials — fragments of wars the world wishes to forget. To them, peace is just the pause before the next calamity. Despite their cynicism, they often act as the moral compass in mixed units, grounding them in harsh pragmatism.

**Allies** — Warfare (combat operations), Evolution (enhanced soldiers), respect granted by most paradigms on merit

**Tensions** — Cognition (differing views on truth and control), Simulation (distrust of fabricated experience), Evolution (views self-alteration as desecration)
