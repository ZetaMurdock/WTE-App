<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Kinba">Kinba</span></h2>
<p><i>The Kinba are a lively and colorful race of beings that dwell in the whimsical realms of the Kinetic Kingdom. These energetic creatures are closely connected to the vibrant forces of motion and animation. With their lively appearances and playful nature, they bring joy and animation wherever they go.</i>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a class="mw-file-description image" data-ext-link="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wonderland-of-the-enigma/images/6/68/IMG_0645.png/revision/latest?cb=20240619222759"><img alt="IMG 0645" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wonderland-of-the-enigma/images/6/68/IMG_0645.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240619222759" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="240" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="IMG 0645.png" data-image-key="IMG_0645.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a class="info-icon" data-wte-link="wte://rules/File:IMG_0645.png"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p><i>In the enchanted lands of the Kinetic Kingdom, where imagination takes form and motion comes alive, a burst of fantastical energy collided with the realm of cartoons. This magical fusion gave rise to the Kinba, a race of animated beings. Created from the essence of animated wonder and boundless energy, they burst into existence with cartoony features that reflect their joyful and vibrant nature.</i>
</p><p><i>The Kinba quickly embraced their animated existence, frolicking through the whimsical landscapes of their kingdom. They reveled in the art of motion and animation, exploring their abilities to manipulate the very essence of movement. Through their adventures and playfulness, they honed their unique skills and developed a deep affinity for the art of animation.</i>
</p>
<h2><span id="Skill.E2.80.99s_and_attributes"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Skill’s_and_attributes"><i>Skill’s and attributes</i></span></h2>
<p><i><b>Motion Manipulation</b>: The Kinba possess extraordinary control over motion. They can manipulate their own movements, stretching, bending, and contorting their bodies in fantastical and exaggerated ways. They can also influence the motion of objects around them, causing them to float, spin, or change direction at will.</i>
</p><p><i><b>Emotional Animation</b>: The Kinba can project their emotions outward, causing their physical appearance to change in a cartoony manner. They might blush with rosy cheeks when embarrassed, emit steam from their ears when angry, or produce heart-shaped eyes when infatuated. This expressive power allows them to communicate their feelings and intentions vividly.</i>
</p><p><i><b>Ink Splash</b>: Kinba have the ability to summon vibrant ink splashes from their fingertips. These colorful splashes can be manipulated to create temporary structures, bridges, or even form whimsical creatures and objects. The ink splashes can also be used defensively, blinding or disorienting opponents with a burst of vibrant colors.</i>
</p><p><i><b>Animate Objects</b>: The Kinba can breathe life into inanimate objects, bringing them to temporary animated existence. These objects gain personalities and abilities corresponding to their nature, allowing them to assist the Kinba in various tasks or engage in playful interactions.</i>
</p><p><i>With their whimsical abilities and boundless energy, the Kinba fill the Kinetic Kingdom with laughter, animation, and joyful adventures. They embrace the fantastical nature of their existence, infusing their surroundings with color, motion, and vibrant personalities. From their playful antics to their incredible animations, the Kinba are the animated heart of the Kinetic Kingdom, forever inspiring wonder and bringing smiles to those around them.</i>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Crestations:">Crestations:</span></h2>
<p><i>* The Crestations, a mysterious race of beings, emerge from the depths of the Enigmatica Universe as enigmatic and powerful entities intertwined with the secrets of the ocean's depths. Born of the primordial energies that flow through the realms, they are attuned to the essence of the deep sea and wield formidable abilities that harness the power of water and marine life.</i>
</p>
<figure class="thumb  show-info-icon" typeof="mw:File/Thumb" style="width: 180px"> 	<a class="mw-file-description image" data-ext-link="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wonderland-of-the-enigma/images/2/29/IMG_0647.png/revision/latest?cb=20240620002415"><img alt="IMG 0647" src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wonderland-of-the-enigma/images/2/29/IMG_0647.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240620002415" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="240" class="thumbimage" data-image-name="IMG 0647.png" data-image-key="IMG_0647.png" data-relevant="1"></a> 	 	<figcaption class="thumbcaption"> 		 			<a class="info-icon" data-wte-link="wte://rules/File:IMG_0647.png"><svg><use xlink:href="#wds-icons-info-small"></use></svg></a> 		 		 		 	</figcaption> </figure><p><i>* In the depths of the Enigmatica Universe, where the ocean's currents converge and the mysteries of the deep unfold, a cataclysmic event occurred that gave rise to the Crestations. As the ancient tides clashed and the primordial forces of water and energy mingled, a surge of aquatic power intertwined with the essence of the ocean depths, birthing this mysterious race. The Crestations materialized as beings of ethereal beauty, adorned with aquatic features that embody the splendor of the underwater realms.</i>
</p><p><i>* Initially, the Crestations dwelled in the hidden realms beneath the ocean's surface, their existence intertwined with the flow of currents and the secrets of the deep. They explored the mysterious abyss, delving into the wonders and perils that lay hidden beneath the waves. Over time, they honed their abilities to manipulate water, commune with marine life, and unlock the secrets of the ocean's depths.</i>
</p>
<h2><span id="Skill.E2.80.99s_and_attributes_2"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Skill’s_and_attributes_2"><i>Skill’s and attributes</i></span></h2>
<p><i><b>* Aquakinesis:</b> Crestations possess unparalleled control over water. They can manipulate and shape water with precision, summoning powerful waves, creating whirlpools, and directing jets of water with great force. They can also manipulate the water within their surroundings, creating barriers or flooding areas to gain a tactical advantage.</i>
</p><p><i>* <b>Marine Empathy</b>: Crestations have a deep connection with marine life. They can communicate with sea creatures, understanding their thoughts and emotions. They can call upon marine creatures to aid them in their endeavors, forming alliances with aquatic beings and harnessing their unique abilities.</i>
</p><p><i><b>* Hydroportation</b>: Crestations possess the ability to teleport through bodies of water. They can dissolve into water and reappear at a different location connected to the water network, allowing them to travel swiftly and unseen through oceans, lakes, and even small bodies of water...</i>
</p><p><i>* <b>Deep Sea Adaptation</b>: Crestations possess the ability to survive and thrive in the most inhospitable depths of the ocean. They can withstand extreme pressures, extract oxygen from water, and move with grace and agility underwater. This adaptation grants them an advantage in aquatic environments, where they can outmaneuver and outlast their adversaries.</i>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Stygians:">Stygians:</span></h2>

<p><i>The Stygians, an ancient race of shadow-dwelling beings, emerged from the depths of the Enigmatica Universe as enigmatic and powerful entities. Born from the primordial energies that flow through the realms, they are attuned to the very essence of darkness, wielding formidable abilities that harness and manipulate shadow itself.</i>
</p><p><i>In the earliest days of creation, when light and darkness were locked in eternal conflict, a cataclysmic event took place—one that would forever alter the balance of existence. As the cosmic forces clashed, a surge of raw, uncontrolled power intertwined with the primordial shadows, giving rise to the first Stygians. They materialized as beings of pure shadow, infused with the essence of darkness itself.</i>
</p><p><i>Initially, the Stygians roamed the realms as formless entities, drifting through the void between realities. They were passive observers, studying the cosmic balance and seeking to understand the delicate equilibrium between light and darkness. Over time, they learned to manipulate shadows, shaping and molding them at will. However, their existence took a dark and irreversible turn upon the arrival of Null—an entity or force so alien that it altered the Stygians at their very core.</i>
</p><p><i>With Null’s influence, the Stygians underwent a grotesque transformation, marking the beginning of what would be known as The Age of Aberrance. Their once-fluid forms became more erratic and amorphous, their very genetic structure corrupted and reshaped by the presence of Null. They ceased to be individuals and instead became an interconnected hive, bound together by vast networks of shadow. Information flowed seamlessly between them, allowing them to share thoughts, experiences, and even abilities across their ever-growing collective.</i>
</p><p><i>With this newfound way of existence, they fully embraced their new identity—The Hive. No longer content with merely observing the cosmos, the Stygians turned outward, seeking to infect, assimilate, and consume. Wherever they ventured—whether a dimension, a reality, or a timeline—their presence alone was enough to ensure expansion. One Stygian’s arrival heralded the emergence of countless others, forming colonies of shadow that grew by feeding on darkness and assimilating other beings into their network.</i>
</p><p><i>It was during this relentless expansion that they crossed paths with the Nigraldi, a similarly dark and enigmatic force. From this meeting, a terrible covenant was forged—The Aberrant Union. With the Nigraldi as their allies, the Stygians delved deeper into the abyss, harnessing the raw power of Null to create something even more horrifying: Null Aberrance—a corruption so virulent that it spread like an unstoppable plague, unraveling the fabric of existence itself.</i>
</p><p><i>Under this newfound power, the Stygians conquered thousands of star systems, dimensions, and realities, even going so far as to corrupt multiple timelines. No force of nature, time, or reality was beyond their reach.</i>
</p><p><i>This is the Stygians—the Aberrant Ones. A race reborn through corruption, bound to the endless hunger of Null Aberrance.</i>
</p>

The Elderooth, guardians of ancient wisdom, shall leave an indelible mark on history, shaping the destiny of Enigmatica itself.</i>

</p><p><b>Perfect Reflexes:</b> The e’Inderitch have perfect reflexes, allowing them to make true opportunity attacks against any creature whose action priority is less than theirs.
</p><p><b>True Eye:</b> After many years of solitude, the e’Inderitch have gained the ability to see the unseen. By rolling Wisdom with a DC of 10 or higher, they can access information about anything they look at.
</p><p><b>Eldritch Body:</b> The e’Inderitch have bodies honed by thousands of years of deep space travel and void exposure. With blood of magma and skin of tungsten, they are immune to all negative status effects. Their only vulnerabilities are to spirit and chaos-based abilities.

<h2><span id="Greahtias.27kin"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Greahtias'kin">Greahtias'kin</span></h2>
<p>Emerging from the scorched dunes of a long-forsaken land, the Greahtias’Kin were born from the ashes of forgotten entities—beings that were neither men nor gods. They trace their origins to a dimension lost to time and memory, known only as Xothos. This enigmatic realm is veiled in mystery, yet its influence is undeniable, as countless enigmas and otherworldly beings trace their beginnings to its shadowed expanse.The Greahtias’Kin are wanderers, drifting across dimensions in an endless search for an artifact of unparalleled power and significance: the Spiral Engine. This device, central to their homeland, once defined their very existence. In the multiverse they now traverse, it is known as the Enigma Engine, a construct that continues to elude their grasp. Their journey has spanned countless epochs, driven by purpose and destiny, though the true nature of their quest is a secret they guard closely.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Physical_Characteristics">Physical Characteristics</span></h3>
<p>The Greahtias’Kin are humanoid in form but possess an otherworldly elegance that sets them apart from mortals. Their skin ranges from the deepest, richest shades of dark brown to the purest, gleaming snow white, often with an ethereal sheen that makes them seem as if sculpted from starlight or forged from shadows. Their natural hair, as diverse as their skin tones, can be any color imaginable, from shimmering metallic silvers and golds to vibrant blues, fiery reds, and even hues that shift like liquid rainbows under different lighting. Their presence exudes an aura of majesty, and their appearance often leaves onlookers in awe—or terror.
</p><p><br>
Most striking of all are the six sets of wings that adorn their bodies, symbols of their divine craftsmanship and boundless ambition. These wings are not confined to their backs but emerge from different parts of their form, each set contributing to their enigmatic beauty:
</p>
<pre>Head: A crown-like pair of wings, often smaller and more decorative, framing their visage like a celestial halo.
</pre>
<pre>Neck/Shoulders: A sleek, protective set that spreads outward in moments of tension or flight, resembling a living mantle of feathers.
</pre>
<pre>Chest: Powerful and broad, these wings radiate strength, with plumage that glimmers like woven light or shadow.
</pre>
<pre>Back: The largest and most dominant wings, designed for long journeys and dimensional travel, their movements ripple with raw energy.
</pre>
<pre>Legs: Streamlined and aerodynamic, these wings aid in agility, granting unparalleled grace and speed on land and in air.
</pre>
<pre>Lower Body (Hips): A final set, smaller yet versatile, used to stabilize their movements or create bursts of speed in close combat.
</pre>
<p><br>
Their wings are masterpieces of form and function, with feathers that shimmer in hues beyond mortal comprehension. These feathers can shift texture and color, reflecting their emotions, environment, or even the energy they channel through their creations. In battle, their wings serve not only for flight but also as shields, weapons, and conduits for their immense technological and dimensional powers.
</p>
<h5><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Engineers">The Engineers</span></h5>
<p>Distant cousins to the e’Inderitch and direct kin to the Elderooth, the Greahtias’Kin share ancient bloodlines steeped in power and mystery. They are among the Weakest to emerge from Xothos, earning the title of Engineers for their unparalleled ingenuity. Architects of wonders that defy comprehension, they stand as masters of creation and innovation.Their most famous achievement is the Dyson Sphere, a monumental structure that harnesses the energy of stars to power entire civilizations. Yet this is only the beginning. The Greahtias’Kin have forged weapons capable of severing the bonds of reality, machines that manipulate the flow of time and space, and constructs that merge technology with living organisms, creating hybrids of life and machine.
</p>
<h5><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Burden_of_Purpose"><b>The Burden of Purpose</b></span></h5>
<p>Despite their brilliance, the Greahtias’Kin are a race burdened by the weight of their quest. Their search for the Spiral Engine has defined their existence, but it has also divided them. Some are relentless and unwavering, believing their destiny is to reclaim what was lost and restore their greatness. Others are disillusioned, questioning the purpose of their journey and seeking meaning beyond the Spiral Engine.This division has led to splinters within their society. Some factions, known as the Ascendants, seek to transcend their physical forms entirely, believing the Spiral Engine is a metaphor for enlightenment rather than a literal artifact. Others, known as the Wardens of Xothos, are fiercely protective of their heritage, using their technological mastery to defend the remnants of their lost dimension and ensure its legacy endures.Wherever the Greahtias’Kin tread, their presence leaves ripples across dimensions. They are both revered and feared—legends of their kind tell of celestial architects whose creations inspire awe but whose power can bring ruin to worlds unprepared for their ambition. Though their connection to the e’Inderitch and Elderooth ties them to shared history, it also creates tension. The e’Inderitch view the Greahtias’Kin’s focus on creation as reckless, while the Elderooth see them as rivals for dominion over ancient knowledge.
</p><p>Yet, through it all, the Greahtias’Kin remain steadfast. Their wings bear them through the endless expanse of the multiverse, and their creations stand as monuments to their genius. They are wanderers, builders, and seekers of the unfathomable, bound to the mysteries of Xothos and the endless enigma of existence.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Skills_and_Attributes">Skills and Attributes</span></h2>
<p><b>Anti Matter Creation:</b>The Greahtias’Kin’s wings possess an innate and awe-inspiring property: they passively generate antimatter. This antimatter cannot be controlled through conventional means and instead reacts only to the movements of the wings. When the wings move, the antimatter shifts in tandem, creating waves of distortion that ripple through the fabric of reality.Any organic matter, including living beings or natural materials, that comes into direct contact with this antimatter is subject to catastrophic displacement. The affected matter is unmade at a molecular level, creating localized disruptions that can destabilize the environment. Even the Greahtias’Kin themselves are not immune to the effects of their antimatter.To mitigate this immense power and protect themselves, the Greahtias’Kin developed a unique and intricate system of movement known as the Dance of Displacement. This elegant, flowing pattern of motion allows them to subtly manipulate the antimatter without causing harm to themselves or unintended destruction to their surroundings. The Dance of Displacement is a testament to their ingenuity, blending artistry and survival into a harmonious display of control.
</p><p><b>Spatial Destruction:</b> The Greahtias’Kin’s unparalleled mastery over antimatter grants them the ability to tear apart the very fabric of space-time in two distinct locations that have been tainted by their antimatter. By exploiting the unstable properties of these areas, they can create a dimensional rift—a gateway that connects the two points.This rift functions as a personal warp tunnel, allowing the Greahtias’Kin to traverse between the two locations instantaneously. Only they may pass through this rift, as the antimatter-infused energy is too volatile for other beings or entities to endure.
</p><p><b>Distant Vision:</b> The Greahtias’Kin possess an extraordinary ability to peer into areas or objects tainted by their antimatter. Once their antimatter comes into contact with organic or inorganic matter, it creates a permanent bond, allowing them to view the world from the perspective of the affected entity or object. This connection is eternal unless the tainted matter is completely destroyed. Through concentration, the Greahtias’Kin can focus their senses to remotely perceive the surroundings of the infected area, regardless of distance or dimension, making them unparalleled in reconnaissance and observation. However, attempting to switch perspectives too quickly can strain their senses, causing temporary disorientation.
</p><p><b>Horizons End:</b> The Greahtias’Kin selects a point within 100 feet, summoning the End Horizon, a warped anomaly where the fabric of space is inverted, appearing as a distorted, upside-down version of its surroundings. Any creature that looks at the End Horizon must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or have their perception of direction permanently inverted for the anomaly's duration, causing any attempt to walk away from it to instead lead them toward it. If a creature collides with the End Horizon, they become trapped, taking 2d15 Suffocating damage multiplied by 1.5 and 2d10 Force damage for every turn they remain stuck. Trapped creatures can only escape with the assistance of another person who successfully frees them. The End Horizon lasts for 5 rounds, and creatures that succeed on their Wisdom saving throw are immune to its effects for the duration. The Greahtias’Kin can dismiss the anomaly as a free action.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Primordial">Primordial</span></h2>
<p>Created from the very essence of the original children of destruction and creation, the Primordials are the Oracle's ultimate enforcers. Towering between 145 to 394 feet, these humanoid titans possess an overwhelming presence. At birth, they are imbued with either the power to destroy worlds or the ability to create and rejuvenate life. A rare few are gifted with both.
</p><p>Sealed away by the Enigma Engine due to their uncontrollable power, the Primordials have now been unleashed upon the cosmos once more, their wrath reignited by the e'Inderitch. As ancient enemies, the e'Inderitch and Primordials engage in epic battles, reshaping worlds and altering the fate of civilizations. The Primordials' dual nature makes them both creators of beauty and harbingers of doom, their actions forever etched into the annals of cosmic history.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="SubDermin">SubDermin</span></h2>
<p>Once thriving on the surface of their irradiated world, the Rock Ghouls were forced underground when their star unleashed devastating waves of radiation. Over generations, their bodies adapted to the harsh subterranean environment, their skin becoming thick, calloused, and dark—hardened like stone to withstand the unforgiving depths.
</p><p>However, deep within the planet, they uncovered a vast hollow-earth expanse, where rivers of luminous blue and green energy coursed through the cavernous domain. When this enigmatic energy came into contact with their rugged skin, it seeped into the cracks, breaking apart the hardened layers and flowing through their very being. This transformation birthed the SubDermin, a unique fusion of their bodies and the planet’s energy, granting them newfound abilities and a connection to the living essence of their world.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Skills_and_Attributes_2">Skills and Attributes</span></h2>
<ul><li>Forsaken Touch the <b>Subdermin</b> possess a unique ability to <b>corrupt through change</b>, not in the traditional sense of decay, but through <b>transmutation and alteration</b>. Due to the radioactive nature of their <b>eldritch home world</b>, their bodies have adapted to process radiation differently. Instead of harming organic life, their radiation <b>acts as a force of creation and transformation</b>. Their anatomy converts radiation into <b>Nexus Particles</b>, which break down and reassemble matter at a fundamental level. This allows them to <b>redistribute</b> radioactive energy as either <b>Positive or Negative Eldritch Energy</b>, granting them the ability to <b>alter the physical properties</b> of phenomena—organic or inorganic—simply by touch. When touching a creature, they can choose to either <b>heal</b> or <b>harm</b> it. The damage or healing dealt is equal to the <b>ode level of the target × d20</b> (XD20).</li>
<li>Radioactive Anatomy Subdermin anatomy is inherently radioactive. The bacteria within their Augo Gland emit dangerously high levels of radiation, far surpassing that of most creatures. This radiation serves as both their primary energy source and a means of passive eldritch energy generation. This eldritch energy allows them to subtly alter their environment over time, bending reality in ways unique to their biology.</li>
<li>Terrestrial Knowledge The Subdermin possess an extraordinary connection to their surroundings. Within any territory they have claimed, they can perceive all forms of terrestrial movement with unnatural precision. Any disturbance within their domain—no matter how subtle—alerts them, even if they are physically outside the territory at the time.</li>
<li>Living Planet The Subdermin can merge their bodies with the planet itself, entering a deep hibernation state. While in this state, they gradually accumulate radiation until they reach critical mass. Upon awakening, all stored radiation is instantly unleashed as eldritch energy, triggering a massive warping explosion. This explosion reshapes the terrain, forming jagged, glowing metallic spikes of green and blue that pulsate with unstable energy. Any creatures caught within the blast are irrevocably altered, their bodies mutating into a distinct “Dermin” appearance—marked by cracked, glowing skin of green and blue, as well as aberrant anatomical shifts.</li></ul>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Valltore">Valltore</span></h2>
<p>The Valltore were the result of the Oracle’s ambitious experiment to create a hybrid between the Kinba, Hyomen, Voaulton, and Mirage, with the Hyomen serving as the primary biological foundation. For eons, the Oracle tirelessly refined their creation, seeking to forge something truly magnificent. After thousands of years, the Valltore finally emerged—a race that, physically, was the most humanoid of all hybrids, possessing an ethereal beauty rivaling, if not surpassing, that of the Greahtias'Kin. 
</p><p>What sets the Valltore apart is their unparalleled ability to manipulate the shape and size of their Augo Gland, giving birth to the creatures known as an Aahg granting it sentience. They can physically materialize their Augo Gland outside of their skin, where it appears to originate from an intricate tattoo-like marking. Once externalized, this gland takes on a life of its own, acting as a completely independent entity—one that can function autonomously even when detached from its host. This symbiotic relationship between the Valltore and their living Augo Gland makes them uniquely versatile and dangerously unpredictable
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Skills_and_Attributes_3">Skills and Attributes</span></h2>
<ul><li>Aahg Manifestation
<ul><li>The Valltore possess an Augo Gland that they can externalize from their epidermis, forming a sentient entity known as the Aahg. This Aahg acts as a completely separate creature while remaining an extension of the Valltore’s life force—if the Aahg perishes, so does the Valltore. The Aahg can attack in tandem with the Valltore and replicate any actions the Valltore can perform. Additionally, the Valltore can imbue their rolls with the Aahg, creating <b>Aahg Rolls</b>, which add their <b>Ode Level</b> as a bonus. Unlike most beings, when determining their <b>Ode Level</b>, Valltore roll <b>4 dice instead of 3</b>, giving them a greater potential for power.</li>
<li>Organic Corruption The Valltore can use their Aahg to <b>hijack and manipulate the organic matter</b> of another creature. As long as the target’s <b>Ode Level</b> is lower than the Valltore’s, they can corrupt their biology, force mutations, weaken bodily functions, or even take partial control of their form. This ability allows for a wide range of offensive and tactical applications, from disrupting an enemy’s body to reshaping organic structures for their own benefit.</li></ul></li>
<li>Bacterial Generation The Valltore can <b>passively and massively generate bacteria</b> within their Aahg, which they can manipulate at will due to the Aahg’s sentience. By altering the bacterial composition, they can <b>change the properties of abilities mid-battle</b>, adapting to new threats or enhancing their attacks. These bacterial modifications can be used to <b>heal, poison, corrode, or reinforce biological and even some inorganic matter</b>, making the Valltore highly unpredictable in combat.
<ul><li>Archetypal Mastery The Aahg, while an extension of the Valltore, is also its own entity with independent potential. Upon character creation, the Valltore gains <b>5 Archetype Points</b>, which they can allocate to any abilities across different archetypes. These selected abilities become permanently available to the Aahg, giving it access to a unique, specialized set of powers separate from the Valltore’s own. This allows for <b>unparalleled versatility</b>, making the Valltore capable of integrating multiple fighting styles into their playstyle.</li></ul></li></ul>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Taekoeshi">Taekoeshi</span></h2>
<p>Born from the Oracle’s experimentation, the Taekoeshi were once simple oceanic predators known as the Taka. These deep-sea hunters lurked in the abyss, their existence defined by an unrelenting pursuit of prey. However, when the Oracle granted them sentience, a divergence occurred—some remained as Taka, while others evolved into the Taekoeshi, a far more intelligent and formidable species.
</p><p>Each Taekoeshi maintains a symbiotic bond with a Taka, which serves as both their companion and their lure. The Taka, often mistaken for a harmless or mesmerizing ocean creature, draws in unsuspecting prey—be it marine life, wayward ships, or even reckless adventurers—before the Taekoeshi emerges from the depths to strike. This predatory partnership has led to countless myths of ghostly lights or strange apparitions beneath the waves, all ending in the same grim fate: complete and utter consumption.
</p><p>Closely related to crustaceans, the Taekoeshi share many of their traits, from hardened exoskeletons to the ability to regenerate lost limbs. Their physiology allows them to thrive in the crushing depths of the ocean, yet their intelligence and adaptability make them just as deadly near the surface. With the hunger of a predator and the mind of a strategist, the Taekoeshi are an ever-present nightmare lurking beneath the waves.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Skills_and_Attributes_4">Skills and Attributes</span></h2>

All creatures connected through this bond become part of a <b>shared mental network</b>, creating a seemingly familial link that persists unless severed by death. Creatures linked to you via this bond are automatically considered allies. You <b>cannot attack them, and they cannot attack you</b>. This bond can only be applied to creatures with whom you share a <b>positive reputation</b>, and vice versa.</li>
<li>Parasitic Companion This species naturally possesses a <b>companion</b> known as a <b>Taka</b>, which can be summoned at any time. The Taka mirrors your <b>available actions</b> and can attack during <b>active time</b>. However, its attacks are treated as <b>Opportunity Attacks</b>, meaning they can be countered. If the Taka is killed, it takes a full <b>in-game day</b> to regenerate, reforming <b>within your stomach</b> before it can be summoned again.</li>
<li>Lure of the deep You possess an unnatural allure that can <b>compel</b> creatures to obey your will. Any creature that fails a <b>Charisma check (DC 14)</b> is <b>forced to follow your commands</b> until the effect is broken.</li>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Cerioisk">Cerioisk</span></h2>
<p>The Cerioisk are an anomaly—a race born from an unstable experiment gone horribly wrong during the Witching Moon, a celestial event that passes by each red-sun system, leaving behind strange and often dangerous consequences. During the Oracle’s era of creation, when countless races and species were being formed, an attempt was made to craft something more obscure—a being that thrived in the depths of darkness. To achieve this, the dying essence of the Seed was fused with the decaying body of a Kinba, an experiment meant to push the boundaries of life and death. However, under the eerie influence of the Witching Moon, the embryonic Cerioisk awakened prematurely. It escaped into the dead of night, leaving a trail of devastation in its wake. Fueled by an insatiable hunger, the Cerioisk preyed upon other creatures—draining them dry, targeting Augo Glands specifically. Their terrifying ability allows them to nullify Augo Gland effects, break down Augo Gland bacteria, and absorb their power. With each consumed Augo Gland, a Cerioisk gains new abilities and grows stronger. Worse still, each devoured Augo Gland spawns another Cerioisk, ensuring their relentless spread. Now, they rule the forests and the night, turning every Witching Moon into a time of fear—when no one dares to step outside, lest they fall prey to the ever-growing swarm of Cerioisk lurking in the shadows.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Skills_and_Attributes_5">Skills and Attributes</span></h2>
<ul><li>Augo Consumption   This species has the ability to <b>consume and absorb the Augo glands</b> of other creatures, using them to enhance its own abilities. For every <b>10th Augo gland absorbed</b>, the <b>Cerioisk</b> enters a <b>cocoon state</b>, which only opens at <b>midnight on a Sunday</b>. While in this state, the <b>Cerioisk</b> undergoes a <b>genetic breakdown</b> of its surroundings within a <b>4,000-meter radius</b>, analyzing the environment and the dominant traits of local creatures. Based on this analysis, the <b>Cerioisk</b> will develop new <b>biological markers, defensive adaptations, and offensive traits</b> suited for survival. For example, if many creatures in the area possess <b>sharp talons or fangs</b>, the <b>Cerioisk</b> may develop the ability to <b>harden its skin</b>. Each day leading up to <b>Sunday</b>, you will be presented with <b>evolutionary options</b> by your <b>Director</b>, selecting <b>one</b> adaptation per day. When the cocoon opens, the <b>Cerioisk</b> emerges with these new traits <b>and gains +1 to all base ability scores</b>.
<ul><li>Corruptive AS reproduction Whenever you <b>consume</b> a creature through an <b>orifice</b>, a <b>new Cerioisk</b> is <b>formed</b> from your <b>waste</b>, created specifically through the digestion of an <b>Augo gland</b>.</li></ul></li>
<li>Lingering Torment If you <b>devour the Augo gland</b> of a creature with a <b>strong lineage</b>, a <b>genetic warning marker</b> is imprinted on your presence. Whenever you come into contact with <b>any descendant or relative</b> of that creature, they will experience an <b>innate sense of danger</b>, triggering an immediate <b>fight-or-flight</b> response. There is a <b>90% chance</b> that they will <b>attempt to flee</b> upon encountering you.</li>
<li>Hexing Presence All creatures with <b>Augo glands</b> within your presence suffer <b>disadvantage</b> on <b>control rolls</b> and <b>Supernal-based rolls</b> related to their Augo gland. The <b>disadvantage value</b> is equal to your <b>Ode Level</b>.</li></ul>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Lowhana">Lowhana</span></h2>
<p>The Lowhana are a variant of Mirage that became permanently genetically tainted after a freak accident. This incident left a singular Mirage with the ability to transform only into dead creatures. In a desperate attempt to survive the cold, it transformed into a wolf, but unfortunately, it still perished in that form. Scientists later discovered the wolf and brought it to a lab run by a scientist named Valin. He preserved the wolf and hung it in a test tube, realizing that it was a Mirage—specifically, a defective one. Valin decided to meld the Mirage's tissue with that of his daughter, who suffered from a condition that would soon lead to her death. Her cells struggled to divide, and the old cells had difficulty decaying, leaving her as a slowly growing, cancerous, tumorous mass.
</p><p>Valin thought that perhaps he could kickstart her cellular process by giving her the genetic DNA of a Mirage. He strapped her to a table and began drilling into her bone marrow to administer the first injection, followed by an injection into her spine. As this happened, the Mirage's DNA accelerated the process that had been slowed down. However, since the Mirage was defective and could only transform into beasts, the same fate awaited her—only worse.
</p><p>She grotesquely transformed into a cancerous, malnourished, and sickly wolf, with elongated arms and fingers, and extremely sharp claws that could almost cut through anything. Her eyes were as black and deep as the abyss, and her fur coat, while beautiful, was terrifying, filled with fleshy appendages. In this feral state, she devoured her father. Afterward, she transformed back and now lives with an alternate being within her body that calls itself the Hyena. As she killed her father, another entity grew from his body, gaining abilities similar to his daughter's and also taking on the name Hyena. They became linked, and the Hyena manifested within her, leading to the creation of a new species known forever as the Lowhana, the Undeath Bringers.
