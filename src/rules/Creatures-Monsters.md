<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Eldritch">Eldritch</span></h2>
<h3><span id="Eldar.27iain"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Eldar'iain">Eldar'iain</span></h3>
<p>The Eldar'iain, an imposing Eldritch being standing at 25 feet, is renowned for its powerful manipulation of space and perception. Its <b>Rewind-Restrict</b> ability creates a formidable control mechanism; any creature within its line of sight attempting to advance will be forcibly pulled back or teleported to its previous location, creating a significant obstacle for positioning and strategy. The <b>Gaze of Displacement</b> allows the Eldar'iain to target and teleport a single creature to a random location within 30 feet, potentially placing them in precarious or disadvantageous situations. The <b>Warping Aura</b> expands its control, affecting all creatures within a 20-foot radius by reversing their movement direction on a failed Dexterity roll, adding a chaotic element to combat and exploration. These skills, combined with its <b>Magical Amplification</b> trait, which boosts the power of its magical abilities, make the Eldar'iain a complex and dangerous opponent that challenges both the tactical and spatial planning of those who confront it.
</p><p><b>Species:</b> Eldritch
</p><p><b>Size:</b> 25ft
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Eldar">Eldar</span></h3>
<p>The Eldar is a colossal being with the ability to wield celestial and cosmic forces to its advantage. It creates zones of astral energy that alter the battlefield dynamically, providing both beneficial and detrimental effects to those within. This unpredictable environment forces players to continually adjust their strategies and positioning. The Eldar can unleash a Starfire Burst, a powerful attack that deals radiant damage to enemies while simultaneously healing allies, and has the potential to blind foes if they fail a saving throw. Its Cosmic Insight ability provides tactical advantages by granting its allies enhanced rolls on attacks and checks, while potentially disadvantaging enemies, thus shifting the momentum of combat in its favor.
</p><p><b>Species:</b> EldritchEldar'ian
</p><p><b>Size:</b> 60ft
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Traits:_2">Traits:</span></h4>
<ul><li><b>Elemental Infusion:</b> Temporarily imbues attacks with elemental damage. Roll a D6. On a roll of 5 or higher, the infused attacks also have a chance to apply a corresponding status effect, such as burning or freezing the target. If burning is applied, activate the talent "Multi-Trait Combination." If the traits you choose are Lightning, Poison, Cold, Radiant, or Necrotic, roll a D40. If the roll is 30 or higher, activate "Magical Amplification." If you don't own it, the effectiveness (damage) will be -10%.</li></ul>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Boaboal">Boaboal</span></h3>
<p>The Boaboal, a formidable 25-foot Descended entity, combines brute strength with a deadly poison capability. Its <b>Soul Drain</b> ability creates a profound impact in close combat; within a 5-foot radius, it inflicts soul damage that forces targets to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 15). Failure results in 1d10 soul damage and disadvantage on all actions until the end of their next turn, significantly impairing their combat effectiveness. This ability is complemented by its <b>Poisoned Strike</b>, which applies poison damage to all attacks. Initially, the poison inflicts 1d6 damage at the start of the target's turn, with a chance to extend its duration and deal additional necrotic damage if the duration is extended. The Boaboal's imposing endurance (END 60) makes it a resilient opponent, capable of sustaining damage while continuously applying its debilitating effects through poison and soul drain.
