<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Inner_Workings_of_a_Character">Inner Workings of a Character</span></h2>
<p><b>Welcome to Wonderland of the Enigma</b>
</p><p>In <i>Wonderland of the Enigma</i>, characters are defined by two core attributes—no more, no less. These are <b>Inherence</b> (your Place of Origin, encompassing background, genetics, culture, and spirituality) and <b>Specialization</b> (what you are and what you become, focusing on your paradigm, role, and field of expertise). Together, these elements shape your character’s identity, capabilities, and development throughout the game’s mysterious expanse.
</p><p>Character creation is designed to be intuitive and immersive, allowing you to craft a unique being who fits naturally into the game’s world. Once the foundations are complete, you’ll assign stats, skills, and specialties—then step boldly into adventure. Remember, all choices should be discussed with your Curator (Game Master) for balance and thematic alignment.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span id="Inherence_.28Place_of_Origin.29"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Inherence_(Place_of_Origin)">Inherence (Place of Origin)</span></h2>
<p>Inherence establishes the roots of your character’s existence. It weaves together genetic, cultural, and spiritual influences to determine who you are and how you interpret the world.
</p><p><b>Step 1: Template (Species Template)</b>
</p><p>Your Template defines your character’s physical form—species or base race—and determines your physiology, inherent traits, and natural aptitudes.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Genetic Traits:</b> Customizable options that express your heritage (e.g., scale color, eye design, spiritual markings).</li>
<li><b>Incepts:</b> Growth-based abilities acquired through in-game challenges. These represent evolution through experience, not initial power.</li></ul>
<p><b>Step 4: Planet Origin</b>
</p><p>Choose your homeworld or realm of birth. Each planet defines your environmental familiarity, cultural ties, and baseline worldview.
</p><p><b>Step 5: Background</b>
</p><p>Select a Background to supply core life experience and learned skillsets. This choice defines your knowledge, social habits, and adaptability.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Field_of_Operation">Field of Operation</span></h2>
<p>defines <i>how</i> your character operates in the world: their philosophy, power system, and combat focus. It consists of your <b>Paradigm</b> and <b>Asymmetries</b> (which include Incept Genera and Varna that evolve over time).
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Paradigm">Paradigm</span></h2>
<p>Your <b>Paradigm</b> is your core operational philosophy—the doctrine that governs your power, technology, and authority. It establishes your role identity in both narrative and combat contexts, dictating your field access, combat style, and technological or psychic focus.
</p><p>Each Paradigm represents a distinct discipline within Wonderland’s complex structure of science, mysticism, and war. Examples include:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Science:</b> Masters of technology and creation. They wield inventions, constructs, and laboratory resources.</li>
<li><b>Simulation:</b> Manipulators of perception, illusion, and sensory networks. They blend stealth and misinformation.</li>
<li><b>Remnant:</b> Agile combatants and field leaders who thrive on adaptability, precision, and close-quarters efficiency.</li>
<li><b>Cognition:</b> Experts of the mind, wielding psychic command and social influence to reshape behaviors and realities.</li>
<li><b>Evolution:</b> Biotechnicians and shapeshifters who mutate or adapt themselves and allies through symbiosis.</li>
<li><b>Warfare:</b> Commanders and tacticians specializing in weapon mastery, machinery, and battlefield dominance.</li></ul>
<p>Your chosen Paradigm determines not only your abilities and combat method but also your <i>Authority Access</i>—the systems, facilities, and tools your rank allows you to engage with.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Genera_Domain">Genera Domain</span></h2>
<p>After choosing a Paradigm, align it with one of the five energy types: <b>Neutral, Eldritch, Null, Elemental, or Kinetic.</b> This choice governs your power’s source and behavior across world interactions and combat.
</p><p>Each Genera Domain comes with <b>Genus Abilities</b>, versatile powers that channel your energy type to produce direct effects—reinforcement, emission, or transformation.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Asymmetries:_Incept_Genera_and_Varna">Asymmetries: Incept Genera and Varna</span></h2>
<ul><li><b>Genera:</b> Advanced abilities linked to your chosen energy domain, unlocked via trials and experiential mastery.</li>
<li><b>Varna:</b> Manifestations of repeated will, divided into <i>Mundane (physical)</i> and <i>Supernatural (mystic)</i> paths. These grow naturally as your character evolves through story and struggle.</li></ul>
<p>With your <b>Paradigm</b> and <b>Genera Domain</b> selected, your Specialization is complete. The rest—your powers, growth paths, and reputation—will unfold through play.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Stats_and_Skills">Stats and Skills</span></h2>
<p>To bring your creation to life, assign values to define performance and capability.
</p><p><b>Base Skills</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Strength:</b> Physical might and melee aptitude.</li>
<li><b>Dexterity:</b> Agility, precision, and balance.</li>
<li><b>Wisdom:</b> Perception, intuition, and arcane sense.</li>
<li><b>Endurance:</b> Health, resistance, and stamina.</li>
<li><b>Charisma:</b> Presence, command, and persuasion.</li>
<li><b>Action Priority:</b> Determines turn order and reaction speed.</li></ul>
<p>Each is rolled (e.g., 1d20 + 5) and capped at 20.
