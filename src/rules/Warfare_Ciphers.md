# Warfare Ciphers

WTE · MECHANICS · PARADIGM CIPHER · WARFARE

## Warfare Paradigm Overview

The Warfare Paradigm distributes its Ciphers through discipline through abundance — its philosophy is not one of scarcity or selective mastery, but of overwhelming preparedness. Every Inquisitor of the Warfare Paradigm is expected to carry more than they need, know more than they're asked, and strike with more than is required.

The Warfare Paradigm operates on a single, unambiguous doctrine: "Strength is the only language order understands." Its Inquisitors are soldiers, strategists, and enforcers — individuals who believe that conflict, properly directed, is the most efficient architect of peace. They do not shy from violence. They codify it, systematize it, and weaponize it as policy — and codified violence is the point: every Warfare Cipher kills the way a soldier does, through its listed damage, Checks, Saves, and Roll Paths, never by decree.

Warfare Ciphers are built around control, dominance, and operational command. They enhance the Inquisitor's ability to subjugate, command, and destroy — but always with an eye toward structure. The Paradigm has no interest in chaos. It manufactures order through overwhelming force, then maintains that order with iron discipline and authority.

**AUTHORITY STRUCTURE** — The Warfare Paradigm operates through strict military rank. Cipher access is tied directly to rank advancement. Private → Corporal → Captain. Each tier unlocks additional operational authority and field autonomy. Rank is not ceremonial — it determines the scope of commands an Inquisitor can legally issue and enforce.

## Offline Ciphers · TL ≤ 2.0 · Fixed / Unregulated

### Weaponize

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 25 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Corporal |
| Component | Inanimate Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When affixed to any inanimate object, restructures the object's form and energy signature to function as a weapon. The object's original purpose becomes secondary; its new function is optimized for offensive application regardless of what it was before.

EFFECT — The weaponized object deals damage as a Tier-appropriate weapon, using that weapon's standard dice and Physical Check — Power whenever an offensive resolution is required. The Inquisitor may choose one of three damage types upon activation: Blunt (wide impact, knockback), Piercing (focused, armor-penetrating), or Slashing (broad edge, bleeding potential). Additionally, the object gains a passive effect tied to the Inquisitor's active Genus Domain — for example, a weaponized chair from a Fire Domain Inquisitor may deal heat-based secondary damage. The modification persists until the object is destroyed or the Cipher is deactivated.

### Trixt Link

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 24 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Corporal |
| Component | Inanimate Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When affixed to an object, generates a tactical link that connects it to other objects bearing the same Cipher within operational range. Each linked object becomes a node in a coordinated network. Node status, orientation, and energy level are communicated in real time across the chain.

EFFECT — When activated, the Inquisitor can trigger all linked objects simultaneously or in programmed sequence. Each object executes a predetermined action — deployment, launch, activation, or detonation (each retaining its own listed damage, Checks, Saves, Roll Paths, and limits) — in the order the Inquisitor dictates. This enables coordinated multi-point strikes, simultaneous utility deployment, and layered field traps. The link operates without signal latency and cannot be intercepted by standard electronic countermeasures.

### Command

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 36 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Private |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an animate subject, establishes a direct command link from the Inquisitor to the target's motor response centers. The link does not override higher cognition — the target remains aware. However, compliance with directives becomes the path of least resistance, as the Cipher suppresses the neurological cost of defiance.

EFFECT — The Inquisitor may issue a single direct command per activation (move, stop, stand down, protect, etc.). The Inquisitor makes a Mental Check — Capacity against the target's Influence Dice Value. If the Check succeeds, the target executes the command. If the Check fails, the target resists but is still mildly affected — hesitating for one beat or experiencing a pull toward compliance that they push through. Commands cannot be suicidal or demand the target harm themselves — the link snaps before it carries such an order. Allies with pre-established trust may receive commands as free actions without a resistance resolution.

### Seize

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 45 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Corporal |
| Component | Inanimate Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When affixed to an object, establishes a hostile takeover protocol that continuously pings the object's current owner or holder, attempting to override their proprietary link to it. If the object has no existing bond, the takeover completes immediately.

On success, the Inquisitor gains full operational control. Objects with no defensive link simply comply. Seized objects obey the Inquisitor as if originally bonded to them.

### Bullwark

### Authoritative

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 45 |
| Action | Standard Action |
| Rank | Corporal |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to the Inquisitor, amplifies the projection of command authority, expanding their presence into a field of perceived hierarchical dominance. EFFECT — While active, the Inquisitor gains advantage on Mental Checks — Capacity made to persuade, intimidate, direct, or establish battlefield authority. ### Deceist

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 15 |
| Action | Bonus Action |
| Rank | Private |
| Component | Inanimate Object |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When affixed to an object, causes it to broadcast a false identification signature — projecting a false reading to any scanning, detection, or analytical system that attempts to identify it. The false signature is precise and stable, indistinguishable from authentic data to Tier-2 and below detection systems.

EFFECT — The object appears to be whatever the Inquisitor designates — a different item, a benign tool, a cleared clearance token, or simply a registered civilian object. If the deception is discovered, the object immediately reverts to its true signature and cannot be masked again by this Cipher for 24 hours.

### Law Bind

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 25 |
| Action | Prepared Action |
| Rank | Private |
| Component | Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an animate subject, creates a binding behavioral protocol tethered to a specific instruction or prohibition issued by the Inquisitor. The protocol embeds into the subject's behavioral layer — not overriding will, but creating a hard rule that the subject's actions are evaluated against in real time.

EFFECT — The bound subject cannot take a specified category of action (attack, retreat, speak, use Cipher, etc.) while the Law Bind is active. When the bind is established, the Inquisitor makes a Mental Check — Capacity and records the result. Whenever the subject attempts to violate the bind, compare that result against the subject's Influence Dice Value. If the Capacity result meets or exceeds the Influence DV, the action is suppressed and the subject is momentarily stunned as the bind reasserts. If the Influence DV exceeds the Capacity result, the action proceeds but the subject takes 1d8 psychic damage from the rupture. The bind lasts until dismissed, until the Inquisitor is incapacitated, or until the subject overcomes the bind three consecutive times. A bind can never prohibit breathing, feeding, or self-preservation — the behavioral layer refuses to hold such a law.

#### Actions

- Cost: 25 SS
- Roll: Mental Check — Capacity
- Fail: Damage: 1d8 Psychic
- Ruling: the recorded Capacity result is compared against the subject's Influence Dice Value on every attempted violation — meeting or exceeding it suppresses the action and momentarily stuns the subject; a bind can never prohibit breathing, feeding, or self-preservation

### Hollow Shell

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SS | 25 |
| Action | Prepared Action |
| Rank | Captain |
| Component | Inanimate Object / Animate Subject |

ACTIVE MODIFICATION — When applied to an object or animate subject, strips away all non-essential structure — everything that makes the target what it is beyond raw form and function. What remains is a shell: intact in shape, responsive to touch, but evacuated of internal content, personality, or autonomous capacity.

EFFECT — An object becomes completely hollow — its structural frame intact but its internal mechanisms, components, or material contents removed. It can still be wielded or carried but has no functional output. If applied to an animate subject, the Inquisitor makes a Mental Check — Capacity against the subject's Influence Dice Value. On success, the subject remains physically present — standing, breathing — but will, personality, and cognitive autonomy are suppressed. The subject follows only the most direct and explicit commands its body can survive — the autonomic core is the one structure the stripping cannot reach, and orders that would end or mutilate the shell simply halt it. They retain full sensory awareness of what is happening to them.

**DURATION:** the evacuated self reasserts within the hour, and the same subject cannot be Hollowed again until the next day; this Cipher tests the upper boundary of acceptable authority over another consciousness, and the Paradigm audits every use.
