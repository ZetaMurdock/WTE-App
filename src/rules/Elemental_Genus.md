<p>The <b>Elemental Genus</b> commands natural forces — fire, water, earth, lightning, light, and the principles behind them. Elemental energy is the most intuitive domain in WTE: what makes Elemental practitioners dangerous is not that they use fire or ice, but that they command the <i>principle</i> behind those forces. A master of Elemental doesn't just throw lightning; they own the concept of electrical force in the space they occupy.
