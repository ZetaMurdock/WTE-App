# Elemental Genus

> "We don't throw fire. We own it. Every flame in this room answers to us."

The Elemental Genus commands natural forces — fire, water, earth, lightning, light, and the principles behind them. Elemental energy is the most intuitive domain in WTE: what makes Elemental practitioners dangerous is not that they use fire or ice, but that they command the principle behind those forces. A master of Elemental doesn't just throw lightning; they own the concept of electrical force in the space they occupy.

## Elemental Abilities · 19 Total

All Elemental abilities are available to any Inquisitor with Elemental domain access. Abilities are not gated by rank — they are gated by SS pool capacity.

### Solidify

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Classification | Trans-modification / Emission |
| SS Cost | 4 SS |
| Activation | Active (2 min) |
| Range / Area | Single Target within 25 ft (or area up to 10-ft cube) |
| Target | Substance (liquid, gas, semi-solid) |

Imposes elemental state change on a target substance, forcing it to solid form. Water becomes ice; gas becomes brittle mineral; loose earth becomes compacted stone. The solidification persists for 3 rounds before the substance naturally returns to its original state. Solidified gas creates a temporary barrier. Solidified liquid can trap creatures (Physical Save — Recovery to resist encasement).

**LIMIT:** Once per substance type per encounter

### Meta Change

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Classification | Trans-modification |
| SS Cost | 15 SS |
| Activation | Active (3 min) |
| Range / Area | One elemental source or active elemental effect within 30 ft Requires Physical Contact |
| Target | One element type |

Transforms one elemental type into another. Fire can become Water, Lightning can become Earth, the converted element retains its original scale, mass, and intensity—a raging wildfire becomes an equally massive flood, while a small spark becomes a small stream. Once transformed, the new element behaves according to its natural properties. This ability cannot convert any element into Null or Eldritch energy. Environmental elements are transformed permanently. Ability-generated elements remain transformed for 3 rounds, after which they revert to their original state. The effectiveness of the transmutation is determined by a Mental Check — Capacity. Full Success: The transmutation succeeds at full potency. Minimum Success: The transmutation succeeds, but with Diminished Effects, reducing its overall effectiveness. Failure: The transmutation fails entirely. Any affected matter or abilities immediately revert to their original elemental state, undoing all changes. Only the Core Four Elements and their recognized Sub-Elements may be transmuted. Minerals may also be transformed into other valid minerals or elements. However, fundamental particles and their byproducts—such as atoms, quarks, and other subatomic structures—cannot be altered through this ability. Earth is the only Element that can be transformed into minerals and vice versa.

**LIMIT:** Once per encounter
