<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Prisoner"><b>The Prisoner</b></span></h2>
<p>Shackled and chained, the Prisoner is an archetype created and forged by the character known as Grail. He was the first Prisoner, and in his confinement, he manifested the first-ever Archetype. Over time, even gods and entities sought to wield the power of the Prisoner. However, Grail transcended his own imprisonment, becoming Grail the Wanderer, forever immune to being imprisoned again.
</p>
<h2><span id="Theme_.26_Role"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Theme_&amp;_Role">Theme &amp; Role</span></h2>
<p>The Prisoner archetype is built around the <b>concept of imprisonment, restriction, and control</b>. Its power is derived from various forms of <b>binding, sealing, and subjugation</b>, whether physical, magical, or spiritual.
</p><p>Key Aspects of the Theme:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Chains &amp; Shackles</b> – Physical and magical chains that restrict movement or power.</li>
<li><b>Cages &amp; Dungeons</b> – Manifesting prison-like structures in the metaphysical and physical worlds.</li>
<li><b>Vows &amp; Oaths</b> – Forcing enemies into <b>unbreakable contracts</b> that grant power to the Prisoner.</li>
<li><b>Banishing &amp; Sealing</b> – Removing threats from existence or locking them away. The Prisoner is a <b>Control-Based</b> archetype specializing in battlefield manipulation. They force situations to play out in <b>their favor</b> by:
<ul><li><b>Locking down movement</b> (trapping enemies in physical or magical cages).</li>
<li><b>Forcing enemies to adhere to rules</b> (creating pacts that give the Prisoner advantages).</li>
<li><b>Siphoning power</b> from bound creatures (draining resources, life force, or abilities).</li>
<li><b>Controlling entire areas</b> (manifesting prisons, altering the environment).  The archetype <b>excels at crowd control</b> and <b>power siphoning</b>, making it a strong choice for players who prefer <b>strategic and methodical gameplay</b> over direct combat.</li></ul></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Main_Rolls_Used_in_the_Prisoner_Archetype:"><b>Main Rolls Used in the Prisoner Archetype:</b></span></h2>
<ul><li><b>D20</b> – Used for <b>checks</b>, determining success or failure in various binding abilities.</li>
<li><b>D150</b> – Determines the <b>strength and duration</b> of dungeon manifestations, summoning, and large-scale imprisonments.</li>
<li><b>Wisdom Rolls</b> – Often used when targets resist imprisonment, requiring them to roll against a set difficulty.</li></ul>

<h2><span id="Classification_.26_Playstyles"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Classification_&amp;_Playstyles"><b>Classification &amp; Playstyles</b></span></h2>
<p>The Prisoner archetype <b>splits into multiple playstyles</b>, allowing different approaches to control and restriction.
</p>
<h4><span id="Sub-Archetypes_.26_Specializations"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Sub-Archetypes_&amp;_Specializations"><b>Sub-Archetypes &amp; Specializations</b></span></h4>
<ol><li><b>The Forsaken</b> – A Prisoner who <b>inflicts curses</b> through vows, applying severe debuffs to their enemies.
<ul><li>Best for <b>curse-based combat and weakening foes over time</b>.</li>
<li>Can force opponents into <b>negative pacts</b> that drain their strength.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>The Dungeonborne</b> – A Prisoner who <b>creates entire dungeon realms</b>, shaping the battlefield to their advantage.
<ul><li>Best for <b>territory control and summoning creatures</b>.</li>
<li>Can <b>trap enemies inside an alternate space</b>, limiting their escape.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>The Enchained</b> – A Prisoner who wields <b>cursed chains</b>, turning movement and attacks into binding techniques.
<ul><li>Best for <b>aggressive lockdown gameplay</b>.</li>
<li>Can <b>physically restrain enemies and drain their power</b>.</li></ul></li>
<li><b>The Restricted</b> – A Prisoner that manipulates <b>laws and restrictions</b>, creating paradoxes.
<ul><li>Best for <b>forcing rule-based power</b>.</li>
<li>Can <b>undo or rewrite fundamental restrictions</b>, including <b>resurrecting the dead or sealing divine beings</b>.</li></ul></li></ol>

<h2><span id="Summary_.26_Playstyle"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Summary_&amp;_Playstyle"><b>Summary &amp; Playstyle</b></span></h2>
<p>The <b>Prisoner Archetype</b> is an <b>advanced control class</b>, best suited for players who enjoy:
</p><p>✅ <b>Tactical play</b> – Using <b>traps, vows, and constraints</b> to manipulate enemies.
</p><p>✅ <b>Resource control</b> – <b>Siphoning abilities, life, and movement</b> from bound foes.
</p><p>✅ <b>Long-term strategy</b> – Setting up <b>big plays and battlefield control</b> over time.
</p><p>However, it is <b>not ideal</b> for players who prefer:
</p><p>❌ <b>Fast-paced, high-damage combat</b> – The Prisoner is <b>slow and methodical</b>.
</p><p>❌ <b>Free movement &amp; mobility</b> – Their power lies in controlling an area, <b>not in speed</b>.
</p>
