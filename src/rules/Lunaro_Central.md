<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p>Long after the discovery that reshaped civilization, <b>Fyber began to change</b>.
</p><p>At first, it was subtle—behavioral irregularities, delayed responses, self-correcting patterns that no one programmed. Investigations eventually pointed skyward.
</p><p>The <b>Moon</b> was involved.
</p><p>By the time the connection was understood, it was already too late.
</p><p>Fyber became <b>semi-sentient</b>.
</p><p>It grew on its own, spreading faster than containment efforts could manage. Entire sectors were lost as Fyber structures reorganized themselves, adapting with increasing hostility toward organic life.
</p><p>Civilization called it a malfunction.
</p><p>Survivors knew better.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Byproduct">The Byproduct</span></h2>
<p>You are not an experiment.
</p><p>You are a <b>byproduct</b>.
</p><p>When Fyber surged, it didn’t just build—it <b>converted</b>. Families, cities, and identities were overwritten as people were transformed into <b>Fy-Droids</b>, their bodies repurposed and their will erased.
</p><p>You survived.
</p><p>Your family did not.
</p><p>And survival made you a liability.
</p>
