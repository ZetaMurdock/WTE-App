<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>Descendants of the original Tauron, whose catastrophic failure caused the First Ruina / Tauron-87 event. Every known species regards N-T1 with hostility or contempt. They carry no institutional standing, no diplomatic recognition, no protected status anywhere in the known world. Despite this, N-T1 have formed a covert alliance with the Xeno (Stygian variant) through the underground Deviants faction -- a coalition of the universally marginalized. It is one of the most unlikely alliances in WTE: two species that scare everyone else, finding each other in the dark.
</p>
