<p>&gt; The **Primordials** are towering enforcers born from the original energies of creation and destruction. Standing between 145 and 394 feet, they possess overwhelming physical presence and innate cosmic abilities. At birth, a Primordial is imbued with either the power to destroy or create, while rare individuals hold both potentials. Sealed for eons by the Enigma Engine due to their uncontrollable power, they now roam the cosmos, reshaping worlds and challenging civilizations.

<ol><li><ol><li>**Innate Species Abilities**</li></ol></li></ol>
<p>1. **Unyielding Strength**
</p>
<pre>   You possess immense physical power. Strength-based checks gain **+5**. On a perfect margin over the DC (e.g., exactly +5 over), you may make an additional attack.
</pre>
<p>2. **Force of Nature**
</p>
<pre>   When losing an Action Priority or Priority clash, you may force through your attack, dealing **half damage plus 1d20 crushing damage**. Only usable if the opponent’s priority is not overwhelmingly higher.
</pre>
<p>3. **Mass Shift**
</p>
<pre>   You can temporarily increase your body’s mass to augment attacks. Choose a value from 20 to 50, adding that to attack damage. This reduces your Action Priority by the same amount for the duration.
</pre>
<p>4. **Hand of Creation/Destruction**
</p>
<pre>   Sacrifice **25% of your maximum HP** to attempt either an instant kill or resurrection. Roll 1d100; on 50+ the effect may fail. Usable **once per day** or once per 10 turns.
