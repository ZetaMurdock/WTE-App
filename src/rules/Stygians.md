# Stygians

<div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #280a18;padding:8px 14px;margin-bottom:12px;font-family:monospace;font-size:0.82em;color:#703050;">
<p><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Species">Species</a>&nbsp;› <a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Omenity">Omenity</a>&nbsp;› <strong class="mw-selflink selflink">Stygians</strong>
</p>
</div><div style="background:#121212;border-left:4px solid #381028;padding:18px 22px;margin-bottom:18px;"><div style="font-size:1.6em;font-weight:bold;color:#702048;letter-spacing:0.06em;font-family:monospace;">STYGIANS</div><div style="color:#703050;font-size:0.85em;margin-top:4px;font-style:italic;">Group: Asternem / Aberrant — Shadow and void-based aberrant species. High Feral threat rating. Progenitor of multiple engineered variants.</div></div><div style="background:#0f0f0f;border:1px solid #280a18;border-left:3px solid #381028;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:18px;color:#a07090;font-size:0.9em;line-height:1.7;">
<p>Stygians carry the highest Feral Eminence of any species (+20). They are systemically flagged as high-threat beings regardless of personal behavior, record, or intent. Every interaction with CAS infrastructure, Paradigm systems, and most institutional frameworks begins from a position of assumed suspicion.
</p><p>Their variants reveal the breadth of what was done to Stygians by external forces. Greys were produced by the Doalith Dominion attempting to create a controlled Stygian. Xeno were engineered as biological warfare assets. Annunaki may share only faint Stygian lineage — their origins point to something older. Not one of these variants came from natural Stygian evolution.
</p><p>The <b>Stygian Hive</b> — a collective consciousness network — maintains some connection across all Stygians, though depth varies per individual. Stygian low Dom / high Rec values mean their traits frequently recede in hybrid offspring: the Stygian presence in a hybrid lineage is often the first thing lost.
</p>
</div>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="INNATE_ABILITIES">INNATE ABILITIES</span></h2>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #280a18;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:16px;">
<table class="wikitable" style="width:100%;background:#1a1a1a;border-collapse:collapse;color:#c0a0b0;">

<th style="background:#200818;color:#702048;padding:8px;">Description
</th></tr>
<tr>
<td style="padding:7px;font-weight:bold;color:#e090c0;">Interstitial Intrusion
</td>
<td style="padding:7px;">Passive
</td>
<td style="padding:7px;">Whenever you position yourself physically between two objects or creatures within your <b>Zone of Influence (30-ft radius)</b>, you violently warp their local space, inflicting the <b>Stinous</b> condition. Under the effects of <b>Stinous</b>, physical geometry distorts unpredictably (e.g., solid walls begin to spiral, and biological targets appear massively bloated in some areas and emaciated in others). A Stinous target suffers a penalty to all checks and rolls equal to your <b>Inspiration Modifier</b>. Additionally, any action or attack they direct at a target other than you is reduced by this same modifier.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td style="padding:7px;font-weight:bold;color:#e090c0;">Engraving
</td>
<td style="padding:7px;">Passive
</td>
<td style="padding:7px;">When a creature or object afflicted with the <b>Stinous</b> condition exits your <b>Influence Range</b>, you can make an opposed <b>Inspiration + Influence Check</b> against their Wisdom. On a success, you <i>engrave</i> your presence into their shadow. While engraved, you may teleport adjacent to them from their shadow as a <b>Bonus Action</b>. Additionally, you can execute any active <b>Genus</b> or <b>Cipher</b> ability using their shadow as the origin point of the effect.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td style="padding:7px;font-weight:bold;color:#e090c0;">Parasitic Shadow
</td>
<td style="padding:7px;">Active
</td>
<td style="padding:7px;"><b>Activation:</b> Standard Action | <b>Range:</b> Touch While standing in shadow, you can extend dark, entropic tendrils to touch the shadow of a target within melee range. Declare a d20 threshold value (e.g., 15). Whenever that target makes a check or save where the natural d20 roll meets or exceeds your declared threshold, the action backfires. The roll fails, and you can redirect the full force, damage, or effect of that action onto any <b>Stinous</b> creature within your <b>Influence Range</b>.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td style="padding:7px;font-weight:bold;color:#e090c0;">Locked in time
</td>
<td style="padding:7px;">Active
</td>
<td style="padding:7px;"><b>Activation:</b> Prepared Action | <b>Range:</b> Zone of Influence While positioned interstitially between two objects or creatures, you can distort their temporal perception, causing linear time to feel agonizingly elongated. To execute any action, an affected <b>Stinous</b> target must succeed on a <b>Wisdom Check</b> against a DC equal to your <b>Inspiration DC + Inspiration Modifier</b>. On a failure, their <b>Action Priority is suppressed</b>, and any action or ability they attempt takes three times longer to resolve (consuming triple the normal AP cost or forcing them to accumulate 2 points of <b>Fatigue</b>).
</td></tr></tbody></table>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="HYBRID_INTERACTION">HYBRID INTERACTION</span></h2>
<div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #280a18;padding:14px 18px;margin-bottom:16px;color:#a07090;font-size:0.9em;">
<p>Stygians Dom 20 / Rec 35 — the lowest Dominance and second-highest Recessiveness in the species roster. Stygian traits are the most likely to recede in hybrid offspring of any species. Shadow Meld (passive) is the ability most likely to persist in partial expression; the Hive Conjugation and Dimensional Flicker are the most likely to suppress. A hybrid with significant Stygian ancestry may appear to carry no Stygian traits at all — and still be flagged by the Stygian Hive network.
</p>
