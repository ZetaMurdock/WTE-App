<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Director_Characters">Director Characters</span></h2>
<p><i>Not every creature the party meets needs six stats and a full ability list. Some people just live here.</i>
</p><p>Director Characters (DCs) are the simplified statblock format used for NPCs, minor enemies, faction members, Stalker contacts, and any other non-player entity that needs mechanical weight but not the full complexity of a player character sheet. The format prioritizes what Directors actually need at the table: enough to run the character, not so much that it slows the scene.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="When_to_Use_Director_Character_Format">When to Use Director Character Format</span></h3>
<ul><li>Bystanders and civilians who might be caught in a scene</li>
<li>Stalker contacts, informants, merchants</li>
<li>Faction soldiers and guards (use the same template across an entire unit)</li>
<li>Minor antagonists and one-encounter threats</li>
<li>Alter Anima at CL 1-2 (early transformation, still functionally human)</li></ul>
<p>For major antagonists, recurring NPCs, Alter Anima at CL 3+, or any creature the party will face as a real combat encounter, use the full Alter Anima or Anima statblock formats instead.
</p>

</p><p><b>Behavior:</b> [One sentence. How do they act under pressure?]

</p>

</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Note for Directors:</b> DC abilities should be simple and immediate. If you find yourself writing more than two sentences for a DC ability, it belongs on a full statblock.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Threat_Levels">Threat Levels</span></h3>
<p>Director Characters are tagged with a threat level to help Directors communicate encounter weight quickly:
</p>
<table class="fandom-table">
<tbody><tr>
<th>Tag</th>
<th>HP Range</th>
<th>Meaning
</th></tr>
<tr>
<td><b>[Soft]</b></td>
<td>15-35</td>
<td>Goes down fast. Useful in groups. Do not build encounters around a single Soft DC.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td><b>[Moderate]</b></td>
<td>36-70</td>
<td>Requires attention. Can threaten individual party members if focused.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td><b>[Hard]</b></td>
<td>71-110</td>
<td>A real problem. Best used as a named threat with a motivation the party can engage.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td><b>[Named]</b></td>
<td>111+</td>
<td>Should have a full statblock. Director Characters do not usually reach this range.
</td></tr></tbody></table>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Running_Multiple_Director_Characters">Running Multiple Director Characters</span></h3>
<p>For faction units (multiple guards, a squad of Stalkers, etc.), Directors can apply the same statblock to the entire unit. Recommended modifications:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Unit HP:</b> Treat the entire unit as a single pool divided by number of members. When one member's share is depleted, they go down.</li>
<li><b>Unit Morale:</b> When the unit drops below 50% (half members down), all remaining members make WIL Check (DC 10) or the unit breaks -- some flee, some surrender, only the most committed remain.</li>
<li><b>Named Units:</b> Give one member a name and +10 HP. The party will focus them. That is useful for Directors who want to create a memorable face for a faction unit.</li></ul>
