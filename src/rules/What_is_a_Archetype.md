<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="What_Archetypes_Are">What Archetypes Are</span></h2>
<p>Archetypes are the foundational identity of every Actor in <i>Wonderland of the Enigma</i>.
</p><p>They define your <b>core mechanics</b>, <b>your natural bonuses</b>, <b>your Aspect</b>, and the <b>pathway to your Subtype</b>.
</p><p>Every Archetype has:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Role</b> – how the Archetype behaves in combat</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Prime Stats</b> – stat requirements necessary to embody the Archetype</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Aspect</b> – an innate ability the player receives at Level 1</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Subtype</b> – a specialization unlocked after gaining <b>3 Asymmetries</b></li></ul>
<p>Archetypes are not restrictive classes; they’re <b>narrative and mechanical frameworks</b> that shape how your Actor interacts with the Stage. Your growth is defined by the stats you choose, the Aspect you master, and whether you branch into <b>Subtype Linking</b> (multiclassing).
</p>
<hr>
<h1><span id="Prime_Stats_.26_Requirements"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Prime_Stats_&amp;_Requirements">Prime Stats &amp; Requirements</span></h1>
<p>Prime Stats represent the <i>minimum innate traits</i> required to function as the Archetype.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="How_Requirements_Work">How Requirements Work</span></h3>
<p>Each Archetype lists several stat bonuses (ex: <i>Strength +3, Dexterity +1</i>).
</p><p>These bonuses represent:
</p>
<ol><li><b>Natural stat increases gained at Level 1</b></li></ol>
<ol><li><b>Your stat thresholds needed if you want to Subtype Link into another Archetype</b></li></ol>
<p>For Subtype Linking:
</p>
<ul><li>You must meet the <b>Prime Stat bonuses</b> of both Archetypes you combine.</li></ul>
<ul><li>You must still reach <b>3 Asymmetries</b> in your primary before unlocking its Subtype.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Linking gives access to the <i>Aspect</i> of your primary only, but allows you to use the <i>skills</i> of both.</li></ul>
<hr>
<h1><span class="mw-headline" id="Aspect_System_Overview">Aspect System Overview</span></h1>
<p>Every Archetype grants a <b>unique Aspect</b> — a thematic core mechanic that shapes your toolkit.
</p><p>Aspects are:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Innate</b> (you get them at Level 1)</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Always active or passively present</b></li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Foundational</b> to your identity and future Subtype</li></ul>
<ul><li><b>Non-transferable</b> (Subtype Linking does not give other Aspects)</li></ul>
