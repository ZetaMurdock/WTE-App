<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Attributes">Attributes</span></h2>
<p>Mechanic Description: Archetype/Subarchetype Attribute Modulation
In this system, characters possess specific archetypes, each associated with distinct abilities and attributes. However, the innovation lies in the concept of Attribute Modulation, where the attributes linked to these archetypes can fundamentally alter the functionality of skills or abilities. Moreover, damage types add an additional layer of complexity and customization to combat encounters.
</p><p>How It Works:
</p>
<ol><li>Archetypes and Attributes:
<ul><li>Characters are defined by their archetypes, such as Alchemical Maestro or Elemental Catalyst, each tied to unique abilities.</li>
<li>Every archetype comes with associated attributes, reflecting their expertise or inherent qualities. For instance, Fear Induction for Thugs or Transmutation Mastery for Alchemy Artisans.</li></ul></li>
<li>Skill Modulation:
<ul><li>Attributes aren't confined to specific abilities but can be applied universally to modify or enhance the effects of skills.</li>
<li>By incorporating an archetype's attribute into the crafting of a skill or ability, its fundamental behavior can be altered. For instance, infusing a spell with Fear Induction causes opponents to be momentarily stunned upon impact.</li></ul></li>
<li>Enhanced Customization:
<ul><li>This mechanic offers players unparalleled customization options, allowing them to tailor their skills according to their character's archetype and playstyle.</li>
<li>Whether it's augmenting combat techniques, spellcasting, or crafting, the attribute modulation system empowers players to create unique and diverse strategies.</li></ul></li>
<li>Damage Types:
<ul><li>In addition to attribute modulation, the inclusion of various damage types further diversifies combat mechanics.</li>
<li>From conventional types like Slashing and Bludgeoning to elemental ones such as Fire, Cold, and Lightning, players can strategically exploit enemy weaknesses or resistances.</li>
<li>Esoteric damage types like Radiant, Necrotic, or Psychic add a fantastical element, offering intriguing possibilities for character builds and encounter design.</li></ul></li></ol>
