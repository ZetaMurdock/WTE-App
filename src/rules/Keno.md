<p>You are awake.

<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background_Lore">Background Lore</span></h2>
<p><b>Keno</b> is not a place you visit.
</p><p>It is a <b>dimension you are taken by</b>.
</p><p>At some point, you interacted with Keno — knowingly or not. This may have occurred through:
</p>
<ul><li>Being <b>[[e’Inderitch]]</b>, or</li></ul>
<ul><li>Direct contact with the dimension itself, or</li></ul>
<ul><li>Interaction with one of its inhabitants</li></ul>
<p>The moment this occurred, Keno <b>engulfed you</b>.
</p><p>Your memories were erased.
</p><p>You awoke inside a <b>dream</b>.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Dream-State">The Dream-State</span></h2>
<p>Your body continues to exist and function in reality.
</p><p>You act, travel, fight, and speak as normal.
</p><p>But your <b>mind and soul are elsewhere</b> — trapped within Keno’s dream.
</p><p>No one can know this.
</p><p>No one can discover it.
</p><p>If another being comes to understand your condition in the same way you did, <b>the same fate befalls them</b>.
</p><p>Keno does not share witnesses.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Your_End_Goal">Your End Goal</span></h2>
<p>One of your character’s ultimate goals is fixed:
</p><p><b>Find a way out of the dream.</b>
</p><p>Whether escape is possible is unknown.
</p><p>Whether you should escape is worse.
</p><p>Even if you succeed, Keno will remember you.
</p>
<hr>
<h2><span id="Xaeil.E2.80.99Untais"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Xaeil’Untais">Xaeil’Untais</span></h2>
<p>Keno is not empty.
</p><p>If <b>three or more characters</b> with the <b>Keno</b> background are present together, they will be <b>called home</b> by <b>Xaeil’Untais</b>.
</p><p>What “home” means is not defined.
</p><p>What answers the call is not negotiable.
</p>
