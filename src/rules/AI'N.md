<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Lore">Lore</span></h3>
<p>The AI'N did not emerge from a lab directive or species-wide program. They emerged from individual Inderi who asked a question the rest of their kind found uncomfortable: if the self is just information, why anchor it to biology? The first AI'N were considered philosophers. The second generation were considered unsettling. By the third generation, they were a recognized variant with documented behavioral patterns, legal standing debates in most jurisdictions, and a dedicated black market for chassis upgrades. They maintain physical form not because they need to but because the physical world still requires interfaces -- for now.
</p>
