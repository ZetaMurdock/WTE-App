<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Alchemist">The Alchemist</span></h2>
<p>Vella Erdora was a fascinating character who played a pivotal role in a war instigated by King Regulus against the Nigraldi. She was the chief creator of potions and tinctures designed to combat the horde of insatiable beasts, making her an indispensable part of the war effort. Though she was vital to the cause and contributed greatly, Vella's fate took a dark turn when she became afflicted by the Nigraldi, resulting in a horrific transformation.
</p><p>However, this curse also enhanced her abilities, amplifying her talents in ways no one could have anticipated. As her powers grew, so did the mistrust from the people she once served, though she was never cast aside. In the midst of her turmoil, Vella found solace in her predicament and embraced the changes she had undergone. Through this acceptance, she cultivated her own unique archetype and became the first Alchemist.
</p>
