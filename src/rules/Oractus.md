<p>&gt; The **Oractus** are the enhanced progeny of the Oracle, designed as a contingency to preserve their creator’s legacy. Physically fragile yet mentally potent, they range from 4 to 9 feet tall and feature heads composed of pure energy reflecting their personalities. Freed from a pocket dimension within the Inner Sphere, they now seek resurgence across the Great Expanse. Oractus rely on intellect, subtle influence, and mastery of spirit and chaos energies rather than raw physical power.

<p>1. **Oracle's Blessing**
</p>
<pre>   Your presence subtly enforces influence. Beings within 30 feet who are less controlled than you take **-2 to rolls and -5 to action priority**.
</pre>
<p>2. **Oractus Oath**
</p>
<pre>   You can enthrall creatures within 30 feet if they fail a Control DC 13 check. Enthralled creatures suffer **-1 to all rolls**. You may siphon minor energy from them to fuel your own abilities.
</pre>
<p>3. **Oracle’s Mind**
</p>
<pre>   Your intellect allows you to process and retain vast amounts of information. Intelligence-based DC checks up to DC 15 automatically succeed once per scene if repeated. Gain **+1 to all skill points in racial and archetype trees**.
</pre>
