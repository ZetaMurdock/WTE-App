</div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;border-left:3px solid #6e50a0;padding:20px 24px;margin-bottom:16px"><div style="color:#909090;font-style:italic;font-size:12px;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.8"><i>They were people. They made choices. The celestial field listened.</i></div><div style="color:#dedede;font-size:11.5px;line-height:1.8">Alter Anima are Stalkers who have undergone prolonged, unmanaged exposure to Anima in celestial-saturated environments. They are not possessed. They are not corrupted from without. What has happened to them is stranger and, arguably, more accurate than possession: they have externalized. The self — the emotional and psychological core of who they are — has become visible. Physical. The celestial field did not change them. It revealed them.</div></div><div style="background:#121212;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:20px 24px;margin-bottom:16px"><div style="font-size:9.5px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:2px;color:#6e50a0;font-weight:700;border-bottom:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding-bottom:8px;margin-bottom:16px">THE PROCESS</div><div style="color:#dedede;font-size:11.5px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:14px">The celestial field that produces Anima does not merely generate hostile entities — it reads and responds to emotional resonance. Extended exposure to Anima in these fields creates a feedback loop: the field reads the Stalker's dominant emotional state and begins to externalize it. This process accelerates dramatically in proximity to Manifested or Apex Anima.</div><div style="color:#dedede;font-size:11.5px;line-height:1.8;margin-bottom:14px">The transformation is not possession. It is revelation. The Alter Anima is still the person they were. Their psychology, memories, and goals persist. What changes is their form — and their capabilities — in ways that perfectly reflect the dominant emotional truth of who they have always been.</div><div style="background:#0d0d0d;border:1px solid #1e1e1e;padding:12px 16px;margin-bottom:14px"><div style="font-size:9px;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:1px;color:#565656;font-weight:700;margin-bottom:10px">SIN-SPECIFIC MANIFESTATIONS</div>
