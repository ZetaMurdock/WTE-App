<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Character_Breakdown">Character Breakdown</span></h2>
<p>Within the "Wonderland of the Enigma" tabletop role-playing game, character creation is one of the most important aspects. When creating a character, there are a few things you should keep in mind: a character concept and a good directional flow. This means knowing how to go about making your character, which is what I will be explaining on this page. I will be breaking down terminology as well, which is imperative.
</p><p>Character creation is not meant to seem daunting or complicated; it really isn't. The system gives you a lot of personalization, so we will try to minimize the daunting nature while maximizing the creative flow. So, let's begin.
</p>

An archetype can be used both in and out of combat. Archetypes give access to particular trees of passives that allow for a multitude of possible abilities, some pre-made and some that you can create yourself.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Archetypes">Archetypes</span></h2>
<p>Alright so with the character creation lets start with the Archetype  but first lets come up with a character Concept the concept we will use a Vampiric Gunslinger that can function as Engineer
</p>
<table class="fandom-table">
<caption>character
</caption>
<tbody><tr>
<th><a class="mw-redirect" data-wte-link="wte://rules/Archetype_Skill_List">Archetype</a>
</th>
<td>The archetype is the first layer. Let's start by choosing an archetype that fits this character concept. For example, if we're a Vampiric Gunslinger, we have a few options such as Dungeonborne. This archetype could be used for familiar gathering and creature containment. Let's dissect an ability to see why:
<p>"The Dungeonborne is able to summon enemies from past battles to fight for them. The strength of the enemy will be determined by the roll of a d150. Depending on the roll, the Dungeonborne can summon monsters varying in strength close to the number rolled. However, this doesn’t mean the monster is under the control of the Dungeonborne; the creature is bound to the dungeon and cannot leave."
</p><p>This ability works very well for this concept, but there are other archetypes that might serve better. Let's explore:
</p><p>Maybe the Constructor archetype could help. Gear Genius would assist with the engineering aspect. Here is an ability from this archetype sublet:
</p><p>"Embraces unconventional thinking to create inventive solutions that defy conventional wisdom, challenging traditional notions of mechanical design."
</p><p>So, we will pick this archetype for the engineering aspect. Remember, it doesn't matter what you decide to pick when choosing something for an aspect of the character, as long as it can serve multiple purposes and not just one. Theoretically, what Dungeonborne does, we can achieve with this by mixing it with our sub-archetype, which we will discuss next.
</p>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<th><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Sub_Archetypes_Skills">Sub archetype</a>
</th>
<td>This is going to be the second layer we will be examining. Let's find an archetype that will be useful. Immediately, we find a good archetype to complement the vampiric nature under parasitic entities, called Bloodsucking. The standout skill from this archetype is:
<p>"Can control and manipulate the flow of blood within hosts." This will be imperative when using the character, and we can potentially mix this ability with our engineering abilities to create things based around blood.
</p><p>However, there is another possibly better option called Invasive. This would give us much broader potential and cover multiple aspects, including control over blood. Let's look at two of the abilities:
</p>
<ul><li>Assimilates host organs, enhancing its own abilities and functions.</li>
<li>Manipulates host cells to promote its own survival and growth.</li></ul>
<p>These abilities align well with the concept and provide our engineering abilities with more to work with. The cell manipulation abilities are particularly effective, especially since we can create unconventional things.
</p>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<th><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Race_List">Race</a>
</th>
<td>Now, finally, what's going to tie a lot of this together is the race. There are a lot of races, but there is one that directly gives us the vampiric nature we're looking for: it's called Hebilgian.
<p>These are the base abilities of Hebilgian, and they seem to fit the vampiric aspect we're looking for. Just like the others, this really enhances the engineering aspects a lot:
</p>
<ul><li>Enhanced Blood Production: The hearts of the Hebilgians produce triple the amount of blood compared to a normal Hyomen individual. Hebilgians have three hearts (one in their brain, one in their chest, and one behind the liver), and all three hearts must be dealt with to kill them.</li>
<li>Bloody Sentience: The blood of the Hebilgians can act autonomously at times. The brain can influence blood behavior and increase blood production, but this leaves the Hebilgian winded and unable to react without a short rest. After resting, all blood-based attacks gain a +5 bonus.</li>
<li>Razor Blood: All attacks involving blood will inflict an additional 3d4 bleed damage and 1d12 true damage over 35 seconds.</li>
<li>Corrupted Blood: Hebilgian blood is highly corrosive and toxic, causing their skin to harden and become damaged. Quick movements result in the Hebilgian taking 1d12 true damage each time they perform a quick action.</li></ul>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<th><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Classes">Classes</a>
</th>
<td>And then there are the classes. Classes are optional, but to enhance the Gunslinger aspect significantly, we'll choose the class called Gunsmith. Gunsmith offers some amazing abilities that specialize in gun-based creations. Here are some of the abilities that we're most interested in:
<ul><li>Create specialized rounds with unique properties, such as armor-piercing, explosive, or tranquilizing effects.</li>
<li>Forge a legendary firearm tailored to your specific style, imbuing it with mystical properties or technological advancements.</li>
<li>Master the art of weapon crafting, pushing the boundaries of innovation to create unparalleled firearms of unmatched quality.</li></ul>
<p>These three abilities will allow us to adapt our blood-based abilities to our guns, potentially making them a hundred times more powerful and adding depth to our character. Now, we move on to more lore-based personalization.
</p>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<th><a class="mw-redirect" data-wte-link="wte://rules/Factions">Faction</a>
</th>
<td>Alright, so factions are important but not crucial for character abilities. Factions are optional and primarily provide your character with a sense of belonging and purpose. Some factions may require certain tasks or commitments, adding substance and meaning to your character. For this character, we will choose the faction called Nigraldi, which offers some interesting abilities:
<ul><li>Toxic Secretions: Many Nigraldi possess glands that produce toxic substances, which they can use as deadly weapons. Their venomous saliva, corrosive spittle, or poisonous stingers inflict excruciating pain or cause fatal injuries.</li>
<li>Horrific Metamorphosis: Some Nigraldi have the ability to undergo nightmarish transformations, morphing their bodies into grotesque and monstrous forms. This grants them enhanced strength, agility, and monstrous physical attributes.</li></ul>
<p>Now, let's move on to the topic of religion.
</p>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<th><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Character_Alignment's">Religion</a>
</th>
<td>Alright, so religions are important but also optional. A religion provides substance and lore significance to your character. If you haven't developed lore for your character, you can use your religion as a foundation. Some religions offer powerful abilities, but for this character, we'll choose the <b>Order of the Shrouded Abyss.</b>
<p>Here are the abilities that we'll take advantage of:
</p>
<ul><li>Shadow Manipulation: Command and manipulate shadows for various purposes.</li>
<li>Void Affinity: Possess a deep affinity and connection to the void or the abyss.</li>
<li>Light Aversion: Feel discomfort and weakness in the presence of bright light.</li></ul>
<p>These abilities suit our needs, add to the vampiric aspect, and allow us to further develop the engineering aspect. Now, let's move on to character alignment.
</p>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<th><a data-wte-link="wte://rules/Character_Alignment's">Character Allignment</a>
</th>
<td>Character alignments are pretty simple; they determine your character's morality and worldview. The alignment for this character will be Chaotic Neutral, with a morality of Nihilism. This suggests that the character doesn't care much for anything and sees things as a means to an end. This aspect can be used to enrich the character's lore and personality. Now, let's move forward with further character development.e
</td></tr>
<tr>
<th><a class="mw-redirect" data-wte-link="wte://rules/Job's">Job</a>
</th>
<td>Finally, we move on to Jobs. Jobs play a small role in a character's overall build, but they can be necessary when needed. The job we will be choosing is Hitman, as it fits our needs the most. Here are its abilities and traits:
<p><b>Hitman:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><b>Assassin's Dagger:</b> A concealed and deadly dagger designed for swift and precise strikes, enhancing critical damage.</li>
<li><b>Shadow Cloak:</b> A magical cloak that grants limited invisibility and improved stealth capabilities.</li>
<li><b>Poisoner's Kit:</b> A collection of rare and deadly poisons, allowing the hitman to coat weapons or create traps.</li>
<li><b>Silent Boots:</b> Boots crafted with specialized materials to minimize footstep noise and increase agility during stealthy operations.</li></ul>
<p>And that concludes the character creation process. The next step involves evolving and growing your character, which will be discussed on another page. I'll send you the link to it here.
